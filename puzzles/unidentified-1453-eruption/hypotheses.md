`refuted` / `superseded by Hn`. Experiments refer to `PLAN.md`.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Kuwae (Vanuatu) produced the 1453 signal | refuted | — | Kuwae's old date was an artefact of Antarctic chronologies tied to the 1453 tree-ring minimum; well-dated cores put the bipolar spike at 1458 (Abbott 2021; Sigl 2013, 2015) |
