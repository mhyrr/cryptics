# The unidentified 1452/53 eruption (UE 1453)

**Catalog entry:** `catalog/entries/unidentified-1453-eruption.md`
**Status:** opened 2026-10-20 · experiments 01–05, 08, 09 run (session 2); next 06 candidate ranking. State in `NEXT.md`.

## Brief
A Greenland-heavy sulfate spike starts in the 1453 layer of polar ice. The
coldest Northern Hemisphere summer in about 800 years of tree-ring density
follows it. No eruption is known to have made it. We want the latitude band,
the season, a ranked shortlist of source volcanoes built from open data, and
the exact wall between that shortlist and an identification.

This is the repo's first `natural-record` dive. The "text" is the ice, the
rings and the chronicles; the author is a physical process. The admission
rule is in `rubric/RUBRIC.md` under "Natural records".

## What a solution would have to do
A hypothesis is judged against these and nothing else.

1. **Deposition.** Produce about 25 kg km⁻² in Greenland and about 5 kg km⁻²
   in Antarctica (asymmetry ≈ 0.83), with sulfate onset in the 1453 layer.
   The emulator or analogue cases must show this ratio is reachable from the
   proposed latitude and season.
2. **Date.** Have an eruption date of 1452 or early 1453 from evidence that
   does not come from polar ice (proximal 14C with IntCal20 calibration,
   dendrochronology, varves, tephrochronology, or a chronicle). A GVP date
   whose method is "Sidereal: Ice Core" does not satisfy this. It is circular.
3. **Size.** Be capable of about 10 Tg S (eVolv2k v4: 9.97 ± 3.07, 1σ),
   about half of it stratospheric (Burke 2023: 54 ± 6 %).
4. **Latitude and isotopes.** Be consistent with the TUNU2013 Δ33S/δ34S
   pattern, which Burke 2023 reads as NH extratropical. A tropical candidate
   has to explain that pattern away, not ignore it.
5. **Climate fingerprint.** Fit the 1453 cooling pattern: strongest at high
   northern latitudes (Polar Urals −6.9 °C, NW Canada −4.9 °C), weak in the
   Alps and Mediterranean, one warm outlier in British Columbia.
6. **Silence.** Account for the absence of a known eyewitness account.
7. **Identification.** Glass shards from the 1453 ice layer match the proximal
   deposit in major and trace elements. Criteria 1 to 6 can rank candidates.
   Only 7 identifies one.

## Scope of this dive
In: everything open data can constrain (criteria 1 to 6), a blind-calibrated
candidate ranking, and a match-ready glass reference set for criterion 7.
Out: fieldwork, lab work, and contacting ice-core groups without Greg's
approval. The first sub-question is latitude and season, because every
candidate ranking depends on it.
