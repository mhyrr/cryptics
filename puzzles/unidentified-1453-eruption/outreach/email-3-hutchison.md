I am an independent researcher working on the unidentified 1452/53 eruption from published open data. Your identification of the 1831 eruption as Zavaritskii, a ~12 Tg S Kuril event absent from every catalogue until its glass turned up in ice, is now my leading model for 1453.

The case in brief. Each method was first tested on eruptions with a known source.
- Burke et al.'s (2023) Δ³³S places 1453 in the Northern Hemisphere extratropics.
- TUNU2013's annual coarse-particle record shows no proximal ash: 1.2× the median, against 8.1× for Veiðivötn 1477.
- A blind-calibrated ranking of catalogued eruptions favours the North Pacific rim.
- The named candidates there (Karymsky, Shiveluch, Aniakchak Half Cone) fail or weaken on their own dating and size.

What remains is an eruption of about 10 Tg S, onset between December 1452 and June 1453, very possibly uncatalogued.
