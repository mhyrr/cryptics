**Subject:** TUNU2013 particle record around the 1453 sulfate onset

Dear Professor Plunkett,

I am an independent researcher working on the unidentified 1452/53 eruption from published open data. Your 2023 paper gives the TUNU2013 particle record as available on request, and I would be grateful for the sub-annual counts over 1452.5–1454.0 (about 81.7–82.2 m), including the < 4.5 µm fraction if it was measured.

Why that interval and that fraction: the annual coarse record (Abbott et al. 2021, Table S7) shows 1453 at 1.2× the 1450–1500 median, against 8.1× for Veiðivötn 1477. That argues against a nearby Icelandic source. Burke et al.'s (2023) Δ³³S places the source in the Northern Hemisphere extratropics, and my open-data tests favour the North Pacific rim. Ash from that distance would be fine-grained, so it would sit below the published size cut.

A second, smaller question: do you know whether the 1453 layer has been searched for glass in TUNU2013 or NEEM? Your 2023 compilation has no Greenland tephra between 1254/5 and 1477, and I can find nothing published since.
