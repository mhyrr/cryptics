## 2026-10-16 (later) — Survey of DigiVatLib cipher material
- Greg asked for a sweep of free Vatican downloads holding undeciphered cipher, key known or not.
- Catalogue keyword survey (39 terms) → 326 shelfmarks, 111 specific; online status probed; every online candidate sampled by eye (`analysis/03-bav-survey/`).
- Borg.lat.898 is the Borg cipher, solved 2017. Barb.lat.9848–49 (Mancini, Madrid 1644–55) have digit cipher with interlinear period decipherment. Cappon.164's "lettere in cifra" are plaintext decipherments. Urb.lat.948/949/998 and Borg.lat.565 are keys. Several catalogue "cifra" hits are plaintext.
- No online candidate found with ciphertext lacking a decipherment or a modern solution. The Barberini agents' series Barb.lat.9850–9886 (Madrid etc., 1644–1705, "cifre, decifrati") is not digitized.
- The catalogue misses cipher volumes catalogued "shelfmark only" (Barb.lat.6956, 6960). Range sampling of the Barberini nunciature series is the only complete method. Not done.

## 2026-10-16 — Opening: Barb.lat.6960
- Recovered from the 2026-10-16 morning session (Vargas thread) the three DigiVatLib cipher volumes recommended then: Barb.lat.6956, Barb.lat.6960, Urb.lat.998. Barb.lat.570 (Greg's link) is an 8th-century Gospel book, used then by mistake as a generic DigiVatLib link.
- Parsed the cached DECODE listing: 435 Vatican records. Barb.lat.6956 has 70 (60 "non-decrypted"), Barb.lat.6960 has 28 + 1, all "non-decrypted"; Urb.lat.998 has 56 key records.
- Downloaded Barb.lat.6960 (`analysis/01-download/`). Images are ~700 px/page microfilm.
- Found Kiewning vol. 2 (1629) on archive.org (`4-2_20200807`). It prints Pallotto's 1629 cipher dispatches from the Rome decipherments (Nunz. di Germ. 118–119) with decipherment dates. Klausis's 2018 commenters were right that vol. 1 (1628) lacks the Barb.lat.6956 letters; vol. 2 does cite Barberini library volumes.
- Read line 1 of image 0005 ("Di Vienna 4 di Agosto") by eye and decoded it with Tomokiyo's 1628 key (`analysis/02-key-check/`): it aligns with Kiewning Nr. 153 "Ho ricevuta la risposta". H1 opened as supported by one line; H2 and H3 opened.
- Consequence: the "all non-decrypted" DECODE status means no plaintext in DECODE, not unread content. The content of at least this letter is printed.
