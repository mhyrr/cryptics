## What is new
- No published or repository reading of ff. 62–64 found. The King's own record of **Saint-Goard's audience** at the
  Bosque de Segovia in June 1578: Henri III disowns Alençon's Flanders expedition, has issued prohibitions, and fears
  "malintencionados" will say it was done with his knowledge; Philip answers that he will judge "por las obras" and
  warns that if Alençon goes on, Spain "no dexará de hazer lo que conviniesse". With f. 89 (Maintenon, Sep 1578) it gives
  two royal audiences on the Alençon crisis, both unpublished.
- An Alençon–Vendôme (Navarre) concert: if Alençon attacked Flanders, Navarre would move on [Spanish Navarre]; the
  viceroy Sancho de Leyva warned.
- Philip's stated wish for the liberty of Mary Queen of Scots, to be conveyed to the Scots ambassador.
