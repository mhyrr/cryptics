## What is new
- No published or repository reading found. The King's view (15 Oct 1578) of a Franciscan's offered verbal message as
  "una de las invenciones de la Reyna madre"; his order to let it drop; his request that Vargas press Henri III to act
  "rostro y severo" against Protestant night preaching in Paris; caution on Alençon and the Guise contact.
