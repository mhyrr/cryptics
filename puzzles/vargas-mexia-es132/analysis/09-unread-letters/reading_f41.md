# ff. 41–43: Philip II to Juan de Vargas Mexía, Madrid, [?] April 1578 — first reading

Four weeks after the murder of Escobedo (31 Mar 1578). Witnesses A, B (`f41_A_norm.txt`, `f41_B_norm.txt`), decoded-text
agreement **92% / 94%**. Date (clear): "De Madrid a [xxix?] de Abril de 1578" (day numeral unresolved by both readers;
Tomokiyo's TOC dates ff. 41, 44, 46 to 29 April 1578). Both readers' decodes write "a" as the letter-group "co" in this
clerk's hand (shown below silently as "a"). **"Antonio Pérez" is decoded at the same five places by both readers.**

## External check
Paz 1914 (SCHOLARLY finding aid), AGS Estado K 1546 (B. 44), "Correspondencia de D. Juan de Vargas Mexía con Felipe II y
con Antonio Pérez", Jan–Jun 1578: "**Negociaciones para hacer una liga con el Rey de Francia contra los calvinistas, sin
nombrar otros herejes para no ofender a los alemanes y a la Reina de Inglaterra y arreglar por este medio las cosas de
Flandes.**" Our letter is the King's account of how that negotiation began in Madrid.

## What is new
- No published or repository reading of f. 41 found (cabinet-noir read ff. 44, 46 of the same day, not f. 41).
- The Madrid origin of the 1578 Franco-Spanish league proposal against the Calvinists (Paz K 1546): the French resident
  ambassador (Saint-Goard), after a visit from a Mos de [V]elli[?], proposed a joint punishment of both kings' rebels
  and a remedy against Elizabeth, asked to deal through **Antonio Pérez**, and Pérez reported to the King and carried the
  King's answer back. Late April 1578, four weeks after Escobedo's murder, Pérez is the King's channel for this secret
  negotiation.
- The King's instruction to Vargas to keep the Guise channel and the Madrid channel apart, "sin que los unos sepan de
  los otros", and to feign ignorance in Paris unless approached.
