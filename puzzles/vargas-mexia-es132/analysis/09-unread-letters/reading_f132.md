## What is new
- No published or repository reading of ff. 129/132 found. One month after Don John's death the King keeps the Gravelines
  plot alive "aunque aya faltado mi hermano"; uses **the padre Hernández** (cf. ff. 174, 185) as go-between with the
  Guises and as a channel already proposed by Vargas in August; announces **don Alonso de Sotomayor**'s mission to Paris
  to exploit the Guise contacts; asks after the Christine of Lorraine–Savoy match and Alençon's English voyage.
