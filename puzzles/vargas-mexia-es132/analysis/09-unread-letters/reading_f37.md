## What is new
- No published or repository reading of this letter was found (exp 09 inventory). Paz summarises Vargas's side only.
- The King's replies, fifteen days after the Escobedo murder: (1) an order to find out everything about the fleets
  being fitted out for the Indies route (numbers, men, time, route, purpose) and report at once; (2) caution on the
  Bordeaux Parlement proposal to seize the lands of the Prince of Béarn, "semejantes propuestas [por] vía de tentativa";
  (3) approval of the arrest of Ayala ("sus delictos lo merescen"), with an instruction involving a don Juan de C…ga.
- No mention of Escobedo, Pérez, Éboli or Don John's secretary in the cipher (Don John appears in clear: a packet "que
  importa mucho que llegue con brevedad y seguridad").
