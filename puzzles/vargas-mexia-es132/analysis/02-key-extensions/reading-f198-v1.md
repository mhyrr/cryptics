## What the cipher carries, in one paragraph (interpretation)
The cipher carries everything Pérez wanted hidden from interceptors. The King had meant to give
him an office "of Vargas", then changed his mind and decided to split it up. Pérez blames the delay
on Escovedo's lawsuit. In his account, that suit ended in his favour: his innocence won, and the
other side's claim fell on "weak foundations". The suit and its "falso testimonio" still cost him
the office. He has asked the King for leave to retire and to live away from the people who seek to
take "his honour and his life". The King refused. The Archbishop of Toledo (Quiroga, by office)
had the Princess of Éboli urge Pérez to stay, swearing that the King would much regret his going,
and promising favours if he stayed. This is three months before his arrest on 28 July 1579.

## Open points
- [ra] is clear "ra" in a cipher run (reader B kept it as clear text): possibly an abbreviation
  mark or a code. Unresolved.
- "Vargas" here may be an office-holder of that name (not the addressee). The cipher reads
  "de Vargas" four times; who is meant is a historical question, not settled here.
- "Quiroga" after "los Vélez" (l. 9) depends on 21_ = qui; it fits the Archbishop of Toledo,
  Gaspar de Quiroga, named by office on f. 199r. Hypothesis, not established.
- The nulls (caret) and A/B disagreements on marks still need a reconciled transcription.
