## Summary
The cipher continues on the Portuguese question: the Catholic King (Philip II) is held to be taking every step, letting no time be lost, and an extraordinary council is mentioned after the assembly.
The writer advises Your Majesty that the direction of affairs requires care, and that the Catholic King means to settle Portugal.
The ambassador resident (of Spain) contradicted what the ambassador in France had told him: that Monsieur de La Mothe-Fénelon and the nuncio resident in France had written about peace / about a mediation (?).
Someone is said to be now in Portugal, which the writer does not believe, on the strength of his latest letters.
Clear text: whatever the outcome in Portugal, the Catholic King will soon leave for Seville to keep up the pressure, his house there is being hastily repaired; the writer will keep Their Majesties informed if he is given the means to do so.
