## Summary
The page names Don Pedro (de Medicis) and the general of the galleys of Sicily, and a Sicilian baron whom someone struck with the sword.
The Roy Catholicque (Philip II) is said to be poorly satisfied with Don Pedro's conduct; Don Pedro complains openly and makes a show of wanting to go home.
The writer promises to send detailed news of the secretary sent to Constantinople to carry the present, and of Jehan de Marian (Giovanni Margliani), the Spanish agent there.
It is said that the Turks distrusted him and would not accept the embassy, and that the talks were a "practicque de trefve" (truce negotiation) meant to deceive.
He will try to learn everything that is done there.
