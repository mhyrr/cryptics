## New or doubtful signs
- ʐ (z-shape with tail): ff in L14 and L25; in L06, after "a", it reads as the "Fait" word sign. It may be the same Mousset sign used both ways ("quelquefois nul").
- Ⅲ (triple-bar #): g in L12 (seigneur) and L31 (ungs); on 41v I read it s. Doubtful.
- "[France?]" in L06: a 9-like sign with a dot, read as Mousset's ·9· (France) from context only.
- Ꝗ in L26 (Portougail) read g; guess.
- L30 "? a s t i a n" read as "[Se]bastian"; the first sign is unread.

## Summary
The writer reports that someone was sent (to the Netherlands) to start new peace talks with the Estates General.
He says the King of France's declaration that he would approve, above all others, the claim of the Roy Catholicque (Philip II, to Portugal) has so advanced Philip's affairs that it seems to hand it to him; this is a check to those who would have opposed Philip making himself lord of it.
Philip means to enter (Portugal) by force without waiting for a ruling on his claim, since he would submit to no judgement; this would give princes reason to defend the right.
He believes the Portuguese are frightened by the size of the preparations ("la seulle peur de tant d'appareil") and are being pushed towards obedience.
Persons named: the King (Henri III), the Roy Catholicque, (Dom) Sebastian, the Portuguese (Portugais).
