## Summary
The cipher ends with a passage about the time it will take, and "pour ce qui touche l'allee du Prince de Condé" (as for the Prince of Condé's going) somewhere; they have taken alarm and suspect it.
The writer says he has always answered (when spoken to about it) as seemed fit, following the orders the King sent in his last letters, and he will keep doing so as occasions arise.
In clear text he then asks the King for leave to go on pilgrimage to Our Lady of Guadalupe ("Nostre Dame de Gouadeloupe"), a place of great devotion, to pray for the King's health.
He says the journey will take at most twelve days and he would not undertake it without the King's leave.
Persons and places: the Prince of Condé, the King (Henri III), Nostre Dame de Gouadeloupe (Guadalupe).
