## Summary
The cipher begins after "donner mauvaise reputation a ses subjectz" (give a bad reputation to his subjects).
It seems to speak of affairs "qui retardent" (that delay) and of a "relation" to the Queen Mother (la Reine mère) and Your Majesty.
"tout ce que je luy en pourrois dire" (all that I could tell him of it) is legible in L04.
L05-L07 mention "les affaires" (perhaps of France) and people who "disposeront" (will dispose) of them, and "aujourd'huy" (today).
Persons named: the Queen Mother, Your Majesty (Henri III). No places legible. Much of L05-L09 is unread.
