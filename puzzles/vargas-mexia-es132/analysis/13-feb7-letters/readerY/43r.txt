## New or doubtful signs
- ⊤-with-box (Mousset p, second form): p in L07 "a propos"; in L06 it stands between a triple long-s and "tousiours", where "j'ay" is wanted. If triple-long-s + this sign = "j'ay", Mousset's J'ai (a hooked ⅂) is not what I see. Doubtful.
- single long-s ʃ: t in L06 "tousiours" (and 42v L26); m in L08 "commender" and in many earlier places. There may be two shapes I am not separating.
- ɋ̶ crossed q: y in L06 "n'ay ouy", L09 "continueray". Confirms y for the crossed form.
- L03 "a la ha·is": could be "la Fère" or "la Haye"; the sign I read ~ (Mousset h) would have to be f for La Fère. Unresolved.
- L04 "? a t e s t e": the first glyph should be m (Majesté); it is a crossed q-form here.

## Summary
Cipher ends on this page: something must be believed to have come about "a raison" (reasonably).
On the matter touching (the prince of?) Condé, "il fault que Vostre Majesté croye" (Your Majesty must believe) that they took alarm at it; the writer has always answered as he thought fit, following the King's latest letters, and will go on doing so as occasions arise.
Clear text: the writer asks the King's leave for a pilgrimage of at most twelve days to Our Lady of Guadalupe ("Nostre Dame de Gouadeloupe"), where he will pray for the King's health.
Closing formula dated "De Madrid ce vij febvrier 1580" (Madrid, 7 February 1580); signature not read.
Persons: the King (Henri III), Condé (?); places: Madrid, Guadalupe, La Fère or La Haye (?).
