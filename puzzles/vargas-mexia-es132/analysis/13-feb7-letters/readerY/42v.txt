## New or doubtful signs
- Ŧ (upright stroke with three cross-bars): null. L01 ("Maria·ne", cf. 42r L16 where I left it unread), L05 ("decla·ration"), L25. Revises my 40v reading.
- Tall flourished 8 / 9 with a dot: [Portugal] at L05 ("le Roy de Portugal"), L10 ("se feist seigneur de Portugal"). The same sign on 40v L02, L06 should be [Portugal], not b or France.
- Σ (2-like with a hat) = pp (Mousset): L06 "approuver", L09 "opposer", L25 "appareilz".
- Ƶ followed by e-loop = fait (Mousset word sign): L05, L25. Distinct from Ƶ = ff in "affaires" (L28)? I cannot separate the two shapes reliably.
- ʎ (inverted-y-like): L17 (twice), L18; value unknown.
- ρ before ꞩꞩ in L21 ("heure ρ toutes"): looks like a null here.
- ʃ in L26 "e[t] reso-": would have to be t; doubtful.

## Summary
Mariane (Margliani) as ambassador is mentioned again; "l'on a despesche devers" (someone has been sent toward) the States General ("les Estatz generaulx").
"la declaration que le Roy de Portugal a faict" (the declaration the King of Portugal has made) approving above all others the claim of the Catholic King (Philip II).
Those who opposed it say Philip made himself lord of Portugal by entering by force, without waiting for his claim to be judged; he would not submit to any judgement.
With all these forces gathered on this side he expects to find the Portuguese in no position to resist; only fear of so much preparation holds them back, though if they were united they would refuse to submit to Castilian rule.
Persons: Margliani, the King (Cardinal Henry?) of Portugal, Philip II; places: Portugal, Castile, the States General (Netherlands?).
