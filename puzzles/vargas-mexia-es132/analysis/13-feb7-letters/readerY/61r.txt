## New or doubtful signs
- "4 with a z-shaped/tailed foot" (ꝫ): m — `monsieur` L20, `mandé` L20, `madict`(?) L19. This settles the sign left `?` at 59v L01, 60r L01 and 60v L21; I have not gone back to change those lines.
- "Y-fork" family: m in `La Mothe` L21; v in `voulu` L11; u in `huict`, `jours` L17–L18; p in `prendroit` L01, `particulier` L09. Fork variants not separated; values by context.
- Closed theta / ∂: l in `voulu`, `faillir` L11, `diligence` L08; z in `ilz`(?) L10.
- Tall "Ŧ" (T with bars): null in `icy` L23 and before `pour` L08; doubtful.
- "·9·" with dots: France (L19). Tall flourished g·: Portugal (L17, L22, L24).
- "ꝓ" long-tailed curl: ss in `assemblee` L06; s or `j` in `desia` L24.
- L02–L05 and L13–L14 are only partly understood.
- Last cipher line (L26) ends with the swash "ep" and "ge" nulls, a "Ŧ", and the δδ double loop, read as nulls.

## Summary
The writer hopes a good expedient will be found to satisfy [the Portuguese], and notes that an extraordinary council has been assembled on the matter.
He has not wanted to delay giving immediate notice of the above, for the good direction of the affairs that Your Majesty wishes to conduct in Portugal.
For over eight days the ambassadors resident here have said that the one in France reported that Monsieur de La Mothe-Fénelon was being sent [to Portugal].
The nuncio resident in Portugal and others here say he is already in Portugal, which the writer cannot believe given [the Queen Mother's] latest letters.
In clear text: it is held that the King Catholic, whatever resolution the Portuguese affairs take, will remain ...; [M. de] Chaloux is to leave soon for Seville.
