## New or doubtful signs
- ϑ (theta-like loop): = ll in L28 "querelle" (also 41r L23 "conseil[l]er"); L10 "nulle". Earlier "pa[s]" (41r L16) was a misreading or a different sign.
- ⨯ (small crossed loop, distinct from the star u): u in L21 "humeurs", "puissent", L26 "s'enfuir". Value proposed u.
- 8 alone: cc in L18 "occasion"; pour in L07-L11. Mousset gives cc; Tomokiyo gives par/pour. Both appear in use.
- ⊤ with box (Mousset p, second form): p in L18 "propo-".
- U-box / H-with-bars: f in L04-L05 "françois/Faix", b in L16 "bien", L22 "debvoir", L28 "Fabrice" (f then b). Two signs I could not keep apart.
- L29: the "M" of "Marc" not seen; a glyph may be missing or misread.

## Summary
Those who were lost in the enterprise; the Moroccan ruler had the heads cut off of all the Frenchmen trading in Fez ("Faix") and Morocco ("Marocos").
Wherever they trade in Africa they have lost all credit, and can no longer trade safely in any of those parts; the commodities the French used to bring are mentioned.
"je prie a Dieu que ces humeurs se puissent bien concilier" (I pray God these humours may be reconciled) and that each keep to his duty.
"la conduicte de don Pedro de Medicis" (Don Pedro de' Medici's conduct) is mentioned; those who can, flee and withdraw.
"une tres grande querelle entre Fabrice Colonne, filz de Marc Anthoine" (a very great quarrel between Fabrizio Colonna, son of Marcantonio Colonna) and another, continued on 42r.
