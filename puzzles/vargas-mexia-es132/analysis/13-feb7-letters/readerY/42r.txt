## Summary
A quarrel involving the general of the galleys of Sicily, "ledict don Pedro" (Pedro de' Medici) and a Sicilian baron, with sword blows.
The Catholic King is said to be ill satisfied with don Pedro's conduct; don Pedro complains freely and makes as if to go home.
The writer will report in detail on the secretary sent to Constantinople with the present, and on "Jehan de Mariane" (Giovanni Margliani), meant to stay there as ambassador, who has returned.
Some say that after receiving the present the Turk ("le dict prince") would not accept him as ambassador, and that the rebels were using this talk of a truce ("practicque de trefue") to deceive.
Persons: Pedro de' Medici, Philip II, Margliani, the Grand Turk; places: Sicily, Constantinople.
