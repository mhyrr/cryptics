- Three-upright crossed sign (L01) read as Mousset's "quant"; context "quant a ce qui touche" supports it.
- Looped Z with a hooked tail (Ƶ shape; L02, L04, L05, L07, L08): read as `que` (the guide's "L-flourish"), not Mousset ff; "catholic[que]" L04/L08 supports it.

## Summary
The clear opening complains of poverty and asks Their Majesties to provide for the writer, who can no longer bear the expense.
The cipher begins "quant à ce qui touche les affaires de [France?]" and addresses "vostre majesté".
It mentions a last dispatch and the ambassadors of the King Catholic (Philip II).
It speaks of a coming arrival ("la venue") and of the King Catholic's "successeur" being already talked of among the people.
Great and small are said to be stirred by it; the page breaks off mid-sentence ("qu'ilz ne peuvent ...").
