# Countersignatures on the King's letters to Vargas (H17c)

Read by the main thread on the native page images (`sources/cache/pages/exp07/`), 2026-10-05, unless another source is
named. "Ant. Pz" = the flourished "Ant.º Perez" signature with the large looped A, as on ff. 12v, 20v, 154, 167.
Before f. 200: Tomokiyo's table of contents names Pérez on ff. 17, 22, 34, 41–46 and Cayas on ff. 32, 37, 39 (1578); our
readers recorded Pérez on ff. 154 (4 Dec 1578) and 165/167 (10 Jan 1579).
