- Tall back-hooked "∂" (like a d whose ascender curls back over the line): proposed s. L01, L04, L06, L12, L14, L19, L24, L25. Not in Mousset in this shape; may be his first s (ʗ with top loop).
- Large hanging "ℓ"/"ℰ" loop (e-shaped head, long tail curling right below the line): read s throughout (Tomokiyo lists an e-like s). Very frequent.
- Crossed 7 / Z-shaped sign "Ƶ" (no C beneath): proposed c. L26 (twice), L30. Fits "prouinces", "pacifi-".
- Z with a C-curl beneath ("Ƶ̧"): read ff (Mousset ff). L26 "pacifi-"; the heavy triple-S next to it there is unexplained (?).
- Z with tilde ("Z̃"): read pp (Mousset pp). L11 only; context not confirming.
- Rectangle with a dot/eye inside, on a stem ("boxed eye"): proposed p. L16 ("pour"), perhaps L10. Doubtful.
- Box with tilde (Ẅ-like): L14 "trou?uera"; value unknown, probably v or null.
- "ꝗ‡" (q with crossed descender): read y in L28 "ayde" and L22/L23 end; elsewhere the Y-fork with crossbar reads m. The two shapes are close; L23 "cay" and L22 "y" are doubtful.
- "Ω"/"δb" double loops: treated as nulls (L20 twice, L31). L21 "et Ω ie" and L31 "a Ω δo vostre" also read as null by sense, not by glyph.
- "/" oblique stroke inside L31 between "ste" and "esto": a slash in the cipher line; not a sign in either key.

- "8" alone stays doubtful (L07 start, L21, L23, L28 first of two).
- A vs Δ (i vs l) is often indistinct in this hand: L21 "luteriens", L26 "les", L27 "i'ont/l'ont" are marked (l/i).

## Summary
1. The page continues a report to Henri III ("vostre Majesté", L17, L31) about Spanish and Swiss affairs; much of the upper third (L01–L16) is only partly read.
2. L20–L21: "et cantons des Suisses tant catholicques que luteriens": the Swiss cantons, both Catholic and Lutheran (Protestant).
3. L22, L27, L32: "le Roi catholicque" (Philip II) recurs: someone "avec le Roi catholicque … envoie" (L27) and "estoit traictant avec le Roi catholicque et ses ministres" (L31–L33).
4. Other readable phrases: "me pardouner" (L11), "ils sont tous" (L12), "ensemble et en armes" (L15), "les prouinces pacifiées" (L26), "croire qu'il" (L25), "par leur ayde" (L28), "le reste de [s]es estat[s]" (L29), "…advis a […] vostre Ma[jes]té" (L31).
5. Names of persons/places found: Henri III (as vostre Majesté), Philip II (le Roi catholicque), the Swiss cantons. No Portugal sign or "don Anthoine" was seen on this page; the page ends mid-word "qu'il pou-".
