## Summary
1. The page continues Saint-Goard's report from Madrid on the Portuguese succession; the opening sign looks like
   "Portugal" and later lines speak of "chasteaulx" and the "circonvoisins" (neighbouring places).
2. He reports advice to "ceulx" who hold places and that "tous les gouverneurs soient a leurs charges", i.e. all
   governors are to be at their posts; L12 "…gron tieres" may be "frontieres" (not secured).
3. There is mention of "au service" (L16, whose service not read), "avec mons…" (L22) and of embarkation:
   "que des s'embarquent ces trois …" (L26).
4. A rumour is reported: "l'on y bruict que c'est …" (L27), and that people should "penser que des …" (L28); the
   Mediterranean ("mer Mediterranee", L29–30) appears.
5. No personal names other than the possible Portugal sign were secured; much of the page is still letter strings.
