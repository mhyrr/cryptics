## New or doubtful signs

- Asterisk/star sign (looks like a scribbled "-◇-"): proposed u/v (Mousset's u "-◇-"). Lines 06, 08 ("viendra"), 14 ("gouverneurs"), 15, 33 ("veulent"), 34 ("du coste"). Reasonably firm.
- "ʒ" with a descending tail, distinct from the plain "3" = e: proposed i (Mousset's second i sign, "↳ʒ"). Fixed by L04 "circonvoisins", L07–08 "l'occasion", L08 "mains". Firm.
- "ꝟ8" (a hooked 4/u over an 8, very frequent): read as a null (Tomokiyo's "18"). Treating it as null gives "chasteaulx" (L03) and "Magesté" (L17). Firm.
- "ep" (looks like a looped "e" with a "p" tail): null, as in Tomokiyo. Throughout.
- "Ω" (horseshoe): null. L15 "et [ve]ulent", L27. Doubtful.
- "∂" (d with a hooked back): proposed l (Mousset's third l sign), not d. L04 "qualite", L22 "Italie", L33 "veulent". Two ∂-like shapes may be mixed up on this page.
- "Δ" versus "A": both occur, and the strips do not separate them reliably. Δ is read l (L03 "aulx", L07 "eulx"), A is read i. L34 "de (l/i) Ocean" left open.
- "‖" (two uprights): read g in L14 ("gouverneurs") but f in L12 ("frontieres"). These are probably two different signs (Mousset's g "Ī͟I" and f "U") that are not separable at this resolution. L12, L13, L14, L20.
- "ʮ" (Y-fork with a hook): p. L09 "prise", L33 "passer". Fairly firm.
- "ꝟ̄" (barred hooked 4): m. L08 "mains". "ʃ" also read m: L17 "Magesté", L26 "s'embarquent".
- "8" alone (no bar or loop): cc in L07 "l'occ|asion". Doubtful.
- "Ƹ" (a Σ/ε shape with a cross-stroke): unidentified. Lines 01, 17, 18, 22, 26. Not y.
- "ꝗ" (a q with a crossed descender): unidentified, possibly n (Mousset). Lines 02, 03, 13, 14, 18, 19, 34.
- Large inked blot or sign at the right margin at the end of L01/L02, and a black blot mid-line in L21: unreadable, written ?.

## Summary

1. The page, as read, is about Portugal and the Spanish frontier: "chasteaulx circonvoisins", "ces frontieres d'Aragon" and probably Catalonia ("et Ca-talo[i]gne") and Roussillon ("conte de Roussi[llon]?"), L03–L13.
2. Something "viendra aulx mains" (will come to their hands) "a l'occasion" (L07–L08). "Tous les gouverneurs" are mentioned, and "et veulent des diligences" (L14–L16).
3. The writer addresses "le service de vostre Magesté" (L16–L17), so this is a dispatch to the King.
4. Lower down: "que les ... s'embarquent ces trois ...", "penser que les" and "devant ces" (L19–L28). This looks like an embarkation of men or ships.
5. The page ends with "ils veulent passer ... [es]troict du coste de l'Ocean" (L33–L34): a passage through a strait on the Ocean side. Names read: Portugal, Aragon, (Catalogne?), (Roussillon?), Italie? (L22). No person's name was read.
