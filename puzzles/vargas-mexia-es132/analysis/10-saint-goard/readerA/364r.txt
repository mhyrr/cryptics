## Summary

The page is the end of the letter, signed "De Vivonne" (Saint-Goard), and is mostly about Dom Antonio, "le dict don Anthoine" (L11, L22-23, L31-32).
The writer says he is waiting on "ce qu'il plaira à vostre majesté" (L08) and asks to know the King's pleasure ("que je sceusse ce qu'il plairoit à vostre majesté", L16-17).
Recurring phrases: "en sa faveur" (L04), "en toute seureté" (L05), "il me semble qu'il ne seroit à propos" (L18), "qu'il y [a] un inconvenient" (L19).
He says he has been able to send "la dicte lectre" by "une plus prompte voye" (L25-26).
The page ends with Dom Antonio waiting "jusque ad ce que vostre majesté commande sa volunté", and something about his service (L32-34). No other persons or places are named on this page.
