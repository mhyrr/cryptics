## New or doubtful signs
- "ɑ with a descending right hook" (like Mousset a3): here behaves as a NULL in L03 (before "occasion"),
  L04 (between "dauen" and "taige", where a would give "dauenataige"), L06 ("sante [·] et"), L07 (final).
  Proposed: null (matches the "2"-shaped null in Tomokiyo), not a. Lines L02, L03, L04, L06, L07.
- "ϥ" (mirrored-q / 4-like with left loop): read p in L02 (puisse), L04 (maplicque), L05 (aprier),
  L06 (prosperite, twice). Not clearly either key's p (Y, boxed ⊤); value fixed by context. Possibly the
  cursive form of Mousset p1 "Y".
- "ꝭ" (looped ∞/u-flourish with tail): u in L02, L03, L05 (du, tout, dieu); matches Tomokiyo u1 /
  Mousset u3 shape loosely. L02 start "lo-u-ignees" gives an odd spelling; doubtful there.
- "α" (Mousset o2) in L01 "et α s lo…": key gives o, context ("esloignees"?) would rather want e. Left as (o).
- "ɛ with long crossed descender" in L07 = m (Mousset m1) by "monarque"; distinct from the
  "q with crossed stem" = y in L05 (m'applicqueray).
- Plain open "8" in L05 before "sa": word sign par/pour (Tomokiyo par/pour column); "pour" fits sense.
  Bold filled "8" in L03 = cc. "8 with top bar" in L07 = Par (Mousset); sense unclear at line end.
- Large "4" written with/over the ℒ (que) in L07: unassigned "?"; sense needs nothing there
  (perhaps ornament or null).
- Clear-text date: the day after "ce" is not cleanly legible; cover note and verso endorsement say 23.

## Summary
The cipher lines close the letter: the matter is in regions so remote and distant that no occasion can be found,
and being unable to do more in the king's service, the writer will apply himself wholly to praying God
for the king's prosperity and health, and that God make him "monarch of all the world".
Clear closing: "Sire, I beseech the Creator to grant Your Majesty ... health, most happy and long life. Madrid, [23] December 1579."
Signed "De Vivonne" (Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Goard), "very humble and obedient subject and servant".
