## New or doubtful signs

- "ʑp" (3/Z-shape with a p-like tail): read as i throughout. It is in Tomokiyo's i column and is
  also his "fait" sign. Mousset gives it only as "fait". Context fixes i: service (L09), Anglois
  (L19), doibt croire (L25), ministres (L33). Every line uses it.
- "=" (two flat strokes, closed at the right): read as u/v. This looks like a quickly written form
  of Mousset's ⊐. Context fixes it: plus (L01), service (L09), avec (L27, L32), vostre (L31), tout (L33).
- "ep" loop sign (a loop with a p-tail): taken as a null, as in Tomokiyo. A similar crossed-x
  plus loop sign seems to be needed as n in L14 (…rdo[n]ne) and as u in L10 (to[u]t). I could
  not reliably tell the two shapes apart at this resolution. Doubtful: L10, L14 and passim.
- Double-loop "∂b" sign: taken as a null, as in Tomokiyo. In L20 "…e[ ]tant" it would read
  naturally as s ("estant"). Doubtful: L20, L21, L31.
- E/ꝗ with a cross-stroke below: read as m (Tomokiyo, second m sign). Mousset gives this shape
  as y. Context: -ement (L19), ministres (L33), also L17. Doubtful m/y.
- H with a left bar or box (Mousset's second b): read as b in ensemble (L15) and doibt (L25).
- Underlined F: [pour/par] (Tomokiyo), L12.
- ⊓: [qui] (Tomokiyo), L08, L25, L33.
- The sign at the start of L19: an H-like sign crossed twice, read as g (‡‡). The value is fixed
  by An-|glois across L18–L19.
- A heavy "6" inside a rectangular frame, L16: not in either key. Value unknown (?). It might be a
  place-name sign; compare Mousset's circled 9 = Italie.
- "∴" at the end of L30: Mousset's third d. Here it may be punctuation that ends a sentence.
- "ɋ" (a-shape with a descending tail): Mousset's third a. Read as a in L28 and L30.
- "/" between "este" and "este" in L31 is a stroke on the page. It may be a correction.

## Summary

The middle of the page is about the Roi catholique (Philip II). He is named with the [Roi] sign
and "catholicque" spelled out in L20–22, L27 and L32. L18–19 mention "…avec les Anglois", and L15
has "…ensemble et en armes aux…". L32–33 read "traictant avec le Roi catholicque et ses ministres,
sçaichant tout ces…". L25 has "doibt croire qui l…", and L31 "vostre…" may be an address to the king.
Most lines give only fragments; no other person or place is securely read. The page continues overleaf.
