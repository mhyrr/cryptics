  [Roi] in L01; context "a recepvoir __ Roi Catholique" suggests the article "le" or a null. Left ?. L01.
- "delta-b" (the d-sign ∂b in Mousset): in L01 ("ca_tholique") and L28 ("Ms e _ igneur")
  it must be a null (= Tomokiyo's null Ω/∂b). Lines L01 (x2), L28.
- 8 (Mousset m): reads as [par/pour] in L01 ("pour leur roy") and as m elsewhere (L28
  "Mseigneur"? where a second 8 must be a null). Doubtful; lines L01, L05, L10, L21, L28.
- Big F with top bar: Mousset gives "Sur"; here it reads "par" ("par ticulier" L07,
  "par aultre" L33) and "pour" (L01-style contexts). Proposed [par/pour] (Tomokiyo). L05, L07, L08, L12, L14, L20, L26, L27, L32, L33.
- "d-with-long-s" (Mousset l third / n third): reads n in "raisonnable" L34 and "affectionne" L36. Proposed n. L34, L36, also L06, L08, L23.
- Big rho/z sign with tilde ("z~"): ff in "d'aff[aires]" L04 and "affectionne" L36. Matches Mousset ff. Doubtful only in L37 ("serv_ice" where plain z = i).
- "#" in "Mageste" L37 appears twice (m a # e s # e); the second must be t. Either a t-variant
  drawn like g or a misreading. L37 (also L05 has H for t there).
- Triple long-s (Tomokiyo null): in L03 "p_euple" acts as null. L03, L07.
- "s-with-loop" capital (big S): m in "Mageste" (L05, L32, L37). Mousset gives S also for [Quel]. L05, L32, L37.

## Summary
Saint-Goard reports that the King and nobility of Portugal want to receive the Catholic King (Philip II) as their king, and that only the people oppose it.
He judges the people can easily be brought round amid the stir of so many affairs touching Your Majesty (Henri III).
The middle of the page (L06-L26) is largely illegible through show-through; it mentions letters, "tous les jours", and a "roine de Castre(?)".
A "Mseigneur" is named, with a commission (charge) and someone's will (volunte) and what he writes (il m'escript).
He closes saying someone is greatly devoted to Your Majesty's service; persons: Henri III, Philip II, King (Cardinal Henrique) of Portugal; places: Portugal, Castile(?).
