## Summary
Verso of the last leaf: no cipher text; the apparent cipher is mirrored show-through from 366r.
It carries the outer address (large flourish, probably "Au Roy" for Henri III).
An endorsement reads, roughly, "M. Mr de St Gouard du [xxiij]e decembre 1579" (Saint-Goard, Madrid dispatch).
A later hand adds "23 decemb. 1579"; a single "K" mark.
Confirms date 23 December 1579 and sender Saint-Goard.
