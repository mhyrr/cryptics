## New or doubtful signs

- c with a long horizontal right tail ("eꝭ" shape; often follows the loop-top s). Not matched with
  certainty in either key. Read as a null: it is silent in "ceste qualite" (L04), "sont hors" (L29),
  "penser" (L28, L32), "leur" (L08), "es et" (L27). Lines: almost all; heaviest L04, L05, L08, L15, L27–L31.
- "ʟʒ"-type sign (written like 3 with a looped tail). Taken as i (Mousset's i2). It gives i in
  "frontieres" (L12), "combien" (L24), "destroict" (L29, L34), "[occ]asion" (L08), "preigne" (L17).
  Tomokiyo has a similar "2e" null, so this needs checking. Lines: throughout.
- "18"-like sign: taken as Tomokiyo's null "18". It is silent in "c'est" (L05), "qualite" (L04),
  "destroict" (L29). Lines: throughout.
- Star-like sign (asterisk/diamond with tails): taken as u/v (Mousset's -◇- written fast).
  Gives "veulent" (L33) and "du coste" (L34); probably "viendr…" (L08). Doubtful in L15–L19.
- Small 4-shaped sign with a looped descender (ɥ with a cross bar below, "Ɛ+"): taken as m (Mousset's m1).
  Gives "monstre" (L22), "et mes" (L17) and "la mer" (L29).
- Small s-shaped sign: taken as m. Gives "s'embarquent" (L26), "combien" (L24) and "come" (L32).
- φ/ψ sign (circle or cup on a stem): taken as p, although Mousset lists φ as c. Gives "penser"
  (L28, L32), "porter" (L28), "pres" (L09) and "preigne" (L17). It is never c in context. Doubtful.
- Heavy H with a double crossbar: g in "preigne/creigne" (L17), but L12 would need f ("frontieres").
  Unresolved; written (f/g) in L12.
- A and Δ are confused in this hand. A gives i in "qualite" (L04), but the same small sign
  must be l in "l'ocean" (L34) and "la mer" (L29).
- Large 8 with a hooked top: written [Portugal?] in L01, L07, L16 and L25. In L07, L31 and L34 an
  m or cc reading fits better. Doubtful.
- Large G-like circle with a hook (L32): possibly Tomokiyo's "le cardinal" sign.
- Big T with a bar or loop (L09, L14, L25, L32): possibly [sur]. Unconfirmed.
- Big "ρ|" sign before ‡‡‡ in L33: proposed ss ("passer"). Doubtful.
- Ink blot over a glyph at L21 (after "p o"): unreadable.

## Summary

Only about a third of the page gives continuous French on this pass. The rest is too uncertain to read as French.
The page concerns military or naval movements near Portugal: troops "s'embarquent" (embark) (L26),
and "frontieres" (L12) and "le destroict" (the strait) are mentioned. The strait is "du costé de l'ocean" (on the ocean side) (L33–34).
There is a passage on what "il fault penser" (one must think) about the ships and those "qui sont hors du destroict en la mer" (outside the strait, at sea) (L28–31).
Persons/places named: Portugal (word sign, about four times, doubtful); possibly "le cardinal" (L32); no other names were secured.
The many ? and the provisional null and m values mean that the gist of this page needs checking against reader A.
