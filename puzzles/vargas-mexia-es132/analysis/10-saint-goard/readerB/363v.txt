## Summary
The page argues that the King of Portugal (Cardinal-King Henrique) and the Portuguese nobility want to receive the Catholic King (Philip II) as their king, and that only the people oppose it.
It adds that the people could easily be won over by the noise of so great a military preparation ("tant d'appareil") of Philip, and refers to "Vostre Majesté" (Henri III).
The middle of the page (L06–L30) is largely unreadable on the washed half-strips; legible fragments: "ce que l'on", "la resolution", "tous les jours", "il y a quelque temps", "qu'il n'en est".
Near the end the writer mentions someone sent "avec charge de …", "la substance", a man he thinks "raisonnable", and "the letter"; he says "je suis bien asseuré" of his great affection to the service of Vostre Majesté.
Names: Portugal (word sign, L01–L02), le Roi Catholique (Philip II), le Roi de Portugal, Vostre Majesté (Henri III); no place other than Portugal and no other personal name was read.
