## New or doubtful signs

- "3 with a p-tail" (the 3 of e with a descending loop to the right). Neither key lists it as such;
  Tomokiyo's null "ze" looks similar. Read here as **i** (Mousset's second i) because it makes
  -oient (L01), catholicque (L21, L22, L27, L32) and envoié … pri- (L27). Doubtful between i and a null;
  very frequent (every line). Lines: all.
- "star / crossed x with a loop" (a small x crossed and looped, like a star). Not identifiable in
  either key. Written (o/s); "-oyent" at L19 favours o (variant of Mousset's alpha-o). Lines: L01, L04, L05,
  L07, L08, L11, L12, L14, L16, L18, L19, L23, L30, L33.
- "q with a cross under it / epsilon over a cross" read **y** (Mousset y first form). Proposed from
  -yent L19, ya L17. Lines: L14, L17, L19, L22, L24, L26, L28, L30, L33.
- "Psi-like fork" (Y with curled arms and long stem) read **p** (Mousset p "Y"). Proposed from
  envoié pri- L27. Lines: L02, L12, L13, L26, L27, L33.
- "#" (two verticals, two cross-bars) — Mousset's s or Tomokiyo's null "#". Read s, except where s would
  double an s already present (L21/L22 "m'empes|che", L33): written (s/·). Lines: L15, L22, L23, L33.
- "T with a looped bar / big looped stroke" after catholic- (L22, L26, L32): not in either key as drawn;
  context gives **que** (a variant of Tomokiyo's "que"). Lines: L22, L27, L32.
- Small superscript t before H ("tH", L09, L11): read t; possibly a ligature or word sign. Lines: L09, L11.
- "∂" alone with a large hook (L01, L04): Tomokiyo null "∂"; written ? where unsure.
- Right-margin marks beside L02–L09 (a cross, "v8", a "2"-shaped hook): left unread; probably
  fillers or line-end marks, not text.
- L04: a stretch of about ten glyphs is struck through and blotted in the original; written ?.

## Summary

1. A hard page: only about half the glyphs could be fixed, and many lines give no connected French; the readings below come from the few runs that parse.
2. Recurring phrase "le Roi catholicque" (Philip II) at L20–21, L22, L27 and L32; L27 reads "… le Roi catholicque l'ont envoié pri[er]".
3. Military/political vocabulary: "en chef" (L01–02), "et en armes aux …" (L15), "et cantons de …" (L20), "croire" (L25), "de ses Estats" (L29), "traicté tant avec le Roi catholicque" (L32).
4. Also "tout m'empesche" (L21–22) and "vostre" (L17, L31); no other person or place name could be read with confidence (a "Cast[ille?]" at L02 is a guess).
5. The letter ends on this page with the clear-text subscription "De Vivonne" (Jean de Vivonne, seigneur de Saint-Goard); the margin carries a later endorsement "…bre 157[9?]".
