## New or doubtful signs

- "ı8" (short stroke or hook joined to a looped 8-shaped descender; once dotted, L08). The commonest sign on the page, in every cipher line. Proposed: null (cf. Tomokiyo's null "18"). As a null it gives "leurs" L05, "et le tout" L06, "saincte" L14, "procha[ins]" L14. The dot in L08 and Mousset's second i sign (an L-hook with a ʒ) make i the alternative. Written · throughout. Doubtful.
- "ʒ" with a loop or flourish to the right (unlike the plain "3" = e). Proposed: i, from "faict" L08, "saincte" L14 and "[procha]in" L15. Lines L03, L04, L05, L06, L08, L12, L14, L15. Doubtful: it may be Mousset's second i sign.
- "ab"-like sign (an a-shape with a b-loop and a star stroke), close to Mousset's d sign "ꝺb". The context points to different values: u in "peu·ple" L16, no clear value in "plus ? procha" L14, d elsewhere. Lines L06, L07, L08, L10, L11, L14, L15, L16. Unresolved.
- Cup-on-stem sign (Y/ψ-like). Read p ("plus" L14, "peuple" L16). In the keys this is p "Y", but it is easy to take for c "ϕ". Lines L12, L13, L14, L16.
- "Ш"/"⊔". Read f (Mousset f), giving "faict" L08 and "force" L16.
- Flattened "Ω". Read h (Mousset "-ʌ-"), giving "procha" L14. Also in L03 and L10.
- "π"/"⊓". Read as the word sign [qui] (Tomokiyo). L07, L09, L14. Doubtful: in L09 it stands between "dans" and "son".
- Large "ɛ" with a cross-stroke. Read as a null in "m·a·rie" L14 and in L15 (Tomokiyo has an ɛ-like null). It may instead be y "E".
- Large "8" with a looped tilde. Read as [Portugal], L07 (the shape is clear).
- Tall single leaf "ʃ". Read as m in "comme" L07 and t in "por[t]" L13/L14, so its value is unresolved. Also in L02, L04, L11, L16.
- "✗" (x with a cross-stroke) in L01: e or the letter x. Context ("aux") suggests x.
- Heavy "F" with a top bar, L02 end. Not in either key: ? (possibly the Sur sign).
- "2" with a blob, or "ʒe" (the fait/null sign), at the start of L01 and L02 and in L05: [fait] or null, unresolved.
- Long diagonal strokes cross the glyphs in L01, L06, L08–L10 and over clear text C01, C04 and C08. Read as later marks, not cipher.

## Summary

1. Saint-Goard to Henri III, Madrid, 23 Dec 1579. He wrote on the 3rd. The King and Queen of Spain (Philip II and Anna of Austria) are in good health and are now at Madrid.
2. In an audience a few days earlier he took up only the case of poor French subjects mistreated at Bilbao. He asked for justice "for the good correspondence and amity between Your Majesties".
3. The Catholic King answered graciously that he would order the matter settled reasonably. Saint-Goard believes him but doubts that the ministers will comply, and will keep pressing.
4. The cipher (from "Quant aux advis…") is only partly read. It names Portugal (L07), Cartagena (L11), the port of Santa Maria (El Puerto de Santa María, L13–14), "the nearest places" (L14–15), and "force" and "the people" (L16).
5. Tentative overall sense: news of Spanish military or naval preparations bearing on the Portuguese succession. Most cipher lines did not yield running French on this pass. 110 of 594 cipher tokens (18.5%) are ?, and the null and i assignments above are doubtful.
