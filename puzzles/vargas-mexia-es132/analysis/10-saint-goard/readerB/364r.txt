## New or doubtful signs

- "ep" loop (epsilon-like stroke ending in a p-shaped loop): read as null, following Tomokiyo's
  null column. Very frequent (L01–L34). Doubt: in L31 "t r e · b i e n" a value s ("tres bien")
  would also fit. Not in Mousset.
- "18" (small 1/hook followed by 8): null (Tomokiyo). Frequent everywhere; splitting words
  (e.g. L08 "vos·tre", "Mages·té") is consistent with a null.
- "3 with p-tail" (written like 3 + curl): taken as i (Mousset's second i sign "L-ʒ"). Fixed by
  L08 "plaira", L32 "jusque". Doubtful: the plain flat-top 3 = e is visually close; some
  occurrences may be e.
- "∂" (d-like sign with loop open to the upper left): proposed u. Not identifiable in either key.
  Context: L25 "elle luy a", L26 "plus prompte", L32 "jusque", L10 "particul…". Also L17, L18,
  L27, L31. Possibly a cursive form of Mousset's u sign, but I cannot match it.
- Big flourished Z/L with a loop below the line: read as [que] (Tomokiyo "que"). L09, L13, L14,
  L21, L24, L27, L29, L30, L32. L01 first occurrence uncertain.
- Pi (Π): read as [qui] (Tomokiyo), giving "ce qu'il" (L08, L16, L26), "qui sera" (L20),
  "qui est" (L33). In L18–L19 followed by A rather than l: "qu'i[l]" doubtful.
- "‡" (double-barred cross): g, as in the key; "Magesté" L08, L17, L32 (period spelling). In L08
  "c ‡ endant" it would rather be p ("cependant"): doubtful there.
- Barred F: [pour] (Tomokiyo "par/pour"). L23, L26. Value pour fits L26 "pour ce qu'il me".
- Tall cross on a box (L13, L29): read as [vous] (Mousset). Doubtful; context unclear.
- q with descender/tail: y (L25 "luy", "envoyer"; L09 "y a respondre"). L10 "q" before "un mot"
  unread in sense.
- 9e (L30, L33): unknown; not Mousset's 9 = b in sense. Left as ?.
- Tall long-s sign with hook (L09, L12, L33): unknown, left as ?.
- Triple wavy stroke (L28) read as t (or tt); doubtful.

## Summary

Saint-Goard writes to the King about the affairs of "don Antoine" (Dom António, the Portuguese
claimant), named in L11, L22–23 and L31–32. He refers several times to what "vostre Magesté"
may please to order ("ce qu'il plaira / plairoit a vostre Magesté", L08, L16–17, L32). He mentions
sending something by a quicker way ("envoyer… avec une plus prompte voye", L25–26) and having to
reply or write a word (L09–L10). The page ends with his service and "sa volunté" (L33–34) and the
autograph signature "De Vivonne". Overall confidence is low. 127 of 1297 cipher tokens (9.8%)
are left as ?, but many of the assigned values are weak shape readings, and only isolated phrases
make sense.
