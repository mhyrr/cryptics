# Saint-Goard to Henri III, Madrid, 23 December 1579 — working text

BnF Français 16106, ff. 363r–366r (Gallica btv1b90096628, views 373–376). Cipher: Longlée's (Mousset 1912, pp. lviii–lix).
Provisional. Text below is reader C's French column (reader B for f. 366r), unedited, one row per cipher line;
`C` rows are the clear-text opening. `[…]`, `?` and bracketed letters are the reader's. See README for limits.

## Checked on the image by the main thread (2026-11-10)

- f. 363v ll. 1–2: *de Portugal a recepvoir … le Roi catholicque pour leur roy, et que le Roi de Portugal et la noblesse le veulent ainsi*
  ("…of Portugal to receive the Catholic King as their king, and that the King of Portugal and the nobility want it so").
- f. 363v ll. 26–28: *… par les chemains; elle le verra par celes de don Anthoine de Castre, seigneur de Cascais, ce qu'il luy plaira …*
  ("…on the roads; Your Majesty will see it from those [letters] of Dom António de Castro, lord of Cascais; whatever it pleases you…").
  Reader C has "se coigne" for *seigneur de Cascais* at l. 28; the glyphs read s-e-·-i-g-n-e-?-u-r d-e … c-a-s-?-c-a-i-s.
- f. 364r ll. 2–7: *place qui est Cascais, qui est toute l'importence de l'entree de la riviere de Lisboune, et qui l'aura en sa faveur
  l'on pourra entreprendre en toute seureté a ce que l'on vouldra d'importence sur Lisboune, dont enfin deppend tout Portugal*
  ("a place, which is Cascais, which is the whole importance of the entrance to the river of Lisbon; whoever has it in his favour can
  undertake in all safety whatever of importance he wishes against Lisbon, on which in the end all Portugal depends").
