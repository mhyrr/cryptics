# Vargas's side, Apr 1579 – 1580, in Paz's Simancas catalogue (2026-10-05)

Source: Julián Paz, *Catálogo IV: Secretaría de Estado … negociaciones con Francia*, Archivo de Simancas, Madrid 1914,
pp. 394–403 (K 1554–55, K 1557, K 1558). archive.org `catlogo4secret01spai`, OCR text (`cache/paz/paz.txt`, lines
19092–19530). Tier: SCHOLARLY (printed finding aid). Paz summarizes each legajo by half-year, not item by item. Read for
two purposes: (1) does Vargas's side mention Pérez after the arrest; (2) do Paz's summaries corroborate our decodes of the
King's replies (README criterion 6).

## 1. Pérez
K 1554–55 is titled "Correspondencia de Vargas con Felipe II y Antonio Pérez" (Apr–Jul 1579). Its Aug–Dec 1579 summary,
K 1557 (Apr–Dec 1579) and K 1558 (1580) contain **no mention of Pérez, Éboli or Escobedo**. This is a negative result on a
summary, not on the documents: Paz's summaries are selective.

## 3. Vargas's end (K 1558)
Apr–Jun 1580: "Enfermedad de Juan de Vargas. — Su codicilo." Jul–Dec 1580: Diego Maldonado asks that the embassy be filled;
"Entierro de Juan de Vargas"; Parma asks that the Rector of the Paris Jesuits and P. Juan Bautista González, Vargas's
executors, keep safe "los papeles de la Embajada que éste les había encomendado sin comunicarlos a nadie".
