# Saint-Goard's 1579 dispatches (Jan–Oct): where are they?

Search of 2026-11-10. The question was where Jean de Vivonne, sieur de Saint-Goard, kept his Madrid dispatches to Henri III,
Catherine and Villeroy for January–October 1579. Two items mattered most: a report of the arrest of Antonio Pérez and the
princess of Éboli (night of 28 July 1579), and any passage saying from whom he "recovers" copies of Spanish state papers.

**Result: not found.** No source consulted cites a Saint-Goard letter written between January and October 1579 with a
shelfmark. Three nineteenth-century editors who worked through fr. 16104–16108 all treat 1579 as a gap, and none of them
found the letters anywhere else. Catherine's own letters of 1579 show that dispatches were sent in that period (see below).

Texts were fetched as archive.org djvu.txt (OCR) and grepped. The scratchpad copies were not checked in. OCR errors in
dates are flagged where they matter.

## What the editors say about the gap

- **Gachard, vol. 2, art. 240** ("Saint-Germain Harlay 228", i.e. fr. 16104–16106). The third volume holds the 1574
  dispatches as pieces I–LVIII, then "de LIX à CXXI, des lettres et d'autres pièces de l'année 1579". In his item list,
  **LIX–CXIV** are "Pièces concernant l'affaire de la succession de Portugal en 1579 : lettres écrites par et à
  Philippe II ; consultes, mémoires, avis de ministres ; deux lettres écrites de Lisbonne à Saint-Gouard" ("Papers on the
  Portuguese succession in 1579: letters written by and to Philip II; consultas, memoranda, ministers' opinions; two
  letters written from Lisbon to Saint-Gouard"). **CXV** is the 12 Nov 1579 letter to the King. Gachard lists nothing
  after CXV because he only noted items touching the Low Countries. So the Spanish state papers Saint-Goard obtained are
  inside fr. 16106 itself, as about 56 pieces. Gachard records no Saint-Goard dispatch among them. SCHOLARLY.
- **Brémond d'Ars, appendix "Sources".** His summary of 16106 reads "lettres de 1574 et 1579, parmi lesquelles une de
  1578". He writes that "les dépêches des années 1575 à 1578 y font défaut, et nulle part je n'ai pu les retrouver"
  ("the dispatches of 1575 to 1578 are missing from them, and I could find them nowhere"). He made up for the gap with
  Archives nationales K 1447, 1526–1539 and 1541–1561: Simancas papers holding Saint-Goard's memorials to Philip II and
  ministers' reports on them. He cites no Saint-Goard dispatch of January–October 1579. SCHOLARLY.
- **Catherine vol. 7, note to the letter of 27 Mar 1580.** The editor found no letters between her and Saint-Goard after
  1 Apr 1578 (referring to vol. 5, p. 197?, OCR unclear). The ambassador's letters to the King and the Queen Mother "commencent
  avec le mois de janvier 1580" ("begin with the month of January 1580"). The only earlier one quoted is 12 Nov 1579.
  PRIMARY (editorial apparatus).

**Arrest of Pérez and Éboli.** No 1579 French report was found. The earliest French ambassadorial mention found is
**Saint-Goard to Villeroy, 13 Jan 1580**, quoted by Mignet ("Bibliothèque royale, St-Germ.-H. vol. 794") and cited by
Forneron as fr. 16107 (f. 8?):
> "Antonio Perès est guéri et est toujours en sa maison avecques gardes sans qu'il ait liberté de parler à personne, ne
> estre visité. Toutesfois la commune opynion est que ses afaires se porteront bien … La princesse d'Evoli est toujours
> en même état, observée et traitée avec toute la rigueur possible."

"Antonio Pérez has recovered and is still in his house under guard, not free to speak to anyone or to receive visits.
Still, common opinion is that his affairs will turn out well … The princess of Éboli is still in the same state, watched
and treated with all possible rigour."

Forneron's account of the arrest (vol. 3, pp. 70–71) rests on Spanish sources only (*CODOIN*, Pérez, Baena). Mignet's
rests on Pérez's *Relaciones* and the Hague manuscript. SCHOLARLY.

## Candidates for where the January–October 1579 series is

1. **Inside fr. 16106 after all, as pieces LIX–CXIV (ff. ~210–358).** Gachard describes these only by category.
   Covering letters may sit among the "lettres écrites par et à Philippe II". This is the cheapest check: open Gallica
   `btv1b90096628` around ff. 210–358 and the 8 Oct 1579 Dor letter.
2. **Copies of the 26 Apr 1579 type.** Catherine's letters of 1579 survive in copies in fr. 3300 and fr. 3319. Those
   registers may also copy Saint-Goard's *doubles* sent to her.
3. **Archives nationales K 1541–1561** (Simancas, returned in part): Saint-Goard's 1579 memorials to Philip II, as seen
   from the Spanish side. This is not the French series.
4. **fr. 17019 ff. 176–197**: the old item list. It would show whether the 1579 dispatches were ever in Harlay 228.
