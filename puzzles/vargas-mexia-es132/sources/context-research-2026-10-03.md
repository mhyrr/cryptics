# Context research, 2026-10-03 (es. 132 f. 198)

Scope: background only. No decipherment of f. 198 sought or recorded. Many fetches hit 403 (Google Books, kb.osu.edu, core.ac.uk, academia.edu, filosofia.org); anything below not fetched is listed under Unverified.

## 1. Rubino (2012)
- Citation (as given by Tomokiyo, cached `cache/cryptiana/spanish3D.htm`): Samantha R. Rubino (2012), "The Secrets of Antonio Pérez Decoded", The Ohio State University, Department of History Honors Theses. Record: https://kb.osu.edu/handle/1811/51582 (CLAIMANT-adjacent / SCHOLARLY-student thesis; undergraduate honors thesis, not peer reviewed). NOT fetched (403); abstract and text unread.
- Tomokiyo's page (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/spanish3D.htm, POPULAR/expert blog, tier SECONDARY at best) says: Rubino "points out that f.198 may contain new facts about the murder of Escobedo... but the cipher letters remain undeciphered". His HTML comment cites "Rubino p.15" for Vargas Mexía's arrival in Paris 1577.
- Tomokiyo's table of contents: no.89 (f.198) "Ant[onio] Perez" to Vargas Mexía, Madrid, 15 April 1579, Vargas Mexía's Cipher 4. Cipher 4 used Sept 1578 to April 1579, also in Pérez's letters; reconstructed by Devos (1950) p.422 from Vargas Mexía's letter of 16 May 1579. Neighbours: f.195 Philip II, 18 Mar 1579 (Cipher 3); f.200, 202 Philip II 21 Apr and 17 May 1579 (Cipher 3).
- Web-search summaries that credit Rubino with identifying four ciphers are conflated; Tomokiyo identified them. Unverified what Rubino herself deciphered.

## 3. Timeline (SECONDARY only; scholarly sources not reached)
- 31 Mar 1578 (Easter Monday night): Escobedo, secretary of Don Juan of Austria, stabbed in Madrid. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Escobedo (SECONDARY). Motives disputed: Escobedo's role in Don Juan's schemes; gossip about Pérez and the Princess of Éboli.
- Accusation fell on Pérez immediately; Escobedo family, backed by Mateo Vázquez, kept denouncing Pérez and Éboli to the king: Spanish summary from Lafuente's Historia general (search snippet of https://filosofia.org/his/laf/p302c22.htm, 403; SECONDARY, 19th c.), https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_del_Hierro.
- 28 Jul 1579: Pérez arrested (night), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_(statesman) (SECONDARY). Later: 1584-85 visita and conviction for corruption/revealing secrets; 1590 torture, escape 19 Apr 1590; Aragón 1591.
- Marqués de los Vélez's role: NOT found. Unverified. Needs Marañón, Antonio Pérez (1947); Dadson; Ungerer (Pérez bibliography and "El secretario Antonio Pérez"-type studies). Titles from memory, unverified.
- Pérez's precise position in April 1579: not sourced.
