# Context checks for the essay update · 2026-10-16

## Papal envoy

Ivan Cloulas, “La diplomatie pontificale médiatrice entre la France et l'Espagne. La mission de l'archevêque de
Nazareth auprès de François d'Anjou (1578),” *Mélanges de la Casa de Velázquez* 5 (1969), pp. 451–459.
[Article](https://www.persee.fr/doc/casa_0076-230x_1969_num_5_1_1006),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3406/casa.1969.1006). **SCHOLARLY**.

Read the first page's text on Persée. It identifies Fabio Mirto Frangipani, Archbishop of Nazareth, as Gregory XIII's
chosen spokesman to Anjou in 1578. It also describes Lalaing's support for the French prince's intervention in the
Netherlands. This supports the essay's short gloss “papal envoy”; it is not independent verification of the secret
instruction on f. 85. The rest of the article was not inspected. The episode's historical context is already studied;
the experiment's priority claim concerns a decipherment of these particular manuscript letters.

## Lansac and Longlée

[“Guy de Saint-Gelais,” section “Treason”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_de_Saint-Gelais#Treason), **SECONDARY**,
read 2026-10-16. It identifies Lansac as a French courtier and soldier. It reports, citing Ribera 2018, that Saint-Goard
used Longlée to investigate the Lansac affair; it also attributes to Le Roux the claim that Philip disclosed the
scheme himself. Those books remain uninspected. The essay note retains Longlée as a candidate and records the
unresolved difference. No identification of the cipher name, or accusation against Pérez, follows from this check.
