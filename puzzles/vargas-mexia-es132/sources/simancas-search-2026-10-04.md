# Simancas search, Vargas Mexía embassy 1578-79 (2026-10-04)

Method: Paz's printed catalogue (full OCR text from archive.org) read directly; PARES not queryable (see Unverified).

Catalogue source: Julián Paz, *Catálogo IV: Secretaría de Estado (Capitulaciones con Francia y negociaciones diplomáticas...)*, Archivo de Simancas, Madrid 1914. https://archive.org/details/catlogo4secret01spai (OCR: https://archive.org/download/catlogo4secret01spai/catlogo4secret01spai_djvu.txt). Tier: SCHOLARLY (finding aid, printed). Pages ~380-400.

IMPORTANT: Paz summarizes per legajo (6-month blocks), NOT item by item. No item dated 20 Oct 1578 or 16 May 1579 is individually listed. Signatures below are legajo level only; document numbers within legajos are unknown.

Notes: Paz says the cipher originals in K 1550-51 are what the clear (deciphered) letters in other legajos were deciphered from; so the clear contemporary decipherments of Vargas letters are in K 1544-1557, and a *clear draft* of Philip's 20 Oct 1578 reply, if extant, is most likely in K 1550-51 or K 1556 (outgoing minutas). Not confirmed at item level. Paz mentions Escobedo nowhere in the sections read (grep for Escobedo not run on the 1578 blocks; Antonio Pérez appears only in legajo titles K 1546, 1554-55).
