**Subject:** Undeciphered Saint-Goard cipher letter, 23 Dec 1579 (BnF fr. 16106): partial reading

Dear Mr Tomokiyo,

BnF Français 16106, ff. 363r–366r (Gallica ark btv1b90096628, views 373–376) is Saint-Goard's letter to Henri III, Madrid, 23 December 1579: about 215 lines in what your henryiii.htm page calls "Longlée's cipher" (key: Mousset 1912, pp. lviii–lix, and your reconstruction). Your page gives no decipherment; we found no published reading, but could not consult Gellard 2014, Ribera 2018 or Vázquez de Prada 2004.

Roughly half the letter reads as continuous French; the rest is fragments. Checked on the image: f. 363v ll. 26–28 "celes de don Anthoine de Castre, seigneur de Cascais"; f. 364r ll. 2–7 "place qui est Cascais, qui est toute l'importence de l'entree de la riviere de Lisboune … dont enfin deppend tout Portugal". Outside check: Castro to Saint-Goard, Cascais, 25 November 1579, French translation (BnF Espagnol 336 ff. 74r–75r; Gallica btv1b100325613, views 131–133), matches on writer, place, the request to relay to the King, and a proposed secret meeting. It does not offer Cascais.

Method, since your site notes AI use: Claude models (Anthropic) read the glyphs from Gallica scans, three independent readings per page; scripted agreement is 0.41–0.82 on the cipher pages, 0.93 on the last. Show-through was removed by subtracting the registered mirror image of each leaf's other side. No paleographer has checked any line. The sign your chart draws among the nulls as "2p/ze" reads i/j here.

Two questions: do you know of any prior decipherment, and do our readings of the checked passages look right to you? Working text, per-line glyph readings and scripts: on request, or at [LINK] once the repository is public.

Thank you also for your Vargas Mexía work. Your 2020 article "Finding the Keys to Philip II's Cipher Letters to Juan de Vargas Mexia" let us read BnF Espagnol 132.

Best regards,
Greg Olsen

## Follow-up, drafted 2026-11-10 (not sent)

**Subject:** Correction: the Saint-Goard letter of 23 Dec 1579 has a contemporary decipherment (fr. 16106 ff. 208–209)

Dear Mr Tomokiyo,

A correction to my message of today. The cipher letter of 23 December 1579 (BnF fr. 16106 ff. 363r–366r) is not
undeciphered. Its contemporary decipherment is in the same volume at ff. 208r–209v (Gallica views 216–218), headed
by a later hand "23 avril 1579, Dechifré de la precedente" and bound after a letter of 15 March 1579. The date is
wrong: the text runs from "Quant aux advis que je luy puis donner" to "monarque de tout le monde" and matches the
December cipher throughout, including "don Anthonie de Castres, seigneur de Cascaiz". Mousset (p. xlviii) noted
similar misplacements in fr. 16105.

So the useful results are different from what I first wrote: a misdated piece identified (there is no dispatch of
April 1579 in the volume), and a full sixteenth-century check on a machine reading of this cipher from microfilm.
The sign note stands: your "2p/ze" reads i/j in this letter.

With apologies for the first version,
Greg Olsen
