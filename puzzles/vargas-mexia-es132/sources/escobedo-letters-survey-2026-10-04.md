# Escobedo test: survey of letters, 10 Dec 1577 – Dec 1578 (2026-10-04)

Source survey only (AGENTS.md rule 9: no readings attempted). Question: did Philip II receive, or ask Vargas Mexía about, a Don John–Guise understanding before Escobedo's murder (31 Mar 1578)? Pérez says yes (Mignet pp. 68–69, quoting Pérez's Memorial pp. 304–5); Mignet (pp. 71–73, appendix E pp. 436–440) says Vargas's reports are "presque toutes postérieures au meurtre".

Tiers: PRIMARY = object/facsimile/finding aid of the object; SCHOLARLY = academic print; SECONDARY = encyclopedic; CLAIMANT = a reading/solution by its author; POPULAR. "verified" = I fetched the source this session and the stated fact is on the page; "unverified" = inferred, OCR-garbled, or taken from a third party without checking the image.

Working files (scratchpad, not committed): Paz djvu `catlogo4secret01spai_djvu.txt`; Teulet vol. 5 djvu `relationspolitiq05teul_0_djvu.txt` (OCR, so page and bundle numbers below may carry OCR errors); Gachard djvu `labibliothque01gach_djvu.txt`; BnF record `ark:/12148/cc34747q`.

## 0. Findings that change the picture (read first)

1. **Teulet vol. 5 (1862) prints Vargas's letters of the window with the contemporary Simancas decipherment ("Déchiffr. officiel").** No decoding is needed for: 31 Dec 1577, 28 Jan 1578, 16 Feb 1578, 16 Mar 1578, 27 Mar 1578 (all pre-murder), then 13 Apr 1578 onwards. They are excerpts with French analytic headings, not complete letters. Source: https://archive.org/details/relationspolitiq05teul_0, pp. 132–146 (verified in OCR text; SCHOLARLY).
2. **Mignet's "Ojo!" letter is Vargas's letter of 13 April 1578** (Teulet pp. 143–146, "liasse B. 44, n. 57" per OCR, "Déchiffr. officiel"): the Duke of Guise's conversation at 5–6 a.m. about an English/Scottish enterprise, with "la union destas dos coronas ... podrian dar ley al mundo, y partirsele entre si". It is post-murder (13 Apr), which supports Mignet's dating. Its subject is a Guise–Lorraine proposal to Philip, not a Don John–Guise pact. (Match by wording: verified. Bundle number: Mignet's OCR reads "liasse ii, n° M 4"; Teulet's OCR reads "B. 44, n. 57"; unreconciled.)
3. **A pre-murder Vargas letter does link Don John and Guise**, as hearsay: **16 Feb 1578** (Teulet p. 137, "B. 45, n. 50", deciphered). Vargas relays the Venetian ambassador: the Scottish ambassador is negotiating through the Guise house to marry Don John to Mary Stuart, and Don John "con su exercito y la assistencia que le diesen los de Guisa" should take England. Vargas calls it "quimeras". Mignet himself cites this item (marriage rumours; his note "liasse 15 [sic], n° 4U") and Philip's reply, but does not treat it as a pre-murder Don John–Guise report.
4. **Philip's reply to those reports is, on a third party's reading, in our cipher letter of 8 March 1578 (f. 17r–20v + duplicate f. 22r–25r, Cipher 2).** Mignet printed two passages of it in Spanish in appendix E (p. 437 n. 2 "Muy bien havais hecho en avisarme de lo que el duque de Guisa havia comunicado..." and p. 439 n. 5 on the marriages), citing "série B, liasse 47, n° 47" (the Simancas minute). The identification of f. 17–25 with B.47 n.47 is made in the cabinet-noir repo (see 3.4), CLAIMANT, unverified by us. If right, Philip was answering eight Vargas letters of 26 Jan – 16 Feb 1578 that touched Guise, i.e. **Guise reports reached the King well before 31 Mar**. This is the strongest lead for H16 (it is ciphered Cipher 2, which we hold).
5. **Gallica has the whole volume (images online, free).** https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x ; IIIF manifest https://gallica.bnf.fr/iiif/ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x/manifest.json fetched OK (288 canvases for 290 ff. per BnF; a third party says ff. 26v–31r are missing from the scan, unverified; check canvas-to-folio mapping before relying on it). BnF record gives a per-letter description and marks "En chiffre / En partie chiffrée": https://archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc34747q (PRIMARY finding aid, verified).
6. **PARES was down or erroring throughout** (503/502 via Playwright, urllib and WebFetch; the search page returned 200 once via urllib to pares.mcu.es but no result queries completed). No item-level signatures or image status for Estado K 1543–1558 obtained. Not resolved (see section 4).
7. **Es. 132 holds the King's side only** (Philip/Pérez → Vargas), and per Gachard "à toutes celles qui sont chiffrées (sauf une seule) le déchiffrement manque". Vargas's own letters and the Simancas decipherments are AGS Estado K 1543–1558 (Paris-era Archives nationales "Papiers de Simancas, série B, liasses 42–51", i.e. the same bundles Mignet and Teulet cite).

## (e) Historians' positions

- **Pérez (CLAIMANT)**: Vargas denounced Don John–Guise "confederation ... sous le nom de défense des deux couronnes"; King alarmed; placed in "first months of 1577" (Memorial) or July 1577 (under torture). Via Mignet pp. 68, 456 (SCHOLARLY, verified in `mignet.txt`). Pérez's Memorial pp. 304–5.
- **Mignet 1846 (SCHOLARLY)**: Vargas reached Paris on 10 Dec 1577 so his reports could not explain a decision taken when Escobedo returned (July 1577); the first poisoning attempts in Feb 1578; Vargas knew only a "grande confidence" between Don John and Guise, "va peut-être plus avant du côté des Guise"; Guise reported in 1578 wants union of the two crowns and an English enterprise, not a faction. Note: Mignet's own text and notes cite pre-murder Vargas reports (31 Dec 1577; 16 Feb 1578) and the King's replies (B.47 n.47) while calling the Don John–Guise information "presque toutes postérieures".
- **Gachard 1875 (SCHOLARLY)**: confirms Vargas arrival 10 Dec 1577; says Simancas holds all Vargas's letters; es. 132's ciphers undeciphered.
- **Marañón, Parker (*The Imprudent King*, 2014), Lovett, Fernández Álvarez, García Hernán, Sánchez Belén, Jensen**: not retrieved. Web searches this session returned only encyclopaedic summaries (Wikipedia/encyclopedia.com/Britannica: SECONDARY) saying Pérez poisoned Philip's mind about Don John's English plan and Mary Stuart marriage, and Marañón's conclusion that Pérez, not the King, engineered the murder (https://alexanderrose.substack.com/p/the-magnifico, POPULAR, unverified). **No modern work was found that examines Vargas's Dec 1577 – Mar 1578 dispatches on the Guise question.**
