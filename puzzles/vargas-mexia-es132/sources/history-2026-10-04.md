## 1. Antonio Pérez

1.1 Pérez was named Secretary of the King on 17 July 1567 and Secretary of State, with the Italian negotiation, on 8 Dec 1567. https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/157767.pdf (Martínez Navas p. 141) SCHOLARLY. verified [text read].
1.2 Mignet's own summary: on his father's death (1567) Antonio replaced him as minister and was, with Zayas, one of the two secretaries of the Council of State. https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY. verified [text read].
1.3 He was arrested the night of 28 July 1579, and the Princess of Éboli the same night (taken to Pinto). Lafuente: "En la noche del 28 de julio de 1579 se ejecutó en Madrid la prisión de los dos más notables personajes de la corte". https://filosofia.org/his/laf/p302c22.htm SECONDARY. verified [text read]. Also https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_(statesman) SECONDARY verified (28 July 1579, "along with Princess of Éboli the same night").
1.4 Pérez was first held in the house of the alcalde de corte Álvaro García de Toledo (about four months), then allowed to go to his own house under guard. Lafuente, same URL. SECONDARY. verified [text read].
1.5 Visita (corruption review) 1584; house-arrest window jump Jan 1585; sentenced March 1585 to two years' prison plus banishment (Wikipedia, SECONDARY, verified via fetch summary). Martínez Navas: sentenced in the Visita to removal from office and ten years' exile from court; in 1585 Philip II "accedió a las reclamaciones de la familia de Escobedo" and Pérez was prosecuted for the murder; Escobedo's sons withdrew in 1589; torture and confession 1590; flight to Aragon 19 April 1590. https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/157767.pdf SCHOLARLY verified [text read]. Lafuente has a secret reserved inquiry by Rodrigo Vázquez de Arce from 30 May 1582 and the King's letter of 4 Jan 1590 pushing the new turn of the murder case. SECONDARY verified [text read].
1.3b Flight to France: 10 Nov 1591 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_(statesman), SECONDARY, verified via fetch summary); died Paris 3 Nov 1611 per Britannica search snippet (https://www.britannica.com/biography/Antonio-Perez, SECONDARY, unverified; not fetched; Wikipedia article lists 7 April 1611 in a search snippet, see Discrepancies).

## 2. Escobedo and the family's suit

2.1 Juan de Escobedo, secretary of Don John of Austria, was killed in Madrid on 31 March 1578 (Easter Monday night, about 9:30 pm, Calle de la Almudena). https://ejercito.defensa.gob.es/museo/en/HECHOS_HISTORICOS/HECHOS_HISTORICOS/03.31_marzo.-_ASESINATO_DE_JUAN_DE_ESCOBEDO.html SECONDARY (Spanish Army museum) verified; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Escobedo SECONDARY verified; Lafuente "(31 de marzo, 1578)" verified [text read].
2.2 Public accusation fell on Pérez at once and the King was also suspected. Lafuente: "La acusación pública de este crimen recayó desde luego sobre el primer secretario de Estado Antonio Pérez". https://filosofia.org/his/laf/p302c22.htm SECONDARY verified [text read].
2.3 The family, "apoyada por un temible enemigo de Antonio Pérez, que lo era Mateo Vázquez", denounced Pérez and Éboli to the King and demanded a court inquiry. Lafuente (same URL) SECONDARY verified [text read]. Mignet: Philip II received Don Pedro Escobedo (eldest son) in audience, took the family's memorials, and promised justice "s'il y avait lieu"; the family's key protector was Mateo Vázquez; Escobedo's advisers named as Don Pedro de Belandi, Pedro Negrete, Diego Núñez de Toledo. https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY verified [text read, c. pp. 119-121].
2.4 How it was suspended: the King had the President of the Council of Castile, Antonio Pazos (bishop of Córdoba), tell Pedro de Escobedo (after Pérez had confided the causes to Pazos) that Pérez and Éboli were as innocent "como yo"; Pedro gave his word "por mi, por mi hermano y por mi madre" to drop it. Vázquez did not desist and recruited another relative. Mignet pp. 126-128 (quoting Pérez's *Relaciones* pp. 12-14) and Lafuente. CLAIMANT-sourced in origin (the account is Pérez's own *Relaciones*, filtered through Mignet; Lafuente repeats it) so the Pazos speech is Pérez's version. verified [text read]. Mignet adds the prosecution reached the courts only "dix ans après". verified.
2.5 Mignet footnote, directly relevant to the 15 Apr 1579 letter: "Au mois d'avril 1579, Perez se plaignit à Philippe II de ce que ce prince poussait la précaution jusqu'à éviter de lui donner audience, et supplia le roi de faire cesser les poursuites de la famille Escovedo"; the King put the gap down to Easter devotions and wrote that he had avoided prescribing anything because that would admit his part in the murder (source cited: "Ms. La Haye, f. 101"). https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY verified [text read]. This is the one place I found a historian pinning an April 1579 Pérez-King exchange about the Escobedo suit.
2.6 Wikipedia (SECONDARY, verified via fetch summary) says the Escobedo family "gave up this claim" a few months after the murder; Martínez Navas (above) says the sons withdrew only in 1589 from the criminal case. See Discrepancies.

## 3. Ana de Mendoza, Princess of Éboli

3.1 Widow of Ruy Gómez de Silva (d. 1573); relation with Pérez: Lafuente asserts "amorosas intimidades" as "fuera de duda"; Martínez Navas says only that rumours of intimacy circulated. Lafuente SECONDARY / Martínez Navas SCHOLARLY, both verified [text read]. Contested (rule 3): Spanish Wikipedia says the nature of the link "remains unclear" (https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Mendoza_y_de_la_Cerda, SECONDARY, verified via fetch summary).
3.2 Confined 28 July 1579 at the Torre de Pinto; moved Feb 1580 to Santorcaz; 1581 to her palace at Pastrana, where she stayed under house arrest; d. 2 Feb 1592 (the same page also gives 12 Feb). https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Mendoza_y_de_la_Cerda SECONDARY verified (fetch summary).
3.3 Philip II wrote to the Duke of Infantado and Duke of Medina Sidonia on 29 July 1579 that she was arrested because she did not smooth, and delayed, the reconciliation of Pérez and Mateo Vázquez. Mignet, https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY verified [text read]. Lafuente: the ostensible cause was "la oposición a reconciliarse con el secretario Mateo Vázquez". verified [text read].

## 4. Gaspar de Quiroga

4.1 Archbishop of Toledo 1577-94, Inquisitor General 1573-94, cardinal 1578 (Gregory XIII), d. 20 Nov 1594. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaspar_de_Quiroga_y_Vela SECONDARY verified.
4.2 In Mignet's account Quiroga belonged to the Pérez party: a 1570s Venetian report (Contarini) names as one faction "l'archevêque de Tolède, du marquis de Los Vélez, d'Antonio Perez, de Mateo Vasquez et de Santoyo"; and Mignet compares the Barajas-Vázquez-Chaves "amistad" to the one "qui existait entre Antonio Perez, le marquis de los Vélez et le cardinal de Tolède, don Gaspar de Quiroga". https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY verified [text read; the Venetian passage is a Contarini relation, c. 1570s, the date was not captured].
4.3 Éboli, asked by the King (through Chaves) for proof of her complaints against Vázquez, "en appela aux témoignages de don Gaspar Quiroga, cardinal-archevêque de Tolède" (and the preacher Hernando del Castillo), who "ne les lui refusèrent point". Mignet, same URL, SECONDARY->SCHOLARLY, citing Pérez's *Relaciones* p. 24-26. verified [text read]. This is Éboli-Quiroga collaboration in 1579 but about the Vázquez quarrel, not about asking Pérez to stay.
4.4 After the arrests, "por orden del rey pasó el cardenal de Toledo a consolar a la esposa de Antonio Pérez doña Juana Coello". Lafuente. SECONDARY verified [text read]. Quiroga later said to the confessor, on the 1590 turn of the case: "o yo soy loco, o este negocio es loco". Lafuente. verified [text read].
4.5 Quiroga later interceded with Philip for Éboli so she and her children could leave Pinto/Santorcaz for the Pastrana palace: web-search summary of a docelinajes/ES Wikipedia item, https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_del_Hierro. SECONDARY, unverified (not found in the fetch summary of that page).
4.6 NOT FOUND in anything I read: a statement that in April 1579 the King (or anyone) used the Archbishop of Toledo "through the Princess of Éboli" to persuade Pérez to remain. A search summary of Mignet claimed "Philip II retained Perez at court by having the Cardinal Archbishop of Toledo and the Princess intervene"; I read the 1846 Mignet full text and could not locate such a sentence (Gallica texte brut was blocked, so the 1874 edition / Muro were not checked). Marañón (1947), Parker, Fernández Álvarez, Muro not accessible. Status for the decoded letter: unconfirmed by the historians I could read; the nearest hits are 2.5, 4.2 and 4.3. unverified.

## 5. Pedro Fajardo, 3rd Marqués de los Vélez

5.1 Born c. 1530, died 1579, councillor of State and of War, mayordomo mayor of Queen Anna, ex-ambassador to the Emperor. Search summary of https://www.regmurcia.com/servlet/s.Sl?sit=c%2C373&r=ReP-21517-DETALLE_REPORTAJESPADRE and https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marqu%C3%A9s_de_los_V%C3%A9lez (death "1579" verified via fetch summary; offices unverified). SECONDARY.
5.2 Mignet: Philip chose Barajas as mayordomo mayor "après la mort du marquis de los Vêlez", and calls him "grand ami" of Pérez; Mignet says Vélez "venait de mourir tout rempli de soupçons et de tristesse" shortly before the arrests. Lafuente: Barajas was named mayordomo mayor "en reemplazo del marqués de los Vélez" before the arrests. https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign ; https://filosofia.org/his/laf/p302c22.htm. SCHOLARLY/SECONDARY verified [text read]. Exact month of death not found.
5.2b So the marqués was dead or dying by the time of the July 1579 arrests; his role in an April 1579 episode is unknown.
5.3 es.wikipedia's Pérez article says Pérez sought the vacant Vargas secretariat "con el apoyo del marqués de los Vélez y del arzobispo Quiroga". https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_del_Hierro SECONDARY verified (fetch summary; paraphrase).

## 6. Mateo Vázquez

6.1 Royal secretary since 29 March 1573 per es.wikipedia; rival of Pérez and Éboli; "uno de los que hicieron estallar el escándalo"; d. 5 May 1591. https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mateo_V%C3%A1zquez_de_Leca SECONDARY verified (fetch summary).
6.2 Feud mechanics (Mignet pp. 128-133): Éboli wrote to the King calling Vázquez "un perro moro"; a paper Vázquez attached to a despacho said Pérez was "no de buena casta" (a month before the arrests, at the Escorial); Philip delayed, writing it would be "bien… expedir las consultas" first; Vázquez, Chaves and Barajas formed an "amistad" against that of Vélez-Pérez-Quiroga. https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign SCHOLARLY verified [text read]; sources are Pérez's *Relaciones* (CLAIMANT in origin).
6.3 After the arrests Pérez was made to swear "pleito homenaje de amistad" to Vázquez. Lafuente. SECONDARY verified [text read].

## 7. Don John of Austria

7.1 Died 1 Oct 1578 of fever at Bouge, near Namur. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_of_Austria SECONDARY verified.
7.2 Alexander Farnese (duke of Parma; "prince of Parma" in the older style) succeeded him as governor of the Netherlands. same URL SECONDARY verified (Wikipedia says "Duke of Parma"; Farnese held the duchy only from 1586, so "Prince of Parma" is the correct 1578 style; the latter is my own note, unverified).

## 8. Juan de Vargas Mexía

8.1 Ambassador in Paris 1577-1580 (successor Juan Bautista de Tassis, from Jan 1581). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_Spain_to_France and https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anexo:Embajadores_de_Espa%C3%B1a_en_Francia SECONDARY verified (fetch summaries). fr.wikipedia gives only the start (1577).
8.2 Rubino (using the printed will clauses in Álvarez y Baena, *Hijos de Madrid*, vol. 3, pp. 120-1): career from 1550, ambassador to Savoy 1573, Paris 1577; hábito de Santiago 24 June 1576; brother Francisco de Vargas Mexía (ambassador at Venice, Rome, Trent). `cache/rubino.txt` pp. 14-15. SCHOLARLY-student, verified (read locally). Primary witnesses (the will) not seen.
8.3 Death: Rubino says "In 1581, Juan de Vargas Mexia died" (p. 3), but also has the revised will drawn up in Paris in 1580 and the letters running to May 1581 (p. 4). Rubino footnote 7 cites Gachard, *La Bibliothèque Nationale à Paris* (Brussels 1875-7) 1:416, citing a Simancas letter: Diego de Maldonado (acting ambassador) to Philip, 23 Sept 1580, announcing that Vargas Mexía's papers were in the hands of local Jesuits. That implies he was dead by Sept 1580. verified as stated in Rubino; Gachard and the Simancas letter not seen. See Discrepancies.
8.4 Link to Pérez: Rubino: the 1577 will (made before leaving Madrid) named Pérez executor and, failing family heirs, left a university trust (c. 30,000 mrs/yr) to Pérez's sons; the revised 1580 will drops Pérez and his family; Rubino infers the 15 Apr 1579 letter was the cause. `cache/rubino.txt` pp. 15-16, 27-28. SCHOLARLY-student, verified as stated; the inference is Rubino's own and untested (CLAIMANT-adjacent). Will itself not seen.
8.5 Mignet notes Pérez named "Juan de Vargas Mexia, ambassadeur d'Espagne à Paris" as the man who informed Philip of a Don John-Guise concert (Pérez's claim; Mignet doubts the date and weight). https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign CLAIMANT-via-SCHOLARLY verified [text read].

## 9. "El oficio de Vargas"

9.1 Candidate: the Italian/Mediterranean secretaryship of Diego de Vargas. es.wikipedia: "Los asuntos del Mediterráneo quedaron al cargo de Diego de Vargas, tras cuya muerte Antonio intentó por todos los medios conseguir asimismo esa secretaría"; "con el apoyo del marqués de los Vélez y del arzobispo Quiroga" Pérez asked for "la vacante de Vargas", the Count of Chinchón and others who feared him opposed, and the King gave Mediterranean/Italian affairs to Gabriel de Zayas. https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_P%C3%A9rez_del_Hierro SECONDARY verified (fetch summary; the page prints the year "1568").
9.2 Dating problem: Quiroga became Archbishop of Toledo only in 1577, and a RAH-derived search snippet gives Diego de Vargas's death as 26 Sept 1576 (https://dbe.rah.es/biografias/30328/diego-de-vargas, SCHOLARLY, unverified: page returned empty shell). So "1568" is almost certainly a mistake for c. 1576-77, and the vacancy plausibly falls before Pérez's 1579 letter. Martínez Navas has Pérez already in charge of Italy from Dec 1567, so "Vargas's office" must be some other part of the Italy/Mediterranean secretariat or a seat distinct from that. Interpretation, unverified.
9.3 Other candidate: an office of the ambassador himself (Juan de Vargas Mexía, Paris) or of another Vargas: nothing found. A vice-chancellorship of Aragon or a council seat held by a Vargas: not found.
9.4 Status: the Diego de Vargas vacancy is a real, sourced candidate for "oficio de Vargas" and the "desmembrar y repartir" fits the 9.1 outcome (split Mediterranean/Italian affairs, Zayas getting a share), but no source I read connects it to April 1579. unverified.

## 11. Dates

11.1 Philip's arrest order: Martínez Navas gives 26 July 1579 for the order and the sources above give the night of 28 July for the arrest; Éboli's arrest the same night. (See Discrepancies.) Mignet: the letters to Infantado and Medina Sidonia explaining the arrest are dated 29 July. verified [text read].
11.2 Proceedings: Visita 1584 (house arrest, window jump Jan 1585, sentence Mar 1585 per Wikipedia); formal murder prosecution of Pérez from 1585 (Martínez Navas); sons withdrew 1589; torture Feb 1590 per es.wikipedia; confession and flight 19 Apr 1590; Aragon: Alterations 1591; flight to France Nov 1591. All SECONDARY/SCHOLARLY, verified above.
11.3 Lafuente: reserved inquiry 30 May to mid-Aug 1582 (Rodrigo Vázquez de Arce); a 4 Jan 1590 royal letter to the judges. verified [text read].

## 12. Reconciliation attempts, 1579

12.1 Lafuente, same chapter: the King "prefirió que el presidente del Consejo de Castilla don Antonio Pazos … hablara al hijo de Escobedo"; "Antonio Pérez pedía a su soberano le permitiera retirarse de su servicio, y Felipe no lo consentía"; "Su grande empeño era que se reconciliara la princesa con el secretario Vázquez, a cuyo efecto hizo servir de intermediario a fray Diego de Chaves"; "Las gestiones del religioso se estrellaron en la altiva firmeza de la de Éboli"; "Intentó luego reconciliar por lo menos a los dos secretarios Pérez y Vázquez; pero aquél … se mantuvo igualmente inflexible". https://filosofia.org/his/laf/p302c22.htm SECONDARY verified [text read]. (Lafuente's text matches the paraphrase that circulates on Wikipedia pages.)
12.2 Pérez asked to retire; Mignet quotes the *Relaciones* (p. 15) "que le dexase retirar de la corte, y de su servicio… el rey no queria esto". https://archive.org/details/antonioperezetph00mign verified [text read]. The "retire" request is the same as Lafuente's, so it is a repeated tradition rooted in Pérez's *Relaciones* (CLAIMANT).
12.3 Éboli's reply to Chaves (Mignet): a person like her could not enter such a thing with that man; Pérez wrote to the King that he handed back the King's promise of satisfaction and begged to retire. verified [text read].
12.4 The role in 1579 of a "Archbishop of Toledo working through the Princess": see 4.6, not found.

## Discrepancies

- Arrest order date: 26 July 1579 (Martínez Navas, SCHOLARLY) vs night of 28 July (Lafuente, Wikipedia, Rubino, Mignet-adjacent). Likely 26 = order, 28 = execution, but not stated.
- Vargas Mexía's death: 1580 (a search summary of a DBE-type source, unverified) vs 1581 (Rubino) vs a Sept 1580 acting ambassador (Maldonado, via Rubino/Gachard) implying death by Sept 1580; Rubino also says letters to him run to May 1581 and Tassis starts Jan 1581 per Wikipedia. If Vargas died in 1580, the "May 1581" end date of es. 132 and "died 1581" cannot both stand.
- Ambassador term end: en/es Wikipedia list 1577-1580; fr.wikipedia list shows Bernardo de Mendoza as the next entry (Nov 1584), omitting Tassis; both unverified for the end date.
- "Vargas" vacancy dated "1568" on es.wikipedia but Quiroga was not archbishop until 1577 and Diego de Vargas probably died 1576 (see 9.2).
- Escobedo family's suit: dropped "a few months later" (Wikipedia) vs persisted to 1585/1589 (Martínez Navas; Mignet "dix ans après"). The reading consistent with all: the eldest son desisted early on Pazos's urging (Pérez's version), Vázquez found other relatives, and the family withdrew only in 1589.
- Pérez's death date: 3 Nov 1611 (Britannica snippet) vs 7 April 1611 (Wikipedia snippet), both unverified.
- Éboli death: 2 Feb 1592 vs 12 Feb 1592 (es.wikipedia, internally inconsistent).
- Éboli-Pérez relationship: asserted by Lafuente, characterized as rumor by Martínez Navas, unclear per es.wikipedia.
