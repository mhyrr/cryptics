# f. 103 transcription notes (blind reader, Cipher 3 guide v2.1)

## Method
Gallica IIIF, canvas 100 (f. 103r, right page) and canvas 101 (f. 103v left page; f. 104r right page). Native-resolution
strips, 1500x260 px, step 200 px, two halves per strip (overlap 80-300 px). Crops in sources/cache/f103-crops/
(r*=103r, v*=103v, sig/perez/low/dock = signatures, docket). No key, analysis or other transcription opened.

## Layout
- 103r: "El Rey" (clear, cross over it) top centre, folio "103" top right, then clear address line
  "Ju° de vargas Mexia" and cipher begins on the same line after "/ po u. de". 33 lines. Last line 103r.33 is a short indented fragment (`16+<v>`).
- 103v (left page of canvas 101): 15 cipher lines. The right ends of lines are cut or darkened by the gutter/binding shadow,
  so line-final signs are missing or doubtful on 103v (and slightly on 103r lines near the right margin: none lost there).
- Date: "Madrid a 13 de octubre 1578" (bar over 1578, heavy stop after). Signature below read as "Yo el Rey" (flourished, low confidence).
  A further signature below, read "Ant.º Perez" (secretary), partly into the gutter. A small separate hand near the foot of 103v ("dupda"? with two dots) looks like a later note.
- So the letter ENDS on 103v. f. 104r is blank apart from the folio number "104" and a faint vertical bleed-through/docket (illegible at native resolution; crop dock.jpg).

## Counts (pass-2 final; 48 numbered cipher lines: 103r.01-33, 103v.01-15)
- Number-based tokens: ~802. Letter-form tokens (u, y, m, L, T, Z, S, g, i, mp, H etc): ~92. Clear-Spanish spans: 5 ([[de]] x3, address line, po).
- Tokens carrying `?`: ~75 (many more are uncertain without a mark: see reliability).
- Reliability: moderate. Pen joins and faint ink made token splits uncertain; dot/plus/hook after-marks and marks above are the weakest.
  103v lines 03, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11 are smudged at the left (faint) and rated low.

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right per crop. Pass 2: right-to-left re-check of each line against the crops (crops already in view; a
sample of crops, e.g. r04L and r16R, were reopened to test the u./2 question). No wholesale rewrite resulted.
Pass-2 changes: Z-shaped flourish letter unified as `Z` (103r.17, 103v.05, 07, 13, 14; earlier `7? o? m`); 103v.08 left start
reread as `10 4<bar> 24p`; 103v.11 gets faint `4? 11p?` start; 103v.12-15 added after row-alignment fix (lines slope up to the right ~60 px per 1500 px, so right-half rows sit higher than the left-half row of the same line).

## v2.1 addendum
Checked "u." glyphs: they have a cup form with an entry stroke and no descender, standing alone before a dot, so kept as `u.`
(not 2). Digit 2s inside numbers are z-shaped and were already written 2; every "12" (short upright + z-2) written `12`.
Tokens changed because of v2.1: none. Marks above re-checked per line; I may still have dropped some (acute/hat) on faint lines.
Doubt: some `u.` may be z-2 plus dot (`2.`): 103r.04, 05, 06, 09, 10, 16, 17, 22-30, 103v.* .

## Curl uncertainty
Curl-joined: every `66`/`166`/`266`/`6618` etc. is a joined curl or a literal 6 pair; I could not separate them reliably.
Unsure curl joins: 103r.01 166<bar>; 103r.07 66 2966; 103r.08 266 / 566<bar>; 103r.21 6618; 103v.08 166<bar>.

## Every `?` and `<cross>`
See transcription.txt (grep '?'). Signs written <cross>: 103r.08 `0<cross>?`; 103r.16 `T.<cross>`, `u<cross>`; 103r.18 `T.<cross>`;
103v.01 `T.<cross>`; 103v.03 `u<cross>`. Also `El Rey` has a cross above (clear text, not transcribed).
