## Method
Pass 1: left to right, 1-2 line strips (about 1500 x 230 px native, two halves overlapping 150 px), worked in the opposite order: 105v first, then 105r from the bottom up. Draft kept in `pass1.txt` (it still uses the pre-pass-2 "27" glyph value and 15p/25p confusion; see below).
Pass 2: complete, right to left, every line of 105r and 105v re-read in fresh crops (three 1100 px windows per line, different cut points than pass 1), plus 105v in four tall crops. Then addendum v2.1 applied.

## Changes between pass 1 and pass 2 (and addendum)
- Glyph "Ƨ" (flat top stroke with a descending body, before p or +) was `27` in pass 1. Pass 2: on 105v `17p` (= 1 + this glyph + p) shows the glyph is not a z-2 followed by 7. I now write it `7` (`7p`, `7+`). All ~45 tokens changed. If the glyph is really "27" they all flip.
- "Zum" / "Zom" on r11, r17, r20: first sign written `2?` (addendum: z-shaped 2 with tail below line), pass 1 had `Z`. 3 tokens.
- "ɪb" glyphs (r02, r03, r05, r08, r15, v01): written `2 6` (z-shaped 2 plus curl) after addendum; pass 1 had `1 6`/`2 6` mixed. Possibly really 1. 
- `u` kept as `u` everywhere (entry swash only, no tail below the line); any could be a 2. Count: 25 or so on 105r.
- `25p` (r01) was `15p` in pass 1. `24+.<hat>` (r18) became `24.<hat>`; `4+<acute>` (r18) became `4<acute>`.
- Marks above added/moved in pass 2: `20p<v>` kept; v attached to the NEXT sign when it floats between signs (`256<v>`, `24+<v>`, `u.<v>`, `15.<v>`, `7.<v>`); `7p<bar>` r13; `m+<bar>` r13; `23.<2dot>` r09, r13, r06; `6p<bar>` r09; `12+<bar>` r07; `23<tbar><bar>` r05, r16 (was `23+`).
- `22+` / `224+` signs (r14, r15 x2) now `22 4+?`.
- v02 `1 6.` (was 16.), v02 `7p<dot>?` new.
