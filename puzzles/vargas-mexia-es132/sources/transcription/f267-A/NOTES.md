## Countersignature

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom: 267r 4 clear + 20 cipher strips (3-line, 1500 px halves), 267v 8 cipher + 8 clear-text strips, plus signature crops: about 60 strips viewed. Pass 2: reverse direction (bottom to top, right half before left) on freshly cut 2-line strips: 267r 24 strips, 267v 10 cipher strips, plus 10 targeted clear-text/signature/address crops; about 45 strips. Pass 2 was completed for the cipher; clear text was re-checked only at doubtful words, not strip by strip. Corrections in pass 2: many lines re-read (digit 4 with tail vs q, curl joins, marks above, 10 vs 20 at the start of 267v line 1).

## Reading policy / cautions
- Cipher quality is poor to middling; expect errors of 1-2 signs per line, especially marks above (hat/acute/v) and 1 vs 7, 6 vs 8, 2 vs 3.
- Letter groups written as plain letters: "q u a m" (267r lines 4, 7; 267v line 5), "c i L Z u m", "Z o m", "Z o o m", "m i L" read as letter-form signs, NOT as clear Spanish. "[[de]]" (267r line 10, 267v line 2) taken as clear word; may instead be letters d e.
- A digit 4 with a long tail looks like q; tail-4 written `4`, `q` only where a bowl is clear (267r l.11, 18, 21, 267v l.7): ambiguous.
- Many `p` marks are shaped like `e`; read as hook.
- Date "xvj" could be "xxj".
- Line 22 (267r) first sign: large looped Z; `Z o o m` (earlier reading Z o m for 267r l.14).
