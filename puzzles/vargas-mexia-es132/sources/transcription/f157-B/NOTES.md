# f157-B notes (Reader B, blind second transcription)

Date of work: 2026-10-04. Folios: BnF Espagnol 132 f. 157r, 158v, 159r (Gallica ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x, canvases 154 and 156; f. 157v/158r blank on canvas 155, not read).

## Method
Overviews at 1000 px (ov154, ov156), then native-resolution strips (1500 x 650 px, left/right halves overlapping about 250 px, rows about 550 px apart), then a second independent pass: native 950 px-wide pieces of every cipher line viewed right to left (files p2_*), with extra close crops for hard spots (z1, z25, d0-d5, h0, p2_L12*). All crops are in sources/cache/f157-B-crops/. No file in the puzzle directory other than these two outputs and the crops was opened. Image resolution is native (no upscaling), so "close" crops add attention, not detail.

## Passes
Pass 1 left to right over all strips; pass 2 right to left over every cipher line (157r l.10, 13, 16-23; 158v l.15b-21, 25-29; 159r l.2-7, 9-11) and the clear lines at the clear/cipher joins. Pass 2 changed: the "!" on 157r l.16 (now "1_"), dot placement on 158v l.15b ("10^ 20_"), the caret on 158v l.17 (belongs to "19" below, not "18" above), "14+" vs "19+" (distinguished by whether a 1 precedes), and "1061" (joined) versus "106 1" (gap) in 157r l.13.

## Date and signature as read
"De Madrid 8 de X.bre 1578." (X with superscript bre; overbars over 8 and 1578; a small grave-like tick after 8). Signature "Ant. Perez." with a large looped A; preceded by "de V.m." Postscript in the left margin.

## Counts (cipher tokens = anything outside [[ ]], descriptor in <...> counted as one token)
Total 369: 157r 161, 158v 107, 159r 101. Tokens carrying "?": 27 (plus two clear items marked uncertain: "[[no]]?" 158v l.18 and "[[por]]?" 159r l.11). Hat/bar/cross marks are recorded as seen.

## Clear/cipher boundary doubts
- 157r l.2-3 dates (2. 7. 13. 14. 22 y 23) look like cipher digits but are written in a clear sentence: kept clear.
- Words standing inside cipher strings and written like clear: "no" (157r l.19, 158v l.18), "para" and "te" (158v l.21), "por q̄" (157r l.22), "por" with over/underline (159r l.11), "y" (kept as a cipher letter-form token inside cipher, clear "y" in clear). The clear sentences appear to alternate with cipher groups that stand for names/words.
- "Lo" (157r l.10) and "se" (158v l.19) at line start are treated as clear.
- 158v l.2 and l.13 numerals are inside the clear sentence.

## Orthography conventions used for clear text
Initial h and long s have near-identical glyphs; chosen by sense (hallo, hablar, hasta, he, ha; se). "ss" ligature written ss; the rotunda r written r; "mejor/mejante" written with j for the long-s-like glyph; "dezir" for z; "Sotomayor", "mayo" read with y for the same glyph as z/ʒ. "san Homer" read for "san ʒomer". Doubtful clear words: auemados (158v l.28, could be quemados), cote (159r l.14, possibly corte), mayo (159r l.21), esste (159r l.20), "he/se dio" (159r l.15 read se dio), "guarde ..." cluster (159r l.26).
