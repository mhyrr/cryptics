# Held-out letter, Antonio Perez to Juan de Vargas Mexia, Sept 1578

Blind transcription of the page images only; no key or repository file was opened.

## Where it is
- f. 87r = canvas 84 right; f. 87v = canvas 85 left; f. 88r = canvas 85 right. f. 86v (canvas 84 left) is the address leaf.
- Date as read: "De Madrid a 13? de sept.e 1578". The day numeral is a flourish that looks like "13" (could be xiii). Signed "de v.m. / Antonio Perez" (flourished signature, partly read).
- Not "casi toda en cifra": it is mostly clear Spanish with short numeric-cipher insertions, and f. 88r has none. The clear Spanish is a best-effort diplomatic reading and is not reliable.
- Other cipher letters nearby, not Perez-signed as principal: canvas 83 right (f. 86r), mostly cipher, "Yo el Rey" plus Perez, dated Madrid "a ix" de sept. 1578 (9 Sept). Canvases 86 right-88 (ff. 89r-91r): royal letter in near-total cipher, dated "a xp? de Sept.bre 1578", signed "Yo el Rey" and Vargas. If the held-out test needs a mostly-cipher text, ff. 89r-91r or f. 86r are the candidates, and f. 86r (the 9 Sept letter) is signed by Perez. Neither was transcribed.

## Cipher token count
About 84 tokens: f. 87r about 33 (5 short runs), f. 87v about 51 (two runs; lines 16-19 are cut at the right by the gutter and are incomplete).

## Hardest readings
- 87r "961 12 361 196 9+ 196 186 2. 156 20": 961 vs 96 1; 156 vs 15 6.
- 87r "En lo de Nazareth" run: the H-like signs with a basal loop (read as H?) and the slashed 7?.
- 87r "no 5.? 12 7<hat> 10": the 5. vs S, and the hatted 7.
- 87r last line: 24+? and the leading 9.
- 87v: dots below 21 and 19, dot above 22; "mo" is read as a token; 2H vs 24+ on lines 16-17; the Sigma/H signs on line 20 ("H? 4? p y Sigma e") are low confidence; the y glyph is the same shape as clear "y".
