## Conventions I settled on (shapes only)
- q-shaped signs read as the digit `4` (`4`, `24`, `4<hat>`); g-shaped open-curl signs with long tail read as `9`; a clearly clear-script y tail as `y` (s1 line 7, s1 line 16/s2 line 2 end, s3 lines 4, 9, 13, 14 `1y`).
- The hook (e-like with tail) after a number is `p`. The tall slanted flat-topped sign with a hook (`7e`) is `7p`; where it has a crossbar it is `7+`.
- z-shaped 2 written `2` throughout (`23`, `24`, `28`).
- `xom`, `xum`, `jim`, `gil`, `cil`, `bel`, `mil` letter groups written as seen (`mil` taken as clear Spanish). `xom`/`xum` could equally be `Rom`/`Rum`; `jim` could be `sim`.
- `62` at the start of cipher segments: a large 6-like G-form followed by a z-shaped 2. Left without `?` everywhere (about 6 occurrences); a different reader may write `G2` or `6 2`.
- `d` / `de` = the curly d-form with long ascender, written as a letter.
- `p`, `pe`, `p+`, `p.<v>` = the p with descender (letter form); distinct from the hook mark.
