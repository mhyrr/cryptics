# f267-B blind transcription notes (ff. 267-268, 1580)

Blind reader; guide v2.2 only; no network; no key.

## Extent
- Letter starts at the top of f. 267r ("El Rey" heading with a cross above "El", flourish, salutation "Juan de vargas Mexia, ...", 5 clear lines, then cipher) and ends on f. 267v (9 cipher lines, 14 clear lines, place/date, "Yo el Rey", countersignature). It is a single letter.
- f. 267r: modern pencil foliation "267" top right. 23 cipher lines (paragraph break after line 6; line 12 is a short line ending with a virgule and dot).
- f. 267v: 9 cipher lines, then clear Spanish "En lo de los arrestos de nauios franceses ..." to "De Merida, a / xxvj de Mayo 1580" (1580 underlined).
- f. 268r: blank except the pencil foliation "268" and show-through. Not transcribed.
- f. 268v: address leaf, text rotated 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" (cross above "el", big oval flourish) and "A Juan de Vargas mexia en Paris" (flourish after "en", "Paris" underlined). A narrow slip at the top of the image shows mirror-image show-through of f. 267v cipher lines plus a dark pen mark; not transcribed.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Iuan de Idiaq(...)" (two tall loops, "de" between, ends in "q" and a big flourish). Last letters not resolved. "Yo el Rey" is a heavy scribble; "Yo" legible, remainder flourish.

## Passes and strip counts
- Pass 1 (top to bottom, left then right), native 1500 px strips: f. 267r 24 strips (6 clear, 16 cipher, 2 blank bottom); f. 267v 22 strips (6 cipher, 12 clear, 4 signature/countersignature crops); f. 268v 3; f. 268r 2 overviews/crops. Total about 51.
- Pass 2 (bottom to top, right then left, corrections applied line by line): f. 267r 22 strips (all clear and cipher strips; the two blank bottom strips were not re-viewed); f. 267v 18 (all cipher and clear strips; the signature crops were not re-viewed); f. 268v 2; f. 268r 1. Total 43. Second pass covered every line of text; it was not repeated on the blank strips and signature crops.
- Line starts and ends were aligned using the line slope (right half sits about 30-50 px higher than left half).

## Image problems
- f. 267v: every line is cut by the gutter shadow at the right edge (x about 3150). The last sign of most cipher lines is partly lost; clear lines end mid-word ("recat", "otra de", "las for", "cas", "yo", "hombre", "siemp", "Villero"). f. 268r's left edge shows the same cut, no extra text.
- f. 267v signature zone: show-through of f. 267r cipher (mirrored) under the signatures and countersignature.
- f. 267r: clean; right edge fine.
- f. 267v clear line 3 ends "massa seg?" (cut/abbreviated). Line 7 begins "bras?" (uncertain reading of first word). "que sono?" (line 11) uncertain. "sean guiados": the initial s looks like d in this hand; I read s.
- Clear text uses overlines on 26, 3, 8 (f. 267r) and abbreviation marks ("vras", "grãs", "aunq"); not reproduced in the transcription.

## Labels I invented (shapes, not values)
- `P`: a flourished capital shape, like an 8 with an added upper-left loop or a "P". Occurs in "P e q 17+" (f.267r lines 2, 5, 9, 18; five times in all), "P. 18 5+", "P+", and at "P 6 4<hat>" (f.267r line 7, f.267v line 1). It may be a variant 8; plain 8 (as in "28") looks different.
- `Z o m`, `Z u m`, `x o o m`, `cil`: groups of letters with a swash initial. Whether Z is a digit 2 form and o is a zero (so 20 m / 2 u m) is unclear. `mil` and `quam` and `de` I treat as clear words in [[ ]].
- `L e q` (f.267r line 17) and `L`, `L+`, `L<hat>` (f.267v): looped L-shaped form.
- `<sign:z above>`, `<sign:2 above>`: a z-shaped or 2-shaped stroke above a sign. `<sign:tilde above>`: wavy stroke above.
- `6.<bar>?` (f.267r lines 2, 12): a long horizontal dash after "6." at mid-height; not clear if it is a bar above a following sign.
