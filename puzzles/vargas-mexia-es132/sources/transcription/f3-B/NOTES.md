# Notes, blind transcription f3-B (guide v1)

Method: local crops (about 1450x520, 3 lines each, left and right halves, right half runs to the page edge), page order 4r, 3v, 3r;
lines in order within each page, left to right. Zoomed re-looks on a handful of tokens only. **No true independent right-to-left second
pass was run**; only overlapping crops (each half overlaps its neighbour) and the zoom re-looks served as a check. Treat as one pass plus spot checks.

## Lines per page
- f. 3r: heading "el Rey" + 6 lines clear Spanish + 17 cipher lines (starts `[[N. J.]]`). Cipher ends at line end 42<curl> and runs on to 3v.
- f. 3v: 26 cipher lines. Ends with `/` after `<abar>`; the next line is on 4r.
- f. 4r: 18 cipher lines, then date line, "Yo el Rey" signature, secretary's "Cayas" rubric. Cipher 1 ends on 4r.
  The 4r line count assumes the page is 12 + 6 cipher lines (two paragraphs separated by `/`).
  A trailing `/` at the end of line 12 (paragraph end).

## Conventions I had to improvise
- `<tri>` is used for every `v`-like glyph, both the plain tick form and the longer "vr/vf" form with a flag; I did not split them.
  Only the form with a distinct cross is `<tri>+`. A reader may want to split `<tri>` into two glyphs.
- `<W><curl>?` : the "wt/ws/wb" glyph (large w with a trailing squiggle) on 4r; on 3v a plainer large w is `<W>`. Not distinguished reliably.
- Small circle above a number (e.g. `12<dot>?`, `75<loop>`, `73<loop>`): the guide has no circle mark; I used `<dot>` or `<loop>` per apparent size.
- A small "v/caret" mark above numbers (3v, e.g. 32, 58, 8, 14, 11) written `<tilde>?` (guide has no caret).
- `x` as a base on 4r line 2: `<sign:small x>`; X-like stroke at the start of 3r cipher line 12: `<sign:X at line start>` (it may be a marginal or stray stroke).
- `{co}?` on 3r line 2 and `{ca}?` on 3v line 15 (cut by the page edge); `{fis}`, `{so}`, `{po}?` are shape-guesses for decorated long-s/p forms.
- `4?`, `7?`, `6?`, `8?`, `<tri>?`, `<theta>?` at line ends on 3v and 4r: the token is clipped by the dark band / page edge (right margin of 3v is shadowed).

## Observations (shapes only)
- 3r l.1, l.10 and 3v... the run `19 85 <omega> <II> <sign:II slash>` recurs (3r l.1, l.10) and `64 ,36 80 38 42 <omega> 52 <abar> 42` is written twice in a row on the last cipher line of 4r (apparent repetition, kept as read).
- Marginal writing on the left of 3r (faint, bleed-through from 3v, plus a small mark pair `:` and a flourish before "Juan") not transcribed. 3r also has bleed-through from the verso text.
- 3v has ink blots through lines 18-20 (3v middle) obscuring marks over `75`, `2?`, `45`.
- Stray strokes: `X`-shaped stroke below 74 44 on 3r l.11; a faint horizontal stroke after final `42<arch>` on 3r l.7.
- A final dot appears before and after the last `/` on 3v l.26 (written as plain `/`), and after `60` on 3r l.12.

## Hardest places

marks vs `<dot>`; the "9"-alone tokens (long-tailed 9 with no visible 1) on 3v.
