Method: overview of each page, then native-resolution strips (1300-1400 px wide, ~230 px tall, overlapping) of every cipher passage; each viewed with Read. Pass 1 left-to-right; pass 2 right-to-left re-check of each cipher line (done on the strips, token by token). Clear Spanish was read mainly from the overview and is NOT sign-checked at native resolution: treat it as approximate (diplomatic spelling is partly normalised in places, many `?` and `(...)` omissions). Cipher is the focus.

P y Sigma e /"). Letter continues on f.88r (clear only). A strike-out on 87r (line "Al rey mostre la carta q el ...") with interlinear "tu xi" above.
Date/signature: "de Madrid [xiij?] de sept.e 1578" (day numeral uncertain; read as c/x-i-j); closing "de V.m."; signature "Ant.o Perez" with large flourish.
Cipher token count (approx.): 87r line "a 961...": 10 tokens + virgula; Nazareth line: 2 loop signs, P, 15, 3., 3., 4, 1? and 2 slashes (~9); last line: S. 12 y 10 9. 96 12 20+ 106 6+ 10 2+ xi = 13. 87v: line A 17, line B 17, line C ~3, line D ~14, line E 5 + 1. Total ~ 100.
Hardest readings: (1) "961" first group (9-6-1 vs 9-8-1); (2) the oval-loop sign with H and plus (Nazareth, twice, and 87v end) and the following "four with loop below" sign; (3) "+" marks: written like a small t/cross on 9, 20, 6, 2, 18, 2H; read as "+"; (4) dots above/below digits (21^, 19_, 22^, 18_) assigned to the whole numeral; (5) "1?" after 4 on Nazareth line may be a slash/virgula; (6) "xi" kept as one token (two close letters x,i); also "xi" in interlinear above struck word; (7) 3.<crossabove> cross above the 3 or the dot; 20.<crossabove> cross above the dot of "20.".
Clear/cipher boundary doubts: "y" inside cipher runs (87r last line "S. 12 y 10", 87v "20 12 y 20 S", final "P y Sigma e") may be the clear conjunction or a cipher sign; "S." before 12 on 87r last line may belong to clear "que no"; "e /" after final Sigma may be clear; virgulas after cipher groups are written as tokens; "no" before S. read as clear (preceded by circled-q abbreviation "q"). "xi" at end of last 87r cipher line may be sign or Roman numeral.
Page 87v right edge is trimmed: ends of lines (and possibly cipher tail on lines B/D) are lost to the gutter.
