# f. 179 blind transcription notes

Pérez to Vargas Mexía, Madrid 26 Jan 1579. Image-only, blind.

- Folios: 179r = canvas 176 right; 179v = canvas 177 left; 180r = canvas 177 right. Folio "179" confirmed top right of 179r; "180" top right of 180r.
- Date as read: "Madrid 26 de eno 1579". Signature: "Ant.º Perez." preceded by "de V.m." and flourish.
- The letter starts on 179r in clear; cipher begins on 179v (first at line 6) and continues on 180r (lines 11-14); 180r ends in clear with date and signature.
- Cipher tokens (incl. uncertain ones, excluding struck word): 139 by script count (`co`, `C.`, `a.`, `du` counted as tokens).
- 179v right ends lost in the gutter shadow; 180r left ends clipped to a few letters in the crop (read from thumbnail).
- Left page of canvas 176 holds a docket/other text, not read.
- Crops in sources/cache/f179-crops (w*.jpg 179v, s*.jpg 180r, r*.jpg 179r).
