# f257 transcription notes (blind reader, guide v2.2)

## Extent
- Letter begins f. 257r ("El Rey" header, salutation "Juan de Vargas mexia", clear Spanish preamble of 7 lines listing the
  letters received, then cipher). Cipher runs f. 257r (20 lines), 257v (29), 258r (30), 258v (29), 259r (9 lines).
- Letter ends f. 259r: "De / Guadalupe à 28 de março 1580.", "Yo el Rey" (read from a heavily stylised signature), "Dupda /",
  countersignature read as "Don Ju[an] de Idiaqz" (Idiaquez: Don + tall-looped J/Iu with tilde + "de" + "Idiaq" + z-like flourish).
- f. 259v: no writing, only mirror show-through (and an ink-transfer stain). f. 260r: no writing, faint mirror show-through of 260v.
- f. 260v: address leaf, text sideways: "Por el Rey" (cross above "el"), long flourish, "dupda" (small, left), "A Juan de vargas mexia."
  Docket at right margin: "28 de março de 1580." (year underlined). Round paper seal/patch on the leaf. All given in [[ ]].
- Margin of f. 259r, left of line 1: large pen flourish with a small dotted u inside (recorded as a sign line).
- Modern foliation numbers (257-260, top right) not transcribed.

## Passes (honest statement)
- Pass 1 (left to right per page: left half then right half of each strip, top to bottom): done on all of 257r-259r.
  Strips viewed in pass 1: about 180 (257r 33, 257v 28, 258r 28, 258v 28, 259r 10, plus overview, signature and address crops).
- Pass 2 (bottom to top, last page first, right half before left half of each strip): done at native resolution on 259r, 258v, 258r,
  257v and 257r. About 124 strips viewed (259r 10, 258v 28, 258r 28, 257v 28, 257r 30; the 257r strip at y=1860 was not re-viewed).
  Pass 2 was a partial line-by-line correction, not an exhaustive one: I corrected the tokens that I could see were wrong in the
  strips (mostly 14+ read as 15+, `p` hooks that are curls, dropped bars/hats/v/2dot, a few `?` resolved, 25p6 as 256, one `x+`),
  but I did not re-read every token of every line, and the `?` list below is only slightly shorter. The first-pass reading still
  dominates; treat the cipher as a lightly corrected single reading.
- Correction applied after pass 1: the glyph `x+` (crossed strokes plus cross) in "3 8+ x+ 6p ..." was first written 25+/2+; it recurs
  six times and looks like a letter form `x`, so all were written `x+`.

## Reliability warnings
- Lines rise slightly to the right; halves were joined on overlap tokens. Some joins (258r lines 24-27, 258v lines 7-9) are inferred
  and may be off by a token or two.

  was not applied consistently; the splitting of these runs is unreliable.
- Marks above (bar, hat, v) were added when seen; expect some dropped or invented on faint lines.
