# f. 167 transcription notes (blind, CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.1)

## Method
- Images: Gallica IIIF ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x. f. 167r = right page of canvas 164 (verified by foliation "167" top right); f. 167v = left page of canvas 165 (168 appears on the right page of canvas 165, blank). Canvas width is 6561 px (not 6614). Native crops (no upscaling) in sources/cache/f167-crops/: 2-line strips, 1500 px halves overlapping 150 px (r*a/r*b for 167r, v*a/v*b for 167v), plus a margin crop and an overlay of the signatures.
- Only the guide was read. No other repo file was opened.
- Pass 1: left to right, every strip. Pass 2: right-to-left re-check against the strips. Honest scope: pass 2 was done by re-reading my pass-1 lines against the strips already viewed, plus one fresh native re-crop (the T stroke at end of r.c4). Changes in pass 2: (a) the "/" at the end of r.c4 was re-read as a long T-shaped stroke (insertion sign), not a plain virgula; (b) the cross over the u at the end of r.c3 is the top bar of that stroke, not a cross-above; (c) a small ink blot in r.c13 is written as a sign, not a dot; (d) "20 20" vs "66 66" in r.c2 left as unsure; (e) r.c8 and v.c15 tails aligned with the r.c2 and v.c8/c15 repeated formulas.
- Confidence: low to medium. Lines are dense, signs ligatured, and many individual signs (especially 2/z, 1, the hook `p` vs 6, and the "o") are uncertain. The text repeats formulas (for example "6. 15+ 16p 24+", "86 20 6p 10 20. 6p", "6.<bar> 20+ 7. y<bar> 7p", "10 6 25. 246 18"), which helped consistency but also means a systematic misreading would repeat.

## Layout
- f. 167r: heading "el Rey" (a small cross above it); 9 lines of clear Spanish (the ninth is "pachar," and the cipher starts on the same line); 20 cipher lines (r.c1-r.c20); date and signatures are NOT on 167r. A left-margin block of 6 short lines (m1-m6) plus a paraph, beside r.c4-r.c10.
- f. 167v: 19 cipher lines (v.c1-v.c19), 4 lines of clear Spanish, the date "a [ornate numeral] de enero 1579", the royal autograph, "Ant Perez" (secretary), and a later rotated annotation at bottom right. Nothing continues on f. 168r (blank). The right ends of 167v lines run into the gutter; the last signs of many lines, and the ends of v.s2 and v.s3, may be lost. Faint bleed-through from f. 168r? at the top of 167v (a ghost "...") is not transcribed.
- Date: the numeral for the day is a large F-shaped flourish and I could not read it (brief says 10 Jan 1579). The year reads 1579.
- Marginal block: it is ciphered (not Spanish), six lines at the left edge, partly hidden by the binding shadow at the left edge. Insertion sign: a long vertical stroke with a short top bar (T-like) at the end of r.c4, immediately before "u<cross> 7p". The mark sits at the far right of the line, level with the first marginal line m1. I did not see a second T-like sign on 167r. Other `<cross>` marks on 167r are small crosses over signs (r.c3: 7, 1; r.c7: u; r.c12: 11; r.c13: u; v.c1: u; v.c12: 6; v.c15: 6; v.c17: 1). On 167v, "u<cross>" occurs after a virgula in v.c1 (same pattern as r.c4), and "6<cross>" after a virgula in v.c12; these may be further insertion or paragraph marks. The margin lines m4 and m3 echo the text of r.c5 ("6+ 6 10 S 1<bar>", "7p<hat>"), a possible hint the margin is a doublet (not interpreted).

## Conventions used (and a systematic ambiguity)
- A small closed "o" after 1, 2 or 3 is written 0 (10, 20, 30); after any other base it is a joined curl 6 (86, 76, 56...). The curl and the zero are indistinguishable in this hand; if the true values differ, every 10/20/30 vs 16/26/36 is affected. The guide's curl rule would also read "1o" as 16.
- Hook-after is written `p` (the "ρ"/"e" with tail); these are very frequent (7p, 24p, 6p, 15p).
- `u.`, `S.`, `q.`, `m`, `y`, `d`, `l`, `g` are letter forms; "gils" (v.c8) and "de" (v.c12, v.c18, v.c19, with "bonp" after de in v.c12) look like Latin letters inside the cipher; written as clear [[ ]] with ? where unsure.
- `<underlined>` (v.c8, 35+) and `<2dot>` marks are my own descriptions.
