# f275-B blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- Text begins on f. 275r (a cross mark with a dot sits above and right of the first line; foliation "275" top right) and ends on f. 275r, at the foot of the text block: last line ends `... y /` followed by a dot. No further cipher on 275v or 276r.
- f. 275r: 2 header lines, then 5 paragraphs (8, 4, 4, 5 and 2 lines). Total cipher lines on 275r: 25. Paragraphs 1-4 begin `21.` / `21. S o m` / `15+` / `21.`; paragraph 5 begins `4<bar>`. Each paragraph ends with `/` (P2 ends with a short comma-like tick, written `,`).
- f. 275v: blank. Only mirror show-through of 275r (including the cross at top). Nothing transcribed.
- f. 276r: blank except foliation "276" (top right) and faint mirror show-through of the 276v block.
- f. 276v: a leaf with a block rotated 90 degrees (read with the page turned counter-clockwise): 4 cipher lines, a long ruled flourish under them, then two clear lines "Para embiar à Jul.º de / Vargas Mexia" with a large flourish. This reads as an address/docket in the hand of the cipher. Its cipher shares phrases with the 275r header (lines 1-2 start the same way) but is not identical.
- Date: none. Signature: none. Address: yes, on 276v (clear, in [[ ]]). No other docket or endorsement seen.

## Conventions I had to adopt (not in the guide)
- The closed-bowl sign with a straight stem (q-shape) is written `4` throughout (it is the 4 inside `24`, `14`, `34`, and appears alone). The open triangular 4 is also `4`. If these are different signs, every `4` needs re-checking. A 9 with a curved tail is `9`.
- The ∂-like loop sign (d with a curl) is written `d`.
- A small closed loop like an `e` after a base, with no descender, is written `e` (e.g. `7e`, `15e`, `24e`, `6e`). A loop with a clear descender is `p` (`6p`, `17p`, `24p`). I could not always tell them apart. A free-standing p-hook with e is `pe`.
- Letter forms: `S` (curly capital-like), `o`, `m`, `u`, `y` (tailed), `e` (in `S e m`, `S e`).
- The sign that looks like an H or a 7 with a plus is written `7+` or `7`; where it looks like two uprights with a bar I wrote `11` / `11+`. Uncertain throughout.
- Marks above that look like an acute lying over a preceding sign or arc above a number group are written `<acute>`; arcs over `118` and `10` (P3L1, P2L4) are not written, only the cross above them.
