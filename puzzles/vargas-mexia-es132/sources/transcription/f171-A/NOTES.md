# f. 171-A blind transcription notes (reader A, guide v2.3)

## Quality warning (read first)
This is a low-confidence reading. At native resolution the digits are small and cramped, and I could not separate
many curls, hooks and marks above with confidence. Treat digit strings, joined/unjoined curls and marks above as
approximate everywhere. Only 19 `?` appear, far fewer than the real uncertainty; the absence of `?` does not mean
a token is secure. Spaces inside the cipher lines were my best token splits, not verified at every sign.

## Conventions I used
- `q` = a q-shaped sign (a 9-like loop with a descender), often with a bar or hat above; I did not map it to the guide's `p` hook. It is frequent and probably a base, but I wrote it as a letter form.
- `p` = hook with descender after a base. `e` after a number = an e-shaped/ε mark after the base (may be the guide's hook; unresolved).
- `y`, `Rom`, `sim`, `sem`, `gil`, `bel`, `bul`, `bam` = letter groups as seen.
- Automatic joining: a free `6` after a bare number was joined to it (curl rule), a free `e` after a number was joined. Both are uncertain.
