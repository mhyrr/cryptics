# f. 26r (BnF es. 132), blind transcription B, notes (guide v1.1 §6)

Image: `sources/cache/pages/f026r.jpg` (3569 x 5452, grayscale). Crops: `sources/cache/f26-B-crops/` (1500x320 strips, left x=420 and right x=1650, y step 200, plus zooms z1-z8). Read bottom line first, upward; file written top to bottom. Facing-page sliver at the right edge (marks at x ~3050+) ignored. Faint show-through of the verso at the top left/upper margin ignored.

## Lines
28 manuscript lines of text: 1 clear heading ("El Rey", with a small cross above between the words; "26" at top right is modern foliation, not transcribed), 1 line starting with the clear name "Juan de Vargas Mexia" then cipher, and 26 pure cipher lines. Last cipher line ends in a virgula and a large closed-loop sign (`g?`); line 4th from bottom (`6 5+ ... 19 /`) ends in a virgula, and the line after it starts at the left margin.
Line pitch about 130 px native; strips overlap about 270 px between left and right halves.

## Conventions I adopted (ambiguities in the guide)
- **`p+`** : a long stem descending below the line, crossed at mid-height, with a loop at x-height on its right, directly after a number group (typically `11 p+`). I read it as a separate letter-form p with a cross, not as `11+` plus hook. Alternative reading `11+e` or `14e` is possible; it is the commonest uncertain group (about 15 instances, all written `11 p+`; find them with grep).
- **`e`** : compact hook with a short descender after a number (`15e`, `17e`, `8e`, `4e`, `13e`, `19e`, `18e`, `12e`, `5e`, `20e`).
- **`<rho>`** : large free loop with a tail dropping and curling below the line. Many.
- **`<sign:z-flourish>`** : a recurring sign: a z-like 2 with a long flat stroke running in from the left above it, a loop or blot in its middle and a rightward tail (looks like `—2v~`). Occurs 8 times (grep for `z-flourish`). Always I treated it as ONE sign. It may be 2 signs (a 2 plus a `v`); not resolved. Next to it on the bottom line the `6` that follows is separate.
- `0` : a plain round circle with no 2 before it, occurring (`0`, `0+`, `0e`). Not in the guide's inventory; I wrote `0`. In several places the same shape is clearly the second half of `20` (z then o), where I wrote `20`. At `2 0+ 10` (lines "<rho> 2 0+ 10", twice) the 2 and the circle have a visible gap, so I split them; `20+ 10` is the alternative.
- `h+` : a letter h with cross (line 9 from top: `14 h+ 4. 5`), hard to tell from `11+`.

## Letter-forms and specials used
- `f_` (f with dot below): top cipher line after `18`; `f+` (f with cross): lines `6 5+ <z-flourish> 14 12. C^ 18+ f+ 19 /`, `13e n^ n_ 2 18+ f+ 19e ...`, `9e 8e 2 11 ... 18+ f+ 19e 17_? 14`.
- `R` (capital R with a long flourish bar at left): lines 2, 3 from top (`R 0`), `R 6`, `R 2`.
- `C^` : capital C with dot above: two lines.
- `n`, `h+`, `p+` as above. `v` I did not use (the flourish might contain one).
- `<venus>.` at bottom line. `<rho>` many.
- No `m`, `P`.

## Hardest places
1. Groups of the form number + cross + descender + hook (`11 p+` vs `11+e` vs `14e`): the pen merges the cross, the stem of the p and the hook.
2. The z-flourish sign and the `—0e 0+` group after it in one line.
3. The 2-versus-0 splits (`2 0+` vs `20+`).
4. Dots in the tight line spacing (about 130 px): the dots above `13`/`4`/`5` often sit midway between two lines. Dots that I put below (`_`) vs above (`^`) were decided by distance.
5. The `1`+`4` groups: the 4 is drawn like a q with a long cross stroke; `14` vs `11+` is not always clear (`14` where there is a visible 4 shape, `11+` where two uprights).
6. Smeared 4-plus-uprights group near the right end of the line 15th from top.

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right per strip, working from the bottom line to the top, at native resolution with 180-250% zooms for z1-z8.
Pass 2 (right to left): I re-read the right halves of the strips from the right edge against the written lines, looking mainly at dots and at the number-plus-hook groups. Changes from pass 1: the `0` signs in `0 4+ 17 19e 5^ 0 20_` line (read first as `0.`, then as plain `0` after the zoom z8); `11 p+` instead of `11+e`/`14e` kept consistently; `<venus>` and the final `g?` confirmed at zoom z7; `4e` rather than `4 p` at the end of the top cipher line.
