# f213-A2 notes (blind transcriber, guide v2.3)

## Extent
One letter, ff. 213r to 214v (all four pages). It starts at the top of f. 213r (header "El Rey" with a long flourish, folio no. 213 top right; then five lines of clear Spanish, a salutation to Juan de Vargas Mexia) and runs as cipher to the foot of f. 214v. It ends on f. 214v with a closing group of four short cipher lines at the left margin, the date line "de Julio 1579." (the word "Florencia" and "vij" stand in clear at the end of the last full cipher line above it), the King's autograph ("Yo el Rey") and, lower right, the secretary's countersignature. No address leaf, docket or second letter in these four images. f. 214v below the signature is empty except for the mirrored ghost of f. 214r. Folio numbers 213 (213r) and 214 (214r) are written in the top margin; 213v shows a later number at top left.

Cipher line counts: f213r 21, f213v 29, f214r 27, f214v 15 (11 full lines + 4 short closing lines) = 92 lines. Plus 5 clear lines (213r), date, King's signature, countersignature.

The lines of 213r (lines 7-21 except those seen in the 1750/2530/3310 crops), 213v lines 25-29, 214r lines 22-24 and 213r/213v right-hand ends were NOT independently re-read. Treat those lines as pass-1 only.
- Changes made in pass 2: f214v lines 3-11 re-segmented (pass 1 had paired the right-half lines of lines 3-5 with the wrong left halves; the seam crops showed a repeated phrase, `24+ 256 4 1.` ... `u. 24+ 256 4 x.`, in line 9); f214r line 6 gained a virgule `/` before `4 u+ 15.`; several look-alike fixes (`6o` vs `6 o`, `32<cross>`, `15e` vs `15+`); clear Spanish of 213r re-read at native size.

## Method notes and caveats
- This hand is hard to split. Strokes often run across sign boundaries and the page is badly show-through (mirror-image ink from the other side, especially 213v and 214v). I transcribed only the dark ink. Faint mirrored text was ignored.
- I read `15+` for the sign that looks like "ist"/"ISt" (a 1 with an S-shaped 5 and a plus) and `15e` for "ise" (same with an e-shaped hook); the hook-form `p` and the e-form (`e`) are not always distinguishable from the image; I wrote `p` where there was a descender and `e` where it was a closed loop. This is a systematic risk.
- The letter-form `S.` is written wherever there is a long flat-topped s followed by a dot. Letter groups written as `Zom`, `Zum`, `Sem`, `Se`, `Lum`, `gil`, `gils`, `quom`, `qua`, `mil`, `de` are as seen. `6o` / `6 o` mean a 6 followed by a round o (kept apart where a gap was visible).
- Many `6` curls (guide section 2) were read as separate `6` when followed by a dot or when standing apart. I did not systematically write curls joined; apart from the runs below, I mostly saw the curl as a stand-alone 6 with a dot (`6.`). This may undercount joined curls.
- `q` with a descender: written `q`. Letter `y` with a long tail: `y`.
- Marks above (`<hat>`, `<acute>`, `<v>`, `<bar>`) are the most likely errors; I re-checked each line once for them in pass 1 but pass 2 did not cover every line.
- `??` marks one or more signs I could not read; `?` after a token marks doubt. Totals: about 227 question marks in the file.

## Clear Spanish
213r salutation (5 lines): "Juan de vargas Mexia, vnas cos? de era. ſx? cvj? y cvj? / del passado, heredero? y aqui se satisfara a lo q / dellos requiere respta. con a pre(s)ente? cerot? primero / mucho el cuydado q teneis de conformarme tan ell? / continuo? de todo lo q ay se sabe, y entretenelle? /". I read the line-1 abbreviations ("cos", "ſx", "cvj") only as shapes; they are uncertain. Date line: "de Julio 1579." Place word: "Florencia" and "vij", in clear at the end of the last full cipher line of 214v, before the date line (the arrangement might also be read as the date line proper beginning "... de Florencia vij" and ending "de Julio 1579.").

## Signatures
- King: "Yo el Rey" (large autograph flourish: tall looped Y with a long descender, then o, el, Rey with wide loops).
- Countersignature as I read it: "Ant.o Pe(re)z?" (low confidence). Shape: a large A-like flourish whose crossbar sweeps far to the left, then "nt." , then a compressed group that I read "ks" or "3" and a closing looped flourish below the line. Could be "Antonio Perez"; I do not claim it.
