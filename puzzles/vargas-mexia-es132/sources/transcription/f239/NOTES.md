# f. 239-240 (13 Nov 1579) blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- Letter begins f. 239r (heading "El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", 10 clear lines, then cipher).
- Cipher on f. 239r: 16 lines (paragraph 1 = 10 lines ending in a virgula `/`; paragraph 2 = 6 lines, running to the foot of the page).
- Continues f. 239v: 8 cipher lines, then 5 clear lines (ending "Del Pardo a xiij de noviembre 1579"), then the King's signature ("Yo el Rey") with a long diagonal flourish, then the countersignature lower right.
- Countersignature as read: "Domingo d'Oriag(a/z)?" (D-o-m-i-n-g-o, then "d'", then a looped O/A, "r i a", final g/z with a large looping flourish). Surname uncertain.
- A separate bold mark at lower left of f. 239v (about y 4650, x 830-1230 of the image): shape "D u n g?" with a long descending tail and a dot. Not read. Possibly a rubric or a later hand.
- f. 240r: blank. Only show-through (mirror image of the f. 240v address) and the ends of f. 239v lines at the gutter. Not transcribed.
- f. 240v: address leaf, text rotated 90 degrees: "Por + el Rey" (small cross above), "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", and a docket at the right edge (rotated), roughly "Madrid ... de su Md de 13 de <struck word> Novi(embre). Rda en Paris a 1? de ... xxij?". Docket is uncertain, given in [[ ]] shape only. A seal with paper loss lies mid right.

## Passes
- Pass 1 (left to right, native resolution): f. 239r 38 strips (12 clear-text, 26 cipher), f. 239v 18 cipher + 12 clear strips + 4 signature crops; f. 240r 2 crops, f. 240v 3 crops. About 73 strips.
- Pass 2 (reverse direction, every cipher strip re-viewed, right half before left half, last strip first): f. 239v 18 cipher strips, f. 239r 22 cipher strips (c00-c10; the two blank strips below the text were not re-viewed). 40 strips. **The clear-text strips (f. 239r lines 1-10, f. 239v clear lines) were not re-viewed in pass 2.** Pass-2 changes: C3 `7+<v>`, C9 `Sp v`, C10 `S<2dot>`, `17.<hat>`, a few `?`.
- Reliability is modest. Many signs are blurred by show-through (mirrored text) on f. 239v rows 3-6, so those rows have more errors than the others.
