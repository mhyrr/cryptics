# f. 11v calibration transcription: notes (blind, shapes only, per CIPHER2-GUIDE v1.1)

## Map used
- Image `sources/cache/pages/f011v.jpg` (3553 x 5452), left page; facing-page sliver at the right edge ignored.
- Crops: native strips 1500x350 (left x650, right x1950, step 270 px down from y1000), then 1100x300 windows at 140% and
  targeted 190-215% zooms for dots and the gutter edge (contrast-stretched edge crops, x3153-3553).
- Text block runs x ~700 to ~3400. Lines are ~115-135 px apart.

## Line counts
- 30 manuscript lines. Cipher-only: 1, 2, 14-17, 20-30 (L30 is a lone `5+` under the end of L29, at the gutter edge).
- Mixed cipher + clear: L4 (clear then cipher), L5 (cipher then clear), L6 (clear then cipher), L18 (cipher then clear).
- Clear only: L3, 7-13, 19. Lines 8, 13 and 19 end in a virgula `/` followed by a dot (written `/.`).
- Clear text is diplomatic with final long-s written as `s`; superscript `no` in "herm^no" (L9); an overbar above XXXVy (L12);
  a tick before "officios" (L6) and an accent-like tick over "otros" (L7) are not encoded.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left-to-right on the strips. Pass 2: right-to-left re-read of every cipher line on zooms. Changes at pass 2:
  - L2: line-end sign is a faint `<rho>` (tail curls), not a 9; L1 line-end is a faint `5+?` under the gutter shadow.
  - L2 `5^` (dot above the 5) added; L15 `13.` and `5_?` split (two dots, one right of the 13, one under the 5).
  - L26: dot after `4` moved to the 2 of `12` (`12^`); `19_` (dot at the 9's tail end) kept.
  - L27 vs L28: one dot sits between the 3 of L27 and the 8 of L28; given to the 3 (`3_`), nearer by about 20 px, and the 8 left bare.
  - L6: `13+` has no separate hook (earlier read `13+e`); `5e 18e` follow.
  - Line numbering re-counted: clear lines 9-13 are all clear; cipher block L20-L29 plus lone `5+` as L30.

## Letter-forms and special signs used
- `p`: L1 `p+`, L2 `pe`, L5 `p^`, L6 `p.e` (a p with long descender; same form each time).
- `f`: L1 `f_`, L20 `f_` (descender ends in a small tick/blob; read as the "dot under stem" of guide section 5).
- `R`: L18 `Re`, L21 `R+`, L24 `R+`, L25 `R+`.
- `C`: L14 `C^`, L24 `C^`.
- `m`: L1 `me`.  `n`: L27 `n^`, L29 `n^`.
- `v`: L22 `v^`, L29 `ve` (a v with a long flag stroke to the upper left, like a bar flowing into it).
- `<rho>`: many (L2, L6, L14, L16, L21, L22, L24-26, L29). `<venus>`: L24 `<venus>+`, L28 `<venus>_`.
- `<bar>`: L15 `5e<bar>`, L26 `19+ 13_ 2` (one continuous bar over all three, written as three), L28 `13e<bar>` and `5e<bar>`.

## Marks outside the guide's grammar
- Sign followed by cross AND hook: written `11+e`, `13+e`, `17+e` (L1, L2, L6, L16, L21, L24, 25). The cross is plain; the hook is
  attached to its end. Counted as one `+e` pair. Similarly `p.e` (L6) and `9e.?`.
- `18e` (L2, L20, L22-L25...): the 8 ends in a horizontal stroke running into the hook; there may be a cross (`18+e`) but it is
  not distinct like the cross on `11+e`. Written `18e`.
- L15 `20+?`: in place of the 2 there is a thick short upright plus a long horizontal stroke (~170 px) running into the 0:
  `|----0+`. Read as a distorted 2; alternative a different sign.
- L17 `12`: the 2's base stroke runs on right as a flourish; not a dot.
- L5 and L14 `20+`: the 0 is blotted/overwritten; read as 20.

## Hardest places
- 9 versus `<rho>` (both have a loop and a tail curling left). Rule used: loop closed on top of a ς/δ-shaped tail = `<rho>`; open
  g-shape with a hooked descender = `9`. L24 start, L21, L22 are the closest calls.
- Dots between lines (L15/L16, L26/L27/L28, L4/L5, L17/L18) and dots under the gutter shadow.
- Line ends L1, L14-17, L20-28 are crowded against the dark gutter strip (facing-page bleed-through also looks like faint
  signs there; ignored unless they matched a stroke pattern).
- Possible unencoded marks: a tilde-like arc above the 18 after `3e` in L23; a small comma tick under L26 near the last `2`;
  a speck dot at the left end of L28.
