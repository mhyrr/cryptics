# f269-B blind transcription notes (BnF Espagnol 132, ff. 269-270, 1580)

Reader: blind, from CIPHER3-GUIDE.md v2.2 only. No key, no other transcription consulted. Line numbers
below are line numbers of `transcription.txt`.

## Extent
- The letter begins on f. 269r (header "El Rey" with rubric and the folio number 269; salutation "Juan de Vargas
  mexia."; five lines of clear Spanish: a reference to letters answered, dates given as barred numerals, and
  "y He holgado mucho de ver todo lo q por ellas, y los papeles q juntamente venian me auisays", after which the
  cipher starts mid-line, at "28 3^ ...").
- The cipher runs unbroken over f. 269r (25 lines, counting the one that begins in clear), f. 269v (29 lines) and
  f. 270r (14 lines).
- It ends on f. 270r: the last cipher line carries the place and date line in clear, "De Merida a 16. de Mayo 1580.",
  then below it the King's "Yo el Rey" with rubric, and at the foot of the page, at right, the secretary's
  countersignature.
- f. 269v ends with a short cut-off sign ("23?") at the right edge of its last line, and f. 270r begins "3<bar> 23.<v>...". This
  looks like a line continuing across the leaf, but I cannot show it.
- f. 270v is the address leaf (rotated 90 degrees), two lines: "Por el Rey" and "Ju.º de Vargas mexia en Paris".
  It also has a large blank area, a faint round stain/seal ghost, and nothing else I could read. Transcribed in `[[ ]]`.
- 269r and 270r have faint show-through of the other side. Neither page's text is hidden by it.

## Countersignature, as read
"Don Ju.º de Idiaquez" (written roughly "Don Iuº de Idiaq..." with a long descending flourish and a looped
rubric). The first word is clearly "Don", then an abbreviated Juan (Ju with superscript o), "de", then
"Idiaquez"; the last letters ("-quez") are swallowed by the flourish, so the reading of the surname's ending is
by shape. The King's "Yo el Rey" is above it, with a large rubric stroke.

## Reading conventions I fell back on (not in the guide; flag them)
These are my own conventions, because the hand does not map neatly onto the guide's list. Anyone who compares this
to another reading should treat them as the most likely points of disagreement.
- The wide omega-like sign is written `m`. (Guide lists `m` as a letter form.)
- The loop-and-tail sign that looks like a Greek delta/"d" is written `d?` or `d`; where it sits among other
  letter-forms in a run (`L u m`, `a l b u l d.`, `t a d e`, `1 d e`) I read the run as letter forms.
  The spans "lum", "al bul d.", "ta", "de" are written joined and look like clear Spanish letters, but they are
  inside the cipher and I have not put them in `[[ ]]`.
- A bold `6`-like hooked sign followed by e-like loop is written `6p`; the very frequent "ʜʜ-e" cluster is written
  `13p`, and similar clusters `23p`, `33p`, `34p`, `74p`. These cluster readings are the least reliable
  part of the transcription. The digit part of a cluster (1/2/3 followed by 3/4) could be wrong; the hook `p` I am
  surer of.
- `u.`/`12.` vs `y.` vs `22.`: a u-shaped sign with a dot is written `12.` (or `22.` when two humps are clear);
  the guide's z/u ambiguity for 2 and 12 applies to dozens of occurrences, and I could not separate them reliably.
- A small z-shaped tick above a sign is written `<sign:small z above>` where it clearly differs from `<2dot>`;
  elsewhere two such ticks were written `<2dot>`.

## Passes
- Pass 1 (guide §6.1): top to bottom, left half then right half, starting from the last page and working back
  (270v, 270r, 269v, 269r). Strips: 3-4 lines each, 1500-1600 px wide, 440 px tall, overlap about 250-300 px, at
  native resolution. Strips viewed in pass 1: 22 on 269r + 22 on 269v + 10 on 270r = 54 line strips, plus
  3 edge crops on 269r, 1 edge crop on 269v, 3 signature crops on 270r, 2 crops of 270v, = 63 views.
- Pass 2 (opposite direction): every line strip re-viewed bottom to top and right half before left half,
  beginning with 269r and ending with 270r: 22 on 269r + 22 on 269v + 10 on 270r = 54 strips. The signature crops
  and the 270v crops were not re-viewed in pass 2. The pass-2 pages were done in the opposite order to pass 1.
  The edge crops on 269r were looked at once only, in pass 1.
- Changes made in pass 2 (about 15): 269v L26 end `16+|120+` (two readings); 269v m<acute> to m<hat> at one
  place; 270r last line `326<under>`, `y<under>` added; `131.?` for the last cipher group before the date;
  several `12.` changed to `22.`; `20+` for an earlier `201`; `18<v><under>?` on 270r line 3; `19.` bar dropped on
  269r; 269r last line `22.`; 269r line 31 end re-read. About half of the changes were underlines found on the
  second look.
