# Blind transcription notes, es. 132 ff. 247-248 (29 Nov 1579)

Reader: blind, guide v2.2 only. No other file opened, no network. Images: exp07/f247r, f247v, f248r, f248v.

## Extent
- The letter begins f. 247r (header "+ El Rey", then "Juan de Vargas Mexia ... Vras cartas de 16 18 Vltimo de Octb.", 7 clear lines, then cipher from "H." 14 cipher lines in two paragraphs: lines 1-11 ending in a virgula, lines 12-14 new paragraph with a large flourished initial `S`).
- Continues f. 247v: 12 cipher lines (paragraph 1 = lines 1-5 ending `/ .`, paragraph 2 = lines 6-12), the last cipher line ends `/` and is followed on the same line by clear "Del Pardo a 29"; next line "Novi. 1579."; then the King's "Yo el Rey" (large flourished signature) and below it two names (below).
- f. 248r: blank; only mirror show-through of f. 248v. Not transcribed.
- f. 248v: address leaf, rotated 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" (cross above), "A Juan de Vargas Mexia"; docket (several lines, rotated, top right) plus a pasted slip at far right with flourishes only (not readable).
- Total cipher lines: 14 (247r) + 12 (247v) = 26.

## Countersignature
Two names under "Yo el Rey". The larger one, right of centre, I read "Don Luis de Sotriag" with a looped flourish on the last letters (reading of the surname very uncertain). A small separate rubric at lower left, about 1000 px below the King, reads approximately "Idiaq" with a long underline stroke (looks like an abbreviated name; I read Idiaq(uez) but cannot confirm). I cannot say which is the secretary's countersignature.

## Passes (guide section 6)
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom, 3-line strips of 400 px height, left and right halves 1568 px wide (overlap about 90 px, not 300; page text is about 3000 px wide), native resolution.
- Pass 2: re-cut with different offsets (420 px, step 300) and read bottom to top, right half before left half. Done for all cipher lines of f. 247r and f. 247v. NOT done as an independent second pass for: the clear Spanish lines of f. 247r, the signatures, the date line, the f. 248v docket and address (each viewed once only; address read from a 25% overview plus docket at native).
- Strips viewed, pass 1: f. 247r 27 (13 left/right pairs of text plus right-margin check, 2 mostly blank), f. 247v 16 (6 pairs plus 4 signature crops), f. 248r 1, f. 248v 3 (rotated overview, 2 docket crops). Pass 2: f. 247r 16, f. 247v 12. Strips are 3-line, so this is fewer than the 25-40 per page asked for; line-level agreement between passes was moderate, not high.
- Honest quality statement: this cipher is small, joined and heavily show-through on both leaves. Expect many errors in digits (1/7/11, 5/6, 20/28/29, p-hook vs 5, plus vs t). Treat every line as provisional.

- Line 8: "seruy" ends with a tail; abbreviation mark not clear.
