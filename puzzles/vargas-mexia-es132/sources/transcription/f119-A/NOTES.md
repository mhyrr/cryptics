## Conventions I applied (not covered by the guide)
- A tail like an `e` after a number (7e, 17e, 15e, 24e, 6e) was written as hook `p`. I could not tell hook from a closed `e` curl in many places; every one of these is `p`.
- Tilde-shaped marks above (a wave, not a clear v) were written `<v>`. There are many; treat `<v>` as "tilde or small v".
- Hyphen-like dashes between numbers (e.g. `23-28`, `36-256`) were written `.` when they sit at mid-height like the dots; the guide has no separate sign.
- The letter groups written as tokens: `Ral`, `su`, `gil`, `pam+`, `Sa`, `x`, `Ham`, `Pam`, `quam`, `ne`, `yum+`, `S`, `Com`, `d`, `de`, `qua`, `xal`, `Vol`, `Zom`, `Na`, `Ja`, `bum`, `bim`, `nel`, `Dom`, `rum+`, `Val`, `bel`, `Bul`, `Oul`, `Jim`, `ril`, `re`, `c`, `xom+`, `Zum`, `gal`, `Ra`, `ym`, `yum`. The shape-only rule was followed; capital C/Z/R/X forms are the most doubtful (`Com?`, `Zom?`).
- Right edge: s03 lines 1 to 12 and s04 lines 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 are cut at the right edge by the image; ended with `<cut>`. Lines in s04 5 and others end with "/" or run to the margin.
