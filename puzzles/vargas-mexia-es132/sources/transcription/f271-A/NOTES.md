# f271-A notes (blind reading, Cipher 3 guide v2.2)

## Extent
- One letter, Philip II to Vargas Mexia, begins at the top of f. 271r ("El Rey" heading, then "Juan de Vargas Mexia | Demas de las
  cartas Vras a que se responde en las otras dos que van con esto / se recibieron a 28 del passado vna de catorze y dos de
  [?o] del mismo a los quales se responde en esto"). Four clear lines, then cipher from line 5.
- f. 271r: 25 lines (4 clear + 21 cipher). f. 271v: 26 lines (25 cipher + the final line, which begins in cipher and ends in clear
  "de Merida a [blotted] de mayo 1580"), then "Yo el Rey" (large, with rubric) and below it the countersignature.
- Countersignature as I read it: "Don Juan de Idiaquez" (shape: "Don", then "Iuan"/"Ivan", "de", "Idiaq..." with a flourish
  and a vertical rubric). Only the first and last letters are firm; the middle is read from shape.
- Date line: "de Merida a [blotted day numeral, overbar above it, ends in j (perhaps xxij or similar)] de mayo 1580". Day not read.
- f. 272r: blank leaf (show-through of the signatures only; one small blot). Nothing transcribed. Fragments of numerals at the
  right edge of the f. 271r and f. 272r images are neighbouring-leaf/gutter fragments; not transcribed.
- f. 272v: address leaf, written sideways: "Por el Rey" (with a cross above the "e"), then "A Juan de Vargas Mexia / en Paris",
  underlined. No docket seen. A faint "16 de ... 1580" docket visible in the gutter strip belongs to the next leaf; not transcribed.

## Passes
- Pass 1: f. 271r then f. 271v, left half then right half of each strip, top to bottom. Strips: 1500x400 px, native
  resolution, halves overlapping by 150 px. Strips viewed in pass 1: f. 271r 22 (+3 header/zoom crops), f. 271v 22 (+5 signature/
  date crops), f. 272r 1 (reduced-scale blank check plus overview), f. 272v 2. Strips are of 3 lines (not 2 per half-line as the
  brief suggests: about 11 bands per page, not 25-40, because the lines are 120 px apart).
- Pass 2 (reverse: last strip to first, right half before left): f. 271v 22 strips, then f. 271r 22 strips; the date strip was
  re-viewed; the signature crops and f. 272r/v were not re-viewed in full. Changes in pass 2 (about 40 lines touched): `8 2 4` read
  as `8p 4`; `11p` corrected to `14p`; duplicate `24 24.` removed in f. 271r line 10; `176` joined where I had split `17 6`;
  `u` with cross above in f. 271r line 7; `[[bul]]`, `[[mil]]` etc. normalised; various marks above added (bars over
  `17.`, `12+`, `30+`); `35p 7` re-read `35p 21`; line 20 and 22 of f. 271v re-split.
- Honest assessment: these are dense, small, tilted lines; I think about 90 percent of tokens are right in base, fewer in marks
  above. The `7` versus z-shaped `2` split is the weakest point (see below). The line boundaries are right.

## Uncertainties of a systematic kind
- A flat-topped 7 versus the z-shaped 2 (guide §7) is not reliably separable in this hand, especially `7p`, `7+`, `7.`. I wrote 7 where
  the top is a flat bar and 2 where there is a tail below the line; expect errors both ways.
- `q` (a 4 with a long descender) written `4`; `9.` where the descender curls back (r. l9, l15).
- A "u"/"21"/"22" confusion: dotted `u.` written `u.`; undotted two-stroke forms written `22` or `21`.
- Marks above: bars and carets are high-confidence; single dots and small v are low-confidence.
