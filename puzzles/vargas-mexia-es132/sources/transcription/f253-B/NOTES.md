# f253-B blind transcription notes (ff. 253-254, BnF es. 132, 1580)

Reader worked from CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.2 only and from the four page images in `sources/cache/pages/exp07/`. No other file and no network was used.

## Extent
- The letter starts on f. 253r (header "El Rey", clear opening, 6 clear lines, then cipher) and ends on f. 253v (3 cipher lines, clear passage on the licence for Geronymo Gondi, place and date, "Yo el Rey", countersignature). It is one letter on one leaf (f. 253 r+v).
- f. 253r: header + 6 clear lines + 19 cipher lines (l07-l25). A paragraph break, with a short dash in the left margin, falls before l19. l23 ends in `/.` and is short (it starts with a large Q-like sign); l24 begins at the normal margin with slightly more space above it, so a further paragraph break there is possible, not certain.
- f. 253v: 3 cipher lines (l01-l03), 4 clear lines (l04-l07, ending "De Madrid / a xxbj de enero 1580 /"), the King's "Yo el Rey" (flourished, below), and the secretary's countersignature lower right. Total cipher lines: 22.
- f. 254r: blank (foliation "254" top right only). Not transcribed.
- f. 254v: address leaf, written sideways: "Por el Rey" and "A Ju° de Vargas Mexia", with a seal remnant. Transcribed in `[[ ]]`. A docket and a seal visible in the right-hand 400 px of the image are on the facing page (the next folio), not on this leaf; not transcribed.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Ioan de Idiaquez" (heavily flourished; "Don", "de" and the initial I are clear; the ending "-aquez" is a flourish I read by shape and plausibility, so treat the surname as probable, not certain).
- Clear-Spanish uncertainties: f253r l01 "Seresspon-" (the long-s pair and the "x"-like letter are unclear; may be "se respon-"); the two dates "x̄cy" and "x̄ciiij" in l03 are written with an overbar and the second has dots above; I give them as seen and do not resolve them. On f253v l05 "destos reynos" has "tos" and "nos" apparently struck or overwritten.

## Passes (honest account)
- Pass 1 (guide §6.1, native resolution, top to bottom): 3-line strips cut with `magick`, left window x 600-2100 and right window x 1800-3300 (right windows re-cut as x 2000-3450 where the edge mattered). Strips viewed: f. 253r about 30 (8 strip positions, left and right, right-edge recuts, extra strips for l15-l18, and 6 early strips for the clear lines); f. 253v about 10 (3-line cipher strips and clear text/signature strips); f. 254v 4 (overview and crops). About 44 strips in pass 1 in total. This is fewer than the 25-40 per page the brief asks for, because the cipher on f. 253r is only 19 lines and f. 253v has only 3.
- Pass 2 (reverse): re-read by single manuscript line, from the last line to the first (f. 253r l25 -> l07, then f. 253v l03 -> l01), each line cut into four 800 px windows (x 650, 1300, 1950, 2600; 150 px overlap) at native resolution and stacked in one image. Windows viewed: 19 lines x 4 = 76 on f. 253r, 3 x 4 = 12 on f. 253v, plus about 10 closer re-crops of doubtful places. About 98 windows in pass 2.
- Differences from the brief: pass 2 went line by line, bottom to top, but the windows within a line were read left to right, not right to left; and pass 2 used narrower windows than pass 1 instead of repeating the same strips. Pass 2 was complete (every cipher line). The final file is the pass-2 reading; pass 1 readings were corrected often (marks above, `p` hooks, and 2/7 forms changed). I did not keep a diff of pass 1 against pass 2.
- Reading quality: this is a dense, small hand. Expect errors in `p` vs plain letter, `2` vs `7`, `4` forms, `+` placement, and marks above. Treat the file as a first reading, not a settled one.

## Conventions I had to choose (shapes only, no values)
- Small raised marks that look like a tiny `r` or `v` above a sign are all written `<v>`.
- `d`: a small closed delta-like sign with a flat top and a loop to the left, usually followed by `.`, `6` or a hook (`d.`, `d 6 4<hat>`, `d p 30.`). Distinct from the cursive clear "de" (ascender d + e), which appears as `[[de]]` in l09, l11, l13 and f253v l03. The two are close and I may have sorted a few wrongly.
- `p` is written for every hook / e-like closing curl after a number, as the guide says.
- `4` also occurs in an open, q-like form (as in the clear numeral "14" in the opening, written like "iq"); I write it `4` or `14` and keep the letter `q` for the q with a descender and a dot (`q.`).
- `2` flat-topped with a diagonal (like a 7) is written `7`; round z-shaped forms are written `2`.
- `6.` followed by a dash at upper level: written `<bar>` (not `<tbar>`; there is no vertical).
- `quam` (clear-looking letters) is written as separate letters `q u a m`; `Qom` (l23 start, large initial) as `Q o m`. I did not put them in `[[ ]]` because they are not Spanish.

## Every `<sign:...>`

## Every `<under>`
f253r l09 `35+<under>` (clear); f253r l10 `356<under>` (clear). None seen elsewhere in the 30s (36, 30, 31, 32, 35 elsewhere have no underline), but a faint one could be hidden by the line below.

## Curl uncertainty
Joined vs separate 6 is doubtful at f253r l08 `76 6p` (could be `7 66p`), l14 `6 5+` (kept separate), l17 `29 6 4<hat>`, l18 `166`, l20 `66 10<hat><bar>?`, l22 `20 6 25.<v>`, l19 `d 6 4<hat>` (the 6 follows the d sign, not a number; kept separate), f253v l02 `6 5.` and `26.`.

## Other uncertainties
- l08 start: `1++` is one upright stroke with two crosses (or one cross and a second stroke); could be `1+ +` or `1+ 1+`.
- Several lines show the same long phrase (`316 256 d p 30.` in l07, l15, l24; `q u a m d 6 4<hat>` in l08, l10, l19). I read each separately and did not harmonise them. The readings agree except l15 `23p 30.` (the sign before `30.` looks like a round 2 + 3 + hook there, a d-sign in l07 and l24) and bars over `256` in l15 only.
- Many signs carry a `<hat>` or `<bar>`; a few are faint or sit between two signs (l09 `12+<bar>`, l13 `18<bar>` may also carry a hat).

## Image problems

- The ink is heavy and the 6 curls sometimes close into blobs (f253r l16 near `12 6p`; l15 start).
- A blot on f253r l16 sits over a 6.
- The right edge of f253r is slightly stained and uneven; no cipher is cut off. On f253v the cipher of l01 and l02 ends close to the gutter band at the right (the facing page f254r has no text near it).
- f254v: the page is damaged at the top and has water staining; the writing is complete. The right 400 px of the image belongs to another leaf.
- f254r is blank; the strip on its left shows f253v text only.
