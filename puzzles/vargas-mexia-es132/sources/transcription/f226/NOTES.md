# f226 notes (BnF es. 132, ff. 226-227, 13 Sep 1579)

## Extent
- Letter starts f. 226r (heading "El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", 3 clear lines + 2 clear lines) and ends f. 226v
  (place/date line, "Yo el Rey", countersignature). Letter = clear opening (5 lines, 226r), 23 cipher lines on 226r,
  9 cipher lines on 226v (the last shares its line with "De San lorenço a xiy.e de Septiembre 1579"), signatures.
- f. 227r: blank; faint show-through of the address leaf only. f. 227v: address leaf ("Por el Rey / Juº de vargas Mexia / 2ª."),
  docket rotated 90 degrees (Sant Lorenço, de su Md. de 14 de septe, recib.da, En Pausa? a 24 del mismo), and "Dup.da"
  (also written bottom left of 226v). Not letter text; transcribed in [[ ]] under its own header.
- Total cipher lines: 32 (23 + 9). Line breaks follow the manuscript; lines were joined across two overlapping crops
  (left x450-1950, right x1850-3350 on 226r) and the overlap sign was counted once.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Iuº de Idiaquez" (Juan de Idiaquez), ornate with long flourishes.
- Date line: the numeral after "a" reads "xiy" with a small raised letter (e/o) above; I could not decide between xiij and
  xiiij. The docket on 227v says 14. Left as `xiy`.

## Passes
1. Left to right in crops of 3 lines, native resolution, left and right halves per line.
2. Right-to-left re-check: only partial. Done for 226v line 3 (re-cropped, several marks corrected), and the "sim/yum" letter
   groups unified across both pages (earlier `2um` -> `yum`). The other lines were re-read against the same crops
   without re-cropping; expect residual errors in marks above and in 2/7/1 shapes.

## Systematic uncertainties
- A short dash to the right of a "6" or "35" (e.g. `6=`): written `<bar>?`; it may be a mark-after, not a mark above.

- "4" with a hat/acute/small mark above: often ambiguous between hat, acute and a dot; `?` where doubtful.
