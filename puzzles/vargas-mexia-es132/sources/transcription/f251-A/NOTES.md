# NOTES for f251-A (blind transcription, BnF Esp. 132, ff. 251-252, letter of 16 Jan 1580)

Guide used: CIPHER3-GUIDE.md v2.2. No other repository file was opened; no network. Line numbers below are
cipher-line numbers counted per page, in the order of `transcription.txt` (clear-text lines in `[[ ]]` not counted).

## Extent
- Letter begins at the top of f. 251r with the King's heading `+ El Rey` (cross above, dot over R, long underline),
  then the salutation `Juan de Vargas Mexia /.` and 9 lines of clear Spanish (acknowledging letters of 9, 13, 16, 20,
  22, 29 Nov and 2, 4, 6, 11, 14 Dec; the numerals are overlined in the manuscript). Cipher begins directly after
  `respuesta /.`.
- f. 251r: 16 cipher lines (two paragraphs; l. 8 is a short line ending `/.`; ll. 7, 9, 15 end in an `=` dash).
- f. 251v: 28 cipher lines (paragraphs end with `/.` at ll. 7, 12, 19, 27; l. 3 is a short line ending `6.?` without a virgula; new paragraphs open with a large
  or flourished initial at ll. 4 (`H.`), 8, 13, 14 (`A`), 28).
- f. 252r: 15 cipher lines. Line 9 is a short line ending `/.`; line 10 opens with a large `D.`; the last cipher line (l. 15)
  ends `... 7 26 K?` and runs straight into the clear date `De Madrid a xvj de En~o` (abbreviated; read as Enero), with
  `1580.` underlined below it. Then the King's signature, read `Yo el Rey` (large flourished Y, o, then "el Rey" in loops;
  partly overlapped by grey show-through of the flourishes on f. 252v) and, lower right, the countersignature.
- Countersignature as I read it: **"Don Juo de Idiaquez"** (Don + flourish, "Iuº" with a tall arched stroke on the I, "de", "Idiaq..."
  with the ending "-uez" lost in a flourish and a looped descender). The ending is not fully legible; "Idiaq" is secure.
- f. 252v: address leaf, rotated 90 degrees on the scan (read after rotating -90). Contents: `+ Por el Rey` (cross above "el",
  long underline stroke), `A Juan de Vargas Mexia`, and a docket top right: `de su Magd de 16 de En.º R.da` / `en Paris a 4 de Hebrero` /
  `respondida a` (not completed). A separate pasted/overlapping strip at the right edge carries a scribble and what looks like
  `Lob` (not transcribed). A circular seal remnant with paper patch lower left of the rotated page. Not cipher.
- Page numbers `251` and `252` (top right) and a pencil number at the left edge of f. 252v are not transcribed.

## Passes (guide section 6)
- Pass 1: left to right per strip, forward page order 251r, 251v, 252r, 252v, strips 1700 px wide (left and right halves overlapping by 300 px),
  native resolution, 3-4 manuscript lines per strip (line pitch is about 135-145 px, so 3 lines is about 420 px; I used strips 480-500 px high).
  Strips viewed in pass 1: **67** (251r: 10 clear-text + 12 cipher = 22; 251v: 22; 252r: 16 + 3 signature/date = 19; 252v: 4), plus 6 reduced overview thumbnails
  used only to locate lines.
- Pass 2 (opposite direction: last page to first, bottom strip to top, right half before left half, reading each line right to left):
  **60 strips** re-viewed (252v 4; 252r 14; 251v 20; 251r 22). Strips NOT re-viewed in pass 2 (all blank, ghost-only or signature):
  252r w6L, w7L, the date strip and the two signature strips; 251v v9L, v9R, a0L, v10L.
  Everything with cipher text was re-viewed in both directions. Changes in pass 2 (examples): marks above moved to the correct sign
  (e.g. 251v l.8 acute from 30. to 12+; l.15 `<v>` from `10` to `15p`), `224+` vs `24+` re-judged by glyph width, duplicated tokens at the left/right strip
  overlap removed (251v l.28, 251r l.10), `15p+`/`25p` alternatives marked, several `?` added. I did not do a third pass on the hardest lines.

## Reading conventions I had to choose
- A `z`-shaped 2 is read as 2 (guide section 7). The glyph group that looks like `2 3 s p` (z, 3, long-s-like stroke, hook) is written `23p` throughout;
  it could instead be `235p`. Same doubt for `35p` (`3 5p`).
- A flat vertical `1` and a z-shaped `2` are very close at the start of `15p` / `25p`; where I could not decide I wrote `15p|25p` or chose by overlap with a repeated phrase.
- The glyph `H` after a `1` (looks like `1 H`) is written `14` or `14+`; before a `+` it is `24+`/`14+`. This is a judgement.
- The curl rule was followed: curls touching the previous number are written joined (hence the long list of 3-digit runs below).
- Repeated phrases occur (e.g. `3 7+<v> 11p 21.`, `12+ 11+ y<bar> /.`, `22<cross><bar> 6.`, `18 26 76 18 21.`); I used them only as a tie-break when a glyph was unclear.

## Image problems
- f. 251r: heavy show-through of the verso text (mirror image) between and under cipher lines, especially lines 2-8; clear text is unaffected.
- f. 251v: a dark vertical gutter shadow runs down the right edge (about x=3140-3190 on the scan). Lines 1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 10 and 13-25 run into it, so the last 1-2 signs of those lines are cut, blurred
  or only partly visible (marked `<sign:cut-by-edge>`, with `?` on the last token). Lines 11, 12, 19, 27, 28 end clear of it. A torn notch and pale strip sit to the right of the shadow around lines 9-11.
  Grey show-through of the recto (mirrored "El Rey", mirrored salutation and date numerals, and cipher) lies under lines 1-3 and 5-9. The first lines show faint ink beyond the shadow at the very right (grey `6.`), not transcribed.
- f. 252r: left side is the dark edge of the facing page (f. 251v) with cut-off fragments of its line ends; not transcribed. Grey show-through of the 252v flourishes (arch near line 9-10, loops under the signatures) and a grey `+`
  above the large `D.`; ink smear (a vertical band at about x=2300-2340, over lines 13-14) damages `18p ? 24+` in l.14 and `22+` in l.13. A white scratched/abraded patch lower left. Line 10's large `D.` has a long flourish stroke to its left.
- f. 252v: scan is rotated; heavy show-through of the cipher of f. 252r under the address; seal patch lower left.
- Unclear items that matter: the first token of 251r l.3 (large G-like 6, read `6p`); the large initials at 251v l.8, 28, 13 and 251r l.9 (`A`); 252r l.14 end region.
