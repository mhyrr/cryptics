# f215-B2 blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- Letter starts f. 215r (head "El Rey" with small cross above; salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia"; the first cipher line begins on the salutation line after "Mexia").
- 10 cipher lines on 215r (line 1 shares the salutation line), ending at a virgula `/`. Then clear Spanish, 15 lines (paragraph break before "Antonio Perez me ha mostrado" and before "Tambien me ha mostrado").
- Letter ends f. 215v: 3 clear lines (the first two run to the gutter and are cut by the binding/shadow at the right edge), date line, "Yo el Rey", countersignature.
- f. 216r: blank (stains, show-through). f. 216v: address leaf, "Por el Rey" / "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", rotated docket at right edge "13. de Julio de 1579".
- Not part of the letter: left-edge slivers of the facing page on all images.
- f. 215v shows strong mirrored show-through of the 215r text under the signatures; it was ignored.

## Countersignature (as read)
Below "Yo el Rey": a large flourished signature. Visible ink: tall "A", a long loop going left and under, "nt" with a t-stroke and a dot, then two looped glyphs like "88" or "83". I read it as "Ant.o P?erez" (Antonio Perez), low confidence on the second word; mirrored "Perez" from 215r lies behind the left loop, which confuses the shape. The clear text of 215r names Antonio Perez three times, consistent with this reading.

- Pass 2 (bottom to top, right half before left), f. 215r cipher only: 20 strips (10 lines x 2 halves, 1600x190, new crop offsets). All cipher lines corrected against it. Changes: bars/carets recovered on lines 3, 4, 9, 10; line 5 start `66<bar><cross>`; line 6 `206 326`; `4+` added in line 9; `76 11.` in lines 7 and 9.

- Fewer strips than the "25-40 per page" asked: the cipher is only 10 lines, so 215r needed 22.

## Curl uncertainty
Every `6` after a number in a run (`246`, `256`, `286`, `236`, `206`, `326`, `156`, `116`) is read as a joined curl. Cases where the curl might be separate: `20 6<v>`/`206<v>` in l.3 and `28 206<v>`; `S6?` in l.5. Standalone `6.` tokens (dot after) are frequent and read as separate.
- A glyph that looks like "e" with a hat, bar or v above sits at the end of many runs; I took it to be the curl.
- `156` is the glyph group that looks like "ise"/"wise"; the "s" looks like a 5. If "15" is wrong, the whole run changes.
