Images: f119_s01 to f119_s05 (5). Read in reverse order (s05 to s01) for pass 1; pass 2 in page order (s01 to s05).
Resolution is low (scan-like, 1.2 to 1.7k px wide); marks above and hook/plus/dot distinctions are often marginal.
