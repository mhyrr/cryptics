# f222 NOTES (blind reader; BnF es. 132 ff. 222-223, 13 Sep 1579)

## Extent
- Letter begins f. 222r (top: "Yo el Rey" heading, salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", 5 clear lines, then cipher) and ends f. 222v
  (cipher, place/date line "de S.t Lorenço ... 1579", King's autograph, countersignature).
- f. 223r: blank (faint mirror show-through of the address; also a heavy ink blot at left edge). Not transcribed.
- f. 223v: address leaf: "Por el Rey" / "A Iuan de Vargas Mexia" (rotated), a docket "23. de setiembre de 157?" (right edge,
  rotated, tail cut by the image), and "Dup.da" bottom left. The same "Dup.da" appears at the foot of f. 222v.
- Clear text transcribed inside [[ ]]. Date line: read "a xiij." (could be xxiij; the docket reads 23). 

## RELIABILITY WARNING
The cipher is small and cramped. I read it in 1700x350 native strips (3 lines each, overlapping), line by line, but
could not resolve many signs. This transcription should be treated as LOW reliability: sign splitting, hook vs 6, 2 vs u,
and marks above are all uncertain. Several lines (flagged by trailing "...", or very short) are partial or merged:
the strips overlap by about one line, and I may have merged/duplicated/dropped some lines on f. 222r between the
"16. p+ 22. 7." line and the "21. 18 33." line. The cipher line count is therefore approximate. I simplified the
hook-shaped "e/p" tail forms uniformly to `p` and the crossed/hooked 7 forms to `7+`/`7p`; these are guesses.
Do not use this reading for statistics without a re-read.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right per strip. Pass 2 (right to left): only a partial re-check of the line ends was done; I did NOT
  complete an independent full second pass. Changes in pass 2: none recorded.

## 3+ digit runs left joined (all uncertain)
864 (f222r l.1), 500 (f222v), 166, 176, 286, 246, 417, 183, 2869, 224, 236 (many; each may be two numbers or a number
plus curl; I could not split them reliably). Frequent shapes: `1 76`, `2 86 4`, `2 4 6`.

## `?` marks
Every `?` in the file (864<acute>?, 6p?, 236?, 2.<acute>?, 2<under>?, 3<under>?, 7+<under>?, 500?, 26 6p?).

## `<sign:...>`
Only the King's autograph description on f. 222v.

## `<under>`
Possible underlines under a 3x number ("3<under>?" twice on f. 222r near the bottom and f. 222v l.3), and under a 7+ on f. 222v l.5;
all marked `?`. I saw at least one clear underline under "35" at the start of f. 222v line 3 (`3 5+`).

## Image problems
Heavy black blot at left edge of f. 223r; page edges of facing page in the gutter; slight show-through of f. 222v text on 222r
is weak. The cipher lines slope slightly upward to the right. Right edge of f. 222v is clipped by a dark gutter band.

## Countersignature
Read as "Don Iuº de Idiaquez" (flourished; "Don Iu^o d Idiaq..." with a large tail loop), i.e. Juan de Idiáquez.
King's signature on f. 222v: large flourished "Yo el Rey" autograph.
