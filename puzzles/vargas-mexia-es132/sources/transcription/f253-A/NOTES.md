# f253-A notes (blind transcription, CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.2)

Images: `sources/cache/pages/exp07/f253r.jpg`, `f253v.jpg`, `f254r.jpg`, `f254v.jpg` only. No network. No other repository file read.

## Extent
- The letter begins on f. 253r with the heading "El Rey" and the clear opening "Juan de Vargas Mexia, en otra carta q va con esta se respon...", six clear lines, then 19 cipher lines (three paragraphs: lines 1-12; lines 13-17, the first marked by a dash in the left margin; lines 18-19, which carry on to the next page without a break).
- It continues on f. 253v with 3 cipher lines (line 1 starts where f. 253r line 19 stopped), then four clear lines ("La licencia para que Geronymo Gondi pueda sacar dos cavallos destos reynos ... De Madrid a xxvj de enero 1580"), then the King's signature "Yo el Rey", then the secretary's countersignature lower on the page.
- Total: 22 cipher lines (19 on 253r, 3 on 253v), 10 clear lines on 253r/v (6 + 4).
- f. 254r: blank leaf (ink spots, folio number "254"). Nothing transcribed.
- f. 254v: address leaf, written sideways (page turned 90 degrees clockwise to read). "Por el Rey" / "A Jnº de Vargas Mexia" and a docket top right, transcribed in `[[ ]]` under its page header.

## Countersignature, as I read it
"Don Iuan de Idiaquez" (Don, then a word with a tall I-loop that I read Iuan, then "de", then Idiaquez with a tall I-loop and a flourished final z; rubric under it). Letters inside Iuan and Idiaquez are partly flourish; the word division is my reading of the shape. The King's signature reads "Yo el Rey" with long rubric strokes crossing below and to the right.

## Conventions I adopted (the guide is silent on these; shapes only)
- A loop that looks like an `e` and sits right after a number (and after `6`, `9`) is written as the hook `p` (guide §2: "e with a tail"). After a letter-form (`L`, `d`, `S`, `m`...) a separate closed `e` is written `e` where it is full size, as in `L e 9.`, `d e 30.`.
- The glyph shaped like `q` (closed bowl with a descender) is written `9` when it is among numbers (the clear text writes "19" as "1q" and I follow that). Inside the word-like group `q u a m` it is written `q`, with `u a m` as letter forms. That group occurs on f253r lines 2, 4, 13. I do not know whether it is clear Latin or cipher; I kept it as signs.
- The x-looking glyph with a dot (`x.`) is written `2.` (z-shaped 2, guide §7). A closed `o` after a number that is not a plausible `10`/`20`/`30` is left as `0`/`o` in the token.
- `d e` (a looped d plus e) is written for the pair that appears after a virgula and before `30.`; `S.` is the flat-topped s with a dot. The pair before `30.` on f253r line 9 looks more complicated (`d? e 30.`) and is the least certain.
- `+` is only written where a mid-height cross is visible; `t`-like crosses are `6.?` or `+`.
- Short tick above a sign that is not a slash, backslash, v, caret, bar or dot is written `<sign:...>`.
- Heavy show-through of the facing and reverse sides is present on f253v; marks that are faint ghosts were not transcribed.

## Passes (guide §6)
- Pass 1, forward (first page to last, top to bottom, left half then right half): f253r 24 cipher strips (1500x400 left, 1550x400 right, steps of 250 px, 3-4 lines each, native resolution) plus 7 strips/crops for the heading and the six clear lines; f253v 10 strips (5 left, 5 right) plus 2 signature crops and 1 lower-corner crop; f254r 1 overview; f254v 3 docket strips, 2 address strips and 1 overview. About 49 strips/crops in all; the cipher strips alone: 34.
- Pass 2, backward (last page to first, last line to first line, right to left within each line): single-line crops, 800x200 px at native resolution, four per line, overlapping about 100 px: f253r 17 lines x 4 = 68 (lines 17 to 1) plus 4 for lines 18-19, plus 6 re-views of the clear-text strips; f253v 10 crops (cipher lines 3 to 1) plus 4 re-views of the clear-text strips; f254v 3 docket strips re-viewed. About 98 crops. These are smaller than the 1500 px strips of the brief (finer, not coarser), so the second pass is by line, not by 3-line strip. Both passes were completed in full.
- What the second pass changed on f253r: all `x.` readings became `2.`; `S e 30.` became `d e 30.` everywhere (the glyph is the one in "d e"); `11p` became `14p` (same glyph as the line 6 `14p`); `7 6` became joined `76` on line 2 and line 12; several joined 3-digit runs were added (e.g. `246` for a 4 followed by a small closed curl on lines 16, 18); `256` gained its bar above on lines 1 and 9; lines 13-17 were split and tokenized again (the first pass had line 13 and 14 partly merged); `12+<2dot>` on line 10 and `24.<bar>` on line 3 were added; `S6<hat>` replaced `S 6<hat>` on line 9. On f253v: `11p` became `14p`; the grave over `6` on line 2 added.

## Image problems
- f253r: right margin is damaged (frayed paper edge, a chipped notch at about lines 9-10) but the text stops before it; the longest lines end within 100 px of the edge and are complete. Left half of line 1 of the clear text is overrun by the long flourish of "El Rey".
- f253v: the right gutter shows a black band (shadow of the binding) at about x = 3150; text of the first cipher line (`18<bar>`) and the clear lines (`ca`, `persona`, `De Madri[d]`) runs up to it, and the last letters ("persona", "Madrid") are clipped by it. Heavy show-through of f253r text, especially under cipher line 1 (a faint arc around `S 1+ 1+`), and under the clear lines. The word "reynos" has "nos" faint or struck (hairline through it).
- f253v lower right: a pasted or folded strip of the address leaf docket overlaps the page edge (rotated fragments reading "Rda", "Lo..." and a rule); not part of the letter, not transcribed.
- f254v: a large round stain with a patch (probably the remains of a seal) at the top left of the leaf; the docket at the top right is partly cut by a pasted-on strip with a second fragment of the docket ("R da", and a rule with "1579" above it). The docket reading "Hebr.º ues?" is uncertain (the last word could be a numeral or an abbreviation). The word under it (`da`, a small loop and `Don.?` with a long rubric stroke down and left) is left as seen.
- f254r: blank except specks and the folio number; the left 400 px is f253v.

## Open points
- The cipher tokenization of `d e`, `q u a m`, `L e`, `S.` as letter forms is a decision, not a certainty (see Conventions).
- The date line reads "a xxvj de enero 1580" with the v written like a b ("xxbj").
- The docket, as read: "Madrid / de Su M. de xxvj de Enº R.da / en Paris a 4 de Hebr.º ues? / da / Don.?".
