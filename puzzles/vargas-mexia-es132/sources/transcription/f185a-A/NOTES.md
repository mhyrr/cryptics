## Ambiguities worth knowing
- **"1H" vs "11" or "14".** Many places show a sign like `1H`, `1H+`: two uprights with a crossbar in between. I wrote `14+` where the form had a plus (s01 line 8/10, s02 lines 4, 5, 14, s03 lines 11, 19, s04 line 14, s06 lines 1, 3, s07 lines 7, 9) and `11` / `11+` elsewhere (s03 `11.`, s04 `11.`, s06 `11 7p`, s07 `20 11 14 12+`, `24 11 14`, `24 11`), but this is not secure: a reader could reasonably call those `14` (with a letter-form h behind it, see guide section 9) or `1 1`. I did not write `h|14`.
- The glyph before `Jim`/`yim`, written `1<cross>? Jim` in s01 line 7 (a small 1 with a cross above).
- `7p`, `2p`, `22p` etc.: a hook (guide p) is written on most 7s, 2s, 12s, 15s and 24s; I wrote it wherever a loop-with-descender followed the number. Where the loop looked closed like a curl I wrote the curl (`76`, `256`).
- Right edge: many lines run into the dark gutter at the right of the leaf (s01-s04, s07) or to the edge of the screenshot (s05, s06). I ended these with `<cut>`. A line ending `<cut>` is probably not a line end.
- Left edge of s02, s04, s06 and s07: a few isolated crosses, commas or flecks to the left of line starts are bleed-through; ignored.
