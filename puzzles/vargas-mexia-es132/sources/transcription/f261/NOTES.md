# f261 (ff. 261-262, 28 Mar 1580) blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- Letter begins f. 261r (head "El Rey" with a cross above, then "Juan de Vargas mexia" and the cipher on the same line).
- 261r: 26 manuscript lines (salutation line = line 1). Paragraph breaks after line 10 (line ends with a heavy `/`) and implicitly at line 11 (indented start).
- 261v: 11 lines. Line 7 ends with `/` (paragraph end); line 11 is a short cipher group `3 14p<2dot> /` followed by clear `De Guadalupe á 28 de março 1580.` and `/`. Then "Yo el Rey" (flourished), the secretary's signature at lower right, and a marginal note "Dup.do" at left (below the King's signature, x~1100, y~3400 on the image).
- Lower half of 261v shows heavy mirrored show-through of 261r; not transcribed.
- f. 262r: blank apart from mirror show-through of the address; nothing transcribed.
- f. 262v: address leaf (written sideways): "Por el Rey" / "Dup.do" / "Juan de Vargas Mexia" (with a heavy underscore flourish). Transcribed in [[ ]].
- Left-edge pencil/modern note "28 de marzo de 1580" at gutter of 261r image belongs to the facing leaf, not transcribed.

## Countersignature
Read as "Don Juan de Idiaquez" (shape: "Don" + abbreviated "Ju(an)" + "de" + "Idiaq-/Iraq-" with a long flourish); only the first word and "de" are firm. Reading of the surname is uncertain.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom. Strips viewed: 261r 20 (18 regular half-strips of ~1700x410 plus 2 extra for line 13) + 4 early test strips and 2 salutation crops; 261v 8 regular + 1 line-1 crop + 3 signature crops + overview crops; 262r and 262v 1 crop each.
- Pass 2: opposite direction (bottom to top, right half then left half). Strips re-viewed: 261r 18 (strips 09..01, R and L; plus the salutation crop), 261v 9 (04..01 R/L, line-1 crop seen again). The extra line-13 crops and signature crops were not re-viewed in pass 2.
- Pass 2 changes: 261r line 10 `24+<under>` -> `34+<under>`; line 7 end `3<acute>`; line 3 caret moved to `7p<hat>`; line 19 start `2|3`; line 22 end `21|31`; line 1 `3.? 6+`; 261v line 3 end `35p 16 6?`; `20.` for the unmarked 2-/3-shaped "o" sign in 261r line 12.
- Honest caveat: the hand is very cramped and the verso show-through is heavy. Many 2/3, 1/4, 14/17, 6/p distinctions are guesses. Treat every unmarked token as moderately confident only.

## Recurring uncertain signs
- `<sign:co-shaped curl pair>`: a "co"/omega-shaped pair of curls, frequent (261r lines 12, 14-16, 20, 24, 26; 261v lines 1, 4, 7, 10). Could be a z-shaped 2 plus curl or a letter form.
- `<sign:blotted z-shaped glyph with hook above>` (261r line 4) and `<sign:blotted glyph with two marks above>` (261r line 14).
- `x` appears as a letter form (261r lines 4, 21; 261v line 4/10).
- `S<hat>` (long s with caret) in 261v line 10.
- 2 vs 3 vs rounded "ʒ" shapes: no firm distinction; the digit-30s were written `3x` only where an underline or an obviously round 3 appeared.
- The glyphs I wrote `14+`, `24+`, `14p`, `24p`, `17+` are often interchangeable at this resolution.
