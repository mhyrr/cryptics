# f. 154 transcription notes (blind)

Source: Gallica ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x. f.154r = right page canvas 151; 154v = left canvas 152; 155r = right canvas 152; 155v = left canvas 153 (the letter ends there: last cipher line, "Del pardo A iiijº De xbre 1578.", king's signature, and a second flourished signature reading "Ant. Perez"). Folio numbers seen ("154" top right of 154r; "155"/"156" on later pages). Crops (native, 1500x380, halves overlapping ~140 px, step 330 px) are in sources/cache/f154-crops/.

## Layout
154r: heading "El Rey" (small t with bar above), clear lines 2-4 (Juan de Vargas Mexia... "se satisfara enesta"), cipher from the end of line 4: 32 manuscript lines in this file. 154v: 30 cipher lines, no clear text. 155r: 33 cipher lines. 155v: 6 cipher lines, then date and signatures. Paragraph-like breaks: short lines ending in "/." or "/" (e.g. 155r lines 13, 25, 33). Capital-letter starts (E, A, P) mark some line/paragraph starts. A catchword-like "96" sits below the last line of 154r (not in the file; 154v begins "9+ 26 y"). A flourish sign ends 154r line 31.

## Date and signatures as read
"Del pardo A iiijº De xbre 1578." (the "iiijº" is written "iuj°"/"ijjº"-like, read as 4th). Then "Yo el Rey" autograph and "Ant. Perez" (secretary flourish). Neither is cipher.

## Method and passes
One sign per token from native strips. The left-to-right pass was done strip by strip; for every strip pair, the overlap region was compared token by token between the L and R crop (this is the only right-to-left check that was actually done at full strength; no separate whole-letter R-to-L reread was made, so passes are NOT fully independent). Joins at overlaps are the most error-prone points.

## Cipher token count
About 2,060 whitespace tokens (154r ~670 incl. a few clear words, 154v 618, 155r 669, 155v 124), counting "/" and "," tokens.

## Systematic ambiguities (decoder-relevant)
- "9": the open-tail g/9 shape is written "9" everywhere ("9+", "19", "96"); I could not tell digit 9 from letter g. "19" vs "y" (a single y-shape with long tail) is the commonest confusion; I wrote 19 when a separate 1 stroke was visible, otherwise y. "19+" vs "18+" and "19" vs "18" also confused.
- "2+" (2 plus) vs "2H": distinguished by the H shape (two verticals); several "2+" may be 2H.
- "1061" written joined; sometimes the final 1 looks separate ("106 1"). "106H"/"106 H", "9H"/"9_ H" ambiguous.
- "4" vs "C+": an open-topped 4 with plus ("4?|C+") appears several times; a clean 4 elsewhere.
- Dot placement: "_" vs "^" often uncertain for 1, 10, 18, 2, 7; "?" not always added. "18_" can be 18 with dot or dot of the line below.
- "5" vs "S"/"s": written as 5 (digit with dot "5."); "5+" vs "S+" uncertain.
- "5^" and "<sign:Sigma>1": Sigma and following 1 often tight; written as "<sign:Sigma>1" when joined.
- "7<hat>" vs "7^": 7 with a hat or dot above, uncertain.
- "<above:106>"/"<above:10>": small superscript numbers above 76/7 on 154v lines 11 (non-standard token, my notation).

## Hardest readings
154r line 6/8 joins; 154r line 13 (ink blot before 6); 154r line 17 "fi"; 154r line 22 "a|d y"; 154v lines 11-12 (superscript 106, "7<hat>61 1 17<bar>?"); 155v line 4/5 ends hidden in the gutter (106?, 10?). 154v right edge (lines 3-5, 6, 7, 17-20, 22, 23, 27, 30) carries fainter ink at the gutter; these tokens are read but marked "?" where doubtful. 155r line 3 first token read "y".

## Clear/cipher boundary
Clear text ends in 154r line 4 after "enesta"; cipher starts "1061 12 9." (could "1061 12 9." be a date/number? unknown). The sign after "hasta" in line 3 ("2/z with bar" then "del / y") and the "/ y" before "teniendo" are in the clear part and uncertain: could be numerals or letters. Clear "que" occurs inside cipher on 154r line 18; other clear-looking forms inside cipher ("fu", "hu", "te", "to", "ci", "co", "lo", "ru", "ro", "ra", "Se", "dn") are written as letters/tokens and may be cipher signs. "800<bar>" in 155r lines 1 and 7 is a plain numeral with a bar.
