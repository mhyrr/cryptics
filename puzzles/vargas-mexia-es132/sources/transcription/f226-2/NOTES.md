# f226-2 reader notes (blind, guide v2.2)

## Extent
- The letter begins on f. 226r (heading `El Rey`, then 5 clear lines: salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia ..." through "... muchas gracias,"), then 23 cipher lines on f. 226r.
- It continues on f. 226v with 8 full cipher lines plus a 9th line: a short cipher tail (`6.- 23+ 86 6p /`) followed on the same line by the clear date "De San lorenço a xiij.º de Septiembre 1579" (overbar above 1579).
- Below: the King's autograph "Yo el Rey" (read as such; large flourished signature with a long tail) and the secretary's countersignature, which I read as **"Don Iuº de Idiaquez"** (Don Ju(an) de Idiaquez; the end of the surname runs into a flourish). Bottom-left of f. 226v: "Dup.da" (duplicada).
- f. 227r: no text of the letter. Only faint mirrored show-through of the f. 227v address and docket, plus the folio number 227.
- f. 227v (address leaf, written along the long axis): a small cross, "Por el Rey", "Juº de Vargas Mexia" (the initial J has a big flourish; there may be a lost "A"), a small "2.ª" below, "Dup.da" at bottom left, and a docket: "Sant Lorenzo / de su Md. de 14 de sept.re / En Pausi a 24 del mismo" (I read "Pausi a"; "Paris" is the expected word). Note that the docket says **14** Sept while the date line reads xiij (x, two minims, tailed y/j with a small superscript). Possibly xiiij; I did not resolve it.
- Cipher line counts: f. 226r 23, f. 226v 8 (+ the tail line 9).

## Passes
- Pass 1: forward, f. 226r then f. 226v, left half then right half of each strip row, top to bottom. 2-3 manuscript lines per strip, halves 1700 px wide at native resolution, overlap about 200 px (not 300). The first two attempts used a row pitch that was slightly too small (the pitch is about 112-118 px) and had to be recut; those views are not counted below. Final grid: f. 226r 8 rows x 2 halves = 16 strips; f. 226v 4 rows x 2 halves = 8 strips. Total 24 strips in the counted pass. In addition about 10 smaller detail crops (date, signatures, address leaf, docket, a few doubtful signs).
- Pass 2: the same 24 strips re-viewed in the opposite direction (f. 226v then f. 226r, bottom to top, right half before left half, each line re-read right to left against the draft). Corrections made in pass 2: added `<bar>` on 19 (f. 226v line 3) and on 25p (f. 226r line 14), `<2dot>` over the 7 in f. 226r line 16 (dots over both 7 and 23p), acute over the 21 and `x u L` in line 6, `<v>?` on 20+ in line 18, `<bar>?` on several signs, and several sign descriptions. The page is fully done in both passes. Fewer strips than the 25-40 suggested because the rows are 3 lines; the second pass is complete.

## Conventions I had to choose (shape only)
- The hook mark: an e/ρ-shaped loop after a number, with or without a visible descender, is written `p` throughout (7p, 15p, 20p, 24p ...). A cross after a number is `+`. A 6-shaped curl touching the number is joined (`86`, `246`, `176` ...).
- Lone tall `S` and the `S`/5-shaped glyph in `6 S`: in 15, 25, 35 the 5 itself is S-shaped, so `6 S` may be 6 + 5 (two numbers). I wrote `S` for a separate large flat-topped s (f. 226r line 12 after 11<cross>, and the `6 S`, `6 S.`, `6 S<bar>` clusters), and `5`/`5+` where it follows the digit shape. Unresolved.
- Clear syllables inside the cipher lines are written in `[[ ]]` (quam, ca, qua, Sal, pam, pal, tim, fim, fi, rom, rum, tam, Sem, humor, de, fal, fom, yum, lum, hum). They look like spelled-out letters/syllables, and some start with a z-shaped r. Some may instead be letter-form signs; I did not decide.
- A short horizontal stroke after a dot at mid-height (`6.-`, `35.-`, `11.-`, `33.-`) is written `<bar>?` on that sign; it may be a different mark.
- `y` (tailed) is written `y`.
