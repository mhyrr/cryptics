## Conventions I had to choose (the guide gives none for these)
- q-shaped signs (a loop or open-top form with a short stem, often with a hat/acute/bar above) are written `4`. The
  tailed, curled `g` form (as in `869`, `934`) is written `9`. The 4/9 call is the weakest in the transcription: the
  q-form with a long descender and a closed loop could be 9 in places. Pass 1 wrote many as 9; pass 2 standardised to 4
  after seeing the same shape in the clear f. 276v copy (`4<hat> 24 6 4<hat> d.`). This is a global caveat on every
  `4`, `4<hat>`, `4<acute>`, `4<bar>`.
- `d` = the eth-shaped sign (a d with a flat open top-left stroke). `e` = an e-shaped loop without a descender. `p`
  = loop with a descender. `y` tailed. `c`, `o`, `u`, `S i m / S o m / S u m / S e m` are letter-like forms written as seen.
  `l` (as in `6 e l`) = a tall loop.
- The final slanted thick stroke of a paragraph is written `/`.

## Passes
- Pass 1 (left to right, top to bottom): f. 275r in 20 native strips (a00-a09, left and right halves, 1500 px wide, 300 px
  overlap, 330-400 px high, 2-3 lines each; a09 was blank page below the text), plus 4 native crops of f. 276v (cipher
  block and clear lines, rotated for reading), 3 native crops of f. 275v and 1 of f. 276r (show-through checks).
  Strips viewed in pass 1: 28 (20 + 4 + 3 + 1).
- Pass 2 (opposite direction: bottom to top, right half before left half, reading tokens right to left): f. 275r re-cut
  with shifted windows centred on measured line positions (3 lines each): 9 strips x 2 halves = 18 strips, plus the head
  of the page in 2 strips (cross mark, folio number) and 2 close crops of the cramped line (n1, n2); f. 276v re-viewed
  in 2 crops; f. 275v 1 crop. Strips viewed in pass 2: 25. The second pass was completed for every cipher line.
- Honest limit: the hand is a fast, joined cursive and a 3-line native strip is the finest view I took. Digit/letter
  calls inside joined runs (2 vs z-shaped 2 vs 7, 4 vs 9, 1 1 vs H, 6 vs curl) were frequently close, so many tokens are
  best readings, not certain ones. The first-pass lines were re-read against new crops; the second pass overrides.
- What changed in pass 2 (main items): all q-shapes unified to 4; the heading line 1 `24 6 4<hat> d.` (pass 1 had an
  extra 9: `24 9 6 4<hat>`); several `6 p` / `6 e` hook calls; `35+<under>` underline found on line 24;
  possible underline under `22.` on line 8; the paragraph-final `/` signs placed; `bel` read as `6 e l`; the
  paragraph break set after `6 e l /` (line 14); line 14's cross above `11` and line 15's cross above `11` added; line 6 gained a stray
  curved stroke at the end; line 16 (`...22+ 80 y ...`) found to be a cramped, small-written line squeezed over a fold.
