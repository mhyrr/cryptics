## Boundary decisions (by the hand)
- "xe" (157r l17, 158v l27): clear. The e is the scribe's cursive e (no cipher letter e exists elsewhere), x is the same p/x-form as in "para", "pero" (long left tail), size and baseline are the clear line's, and at l17 it ends with "/." exactly as clear clauses end ("mias /."). Weak: could be cipher; flagged.
- 157r l12 tail "P 6 1 0 t": cipher. After "segun" (clear, final n with flourish) the ink changes to upright, bold, evenly spaced signs of cipher size; the stem-foot P matches `P_`/`P<hat>`; 6 / plain 1 / 0 / t-with-cross are cipher shapes.
- "por q" 157r l22: clear (cursive p with long tail, o r, loop q as in l14). Similarly "por q" and "xe por q" 158v l27.
- "por" 159r l11 (over/underlined, arc above): kept clear `[[por]]` (letters are clear ductus); the over/underline and arc are recorded here only. Unresolved whether it is a cipher marker.
- "no" 157r l19: clear (same ductus as l16 "no"). 158v l18 "ho/no": cipher `ho?` (heavy initial stroke).
- "para" and "te" 158v l21: both clear (cursive p-form; te small raised with cursive e). "en los de ay" 157r l19: clear. "con" 158v l18 end and "se" l19: clear. "se ha con-" 157r l10: clear.
- 157r l2-3 numerals "2. 7. 13. 14." with overbars: kept clear (like "22 y 23"); A had them as cipher. A virgula after "8bre" present in the ink but in neither reading, added.
- 158v "d" before P<hat> (l25): clear d.
- 158v l28: ink reads "y fueron auemarias q rezaron contra si" (a-u-e-m-a-r-i-a-s; neither "quemadas" nor "auemados"); the "q" is the loop-q; clear through "si", then cipher `22. 20`.
- 159r l2-3: "[[y dize q toda]] / [[via procure V.m. de]]": clear; "procure" third letter after r reads like a (proaure) but kept "procure"; low confidence.
