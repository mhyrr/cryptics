Extra conventions: `<y7>` = flat-topped 7-shaped y (reader A's convention), `y` = tailed cipher y, `S<hat>` = curly S, `5` = flat-topped s-like glyph, `f` = looped f with crossbar, `<comma>` = short slanted comma-like stroke, `<acute>` = tick above, `<underline>` = short bar below, `ra`/`hu`/`vo` = letter pairs written at cipher scale (not in [[ ]]).

## How "ra" and "hu" were judged
Measured on the crops: the x-height of "ra" (and "hu") is about 22-25 px, the same as the adjacent digits and as `vo`, while clear-script letters in the same lines (adonde, quieza, pero) are about 40-50 px; pen weight and the closed-bowl a match the cipher letter signs (a., a_, a+). "ra" is therefore written as a cipher sign `ra` (not [[ra]]); `hu` likewise. "Ha" on f. 198v (l. 23) is different: capital H about 1.5x digit height with a left flourish, taken as clear script [[Ha]]. A 7-shaped y at clear-script size next to clear words (after "desengaño", after "y por el", after "permaneciesse") is taken as clear [[y]]; 7-shaped y inside digit runs stays `<y7>`.
