## Systematic rulings

**(a) The "ɪb" sign (A2 `16`, B2 `2 6`): ruled `16`.**
Evidence: the sign begins with a short hooked upright (flag at top left, foot sweeping right into the loop). At 3-4x the same stroke is the "1" of `10` (line 2, after `/`; crop zz2x), of `15p` (line 1, zax) and of `14+`. A true 2 in this hand (`20p` line 1, `24+`, `12+`) opens with a long flat top stroke and ends in a flat bottom bar, which the sign does not have. So it is not a 2. The loop after it is a 6, so the sign is the number 16 (1 + 6), written joined as `16`. Applied to lines 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 15, 16, 20 and 105v v1, v2. Where a second small loop follows (`16 6`, lines 3, 8, 20) both readers split it as its own token; the curl rule would give `166?`; I kept the readers' split (see Remaining uncertainty).

**(b) The flat-topped glyph before p / + (B2 `7`, drafts `27`): ruled `7`.**
Evidence: one stroke, not two: a flat or slightly rising top bar, then one diagonal descending left with a foot that drops below the line (and below the + bar). No second 2-shaped component. The 2 in `12+` (lines 2, 7, 10, 11, checked at zoom: e1x, e2x, e4x) has a round top and stops at the bar, so the two are distinguishable. `17p` on 105v v2 and `7+` on line 1 (zfx) show the same glyph. Consequence: `7p`, `7+`, `7.` stay `7`; and two signs read `12+` change to `17+` (line 8, 105v v2 end) because their foot drops below the bar.

**(c) A closed oval with a hole and no rising tail is `o`**, not a 6-curl: after S (line 3), after 6 (lines 8, 14). A 6-curl has a tail. On line 11 the oval touches the 2 exactly as in `20p`, so it is `20`.

**(d) Floating marks above (v, hat, bar) after a dot.** Attach to the sign whose body the mark overlaps. A mark in the gap right after a dot goes to the following base only when its right arm overlaps that base (`24+<v>`, `15.<v>`, `256<v>`, `7.<v>`); otherwise it stays with the dot's sign (`23.<v>` line 6, `m.<v>`).

**(e) Bar over a dot** (a short bar floating above the dot-after: 1., 16., 6.): written `X.<bar>`. The dot is real; the bar belongs to the sign. Lines 2, 7, 10, 16 and 105v v2.

**(f) T-bar vs plus.** A horizontal stroke with the vertical at its right end is `<tbar>` (23<tbar>, 7<tbar>, S<tbar>, 17<tbar>). A vertical crossing the bar is `+`.
