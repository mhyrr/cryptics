## Passes
Pass 1 was left to right on native strips for every line. Pass 2 (right to left against the strips) was done in full only
on the places listed below; on the remaining lines it was a second look at both halves, not an independent re-reading,
so the pass-2 claim is weaker than the guide asks. Changes made in pass 2 / on zoom:
- 17r line 1 (`11+e`, `2 v 6`): zoomed; the cross belongs to the second upright of the 11 and the hook follows it, so I wrote `11+e` everywhere that shape occurs.
- 17r `hazer 11+e ... 2e? 17e`: zoom showed a blotted 2 followed by a hook; written `2e?`.
- 17v lines 11 (`22<bar> 17 8<bar>? 17 10 5^ 10 <o+> 6 8<bar> 1? 8.`): re-zoomed twice; the "1 7 8" cluster under one bar stays ambiguous.
- 20r lines 11-13 and the 13 + 14 merge: re-aligned using the overlap between left and right halves; I had first read one long line as two.
- 19v (last two lines) and 18v (line 6 cut at the fold band): kept as seen.

## Conventions I had to add (not in the guide)
- Two marks after one base (the guide allows one): `11+e` (cross then hook; very frequent, about 60 times), `4+_` (17r, hazer line), `5+ ...` etc. `+e` always means cross followed by hook on the same base.
- `<o+>`: a small circle (o) with a cross, written like `0+` in the images; it is **not** a 10 (10 is written 1 + o and occurs separately, sometimes right next to it: 17v line 11 `10 <o+>`). 22 occurrences. Never preceded by a 1 except where noted.
- `<ink-blot>` / `<sign:ink-blot>`: a real blot or smear in the ink that hides or replaces a sign (18r, 19r, 20r, 20v); `<bleed>` / `<bleed-...>`: signs I could not read because the show-through (mirrored writing from the other side of the leaf) overlays them. These are not signs of the cipher.
- `<rho>` (70 times) was used for every large loop-and-tail form, including several that look like a `g` with a long curled tail (these are the same shape family; a few might be `9` without a mark, see "hardest places"). The small hook is `e` as in the guide.
- `<sign:o+>`, `<sign:y-like>` , `<sign:l-like-with-loop-above>` etc. are one-off descriptive signs.
- `pe`, `Pe`, `P+`, `Ce`, `fe`, `f+`, `h+`, `h+e`, `me`, `m+`, `m<bar>`, `n`, `n+`, `n_`, `R`, `C`, `C^`: letter-form bases with marks (see list below).
- `|` was not needed; `[[ ]]` clear text with `q` for the abbreviation that is drawn like a numeral 6 in clear Spanish (e.g. 17r "lo q mas", 18v "q los"); a cipher `6` and this clear-text `q` are the same stroke, so a lone 6 inside clear text was written `q`, and in the heading of 19r "de 6 del passado" is a true numeral 6 (the 6th of the month) and is written 6.
- `<bar>` after a base always means a bar above. Where a bar lay between two lines (equally close) I wrote `<bar>?` or attached it to the lower sign and said so below.
- Clear text spelling: diplomatic as far as I can read it; abbreviations not expanded (`chmo`, `tpo?`, `herº` written `her^no` for the small "no" above). Uncertain clear words carry `?`.

## Hardest places
1. **`2 v`**: the 2 is written with a long swash (a ligature) that looks like a `v` and joins the next sign. I wrote `2 v 6`, `2 v 10`, `2 v 14`, `2 v 2` where the swash ends in a visible bend; it could instead be a long-tailed `2` alone. About 14 occurrences (17r 1, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16 area; 17v 12, 20; 18v 16; 19r 7, 21; 19v 13; 20v 6).
2. **`<o+>` vs `10` vs `0+`**: see above; the line-start forms in 17r (hazer line, 13, 14) have no 1 before them.
3. **`<rho>` vs `9e` vs `3e`**: tall loop forms with a curl at the bottom: if the key distinguishes them it matters. A few `g`-shaped signs on 18v/19v/20r with a closed bowl might be `9` (no hook) rather than `<rho>`.
4. **`11+e`**: always cross + hook; a reader who sees 11 with `+` and `e` as separate would count differently.
5. **Show-through**: 18r (left halves of lines 3-8, 15-21), 19v (everywhere), 20v (lower half) and the first lines of 19r; I used `<bleed>` instead of guessing in the worst places.
6. **Dots**: between two lines a dot can belong to either; I decided by distance, wrote `?` only when it was close to 50/50.
7. **Fold band**: on 17v, 18v, 19v, 20r lines end against a dark band (page edge / paper stub), so a last sign can be cut or hidden (I wrote `?` after the last sign where it looked cut).
8. The bar over `15` / `18` / `22` etc.: some bars look like they belong to the sign below, others to the one above; I used `<bar>` for the nearer sign.

Several lines look like repeats of earlier cipher stretches (e.g. the string `9+ 20 18 19 11 16+ 12+ 6 18 f 20 18 18 6+ 11+e` opens 17r line 1, 18r line 17, 19v line 10 and 20v line 1; `5+ 20 18 ... 9e 20 2 5+ 18 6 13 19+ 13 5 5^` recurs on 17v line 16, 18r line 20, 20v line 1).
