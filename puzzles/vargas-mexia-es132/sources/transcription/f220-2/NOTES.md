# Notes: BnF es. 132, ff. 220-221 (13 Sep 1579), blind transcription

Reader: blind transcriber, guide CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.2 only. No key, no other transcription, no network.

## Extent
- The letter starts at the top of f. 220r ("El Rey" heading, salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", five lines of clear
  Spanish, then cipher) and ends on f. 221r (6 cipher lines, place and date line, "Yo el Rey", countersignature).
- f. 220r: heading, 5 clear lines, 24 cipher lines (A-X). Cipher paragraphs end with `/` at lines I, N, S, U, X.
- f. 220v: 30 cipher lines, no clear text. Virgulas close lines 5 and 30 (end of cipher on this page).
- f. 221r: 6 cipher lines, then "St. Lorenço a xuj. de septie 1579." with a bar over the year, "Yo el Rey" (autograph, flourished),
  and the countersignature lower right, read as "Don Antonio de Eraso" (large flourishes; the name is legible as
  "Don Anton[io] de Erag/Eraso"; the last letters and the closing flourish are partly guesswork).
- f. 221v: address leaf, written turned 90 degrees (read by turning the image counter-clockwise): "Por + El Rey" (cross over the
  L of "El"), "Juan de Vargas Mexia", with a small mark below ". l ." and a squiggle above it. Docket in a second, small hand
  at the right edge, three lines: "Sanct Lorenço" (faint), "De su Magd de 13 de septie Rdo.? Jaº ?" (tail
  uncertain), "Paris a los 24 del". A wax seal blot and a few faint marks near it (not read). Faint show-through of the
  f. 221r countersignature at the lower left. Not cipher.
- The date line reads "xuj" as written (the docket says 13). Not resolved.

## Conventions I had to choose (the guide is silent)
- Two shapes look like "q": a straight-descender form (written `4`: it behaves as a 4 in context, e.g. `24.`, `4<bar>`, and
  starts lines with a bar above) and a curved-tail form (written `9`). I settled on `4` for the straight form. Some
  lines may mix them.
- `pe`: a letter-form `p` (long descender) followed by an e-shaped loop. Unresolved whether the loop is a hook mark or a
  letter; written as one token `pe`. A bare `p` or `p+` is the same descender shape with no e-loop.
- `p` after a number (`24p`, `7p`) is the guide's hook mark. A hook-like glyph after a letter-form also written `p`.
- `S` followed by `u`/`i`/`o`/`e` and `m`: written as separate letter tokens (`S u m`, `S i m`, `S o m`, `S e m`). The S
  has a descending tail. `b e l` and `c i L`, `i L` are written as letters too. I could not tell digit `1` from letter `i` in
  `Sim`/`cil`.
- A bar-and-curl `H`-like glyph is read as a 4 (`14`, `34`).
- The sign "s" in `1s+` is read as 5 (`15+`, `25+`).
- Marks above: hat, v, bar, two dots are as the guide. Where the same sign shows two marks I wrote them in sequence.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom, native-resolution strips 1500 x 330 px (2-3 lines), left and right halves overlapping
  by about 200 px (not 300). Number of strips viewed in pass 1: f. 220r 26 (4 clear-text, 2 for the first cipher rows, 20 cipher),
  f. 220v 26, f. 221r 8, f. 221v 4 (rotated crops) = **64**, plus about 8 auxiliary crops (margin, bottom-of-page, one local re-crop of a
  line with many small marks, signatures).
- Pass 2: reverse order (f. 221r first, then f. 220v bottom to top, then f. 220r bottom to top; each strip right half then left half;
  every line corrected against the strip). Strips viewed in pass 2: f. 220r 23 (19 cipher, 4 clear; the right half of the last strip, f220r_10R,
  was only a blank margin and not re-viewed), f. 220v 26 (+1 local re-crop), f. 221r 8, f. 221v 1 (address) = **58**. The f. 221v docket
  crops and the two signature crops on f. 221r were viewed once only, not twice. So: pass 2 is complete for all cipher lines and
  the clear text of f. 220r; it is not complete for the f. 221v docket and signatures.
- Changes in pass 2 (many): added marks above that I had dropped (hats, v, bars), corrected `18+`/`28` to `15+`/`25` where the long "s" was a 5,
  turned `q` shapes into `4`, split or joined some digit groups, found two `<under>` that were really `34`/`35` readings, joined
  `7p`/`2p` variants, read `Sim` for what was first `Sum` in some places.

## Image problems
- f. 220v: right edge is a dark gutter shadow; the last sign or two of many lines are dim or cut. Marked with `?` or `<sign:...>` where I noticed.
- f. 220r: mirrored show-through of f. 220v marks along the right margin (small `o`, `it`, `/`, `+` shapes beyond the line ends). Not transcribed.
- f. 221r: show-through marks at the right edge; the facing page 220v is visible at the gutter. f. 221v: seal blot and rotated text.
- Several lines have flourishes from the line above that touch the next line's signs (descenders of `y`, `p`, `S`); I treated these as flourishes unless a
  clear mark.
- Row density: the lines on f. 220r are about 100 px apart, on f. 220v about 120-160 px; the 2-3 line strips cut some lines at the top or bottom, which I
  compensated for with the overlapping rows.

## Honest limits
This is a hard hand; a number of signs are probably wrong, especially `2`/`7`/`1` look-alikes, `p` versus `pe`, `q` versus `9`, and the digit
runs. Do not take any single token as certain.
