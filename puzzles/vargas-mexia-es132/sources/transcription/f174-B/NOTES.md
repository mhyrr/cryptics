# NOTES: f. 174 (+175) blind transcription, reader B

Guide: CIPHER3-GUIDE.md v2.3. Images: `sources/cache/f174-blind/f174_s1 ... s7` in page order (s5 carries the header 175, so s1-s4 are f. 174 and s5-s7 are f. 175). Line numbers below are per image, counting every manuscript line of that image (clear Spanish lines included), as in `transcription.txt`.

## Confidence, stated plainly
This is a low-to-medium confidence reading at the digit level. The hand is small and joined, the screenshots are around 1500 px wide, and I did not upscale. Several recurring shapes are read by shape only and could be wrong in the same way everywhere: the z-shaped 2 against 3 and 13; `q` (letter) against a 4 or 9; `d` (written like a closed `d`/`∂`); the hook glyph written `p` (the guide's hook mark; in a few places it stands alone with no base, e.g. `p<v>`, `p+`, `d p`); the `h`/`7`/`14` family (written `h`, `7p`, `14+` by how it looked, never decided with certainty); a `10` with a mark above that could be `6` plus a circle. Treat the `?` list and the 3+ digit list below as the places to test first.

## Image problems
- Bleed-through (show-through from the verso) appears as grey cipher between lines, strongest in s1 (between the clear lines), and as a grey `300`-like group at the right end of s7 line 7 (not transcribed). Everything light grey was ignored.
- Lines slope upward to the right. My first fixed grid cut some right halves at the top: s7 grid strips 03-05 were abandoned and re-cropped from the line positions (2 views wasted); s4 line 1 and line 4 right halves and s3 line 10 right half were re-cropped. Strips are therefore not a uniform grid and a few contain 3 lines plus a sliver of the next.
- Not transcribed: the signature below the date on s7; a rotated word in the margin at the lower right of s4; the folio numbers 174, 175; the `el Rey` header was transcribed as clear text (a cross-shaped mark sits above its middle).
- Line 1 of s6 begins at the very top edge; line 1 of s4 is partly clipped at the top and read from the recrop.
- Right edge: lines that run into the page edge or the dark border are marked `<cut>`; I could not tell a true continuation from a clipped end in every case.

## Passes (guide section 6)
- Pass 1: images s7 down to s1 (reverse order), strips of 2 to 3 lines, left half then right half, left to right, at native resolution. 84 images viewed (including 8 whole-image views and about 8 recrop or abandoned-strip views).
- Pass 2 (done in full): images s1 to s7 (opposite direction), each line checked from its right end back to its left end. To stay near the image budget the second pass used the same crops with the right half stacked above the left half in one image per strip (35 views). I did not view the halves separately in pass 2.
- Changes made in pass 2 (the only ones): s4 line 3 end `d.<acute> 24` (was without acute); s6 line 1 `p+<v>` (v mark added); s6 line 17 end `7p<hat>` (was `h<hat>`); s6 line 7 `286<dot>?` (a dot above the 2 added). Everything else was confirmed on re-reading.
- Harmonised, not seen differently: s7 line 1 `20 14q` (first pass `20 12q?`), because the same group `20 14 q d.` recurs on s2, s3 and s6; this is a judgement from repetition, not a new reading of the pixels.
- Images viewed: pass 1 about 84, pass 2 35, total about 119.

- `<2dot>?` is used where small dot-like or small `2`-like marks sit above a sign; they may be bleed-through or small `2`s.
