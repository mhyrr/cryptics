# f215-A notes (blind reader, forward order; images exp07)

## Extent
Letter begins at top of f. 215r ("El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", then cipher in 10 lines, then 15 lines of clear Spanish) and ends on f. 215v (3 clear lines, date "Pardo A cuj de Julio 1579", the King's signature, a countersignature). f. 216r is blank (show-through, specks; ~"de au / y del" at left margin is the facing 215v). f. 216v is the address leaf: "Por el Rey" and "Juan de Vargas Mexia," written sideways, and a later-looking docket "13. de julio de 1579" on the right edge. Docket and address are not part of the cipher.
Cipher lines: 10 (all on f. 215r, as one block directly under the salutation; line 1 shares its line with the name).

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right on 1100x260 native strips (3 columns per band). Pass 2 (right-to-left) was a re-scan of the same strips from line ends; it changed little: line ends and the join of strips at x~1650 and x~2600 were re-aligned (overlap duplicates removed), and `<bar>`/`<hat>` marks were re-checked. Weak pass: line 1-3 read at lower confidence; the cipher hand is cramped and my readings of 2/4/6/e/o forms are uncertain.
