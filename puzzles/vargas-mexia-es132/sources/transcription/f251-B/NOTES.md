# f251-B notes (blind transcription, guide v2.2)

Line numbers below count every non-comment line of transcription.txt on that page (clear lines included).
On f. 251r: l.1 = El Rey, l.2-10 = salutation and clear text, l.11-18 = cipher paragraph 1 (A1-A8), l.19-26 = paragraph 2 (B1-B8).

## Extent
- The letter starts at the top of f. 251r (cross, "El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", nine clear lines ending "respuesta /.") and ends on f. 252r with the date line ("De Madrid a xvj de En", "1580."), "Yo el Rey", and the secretary's countersignature. Cipher: 16 lines on 251r, 28 on 251v, 15 on 252r (59 cipher lines; the last one runs into the clear date line).
- f. 251r: clear text 9 lines + 2 cipher paragraphs. Modern foliation "251" top right. The ~400 px at the left edge is the facing page (gutter), not transcribed.
- f. 251v: cipher only (28 lines, 5 paragraphs). Mirror-image show-through of f. 251r (the "El Rey" heading, "Juan de Vargas Mexia", the cipher) is visible throughout, strongest at the top and right.
- f. 252r: 15 cipher lines (the 15th ends in the clear date), then 1580., then the King's signature "Yo el Rey" and below it the countersignature. Modern foliation "252" top right. Left margin shows the facing page edge.
- f. 252v: address leaf, turned 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" (with a cross above "el") and "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", and a docket in another hand: "De su Mt de 16 de En. Rda / en Paris a 4 de Hebrero / respondida a" (the "Rda" with raised "da"; probably "Recibida"; the line after "respondida a" is not continued). A paper seal remnant (grey, with a hole) sits at the left of the docket. Faint mirror show-through of the countersignature at the bottom. Not cipher.
- Possible catchword: the last sign of f. 251r (`7p<hat>`) is the same as the first sign of f. 251v (`7p<hat>`). Noted, not removed.

## Countersignature
Read as "Don Iuan de Idiaquez" (cursive, the first name a looped I/J with "uã"; "de"; the surname begins with a tall I, then "d i a q", long bold descender, ending in a loop with a flourish). The surname is the weakest part: "Idiaq-" is clear by shape, the ending "-uez" is a flourish.
King's signature: "Yo el Rey" (large, looped).

## Method note
Marks above that I could not tell apart (a bar, a tilde, a small 2) were written as `<bar>`, `<v>` or `<2-above>`. Arrangement of hat/v above 7+/6+ near the right edge is the least reliable part.
