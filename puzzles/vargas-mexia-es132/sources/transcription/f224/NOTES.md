# NOTES: blind transcription of ff. 224r-225r (letter of 13 Sep 1579), guide v2.2

## Extent
- Letter begins at the top of f. 224r (heading "el Rey" with a cross above the "l", folio number 224 top right), salutation
  "Juan de Vargas. Mexia." and four clear lines, then cipher.
- Cipher: f. 224r 24 lines (five paragraphs ending in `/`: 9+5+5+2+3), f. 224v 30 lines (the first four continue the last
  paragraph of 224r; `21. 29 /` is a short closing line, then 25 more lines), f. 225r 4 lines. Total 58 cipher lines.
- The letter ends on f. 225r: the last cipher line ends `... 22. /` followed by clear "de St Lorenço" on the same
  manuscript line, then the date line "a xiij de septie 1579", then the King's flourished signature and, lower right,
  the secretary's countersignature with rubric. A vertical marginal word between the two signatures, heavy ink, rotated 90 deg,
  possibly "Vargas" (shape only; low confidence).
- f. 225v: address leaf, no cipher. Rotated text "Por el Rey" (cross above "el", long ruled flourish) and "Juan de Vargas
  Mexia"; a docket upside down along the right edge "13 de setiembre de 1579". Transcribed in `[[ ]]` under its own header.
- "Dup.da" (Duplicada) in a different hand, lower left of ff. 224v, 225r and 225v. Not transcribed as letter text.

## Countersignature as read
"Don Juan de Idiaquez" (Don Iuan de Idiaquez), with a long looped rubric. The King's signature is a heavy flourish; I give
`Yo el Rey?` from the overall shape only (large "Y"-like loop at left, then looped scrawl); not legible letter by letter.
Clear-text oddities: the numeral in "xxuj." (the date of the letters answered) is my reading of x x u j with a small mark above; it might be xxvj or xxiij.
"de St Lorenço": the "t" is above the S; the last letters are cramped.

## Passes
1. Left to right, at native resolution, in strips of 3-5 lines. The page is wider than one view, so every line was read in two
   halves (left crop x 500-2000, right crop x 1850-3500 on rectos; x 600-2100 and 1950-3600 on versos). The right crop
   was offset up (lines rise to the right by 60-150 px), and the two halves were joined on the overlap words.
2. Second pass, right to left: fresh crops centred on the join (x 1500-3000) for every strip of 224r (5 strips), 224v (strips 1-5, down to
   about line 19 of the page) and the three lines of 225r seen in the first pass; each line re-read from its right end
   to the join and the join words compared with the first pass. Changes: `d p` -> `d e` (line 3 of 224r); `<hat>` added over e in
   "b e d" and "d e d" (lines 1-2); `<bar>` added over the d before "6 246 ... S i m" (line 9); the end of paragraph 3
   `13 5+` -> `13 5e`. The last lines of 224v (about 20-30) were not re-cropped in a second pass; they got only the first-pass
   reading plus a second look at the right-hand crops.

## Honest limits of this reading
This hand is dense, small and ligatured, and many signs are shapes I could not assign with certainty. Expect
errors in: digit 4 vs 9 vs `q` (a tailed stem-and-bowl form, often with a hat or bar above; I wrote `q` for the form with a
descender and `9`/`4` only when the top was open or closed unmistakably; many are guesses), 1 vs 7 in `17`/`12`, 5 vs 3 in `35`,
`6` joined or free (I wrote the curl joined wherever it touched the preceding number, e.g. `86`, `236`, `246`), `e` as a separate
base (I wrote a letter `e` where the hook-and-eye shape follows a number, e.g. `7e`, `15e`, `23e`) and the letter forms
`b`, `d`, `p`, `y`, `Z`, `S`, `f`, `g`, `l`, `i`, `m`, `n`, `a`, `u`. `+` after a base was read where a cross at mid height touches the number; it
is sometimes hard to tell from the stroke of a 4.

## Image problems
- 224r: show-through of the verso (ghost text) at the top and in the margins; a mark `I` descender and flourish overlays between lines;
  a dark smudge crossing lines 6-7 near x 2400 (right half); no cut edges.
- 224v: black gutter bar on the right edge cuts the last 1-2 signs of many lines (lines run into it); the right end of the lines is
  sometimes lost: the last token may be incomplete (e.g. `d6`, `2?`, `24`, `5?`). Ink blots and bleed in the lower half (lines 9-12 of the second group), esp.
  around `24+<bar>?` in the line starting `28 7e<hat>`; a heavy blot and cancellation-like X in the line with `b 6p`.
- 225r: show-through at the top; a short vertical scratch/fold line at the right of the signature area.
- 225v: show-through of the cipher on the verso, ink blots near the right edge, a gutter shadow along the right edge.
