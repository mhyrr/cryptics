## Conventions I applied (shapes only)
- Looped, descending q/g shape: `9`. Small q with no loop, mostly after a curl or after 2 and before 22: `4`. This is a shape decision, not a value claim, and is the least certain of my conventions.
- "1H" (1 followed by an H-shaped sign with crossbar): `14`, per guide section 9.
- Hook ("e", "pe", "ρ" with a tail after a base): `p`. A large free-standing p with a serif, not attached to a number: `p` (alone) or `p+`.
- "d"-shaped letter with a loop (looks like ∂): `d`; "∂e": `dp`.
- "y" tailed letter form: `y`. Letter groups: `Rom`, `Rim`, `Rum`, `bel`, `gil`, `cil`, `qise`. "Gz": written as a letter group `Gz` (capital-G-like sign plus z). One of these `Gz` signs (s3 line 16) was first read `6 2`; pass 2 changed it to `Gz` because it matches s1.
- Digit after 1 that is z-shaped: read as `12`; flat-topped or 7-like: `17`. The two are hard to separate in this hand (`12`/`17` and `12`/`13` are the commonest confusions; "v3" read `12`). I did not mark each one with `?`.
- "2s" with a flat-topped s: `25`, but several `28`/`25` forms are close; not individually marked.
- Marks above attached to the sign they sit nearest to. Where an acute sits between two signs (`4' 26`, `q' 18`) it is marked `?`.
- A 6-shaped curl with its own hook, dot or plus is its own token (`6p`, `6.`, `6+`); a bare touching curl is joined.
- Bar vs tilde above: all flat or wavy horizontal strokes written `<bar>`; wavy ones at s1 line 7 (`10<bar>`) and s2 last line (`18.<bar>`) are the least certain.
- Clear Spanish in `[[ ]]` is in expanded or diplomatic spelling lightly; "vra" = vuestra, "dize." = abbreviation; dates read: xxiiij, xxvij, xxix, xxx, vij, ix, xxiij, xx, xxj for s1, and "xxvij" for the date line, all uncertain.
