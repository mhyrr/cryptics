# f245 notes (blind transcription, guide v2.2)

## Extent
- The letter (29 Nov 1579, "Del Pardo") runs f. 245r to 245v. It starts at the head of 245r ("El Rey", salutation to Juan de Vargas Mexia), has 8 clear lines, then two cipher paragraphs (9 lines, then 7 lines; the second begins with a long flourish) that end with a short line `6. 15p quam 1<cross> Jims 1.`
- Page order of the content: 245r clear opening + cipher; 245v clear text (7 lines, ends with the place and date line "Del Pardo a xxix de noviembre 1579"), then the King's signature and the secretary's countersignature. So the clear text on 245v is the end of the letter, written after the cipher paragraphs on 245r. 245v has no cipher of its own: everything dark below the signature is mirrored show-through of the 245r cipher.
- 245r clear line 1 ends "ervij / vlti-" and line 2 begins "mo de octubre", and the 245v lines are cut at the right by the page edge ("han Ed", "y cu" / "ros", "lo qu", "Portugues", "m", "q"): the line ends are lost or run into the edge.
- 246r: no writing of its own (faint mirrored show-through of the 246v address). Not transcribed.
- 246v: address leaf, rotated 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" with a cross, "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", a seal remnant, and a rotated docket at right (transcribed in [[ ]] with readings uncertain: "De Su Md de 29 de Noue.. / R.da? Enparis a 17 de Dize? / con cifra / Respon..", with "Pardo" above).

## Signatures
- King: ornate scrawl read as "Yo el Rey" (Y with loop, large rubric below). Read from native strips, shape only.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Juº de Idiáq" (Don / Ju with raised o / de / Idiáq...), long rubric with an oval flourish crossing the first line of the second cipher paragraph. I read it as Idiáquez (Juan de Idiáquez).

## Conventions I had to choose (not in the guide)
- An open e-shaped loop after a number (`24e`, `15e`, `20e`) is written as the hook `p`. This is the most common mark; if it is a different mark, every `p` here must be revisited.
- Clear-looking cipher words "quam", "Jims" are written as letters, "Sp 30." for the sego-like group (S with hook, then 30), "d p m" for the "dem"-like group, "T 1<dot> L" for the "til"-like group. All uncertain.
- `15` is read where a long-s-like 5 follows 1 (`15+`, `15p`).
- Isolated stroke before `y` on line 2 (a small curl) not written; `y?`.
- Trailing ticks at the right margin ("=", "-", "'") ignored; a lone `+` at the right margin of line 8 written as a sign.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom, native-resolution strips (1500 px wide, ~3 lines tall; left half x 450-600 and right half x 1900). Strips viewed in pass 1: 245r clear head 8, 245r cipher 12; 245v 6 (clear text) + 2 (signatures); 246v 4 (address and docket crops); 246r 0 strips (viewed at 40% overview only). Total 32. Plus 8 reduced overview crops (clear text on overviews only to locate lines). A few 2x enlargements (3) were used to diagnose individual glyphs.
- Pass 2: right to left, bottom to top, 14 re-cut strips of the 245r cipher with different line offsets (band starts y=1850, 2220, 2590, 2900, 3280, 3620, 3900, two halves each). Corrections made: line 2 of para 2 `22` with a small 2 above; line 3 `6<bar><cross>`; `15p<dot>` on line 6; `u.` after `1 6.`; several hats and bars confirmed.
- NOT second-passed: the clear text on 245r, 245v and the 246v address/docket (single reading only, each viewed in native strips, no independent re-read). The cipher second pass was not a fully independent reading of every sign; it re-checked the line against new crops.
- Confidence is low for the cipher token splits. Read it as a first-pass tokenization with many `?`.
