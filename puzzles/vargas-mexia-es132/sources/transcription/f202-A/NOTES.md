# f. 202-A: blind transcription notes (reader A, ff. 202r-203v, guide v2.3)

Worked from the four local page images only, with the guide as the sole other file. No network, no other repository files, no decoding attempted.

## Extent
- One letter, starting at the top of f. 202r ("+ El Rey / Juan de Vargas Mexia / A vuj. del presente se recibieron vras cartas de xxvij. y xxv. del passado", cipher begins mid-line 2) and ending on f. 203v with the place and date line ("De Aranxuez a xvij. de Mayo. M. D. lxx/x."), the King's signature ("Yo el Rey") and the secretary's countersignature below it. The address inscription "A Juan de Vargas mexia /" is on the foot of f. 203v, same leaf.
- Layout: f. 202r clear opening (2 lines) + cipher block (6 lines, the first sharing a line with clear text) + clear paragraph (8 lines) + cipher block (7 lines). f. 202v: 7 clear lines + 16 cipher lines. f. 203r: 17 cipher lines + 3 clear lines. f. 203v: 6 clear lines (the last is the date), signatures, address.
- Cipher lines: 13 on f. 202r (one shared with clear text), 16 on f. 202v, 17 on f. 203r = 46.
- Countersignature, as read: **"Cayas"** (a large looped initial that reads as C or Z/Cedilla, then "ayas", then a paraph; the secretary's name is probably Zayas, but I wrote only what the shape gives me).
- The letter is a single piece across the four pages; nothing else is on them except the items listed under image problems.
- The numeral in the date: "xvij." (one large x then v, i, j with two dots over the j). It could be a ligatured "xx" ("xxvij") because the f. 202r line 2 has "xxvij." for the same kind of numeral; I wrote what I saw.

Strips were 1650 px wide (f. 202v right halves 1986 px) by 420-470 px tall, three lines each, native resolution, halves overlapping by 150 px.
- Pass 2 (reverse: f. 203v back to f. 202r, bottom to top, right half then left half, strips cut at different offsets from pass 1): 74 half-width strip views (f. 202r 22, f. 202v 22, f. 203r 22, f. 203v 8). Pass 2 was complete for every cipher line of every page. It was not repeated for: the left halves of clear lines 4-6 on f. 202v, the address inscription and margins of f. 203v, the foot of f. 202r/203r (French lines), and the foliation and flourishes at the top of f. 202r.
- Changes made in pass 2 (all in the marks, not the numbers): added bars over `24p` (f. 202r block 2 line 3; f. 202v lines 1 and 15; f. 203r line 9), over `sol` (f. 203r line 1), a bar+cross on `138` handled as `138<cross>` (f. 202v line 4), a bar over `36+` (f. 202v line 8), dots over `15p` (f. 203r lines 4 and 14), a tilde over `16+` (f. 202v line 12) and over `22+` (f. 203r line 8), `55` bar (f. 202v line 4), and the dot-after `.` after 17 on f. 202r line 2. Reading changes: `10d?` unified for the three-letter group on f. 202r block 2 line 4 (first read `cod`), `yum` for `gum` on f. 202v line 12, `R?` for the big hook after `17.` on f. 202v line 7, and `3 6+` for the two-glyph `36+` pairs. ## Conventions I used where the guide is silent
- A number written directly against a different number (`6 7`, `9 2`, `9 1`, `6 3`, `5 1`, `5 5`): written with a space because the numbers run 1-37 and these are two bases. A curl touching a number is always joined (`66`, `86`, `246`).
- `<2dot>` for two dots over a sign, `<v>` for a tilde-like mark or a small z-shaped mark over a sign (I could not tell them apart at this size), `<hat>` for a caret, `<acute>` for a short slanted tick.
- `a` and `e` are written as letters when they stand alone (f. 202r line 7, f. 202v line 16, f. 203r lines 7, 14); `d.` with an acute (`d.<acute>`) is a letter d with a dot and tick; `ta`, `xal`, `Bam`, `Rom`, `ral`, `rom`, `fom`, `fum`, `pal`, `som`, `lum`, `quam`, `ham`, `cu`, `co`, `de`, `fil`, `gil`, `mc`, `sol`, `Ral`, `ra`, `te` are letter groups written as one token.
- The tall `1H`-looking sign is written `14` (see `h|14` below); I never saw an unambiguous h.

## Image problems
- Gutter / fold: on f. 202v every cipher line and the clear lines run into the fold at the right, and the images show the same cut. The ends of cipher lines are therefore partly cut (the last sign at the right edge of ff. 202v lines 2-9, 11-16 may be incomplete; marked with `?` where I could see only a stub). The 400 px of facing page at the left of f. 203r repeats the same line ends (I used it as a second view and nothing is hidden beyond the fold).
- Show-through: on f. 202r, f. 202v, f. 203r and f. 203v the verso text shows through as mirrored faint cipher or clear text between the lines. I treated everything fainter than the ink of the line as show-through and did not transcribe it. On f. 203r the first cipher line has a faint mirrored row above it.
- f. 202r: a seal and a dark stain at the left, a marginal docket written vertically at the left margin ("Des. 105"-like, not transcribed), a vertical date-like docket at the lower left, and two lines of a French-looking hand at the foot of both f. 202r and f. 203r (not part of the letter, not transcribed; the lines are the same on both pages and look like the facing sheet or a bleed). A ruled flourish runs from "El Rey" across the top.
- f. 202v: some wash at the lower left; the last cipher line ends a little above a faint mirrored line, which is show-through. Page ends after the 16th cipher line.
- f. 203v: sideways docket marks at the foot right (two or three letters "por"/"a" and a number, not transcribed), a small speck pair at top left, a faded foliation at top left.
- Short horizontal dashes at the right end of several cipher lines (f. 203r lines 3, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15; f. 202r line ends) were not transcribed; they look like line-fillers or page-edge marks. Several lines (f. 203r lines 9 and 14) end in a comma-like stroke, marked `,?` in line 9.
