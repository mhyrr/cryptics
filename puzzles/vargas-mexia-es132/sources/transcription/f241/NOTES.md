## Countersignature, as read
"Don Luis de ?diaq...z": after "Don" a tall ascender loop and "uide" (first name could be read Luis or Juan; I cannot separate "Luis" from "Juan" with confidence, the tall stroke is an L or J), then "de", then a surname that starts with a looped capital (I, Y or S) followed by "diaq" and a closing flourish like a z/3 with a small "8" loop. My reading is therefore "Don [Luis/Juan] de [I/Y]diaq[uez]". I did not open any source to settle it; the surname reading "Idiaq..." rests on the shapes alone. (Context outside the files, the git log shown in my prompt, mentions an Idiaquez; I flag that this may have biased the surname reading.)
The King's signature: "Yo el Rey" with a large flourish, a long diagonal tail descending to the right.

## Reader's general warning
The hand is very dense; signs 2, 3, 7, 8, S, 5, 1, 6, 0 and "o" curls are often not separable. The transcription is a best reading, not a certain one. Whole lines have an estimated 10-20 percent of tokens wrong or wrongly split.
