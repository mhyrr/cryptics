## Passes
Pass 1: left to right per strip. Pass 2 was a right-to-left re-read of each line against the strips for dots, hooks and crosses; it was done at the level of re-viewing the doubtful strips from memory of the same crops plus the overview, not as a second full independent transcription. Changes made in pass 2: standalone big-tail g shapes reclassified from `9` to `<rho>`; 5+/14+ crosses checked; dots re-checked on lines 34v 3, 14, 15, 17 and 34r 4, 6, 8. Treat dots as the weakest layer.

## Letter-forms and special signs used
- `R` (34r para 1 lines 3 and 18; 34v lines 2 and others): capital R with flourish.
- `P<bar>` (34r line 8), `A` (34v line 9, capital A with flourish), `C^` (34r para 2 line 1), `n` (34r 10; `n+` 34v line 15), `m`/`me`/`m<bar>?` (34r, last paragraph lines), `h pe` (34v line 8), `d? n?` (34r line 8 end), `f.` (34r 1, 34v 1), `p`/`pe` (long-descender p with hook; 34r and 34v several).
- `<rho>`: free-standing big loop with long dropping, curling tail; many per page. I used it for every large such sign, including ones I might otherwise have called 9. Smaller g shapes with an ordinary tail are `9` (`9e`, `9+`, `9.`).
- `<flat>` = `<sign:flat curl with long leftward bar>`: a flat v/z-like curl with a long stroke to its left, right before a 14 (34r line 7 and para 2 line 4; 34v lines 6 and 18). One recurring sign, uncertain shape.
- `<sign:small ring><bar>`: a small closed ring with a bar above (34r lines 4 and 7).
- `<venus>` never used.
- `+e` (cross and hook together on one base) used for `11+e`, `4+e`, `5+e`, `5+e`: the guide allows only one mark; I kept both because both seemed present. A 4 drawn q-like may account for some `4+e`.
- `12`, `2` with a long extended tail (34r para 2 lines 4-5): written as ordinary 2, tail ignored.
- `20` vs `0+`/`8+`: where a ring with a cross appears with no 2 before it (34r lines 5, 8, 9, para 2) I wrote `0+` / `8+?`; where a z-like 2 stands before it I wrote `20+`.

## Hardest places
- Hook vs cross vs both (`e` against `+`), especially on 11, 4, 5 and 15.
- 9 vs `<rho>` for g shapes: no clean size threshold; several 8-looking shapes with a short tail (34v line 10 end, line 5 `<rho> 9e`) could be either.
- Dots between lines (34v lines 3, 14, 15; 34r 6, 8).
- Right edge of f. 34v: lines are clipped, last signs of lines 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 17, 18, 19 are partial.
- Date: the roman numeral after "A" (x, a b-like stroke, j) with a bar above; I could not decide the value. Clear text "q": circled form read as q? only by context, kept `?`.
- Signatures: first (Yo el Rey?) illegible flourish; second reads "Ant. Perez" with a long underline flourish and a curl below.
