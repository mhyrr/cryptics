# f. 85 ff., blind transcription B: notes

Reader B. Guide v2.3 only; no other repository file opened; no network. Images: `sources/cache/f85-blind/f85_s01.png` to `f85_s05.png` (page order). Crops in `/tmp/claude-501/f85B/`.

## Method and passes
- Crops: strips of about 200 px (2 to 3 lines), left half 0-850 px, right half from 550 px (about 300 px overlap), native resolution.
- Pass 1: images in reverse order (s05 to s01), each strip left half then right half. Draft kept in `/tmp/claude-501/f85B/pass1.txt`.
- Pass 2: images in order s01 to s05, right half then left half, every strip re-viewed (s01: only the cipher strip and clear-text right halves were re-viewed, not every clear-text left half). Changes made in pass 2: moved marks above after the dot (`21.<acute>`), added `<bar>` on `28.`, `22<bar>`, `16p<bar>`, `166<bar>`; `7+<bar>` became `7+<v>` in s03 line 5; added `<acute>` on the second 4 of s04 line 4; `16 1 28` replaces `16 12 8` in s04 line 11; `246<under>?` added in s04 line 12; `66.` (dot after 66) in s02 lines 13 and 15 and s05 line 6; `24` (z-shaped 2) replaces a doubtful `x+` in s02 line 14; `15|18p?` in s03 line 9; `15<v>` in s05 line 1; cut edges re-checked.
- Views: pass 1 about 63 images (including 5 whole-image overviews and 1 extra strip), pass 2 about 56 images. Total about 119.
- Pass 2 was completed in full for ciphertext. It is a re-reading by the same reader from the same crops, so it catches slips and not systematic misreadings.

- Resolution: at native size, the digit 5 and S-like 15 and 18, 12+ and 124, and the H-shaped signs are the main ambiguities.

## The H-shaped sign (largest uncertainty)
Every sign that looks like `1H` (a 1 or tall stroke followed by an H-like arch with crossbar) is written `14|h?` with its marks, as guide section 9 instructs. 25 such tokens. In some places the same H-shape also stands for an open 4 after a 2 (written `24`). I could not decide, in the images, between h, 14 and an open 4 preceded by 1. They are counted in the `?` total below.

## Curl-joining doubts (guide section 2)
- `296`, `256`, `166` etc. are all written joined; in most of them the curl touches the number. The least certain: `176`/`166` (s05 line 6), `206` and `306`. `36` in `36+`, `36p`, `36<under>`: a number 36, not 3 plus curl; `3 6+` is possible.
- `6+` after a number (e.g. s05 line 7 `36+`) could be `3` + `6+`.

## Other reading choices
- `Av` (s03 line 4): a capital A followed by a small v, written as a two-letter group.
- `mp`, `np`, `m`, `n`: letter forms; `mp` and `np` carry a hook-like tail.
- `x`: a letter form, often with a dot or plus (`x.`, `x+`).
- `4<bar>` with a long bar and `4` with an oblique stroke are not always distinguishable.
- `8 1` (s02 line 1, s02 line 4, s04 line 3): an 8 followed by an upright stroke; read as two tokens; could be `8+`.
- `66.` (s02 lines 13, 15; s05 line 6): a dot between the 66 and the next sign was read as a mark after 66.
- Date line of s05: `a x.` could be `a 10`; the sign after `a` looks like a Roman x with a bar.
- Clear text: line 1 of s01 `El Rey` has a cross above and a flourish; first letters of lines were read diplomatically. Doubtful words: `xy.` (s01 line 2), `huzo` (could be `hizo`), `Mantigono`, `xby. xxiy` (s01 line 3), `auisarmeeis` and `enste` (s04 line 8), `conuiniere` and `os` (s05 line 2).
- The virgula after `se huuiere` (s04 line 9) and after `x.` at the end of s02 line 12 is followed by a dot.
