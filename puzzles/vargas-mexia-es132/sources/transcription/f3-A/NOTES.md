# f3-A notes (blind transcriber A)

Crops: `sources/cache/f3-A-crops/` (per-line strips 1500x230-250, left and right halves overlapping about 350 px; a few 2x zooms).

## Line counts
- f. 3r: 24 lines in the file = "El Rey" (1) + clear Spanish (6) + cipher (17). Folio "3" seen top right.
- f. 3v: 26 cipher lines, no clear text, no folio number seen. Ink spots/stains around cipher lines 18-19.
- f. 4r: 18 cipher lines (12, a "/" virgula after line 12, then 6), date line, "Yo el Rey"(?) line, "Cayas" line = 21 lines. Folio "4" seen top right.

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right on the left halves. Pass 2: the right halves were read separately (right to left within the half, then reconciled in the 350 px overlap), not a full independent re-transcription. Changes in pass 2: "<arc> 18<tail>" in 3r line 3 (was read as one unit), 3r line 1 `<theta><curl>` (first theta), 3v/4r line-label alignment fixed after seeing crops were offset. I did NOT make a full second independent read of every line.

## Marginal material (not transcribed)
Left margin of 3r: faint small marks beside cipher lines 3-4 and 17 (visible in the overview). Left margin of 3v: faint marks. 4r: a column of rotated writing in the left margin (a note, not read), and a seal at lower left. 4r bottom: round seal below the signature.
