# f105-B notes (blind transcription, Sonnet reader B)
- Letter: begins f. 105r (canvas 102 right), 21 cipher lines, then continues f. 105v (canvas 103 left), 2 cipher lines + clear closing: "...nro señor guarde y prospere la Ill(ma) persona de V.m. como yo deseo. de madrid a 13 de octubre 1578", signed with a flourished signature (read as Antº Peres/Pérez). Docket on f. 105v/106r: "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", "13 de octubre 1578". Address on f. 106r. So the letter is Pérez to Vargas, 13 Oct 1578.
- Cipher token count: roughly 520 (rough, from lines).
- QUALITY WARNING: Done in a single pass with 2-line strips; the second right-to-left pass was NOT done, and the f. 105v part is hurried and sign-level unreliable (marks, 6/6p/b readings). Dot-groups split at dots into separate tokens. Small marks above like a tiny r/v shape recorded as <v>; carets as <hat>.
- Hardest: 7p/7+ vs 2p groups, 6 vs b, 24 vs 29, 12/17/14 ligatures, line 1 of 105r start, the ?-marked signs, "m+" with marks, and 105v lines 1-2 (cropped at right edge).
