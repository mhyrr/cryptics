# f255 blind transcription notes (BnF es. 132, ff. 255-256, 28 Mar 1580)

Reader: blind, guide v2.2, no key, no other repository files, no network.

## Extent
- Letter begins on f. 255r: "El Rey" heading, then "Juan de Vargas Mexia," and the cipher starts on the same line as the salutation.
- Cipher runs 26 lines on f. 255r and 10 lines on f. 255v (36 lines in all). f. 255r ends mid-text; f. 255v line 7 is a short line ending in a bold virgula (paragraph break), then three more lines.
- Cipher line 10 of f. 255v ends with a bold `/` and the clear word "De"; the next line is "Guadalupe à 28 de março 1580." then a `/` and a small flourish. So the date line reads "De Guadalupe à 28 de março 1580". The "28" is written in Arabic figures with a heavy bar over it.
- King's signature below the date: large ornate "Yo el Rey" (read by convention; the ductus is a tall flourished mass with a long sweeping descender to lower left, not legible letter by letter).
- Countersignature, lower right of f. 255v (about 1/3 of the page below the date): "Don Antº de Eraso" as I read it, with two tall parallel ascenders over "Antº"/"de", the final letters ("-aso") run into a rubric of a vertical flourish and a looped tail. I read the surname as Eraso (possibly written with double s); the first name is abbreviated and I cannot exclude "Antonio" being written out.
- f. 255v below the countersignature is empty except for mirrored show-through of the cipher lines of the recto. The bottom right corner shows fragments of the folded address-leaf docket (see f. 256v).
- f. 256r: blank. Only show-through of f. 256v, ink spots, and a faint vertical strip of show-through. Page number "256" at top right. Nothing transcribed.
- f. 256v: address leaf, text runs sideways (rotate -90 to read). Lines: "Por el Rey" (with a small cross above "el" and a long oval rubric below), "A Juan de Vargas Mexia" and "en Paris" (underlined) with a virgula. Upper right, in another hand, a docket of five lines: "Guadalupe" (partly covered by the overlapping leaf above), "de su Mag.d de 28 de março", "R.do en Paris a 16 de Abril", "respondida . vmo cozoa bull?" (the last three words are uncertain; shapes only), "Martin" (an ornate initial M, perhaps a docketing clerk). To the far right, partly under a flap, another fragment with "1579" and "Lcb" (belongs to a different item; not transcribed).

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom. 22 strips of f. 255r (11 rows x left and right halves, each 1500x400 px at native resolution, 2-3 full lines plus part of the next) and 10 strips of f. 255v (5 rows x 2 halves, 1500x420 px), so 32 cipher strips. Plus: 1 left-margin column crop of f. 255v; 7 native crops of the signature, countersignature, date end and bottom-right corner; overview and one mid-page crop of f. 256r; overview, 3 docket strips and 2 address strips of f. 256v.
- Pass 2: right to left, bottom to top, re-viewed all 32 cipher strips (f. 255v first rows 5 to 1, then f. 255r rows 11 to 1, right half before left half) and corrected line by line. The docket and address strips were viewed once, at native size, not twice.
- Strips are 2-3 lines each rather than strictly at most 3 with no extra partial line; some contain a partial fourth line at the edge. Line pitch is about 110-150 px.
- What pass 2 changed: added many marks above I had dropped (bars over 17+, 24., 22., 15p, 66, 166; acutes over 16., 24+, 6.; a hat over 24+ and over 6p; 2dots over 14. and 24+; a cross over 113); merged `3 6+` into `36+` where tight; re-split `176`/`161` strings; changed several `15`/`25` and `14`/`12` readings; demoted a number of readings to `?`.
- The cipher hand is cramped and heavily affected by show-through (see below). I think a fair share of my number readings are wrong at the 1/2, 4/5, 21/24, 25/28 level. The blind-key condition means I could not use plausibility to resolve them.

## Three or more digit runs left joined
Curl rule applied: a curl touching the number before it and without its own mark is joined.
- `166` (f255r l.4, l.5, l.17 x3, l.20, l.12, l.15; f255v l.8 `66`): number 16 + joined curl.
- `161` (many): 16 + `1`, or 1 + 61, joined as seen.
- `176` (f255r l.4, l.23): 17 + curl.
- `178` (f255v l.6): 17 + curl-like 8; could be `17 8`.
- `113` (f255r l.3): 1 + 13, or 11 + 3.
- `126` (f255r l.7, l.15, l.18): 12 + curl.
- `156` (f255r l.11 x1, l.21, l.26; f255v l.1, l.6): 15 + curl.
- `206` (f255r l.18, l.20): 20 + curl.
- `201` (f255r l.6, l.19, l.24, l.25, l.26): 20 followed by a vertical tick, no cross as in `20+`; may be `20+` with a missing crossbar or `20 1`.
- `226` (f255r l.16; f255v l.1; f255v l.8 with 2 dots above): 22 + curl.
- `231` (f255r l.11 x2, l.14 with bar, l.15), `236` (l.11, l.13, l.26, l.25 with 2 dots), `246` (l.16, l.17, l.25, l.26; f255v l.3, l.4), `236` (f255v l.1): 23/24 + curl or + 1.
- `241` (f255r l.19), `256` (f255r l.7, l.19; f255v l.4, l.8), `276` (l.7), `286` (f255v l.6), `306` (f255v l.5), `86 6p` (l.2).
- `11p`/`11.` are two uprights read as `11`.
- `1 13`-type: see `113`.

## Every `<sign:...>`
- f255r l.12, last sign: `81<sign:z-shaped mark above>` (a small z-like or 2-like stroke over the final 81).
- f255r l.21: `23.<sign:small 2-like mark above>?` (a small 2-shaped mark above the third `23.`; may belong to the line above).

## Every `<under>`
f255r l.8 `3<under> 86<under>` (one continuous stroke under the group; I could not tell whether it is a single sign `386` or two); l.9 `30+<under>` and `34+<under>`; l.25 `y<under>` (short dash under the letter y). f255v l.3 `34+<under>`.
No `<under>?` hedges used, but the l.8 group is doubtful.

## Doubtful curls (joined vs separate)
`3 6+` (f255r l.1 joined, l.19 separate) and `36+` generally: a number 36 with plus, not a 3 + curl. `3 8p`, `6 4+`: a base followed by a base. The rest follow the curl rule.

## Other uncertainties
- 12 vs 14 and 15 vs 25 are not reliably distinguishable in this hand (second numeral is z-shaped or a closed form); I used `12+` when the second stroke was clearly z-shaped, `14+` otherwise.
- The letter-forms `x` (f255r l.8, l.10, l.20, f255v l.9) and `y` (with and without bar above) are as in the guide; the `x`-shaped sign with plus or hook is written `x+`, `xp`.
- `3` with tail under the line appears often as an independent sign; written `3`.

## Image problems
- Heavy show-through on both folios: the verso text shows through mirrored and runs between and across the recto lines. Worst on f. 255r left-hand half and f. 255v lines 3-7. The mirrored, grey text often sits just above or below a sign and mimics marks above.
- The right edge of f. 255v (x > 3100) is in the gutter shadow.
- f. 255v bottom right: folded address leaf cuts across, hiding the end of the docket's top line.
- No cut text edges on f. 255r or 255v cipher lines. Last line of f. 255r is short (ends about 3200 px).
- Blots: f. 255r l.4 hook over 20, l.14 blot over `16p`, l.22 blot at the end of `6p.`; f. 255v l.5 `71` (heavy downstroke), l.3 `y`.

## Countersignature as read
"Don Antº de Eraso" (secretary). Shape: Capital D with a large flourished bowl, "Ant" with two tall parallel strokes rising above the line (tick tops), "de", then "Eraso" with a long vertical heavy stroke through the last letters and a looped tail below.
