# f. 54-56 blind transcription A: NOTES

Reader A, guide CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.3. Images: 9 screenshots `f54_s01.png` to `f54_s09.png` (f. 54 recto begins in s01; f. 55 begins in s05, page number "55"; f. 56 begins in s09, page number "56"). Line numbers below are line numbers of `transcription.txt` (header lines count).

## Deviations from the brief (read first)
- Strips: the brief asked for 3-line strips cut into left and right halves overlapping by ~300 px. That would be ~125 views per pass and about 250 in total, which is over the "about 120 images" cap. I cut **full-width strips** (1450 px, native resolution, no scaling) of about 3 lines (235 px tall, step 190 px) instead. 62 strips, so **62 views in pass 1 and 62 views in pass 2 (124 total)**. No halves.
- Strip cuts are at fixed offsets, not at line gaps, so strips begin and end mid-line; lines were tracked across strips by their content and by the repeated overlap rows.
- The pages are 1410-1450 px wide with a dark band (the page edge) at the right in s03 to s08. Lines run into it; I wrote `<cut>` where the last sign is clearly truncated by it (22 times). Lines without `<cut>` might also lose a sign there.
- No decoding attempted. No file other than the guide and the images was read.

## Assumptions that apply across the whole text
- A large flat-topped z-shaped sign standing alone (often with a tail) was written `2` (guide section 7). It could be a 3 or a letter z. The same sign is very frequent.
- A "u"-shaped first stroke before a z-shaped second stroke was often read as `22` or `23` (guide: 12 can look like u + z). Many `22`/`23`/`12` readings of `vz`-shaped pairs are uncertain even without a `?`.
- "b"-shaped curls after a digit are written as a joined 6 (`16`, `26`, `36`); "e"-shaped hooks as `p`. Where both a curl and a hook follow a digit (shape like `1be`), I wrote `166p` or `16p`; the choice between these is not consistent and is uncertain (see joined runs).
- Marks above (bar, hat, acute, 2dot, v) are the most error-prone part of this transcription; a tilde above a sign was written `<bar>`.

## Image problems
- Dark band at the right edge in s03 to s08 (page edge of the microfilm-style image) hides or distorts the last sign of many lines.
- s02 to s03 and s06 to s07: no overlap, so a missing line between them cannot be ruled out.
- High contrast black-and-white reproduction; marks above and curls are small and frequently touch the line above. Strips were viewed at native size only.
