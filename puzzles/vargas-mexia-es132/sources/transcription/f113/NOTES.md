# f. 113 (BnF Esp. 132) blind transcription notes

Method: Gallica IIIF, ark:/12148/btv1b10032556x. 113r = right page of canvas 110 (folio no. "113" read top right).
113v = left page of canvas 111; 114r = right page of canvas 111 (folio no. "114" read; page blank, no docket seen at
10% overview). Crops saved in sources/cache/f113-crops/ (native-scale 1500 x 170 px half-line strips; r01-r26 L/R for
113r, w01-w17 L/R for 113v; ov20.jpg, ov111.jpg overviews; vsig/vdate/vant = date and signature). Line positions on
113r from a row-ink profile (26 lines); on 113v by pitch (17 cipher lines). A first look used 2-line strips (later
deleted), the line strips are what the final text is read from. Reader was the same agent for all passes.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right per strip, drafted line by line (pass1.txt = transcription.txt without headers).
- Pass 2: every line re-viewed, right half before left half, whole letter (113r and 113v). Changes from pass 2:
  r01 `d e` -> `∂ e` (same glyph as the other ∂); r02 `7618` -> `76 18`; r05 `28618` -> `286 18`; r06 duplicated
  `21.` at crop overlap removed; r04 added `18<v>?`; r20 final q gets `<grave>` (the stroke sits above that q);
  r21 `6618` -> `66 18`; r22 flourish after last 166 noted; v01 `15.<hat>` added; v02 `29.<v>?` added; v05 `,?`
  added; v06 `1H` -> `1H+`; v11 hat on 236 dropped; v12 `12+<2dot>` added, `13<bar>?` -> `23`.
- Addendum v2.1 (z-shaped 2): applied to the whole text. I had read the u/v-like first glyph in `ʋ3`, `ʋH`, `ʋ2+`
  as 2 from the start, so no token was changed from `u`. Tokens decided by the addendum: `23H`, `2H` (u-glyph + H),
  `22+` (v10 `6. 22+ 24+`, glyph u + z-2 + plus), `23<v>` (r13), v12 `6. 23`. `S u m` keeps a true `u` (no tail).
  Marks above were re-checked on every line in pass 2 (the additions listed above).

## Layout
113r: heading "El Rey" (large flourished, cross above) then 26 lines. Line 1 opens with the clear name
`Juan de Vargas Mexia.` (large J flourish at left margin), then `/ <dot>` and cipher. Cipher runs to the end of
l. 26 (`... ∂ 6.`) with no break. 113v: 17 cipher lines, then clear date, `Yo el Rey` (signature), and "Ant. Perez"
(secretary, right of signature, cut off by the gutter/binding). Left margin of 113r has a few pen marks (small
arrows/crosses) near ll. 8 and 20, not transcribed. No docket on 113v/114r seen. Right ends of the 113v lines are
lost in the gutter shadow (`<cut>`, 15 lines); the 113r lines are complete.

## Date and signature as read
`de Madrid a xvy? de octubre 1578 /`: "de Madrid", a (alpha-like), a bar-topped numeral that looks like `x` plus a
y-shaped letter (`xvy` / `xij`-type), then "de octubre 1578" and a slanted stroke. The day number is NOT securely
read; it is not obviously the "13" given in the task, but an x + ij ligature (xiij) would fit the shapes. Unresolved.

## Unsure curls (joined or separate) and other doubts
- Many lines: whether the curl of `166`, `66`, `16 e` is joined to the previous digit (kept joined everywhere).
- `7 6 6 / 760`: r03, r13 (`760<hat>`, `760?`) round sign after 76 may be curl or `o`.
- `1 0` / `6<acute>` family: the "lo"-shaped sign (curl plus small round o, often with acute above), written
  `6<acute>` (r03, r06, r08, r10, r24), `6o` (v09), `10` (r07, v03), `10<acute>?` (r18), `10<bar>?` (r22),
  `10<hat>` (r07). These may be one sign; I did not unify them.
- `H`: an H-shaped sign (`1H`, `7H`, `23H`, `3H`), written `H`, not identified with any letter.
- `S`: the large curled sign before `om` / `um` (r17, r20, v08, v09, v11, v16, v17) written `S` per the guide's
  long flat-topped s; it looks more like a curled brace. Doubtful.
- `300<sign:loop-above>?`: `30` plus an o-like sign with a loop above it (r05, r09, r23; also r09 reads `305`-like).
- r01: slanted stroke after `Mexia.` written `/` plus a lone dot; `23p . q` has a dot between hook and q.
- `p`-hook vs `e`: `p e`, `6p`, `16p`, `17p` kept as p (hook) / e (letter) per shape.
- `q` vs `4`: descender/closed top decides; `24 e?` (r12, r12R), `86 4`, `4 24 e` are close calls.
- `5` vs `8`: `35.` (r04) could be `25.`; `28`/`25` in `7+ 28 6 e` (v14) uncertain.
- v06 `24+<acute>?` is overwritten/struck (heavy ink); v04 `24+?` likewise. v06 `2+?` is a z-2 plus a plus.
- v05 `,?`: a comma-like stroke under the line between `23H` and `p+`.
- r15, r16: `65 e?` could be `15 e`.
