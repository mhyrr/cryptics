# NOTES: f. 26r, reader A (blind, Cipher 2 guide v1.1)

## Map
- Image: `sources/cache/pages/f026r.jpg` (3569 x 5452). Only f. 26r imaged. Top: header "El Rey" (a small cross after "El")
  and folio number "26" at top right (not transcribed). A facing-page strip at the right edge carries marginal marks
  (p, :, ., 11, 8, 7, 8p, 8p, l); not transcribed.
- Lines: 28 manuscript lines = 1 header (L00 "El Rey") + 27 body lines (L01-L27). L01 starts with clear
  "Juan de Vargas Mexia" followed by cipher. All other body lines are pure cipher. Clear text total: 2 runs (header, name).
  Last line L27 ends in a large `<rho>`. L24 ends with a virgula `/`, L27 has a `/` before the last rho.
- Crops: native strips 1500x400 (r0-r10, L/R halves, 300 px overlap) for pass 1; pass 2 used 1100x260 bands enlarged
  180-250% (some 300%) for L01-L27, working right to left. Crops in `sources/cache/f26-A-crops/`.
- Stray ink dot at the far left margin beside L13. Heavy show-through of the other side's text is visible; ignored.

## Special signs and letter-forms used
- `<rho>`: very many (L02, L03 x2, L04, L05 x2, L06, L08 x2, L09 x2, L13, L14, L15, L17, L18, L19, L22, L23, L25, L27 x2).
  Large loop with a long tail below the line. A few (L04 after `2`, L05 after `2`/before `13`, L08 after `2`) are joined
  to the preceding sign; I treated all as free `<rho>`, not hook `e`. Dot above a rho: L05 `<rho>^`, L15 `<rho>^`.
- `<venus>`: L12 (`<venus>+`: a loop on a stem with a long underline bar below, cross at right) and L27
  (`<venus>.`: same shape, dot at right). Both are shaped like the guide's circle-with-bar; the L12 one has a loop that
  could be read as a g/9 with an underline, so check it.
- Letter-forms: `R` (L02, L04, L08 x2, L15, L26; some with a left underline/flourish), `f` (L01 `f_?`, L20 `f+`, L24 `f+`,
  L26 `f+`), `h+` (L09, a tall h-like stem with cross), `n` (L08 `n^`, L10 `n^`, L18 `n^`, L20 `n^` and plain `n`),
  `C` (L13 `C^`, L24 `C^`; capital C with dot above).
- `<sign:swash>`: a long horizontal stroke leading into a small v/u curl, ending in a tail (L02, L05, L14, L17, L19, L24, L27).
  Same shape each time. Not a letter I could name. In L12 a similar long dash before `0e` is written
  `<sign:long dash>`; it may be the same swash with the curl read as `0`.
- `<sign:struck-through cluster>`: L13 end, after `4`: a scribbled/struck group (dots/uprights with a hook-tail, a
  horizontal strike through it). Possibly a cancelled sign group.
- Standalone `0` (not part of 10/20): L02 (twice), L04 (twice), L12, L13, L15, L10 `0+`, L12 `0+`, L23 `0+`. Written as a
  round o with or without + or hook. In `2 0+ 10` (L13, L23) I read the `2` as separate because of a clear gap; elsewhere
  `20+` is tight. Guide says 20 is two-stroke 2 plus 0, so this is a risk.

## Every `?` and alternative
- L01 `4e?`: last sign has a hook and a doubled cross-like stroke; may be `4+` plus hook. `f_?`: the dot sits right of
  the f's tail and directly above the R of L02; could be `R^` instead (dot above R).
- L02 `5e.?`: faint dot(s) after 5e.
- L03 `18_?`: dot between `18` and the barred 6 of L04, nearly equidistant; may be `6^` (dot above the bar) in L04.
- L06 `13_?`: dot below first 13, partly cut by crop.
- L07 `17|12e`: the sign after `13+` looks like 12 or 17 with hook.
- L10 `15^?`, `19^?`: dots above the 1 of each, light.
- L16 `17_?`: dot below last 17 seen only in the first pass strip (second-pass band cut it off).
- L21 `19.?`: dot at right of the 9 tail, low; L25 `19_?`: dot at tail, right-below.
- L23 `13e_?`: two specks below `13e`. L25 `3_?`: dot right-below the 3.
- L18/L15 `5e.`: heavy blob at the end of the 5e; read as a dot at right (may be ink blot). L17 `18e` has a blob on top
  (possible `^`; not written). L21 `18` is a heavy ink blob.

## Hardest places
1. Hook vs rho vs 4/17 look-alikes in L04-L08 and L16-L18 (`17e`/`12e`/`13e` all close).
2. Dots between lines: L01/L02 (f/R), L03/L04 (18/6), L05/L06, L09/L10, L21/L22.
3. The `0` signs: separate vs part of 20 (`2 0+`).
4. Bars: L26 bar spans `11e` and `11` (I wrote a bar on both); L22 `11<bar> 18<bar>` bars read across blobs.
5. The `7`: L10 `7.`, L16 `7e`, L20 `7+` read as 7 (not 17); the guide's range is 2-23 so 7 is fine, but check.
6. `1` forms: many `1`s have a leading serif or look dotted (`i8`); I did not write a dot unless clearly separate.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right on 1500x400 native strips, plus targeted 2-3x zooms.
- Pass 2: right to left on 1100x260 bands at 180% (and 200-300% zooms), every line. Changes from pass 1: L01 f dot
  re-read (`f_?`), `11^` added (L01, L04, L21, L22), R^ vs 18_ dot decisions, `15<bar>` confirmed in L03 (not 18),
  L08 `n^`, L10 `13^ 15^? 18_ 19^?`, L12 sign changed from `g?+` to `<venus>+`, L13 end struck cluster, L15 `18^`,
  L16 `5` (dot removed), L19 `12_`, L20 `19^`, L23 `12^` (was `12_`), L27 final sign read as `<venus>.`.
