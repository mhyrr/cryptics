## Honest confidence statement
This is a low-confidence reading. Many signs are unclear (pen joins, heavy show-through of the facing side on 213v and 213r,
and several lines overwritten by ghost text). Treat digit strings as approximate; the `?` marks are far from exhaustive.
Placement of spaces and the choice between a number and a letter form (e.g. `15+` vs `1 5 +`, "ibe" read as `166`) is
the reader's own convention and may differ from other readers. The unpunctuated clear Spanish and the place/date line are the most reliable parts.
