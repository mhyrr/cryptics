# Reader A notes (blind transcription, BnF Esp. 132 ff. 198r-199r)

Method: Gallica IIIF full-resolution spreads (canvas 195, 196), cut with sips into 600 px strips; crops in sources/cache/reader-A-crops/.
No key or other repo file consulted.

Passes:
- Pass 1: f. 198r read from 3200x600 strips (shown at ~62% scale), draft only.
- Pass 2: every page re-read from half-width 1700x600 crops at full resolution (f. 198r, 198v, 199r), and the transcription was rewritten from those. f. 198v and f. 199r therefore had one full-resolution reading plus spot re-checks of overlaps, not two independent full passes. Treat marks (dots, hats) as the weakest layer.

Cipher token counts (script count, includes uncertain `?` tokens, excludes `/`, lone `.`, clear text): f.198r 266, f.198v 312, f.199r 170, total 748.

Conventions beyond the brief:
- `5`: the flat-topped s-like glyph (as in "15", "56", "5+") is written as digit 5; `S` not used.
- `<y7>`: flat-topped, 7-shaped y (same shape as the clear-text "y"). `y` = the tailed cipher-style y. Often not separable; may be clear Spanish "y".
- `2_?` / `3_?`: a ?-shaped glyph with a dot below; could be 2 or 3 with dot below.
- `<dash>`: long line-end dash. `<+above>`: cross over the token. `ε`: reversed-3 sign. `~<hat>`: tilde-like sign with hat above.
- `/` and `/.` are slash strokes between cipher groups (sentence-ish breaks), kept as separate items.
- Gutter: lines on f. 198v lose their last 1-2 glyphs in the gutter shadow; marked `?` or cut.
- Faint bleed-through from the verso and a faint "y chri..." beside f. 198v lines 18-19 were ignored.
- Lighter-ink words on f. 199r (Sasesso deziral, permaneciesse, debaxo de grandes juramtos, etc.) are transcribed as clear text; cipher groups in the same lines look lighter too.
- Lower-left block on f. 199r (burlo / falta de tpo / escrevir...) is in the gutter; line beginnings are cut ([[?...]]).

Hardest readings (least certain):
1. f.198r l.9 `206<bar>? 6. 15 5<hat>? C<bar>_? 1 C61?` (C-like signs, overstrikes).
2. f.198v l.7 `1? [[y q esto fuesse]]`: whether this is cipher "176" / "17" + ring-q, or "1" + clear "y q".
3. 2/3 with dot below (`2_?`, `3_?`), and `21_` vs `2` + `1` with dot (many sites).
4. 24+ vs 2+ (H-like 4 vs plain cross) and `4+?` for the H glyph; 156 vs 186; 36 vs 26.
5. f.198v l.20 `8<blot>? 19+<+above>? 22p?` and f.199r l.1 "pero" + `20`.

Unclassified marks: tiny tick above `15` (f198v l.26), tick after `196,` (f198r l.18), the `J+?` glyph (f198r l.20), `o+?` (f198r l.6), `p f<bar>` (f198r l.5, 9), small "3." under "pero" (f199r l.1, not transcribed separately), isolated stray dots at line ends.
