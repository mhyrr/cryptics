## Conventions I added
- `<sign:flick>`: a short hairline tick after a number, lower right (23, 20, 17, 16, 7, 2, 11). Not a `+`, not a clear dot.
  It is frequent and may be a deliberately drawn mark; unresolved. Where it follows a "z"-shaped 2 it looked like "2-1" and
  I wrote `2<sign:flick> 4` rather than `21 4`.
- Letter-groups inside the cipher written as letters, not in [[ ]]: Zom, Zum, Quam, Qua, Xal, Jam, Pam, Ham, Ja, Jim, vel,
  gil, yil, lo, de, d, g, v, o, a. Initial capitals as drawn (the Z of Zom has a hooked tail; Jim/Jam initials are J/I
  looped).
- "6 7", "6 8", "6 2", "6 5": 6 (or 6 with curl) followed by a separate digit; 67/68/62/65 are not numbers in 1-37, so I
  split them. Doubtful whether the second digit is a mark rather than a number.
- Clear line 2 of 32r ("6. 10. 11. ... y 27. de Hebrero,") bracketed as clear Spanish (dates of letters).
