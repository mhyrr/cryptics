# Blind transcription notes: BnF es. 132 ff. 228-230 (13 Oct 1579)

Reader: blind transcriber, guide `CIPHER3-GUIDE.md` v2.2 only. No other repository file was opened, no network used.
Images: `sources/cache/pages/exp07/f228r.jpg` ... `f230v.jpg`, cropped with `magick` into `/tmp/claude-501/t228/`.

## Extent
- **Starts** f. 228r: top-centre heading `El Rey`, then (same line) the salutation `Juan de Vargas Mexia /.` and a 12-line clear
  paragraph (acknowledges receipt of letters of early September up to the 19th and 20th, "como alas de Cayas", asks him not to
  need to continue reporting), then a cipher block of 12 lines.
- f. 228v: cipher only, 26 lines in three paragraphs (9 + 13 + 4 lines); each paragraph ends with `/.`.
- f. 229r: cipher only, 26 lines in four paragraphs (4 + 5 + 9 + 8 lines); paragraphs end `/ .` except the last (line 26 also ends `/ .`).
- f. 229v: cipher lines 1-9; line 9 ends `/` and continues in clear: `de sanct lorenco a 13 de 8bre 1579.`
  Below it, the King's signature `Yo el Rey` and the secretary's countersignature (see below). **The letter ends here.**
  Bottom left of the page, a short note, `Dup.do` (see below).
- f. 230r: blank recto (modern folio number 230 at top right; faint mirror-reversed show-through of the address on the verso). Nothing transcribed.
- f. 230v: address leaf. `A Juan de Vargas Mexia /.` and `Por el Rey`, both written across the leaf rotated 90 degrees to the fold
  (a small cross above `el`; remnant of a seal at right, mid-leaf). Transcribed in `[[ ]]`.
- Cipher lines transcribed: 12 + 26 + 26 + 9 = **73**.

Line counting in the file: one output line per manuscript line. On f. 228r the first 13 lines of the file are the heading and the
12 clear lines, then 12 cipher lines. Line numbers below (`l1`, `l2` ...) count cipher lines on the page.

## Signatures, date, notes
- Date line (f. 229v l9, after the cipher): `de sanct lorenco a 13 de 8bre 1579.` with an overbar above `13` and above `1579`; `bre` is
  a raised superscript after the `8`. The `de` at the start has a flourished `d`.
- King's signature, as written: flourished `Yo el Rey` (large looped `Y`, `o`, `el`, `Rey` ending in a long tail swung to the right and
  down), transcribed `[[Yo el Rey]]`. Reading of the middle word is `el`, the loops are heavy.
- **Countersignature as I read it: `Don Iuº de Idiaqz`, i.e. "Don Ju[an] de Idiaq[ue]z" (Juan de Idiáquez).** Shape: `Don` clear;
  a long upstroke from the end of `n`; then a short word of 3-4 minims that I read as `Iu` plus a raised letter; `de`; then a
  capital `I`-form, `diaq` and a final `z` that runs into a large `8`-like loop and a long swung tail underneath. Reading of the word
  after `Don` and of the middle of the surname is uncertain; `Don ... de Idia...z` is secure, the rest is not.
- f. 229v bottom left: `Dup.do`, a D with loop, `u`, `p`, a raised abbreviation mark (looks like `do`) and a dot, with a heavy underline
  stroke. Probably an abbreviation of "duplicado" (a marking of a duplicate copy); transcribed `[[Dup.do]]`, expansion is my guess only.

## Passes (guide §6)
- **Pass 1** (forward, first folio to last, per page top to bottom, left half then right half). Crops: 3 manuscript lines high, 1500 px wide
  halves overlapping by 300-400 px, native resolution. Images viewed: f228r 22 (8 clear, 14 cipher), f228v 25, f229r 21, f229v 13
  (incl. date, signature, countersignature and note), f230r/v 4 address crops plus the two overviews. **About 85 strips.**
- **Pass 2** (opposite direction within each page: bottom to top, right half before left, with differently offset crop heights so every line
  and every mark above fell inside a strip; the page order itself was not reversed). Images viewed: f228r 16, f228v 19, f229r 18, f229v 6,
  f230v 4. **About 63 strips.** Every cipher line of every page was re-read in this pass; the clear text of f228r was re-read as well.
- I did not reach the 25-40 strips per page that the brief expected. Each page here holds 12-26 lines, so 3-line strips in two halves give 8-18
  strips per page per pass; I added extra strips only where marks were cut off. Pass 2 therefore has fewer strips than pass 1.
- Pass 2 changes (main ones): f228r l1 `22+` re-read as `24+<2dot>?` (same sign as f228v l3); f228v l5 bar on first `3` after `17+`,
  hat on first `24+` found; f228v l13 `<hat>` above `12+` and `<v>` above `23.` found; f229r l25 bar plus `2` above the `6.` and acute, hats
  above `13` and `7p` found at line end (cut off in pass 1); f229r l7 `2 11+` replaced `21 1`; f229r l9 double counting of `24+ 7p 3` removed;
