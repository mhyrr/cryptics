# ff. 237-238 (3 Nov 1579): blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- The letter begins at the top of f. 237r ("El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", clear opening paragraph, then cipher)
  and ends on f. 238r (cipher line, then clear "/ Del Pardo A iij? de Nouiembre, 1579.", "Yo el Rey", and the
  countersignature "Don Iuan de Idiaq" with flourish).
- f. 237r: 6 clear lines + clear heading, then 19 cipher lines (three paragraphs: lines 1-7, 8-13, 14-19). Page ends at line 19 (blank below).
- f. 237v: 28 cipher lines (paragraphs: 1-4, 5-23, 24-28; paragraph starts are ornate initials). Lines run to the right edge, where the facing page's edge/gutter may clip the last sign.
- f. 238r: 8 cipher lines (paragraphs 1-4, 5-8), then date line, "Yo el Rey", countersignature. Total 55 cipher lines.
- f. 238v: address leaf, page image rotated 90 degrees. "Por el Rey / Juan de Vargas Mexia" (heading and addressee, with a cross over "el"),
  plus a docket in a later hand along the right edge (reading uncertain), plus one line cut by the edge of the leaf (unreadable). Also on f. 238v: a seal/wax patch with a rectangular blank
  (image redaction or loss) and mirror show-through of f. 238r. Not cipher; transcribed in [[ ]] only.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Iuan de Idiaq" (abbreviated, long flourish); the King: "Yo el Rey". A small separate scrawl with underline at the bottom left of f. 238r (about x 900, y 4300) is not transcribed (illegible, different hand?).

## Passes and strips
- Pass 1 (left to right, forward page order): f237r 16 strips (8 three-line bands x left/right halves; the last band is blank cipher-free), f237v 22, f238r 8: 46 strips of 3 lines, 1550-1600 px wide, native resolution, plus about 12 extra crops for headers, signatures and the docket.
- Pass 2 (opposite direction: last page to first, bottom to top, right half before left): f238r 8, f237v 22, f237r 14: 42 strips. Lines were corrected where the second view differed (mostly marks above, virgulas, and a few digit runs).
- I did not reach the 25-40 strips per page asked for: the bands are 3 lines, two halves each, with only ~100-200 px overlap, so about 14 per page on the full ones.

## Reliability (read this)
- f. 238r is clean ink and should be fairly reliable. f. 237r and 237v carry heavy mirror show-through from the other side; many tokens there are doubtful. 50 lines carry at least one `?`. Treat f237r lines 2-3 and f237v lines 12-14, 25-27 as the weakest.
- The hook `p` versus plus `+` versus a plain tail, and caret versus tilde above, are often undecidable at this scale; I used `p`, `+`, `<hat>`, `<acute>` by best fit.
- Digit 2 is z-shaped throughout; I wrote it as 2 (so "ʒ"-like signs inside numbers are 2, a free-standing "ʒ" I wrote `3`). A free-standing 3-like sign with a hook may be either.

## Clear words inside the cipher (written in [[ ]])
- `quam` (lower case, many times; capital [[Quam]] at the start of f237r line 14), `de` (several, e.g. f237r line 2, f238r line 4). `de` might be two letter-form signs d, e; `quam` looked like plain Latin letters. I also wrote `d.` and `L`, `T`, `m`, `y`, `u`, `e` as letter-form signs.
- "bul" (f237v lines 8 and 20, written `6 u L`/`u l`): it looks like b-u-l in a plain hand; could be letter forms or a clear word.
- `30 m` (digit 30 then m) occurs on f237r line 13, f237v lines 2 and 24; on f238r line 1 the first part has a bar above it: `30<bar> m`.

## 3+ digit runs left joined (curl rule or run of digits)
256 (16 times, e.g. f237r lines 5, 8, 9; f237v lines 3, 6, 9), 176 (6), 236 (f237r line 1, 14?, 17), 246, 156 (f238r line 4), 126, 296, 269, 456, 226, 206, 166, 240, 621, 2626 (f237r line 6). In all of these I wrote the curl joined to the number before it. Doubtful whether the curl is joined: all `256`, `176`, `126` and `166` runs. I wrote a free-standing curl after 8 as `86` (joined, two digits) in "quam 86 3" and variants.
- 237, 238, 1579 in the list above are folio numbers and the year, not cipher.

## `<sign:...>` (all are ornate paragraph initials or flourishes, not decodable shapes)
f237r first cipher line: ornate H-like initial; f237r cipher line 4: a-like flourish before "23?", f237v line 1 initial with long left flourish, f237v line 5 large H, f237v line 24 large looped initial, f237v line 26 looped initial, f238r line 5 large V-shaped initial stroke, f237v line 2 D-like sign with hat, f238r line 6 small 4-like mark above a 6. 

## `<under>`
Two: f237v line 8 `30p<under>` and f237r line 12 `30.<under>` (both clear: a short stroke below the 30). I looked for underlines on every 3x number; no others were seen with confidence (f237r line 13 "30 m" has none).

## `?` (list by place; there are 50 lines with one or more)
All lines marked with `?` in transcription.txt. The groups to know: the opening sign of f237r line 1, first paragraph (the ornate initial, `236 6p`), all `86?` readings of the sign after "quam", `62+?`, `23?`/`4+?` in f237r lines 2-3, and most of f237v lines 12-14, 20-22 and 25-27 where show-through hides the pen strokes.

## Image problems
- Show-through (mirror writing from the other side) on f237r and f237v, worst where lines on the two sides coincide; reduces reliability of marks above (hat/bar) and dots.
- f237v: right end of each line touches the facing page edge/gutter (dark vertical line); last sign may be clipped or lost. Page tilt about 1-2 degrees.
- f238r: ink blot on the "8" after quam in line 3 and on "Del" in the date line; the King's signature flourishes and the countersignature are clear. f238r: left side has the f237v facing page edge.
- f238v: rotated 90 degrees in the image; a seal patch with a blanked rectangle; mirror show-through of the whole of f238r; docket read as: "de su M. dey de Nou?", "?dag en Paris a lotro? 20 del co", "dupp. de la quetra e Don Ju. de Acuña?", "?abaeha ora no sa llegado,": low confidence on every word.
- Date line "A iij?": the number after "Pardo A" looks like iiy/iij with a tail; read as the day 3 (consistent with the task's date), not confirmed.
