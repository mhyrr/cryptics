- Each cipher paragraph ends with a `/` virgula followed by a dot on the 34r para 1, 34r para 2, and 34v para 1 lines (written `/ .`). In 34v para 2 the `/` occurs inside lines (W7, W8, W12), written as `/` tokens.

## Passes
Pass 1: left to right per strip. Pass 2 was a reduced right-to-left check, not a full independent re-read: I re-read the line ends and the doubtful clusters at 150-220% zoom (34r lines 1-4 and 7, the `15e`/`11+e` clusters, the line-start `<rho>` signs, the date). Changes in pass 2: line-start and line-end big loop signs changed from `9?` to `<rho>` (the curled tail is clearly present in zooms); `ue` read as `ve?`; dot of `19_` (below the tail) not `19.`; `12.^?` added for the tick above `12` at the end of 34r line 3. Everything else was taken from pass 1. Treat the rest as single-pass.

## Special signs and letter-forms used
- `<rho>`: loop at mid-height, long tail dropping below and curling back; very frequent at line starts/ends, after `/`, after `R 2`, `22`, etc.
- `f.` (f with dot right) 34r line 1, 34v line 1. `R` (capital R with the large flourish): 34r L3, L4(?), P4, P6; 34v L2. `C^` (capital C, dot above): 34r P1; `C^e`: 34v V4. `p`, `pe`, `pe_?` (long-descender p, 34r P2 has a stroke under it). `m` / `m e`: 34r P7, P8. `n+`: 34v W7. `h`, `h?`, `he`: 34v V8, W10. `v` / `ve?`: 34r L1 and the `2 v 14` / `5+ v 14` cluster (a leading dash joins the previous sign). `P<bar>?`: 34r L8. `A` (capital A): 34v W1 first sign.
- `0` and `0+`, `0<bar>`: a circle (sometimes with + after, sometimes with bar above) occurs often, not in the guide's digit set. Unsure if it is the `0` of 20/10 written alone or a sign of its own. Written as `0` throughout.
- `2<bar>`, `9<bar>e`, `12<bar>`, `16<bar>`, `18<bar>`, `22<sign:above...>`: bars above; the 22 and the 18 on 34r L1 carry a larger loop-with-bar flourish (not a plain bar).
- Double marks: `11+e` is used where a `1 1+` is followed by a hook (cross and hook both). The guide allows only one mark after. These are the same cluster each time: it may be a `15`/`5+` squashed form. Unresolved.
- `/` virgula in cipher, with trailing dot.

## Every `?` (location, reason)
- 34r heading/clear: `Temiendo?` (m or n), `Yo el Rey?`, `xvij?` (date numeral, bar above).
- 34r L1: `ve?` (u-like sign with hook; could be 4e or 14e).
- 34r L2: `5? 8` (last two signs; could be `2 8`/`8 8`).
- 34r L3: `11+e?`, `12.^?` (tick above 12). 34r L5: `0+?`, `19_`.
- 34r L8: `P<bar>?`, `<sign:d-like...> v?` (last two signs "du").
- 34r P2: `pe_?` (underline under p, could be a dot below or a stroke). 34r P3: `3?`. 34r P9: `<rho>?`.
- 34v V6: `4<bar>?`. V7: `5e?`. W1: `17^?`. W9: `5^?`. W10: `h?`.
- A dot below/right of a 3 or 13 (`13_`, `3_`) is usually a dot below the tail; checked on the zooms for some, not all.

## Hardest places
1. The `15e` / `11+e` / `1ʒ+` family (S-shaped 5 squashed after a 1 with hook): single most frequent doubtful cluster; I may have mixed `15e`, `11+e`, `11e` and `12+`/`17+` (the 2 and 7 differ only by the stroke at the top).
2. `12` vs `17` vs `17+`: low-contrast on many lines. I read by the flat top (7) vs curved top (2) at the strip scale; not zoom-checked everywhere.
3. 9e vs <rho> vs 9: all three are g-like; loop-with-curled-tail = `<rho>`, open tail = `9`, g plus small hook = `9e`. Boundary cases: 34r L10 `19`, 34v W6 `2 9 2<bar>`.
4. Dots: the dots between lines (above 5, below 20, below 13) are marked where the distance was clear; the `20_` dot (under the 0) is consistent and probably real.
5. The 34r P4 stretch "9e 4+ 17" vs "9e 9e 4+ 17": written the shorter way after the zoomed overview.
6. Line ends on 34v are cut at the gutter; signs shown as `1`, `5`, `19`, `h?` at the edge may be partial.
7. Date on 34v: the day numeral under a bar, `xvij?`; year `157` then cut. The next digit is partly visible in the f035r sliver but not readable.
8. Signatures: "Yo el Rey" and "Ant. Perez" are flourished; readings are by shape only.
