# Reader B notes (blind transcription, ES 132 ff. 198r-199r)

Method: Gallica IIIF native-resolution crops (about 1400-1550 px wide, 640 px tall, 3 lines each, left and right halves overlapping by about 150 px) saved in sources/cache/reader-B-crops/. No key or other repository file opened. No upscaling is available (server 404s on >100%), so marks are read at native size only.

Passes: (1) left to right per crop; (2) halves joined by matching overlap tokens, which also checked for dropped or doubled tokens at the seam. The right-to-left re-read of each line was only partial (done where seams or marks were doubtful), not a full independent second reading. Treat marks (dots above/below, hats) as lower confidence than digits.

Cipher token counts (approximate; standalone <comma>/<tick>/<blot> not counted): 198r about 267 (21 lines with cipher, 24 body lines); 198v about 318 (27 of 29 lines); 199r about 170 (13 lines). Total about 755.

Conventions I added: `2H` = digit 2 followed by an H-shaped sign (distinct from a closed 4, which also occurs); `H` alone also occurs; `<sign:Sigma>` = Sigma/E-like sign; `y` = y-shaped cipher letter with tail (vs clear conjunction y written 7-shaped, kept inside [[ ]]); `y?` where 7-like; `24?` = 2 plus a cross with an elongated lower stem (could be 2+, 24 or 2H). `vo` is read as two letters v,o in the cipher stream (very frequent). `<tick>`/`<comma>`: small stroke before / comma-like mark after a token, not classified.

Ambiguous clear-vs-cipher: `ra` (after "8 3", "3", "9+ 3") taken as clear [[ra]]; `hu` (198v l.6), `Ha` (198v l.22), `qua` (198v l.9) uncertain; circled q written (q).

Hardest readings: 198r l.5 `p o+?` (p plus looped sign with cross); 198r l.8 `S<hat> C<hat>_? 1 C61?` (flourishes, 6 vs 1 split); 198r l.12 the two signs after `20` (u-like, 4-like with loop); 198r l.15 `22+?` and the blotted `5_`; 198v l.20 `22p?` and the blotted R-like sign; 198v l.29/14 gutter-shadowed line ends; 198v l.3 and l.14 `10?`, `10+? 1?`. The `2H` / `24` / `2+` distinction throughout is uncertain. Dots above vs below the token (`19^`, `10_`) are frequently hard to decide.

Unclassified marks: long horizontal stroke after `G<hat>` (198r l.19); ink blots (198r l.15, 199r l.8) which may hide a token; faint text in gutter shadow at ends of 198v lines 2-4, 13-14, 17-19, 21, 27-29 (readings marked `?`). Lighter-ink tokens on 199r l.6 and 198v l.10-11 may be show-through or later ink.
Clear text: transcribed roughly (diplomatic spelling not checked); postscript block and subscription at lower left of 199r transcribed approximately.
