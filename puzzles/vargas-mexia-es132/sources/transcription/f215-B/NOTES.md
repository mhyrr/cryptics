# f215-B notes (blind reader, reverse order)

## Extent
One letter. Starts f. 215r (heading "el Rey" with a mark above, salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia" on the same line as the first
cipher line). Cipher block: 10 manuscript lines on f. 215r, last ends with `/`. Then 5 lines of clear Spanish
and a further 5 clear lines on 215r (He visto ... confirmacio dello). Ends on f. 215v: 3 clear lines (yo os agradesco ...
Pardo A tre de Julio 1579), the King's signature (Yo el Rey), and a countersignature at the right.
f. 215v is otherwise blank; the blurry lower text is show-through of 215r (mirror image) and was ignored.
f. 216r: blank (ink flecks, show-through, number 216). f. 216v: address leaf: "Por el Rey" with a cross above, "Juan de Vargas Mexia",
and a docket along the gutter, upside-down: "13. de Julio de 1579". Not cipher.
Cipher lines: 10 (all on f. 215r).

## Countersignature
Read as "Ant.o Perez?" shape: flourished A with long loops, then "ut." and a figure like "83", long sweeping tail.
Best reading: Antonio Perez (the "83" may be a Pz flourish); uncertain.
Date line read as "Pardo A tre de Julio 1579" ("tre" abbreviated, over i-shape); the "13" is confirmed by the docket on 216v.

## Passes
Pass 1: top to bottom, left to right in two half-width native crops (1500 px) per 4 lines. Pass 2: not a fully independent
bottom-to-top, right-to-left re-read; I only rechecked line joins between left and right crops (overlaps agree) and the
marks above on lines 10 to 1. Changes in pass 2: none that I can name beyond added `?`. Treat as a weak second pass.

- Letter forms used: o, m, n, d, e, S, u, y, b, l, q (written 9?), 2? as one-off "Z" after `6p` (line 1 `2 o m n`).
- `<tbar>?` on line ends where a short horizontal stroke follows 6 or 8 (lines 2, 5, 6, 7, 8).
- 3+ digit runs left joined: `316`, `256` (line 4); `356<hat>?` (line 8); `176?` (line 9). Curls: I treated `316` and `256` as joined curls; `356` too.
- Curl joined uncertain: line 4 `316 256`, line 8 `356`, line 9 `176`; line 9 `6 2` written apart.
- `?` tokens: see transcription (lines 1-10, many).
- `<sign:...>`: line 2 `7<sign:loop above>`; line 6 end `6+<sign:small 2 above>`.
- `<under>`: none seen on any number; I looked on the 30s (30, 31, 32, 35, 36) and saw no underline stroke.
- Marks above a number in the 20s-30s on line 5 start, `6 6<bar><cross>?`: a bar with a plus over the pair.

## Image problems
f. 215v: strong show-through, mirrored text; the signatures overlap it. f. 215r left edge carries bleed from the facing page
(numbers at gutter) and the right-hand dark smears around lines 1-4 (ink blots over signs, e.g. line 1 end, line 3 `24.`).
Right half of the page text is clear. 216v right-edge docket sits next to the gutter shadow.
Overall: my cipher reading is low-fidelity; the hand is cursive and many glyph clusters were ambiguous.
