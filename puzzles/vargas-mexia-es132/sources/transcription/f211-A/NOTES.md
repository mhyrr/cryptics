# f211-A notes (blind transcription, CIPHER3-GUIDE v2.2)

## Extent
- The letter begins on f. 211r (heading "El Rey", then the salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia" with the cipher running on from the same line)
  and ends on f. 211v (4 cipher lines, then the place and date, then the King's signature and the secretary's countersignature).
- f. 211r: heading + 25 further lines; 24 of them carry cipher (the salutation line included). Lines 18-19 of the page
  (counting the heading as 1) are clear Spanish. f. 211v: 4 cipher lines, 1 date line, 2 signatures.
- Cipher lines transcribed: 28 (24 on 211r, 4 on 211v). Clear-only lines: 3 on 211r (including "El Rey"), plus the date line and signatures.
- f. 212r: blank leaf (stamp "212" top right). Not transcribed.
- f. 212v: address leaf, rotated 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" (a small mark like ":" before "Por") and "A Juan de Vargas Mexia" with a flourish. No cipher.
- Date line as read: "Lorenço A Treº de Julio 1579." preceded, at the end of the last cipher line, by clear "de Sant" ("de Sant / Lorenço").
- Signatures: King "Yo el Rey" (my reading; long rubric with loops). Countersignature: "Ant." followed by an abbreviated surname,
  two small 8-like loops and a long flourish; read as Antonio Perez ("Ant.º Pz" or similar), surname itself not legible to me.

## Passes (guide section 6)
- Pass 1 (top to bottom, left half then right half, on strips of 3 lines, 440 px high, 1550 px wide, 850..2400 and 1950..3500, 380 px step): 
  20 strips on f. 211r (10 rows x 2 halves), 4 strips on f. 211v, plus 5 extra crops (2 zooms of the clear text on 211r, date line, King's signature, countersignature). f. 212r and 212v viewed at overview only, plus 1 rotated crop of 212v.
  Total viewed in pass 1: 24 strips + 5 extra crops + overviews.
- Pass 2 (opposite direction: 212v, then 211v then 211r, bottom to top, right half before left half), on strips offset by 190 px from pass 1 so that each line is centred:
  20 strips on f. 211r, 4 strips on f. 211v, plus the countersignature crop, King's signature crop and the rotated address crop.
  Total viewed in pass 2: 24 strips + 3 extra crops. Pass 2 was completed for every strip.
- Pass 2 changes: `14 1 10` became `14 10<hat>` (the "1" belongs to the 10); several `6 6` runs unified; "bar" read as mark above a dot (`6.<bar>`) instead of a dash sign; `y<bar>` replaced a tailed-y read as digit; several `256` curls re-read from "25 o"; `11 7p<hat> 66` unified between lines 14 and 15; `10 6` / `106<v>` re-read; `35` and `25.` distinguished at line 24.
- Strip height 440 and 3 lines per strip, but lines on f. 211r run to about 26 per page, so there are fewer than 25-40 strips; I did not downscale or upscale cipher strips (the two zoom crops of clear text were enlarged 150-160 percent; no cipher was read from enlarged crops).

## Every 3+ digit run left joined
- `206` (line 2, line 21), `156` (line 4), `226` (lines 5, 17, f211v 1), `316` (lines 3, 6, 13, 21), `326` (lines 8, 9), `256` (many), `246` (lines 14, 22, 24), `296` (line 26), `113<cross>` (line 20), `106<v>` (line 16), `236` (f211v 3), `166` (line 26), `246<bar>` (line 13), `256 4`-type runs written `256 4<hat>` as two tokens.
- All of these are two numbers or a number plus a closed curl; the curl rule was applied where the 6-shaped curl touched the number.

## `<sign:...>`
- Line 19 end: closed loop or lozenge with a dot, after the comma following "ofresiere".
- Countersignature description on f211v.

## `<under>`
- `31+<under>` on f211r line 21 (clear underline under "31+"). `30+<under>?` on f211v line 3: a hatched smudge or double stroke under 30+ (could also be show-through of the recto or a flourish).

## Things that may be clear Spanish or cipher letters (left unbracketed on purpose)
- `de` (lines 10, 12, f211v 3, 4): looks like the letters d and e, always in the cipher flow after a `q.` or `S.`. Only "de Sant" at the end of f211v line 4 is clear (bracketed, followed by "Lorenço").
- `dem` (lines 13 and 26) and `gil` (line 14): written as letters; they look like Latin letters in the cipher hand. If they are clear words the reader should say so.
- `[[de que loose]]` at the end of line 17 is clear script, but I could not make sense of "loose" (could be "lo ose" or a closing tilde); written diplomatically.
- `q.` appears as a letter form throughout. `L e q.` on f211v 1 opens with a large L.

## Image problems
- Heading "El Rey" on f211r: faint show-through of f211v ("Fo" blot at left) near it; "Juan de Vargas" has a strike or blot over "Vargas" ("var" overwritten or inkblot).
- f211v: right edge of the four cipher lines is cut by the black gutter band (x about 3320); the last glyph of lines 1, 2, 3 and "Sant" in line 4 is partly lost. Lower half of f211v shows strong show-through of the recto cipher (mirror image) behind and below both signatures; not transcribed.
- Frequent doubled hook after 24 ("24pp") read as `24p`; and a few marks above are tiny ticks that could be `<v>` or `<acute>`.
- The marginal stamp "261" at the top right of f211r and "212" on f212r are modern foliation, not transcribed.
- A stray ink blot below "ofresiere" (line 19), and another before "Por" on 212v.
