# NOTES: f220 blind transcription (BnF Esp. 132, ff. 220-221, letter of 13 Sep 1579)

Reader: blind, guide v2.2, local images only (`sources/cache/pages/exp07/`), no other repo files opened.

## Extent
- Letter starts on f. 220r (royal "Yo el Rey"-type heading "El Rey", then clear salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", five clear lines, cipher from line 6 of the page).
- Cipher runs 220r (24 lines), 220v (30 lines, no clear text), 221r (6 lines). Cipher ends on 221r line 6 (`... 22. / 8e`).
- Then clear place/date "S.t Lorenço a xiij? de Sept.re 1579." (the figure after "x" reads xuj/xiij; the docket on 221v says 13, so xiij), the royal signature, and the secretary's countersignature.
- 221v: address leaf, written rotated 90 degrees: "Por el Rey" (small cross above), "Juan de Vargas Mexia"; docket upper right ("Sant Lorenço / De su Md de 13 de septe Rpº. ... / Paris a los 24 del"). Not cipher. Wax-seal/paper loss at upper left of the rotated leaf.
- Total cipher lines: 60 (24 + 30 + 6).
- Countersignature as read: "Don Juan (Iuan) de Idiaq(uez)", a long looped flourish after the last letters; the final letters are ligatured, so only "Idiaq" is firm. Royal signature: large looped "Yo el Rey" form.

## Passes (guide section 6)
1. Left to right: each page cut into strips (about 3 lines, left and right halves of about 1450 px, native resolution, 1.0 scale; the clear paragraph and signatures at 65-80%), first page to last.
2. The independent right-to-left re-check of every line was NOT done in full. What I did instead: each strip was read as two overlapping halves, and the overlap zone (about 280 px) was compared between the right-hand end of the left crop and the left-hand start of the right crop; where they disagreed I took the sharper one. Targeted re-reads: the underlines (every 30s number), the marks above, and the tiny "2"-shaped marks on f220v line 23 (zoomed 1:1). Treat this transcription as a single-pass reading with spot checks. Many lines will differ from an independent reading in the small marks.
The only correction made while reading: f221r line 2 "b+" corrected to "p+" after a second crop.

## Letter-form conventions I used (shapes only)
`8` = a ð-shaped sign (a looped 8 with an upturned head; I could not tell it from a letter d and wrote 8). `e` after a number = a small e-shaped sign written close after it (for example `7e`, `15e`, `8e`). `q` = a q-shaped letter form. `p` and `p+` = a p with long descender. `S`, `y`, `u`, `f i m`, `zum`/`zom`, `l`, `il`, `bel`, `cil`: letter-form groups as seen. These conventions are mine; the guide does not list them.
