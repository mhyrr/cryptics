# f. 235-236 (3 Nov 1579) blind transcription notes (guide v2.2)

## Extent
- The letter begins at the top of f. 235r (cross above the heading, "El Rey", salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia", then
  7 clear lines, then cipher from the 8th line). It continues over f. 235v (30 cipher lines) and ends on f. 236r after
  6 cipher lines, with the place "del pardo", the date line "a iij de nouiembre 1579.", the King's "Yo el Rey" and,
  far below, the countersignature.
- f. 235r: 7 clear lines + 21 cipher lines. f. 235v: 30 cipher lines (no clear text). f. 236r: 6 cipher lines.
  Total 57 cipher lines.
- f. 236v: address leaf, written rotated 90 degrees (read from a rotated view): "Por (cross above) el Rey" /
  "A Juan de Vargas Mexia." Docket at top right "a 3. de no.re de 1579". Wax-seal remnant. A small signature-like
  flourish at lower right (rotated; not transcribed). Mirrored show-through of f. 236r is visible.
- Countersignature as read: "Don Juan de Idiaquez" (flourished; the middle letters are uncertain; shape: "Don", a
  tall-stroked "Iuan", "de", then "Idiaq" ending in a z-like tail with a large descending flourish).
- "Yo el Rey": large flourished signature with a long sweeping underline.

## Passes
- Pass 1: left to right, top to bottom, strips of 3 lines, left and right halves (1500 px wide, ~300 px overlap),
  native resolution. Strips viewed in pass 1: f235r 22 (3 clear-text strips x2, 7 cipher x2, bottom x2); f235v 22;
  f236r 6; plus detail crops (f235r 1, f235v 1 for the far-left start of line 23, f236r 2 signature crops, f236v 3
  overviews). About 54 images.
- Pass 2: opposite direction (last strip first, right half before left half), every strip re-viewed against the lines:
  f236r 6; f235v 22; f235r 14 cipher strips + the right halves of the 3 clear strips = 17. Total 45.
  Not done: the left halves of the three f235r clear-text strips were read once only (their right halves twice).
- Pass 2 changes were small: line-start flourishes written `L 4`, `L 6`; `<grave>` on f235r line 24 (page numbering
  below); `<tbar>` on f235r line 13; several clear-text words; `[[xom]]`.
- Honest caveat: the hand is dense, versos have heavy show-through, and this transcription is certainly noisy in marks
  above, in the 5 / 15, 2 / 7 and 6-curl calls. Many `?` are not recorded for every doubt.
- Line numbers in this file count manuscript lines on the page, 1 = first line (clear lines are lines 1-7 on f235r;
  the first cipher line is 8). In the transcription file the f235r lines appear in the same order.

## Image problems

  Heavy mirrored show-through from f235r over the whole page. Line 23 starts much further left than all others.
- f235r: show-through of f235v behind the clear lines; facing page f234v visible at the left edge (ignored).

## Every `?`
Use grep for `?` in transcription.txt (f235r 11 lines, f235v 15 lines, f236r 3 lines carry one or more), plus the
clear-text query `[fe?]` in f235r line 4. The heaviest doubt: line-start signs, the `S`/`5` forms, the `2`/`7` forms.

## Every `<sign:...>`
f235r l.1 cross above "El Rey"; f235r cipher line with `L 4 L 6` small 2 above a 15; f235v l.4 large J-shaped sign
with a top bar at line start; `<cut>` at the gutter (f235v lines 5 and many others); f235v l.19 small 2 above a 25;
f235v l.28 a cross inside a loop (linked to the descender of the previous line); f236r l.1 end-of-line flourish after
"760"; f236r l.6 long flourish before "lum".

## Every `<under>`
f235r `4<under>?` in cipher line 9 (a black blot under a 4, possibly just a blot); f235r `30<under>.` (cipher line
20, underlined 30); f235r `q<under>?` (stroke under q, cipher line 23); f235v line 9 `30p<under>` (clearly underlined).
Other 30s I saw carried no underline.

## Other doubts
- Flourished capital before "om"/"um" in cipher lines (f235v 1, 17, 24, 28; f235r 21) is written as seen:
  `[[Zom]]`, `[[Zum]]`; I did not decide whether the first letter is Q, C or Z. f235v 24 reads `[[Zom]] u.`.
- "dem" (f235v l.16) might instead be `S e m` cipher letters. "de" is clear several times inside cipher lines.
- `L` + digit pairs (`L 4`, `L 6`, `Lp`) are letter L plus sign; the L could be a flourished stroke of the 4 or 6.
- The digit written like a long "s" with a crossbar is always read as 5 (`15+`, `25+`).
- "y" with a long swooping tail occurs at the start of several lines.
- Clear line 4 on f235r: the word after "la de" is a tall flourished letter plus "e", read `[fe?]`.
- f235r clear line 3 "xxvj xxxJx": shape as written, not interpreted.
