# Reader B notes, BnF es. 132 ff. 11r-12v (Cipher 2 guide v1.1)

Blind shape transcription. Only the guide and the four page images were used. Pages were read in reverse order
(12v, 12r, 11v, 11r), lines in order within a page; `transcription.txt` is in manuscript order.

## Page map
Local files `sources/cache/pages/f011r.jpg`, `f011v.jpg`, `f012r.jpg`, `f012v.jpg` (about 3,553 x 5,452 px each).
Crops in `sources/cache/f11-B-crops/` (`v12_*`, `r12_L*`, `v11_L*`, `r11_L*`, `chk_*` = pass-2 crops, `z_*` = zooms).
Each line was cut as three overlapping strips (1250 / 1250 / 1000 px wide, 1100 px step, 200 px high), stacked.
Right edge: on 11v and 12v the text runs to the leaf edge or under a dark shadow band (a tab or overlapping leaf);
signs under the shadow are lost or marked `?`.

## Conventions I used beyond the guide
- Long s is written `s`; the scribe's `r` shaped like an `x` is written `r` ("hareis", "sereduzcan"). Clear text is otherwise
  diplomatic (no expansion), joined or split as the scribe does.
- Virgula `/` for the short slanted tick, `,` for a curved comma, `.` for a low dot after a word.
- A bar spanning two signs (11v line 26, over `19+ 13`) is written on the first only.
- A sign that carries both a dot below and a mark after (for example `5e` with a dot under it) is written with the mark
  after only; the dot is listed below.
- `<venus>` is used for the circle-with-stem-and-bar sign (and `?` where the bar is replaced by a dot).

## Letter-forms and special signs
- `f`: 11v l.1 (`f_`), l.20 (`f`); 12r l.6 (`f<bar>`); 11r l.24 (`f_`).
- `p` / `P`: `p+`, `pe`, `p^`, `Pe` on 11v l.1 (`p+`), 2 (`pe`), 6 (`pe`); 12r l.1 (`p^`, `Pe`), 3 (`pe`), 11 (`p+`), 18 (`p+`); 11r l.25 (`pe`);
  12v l.3 (`pe?`). It is always the p with long descender and a serif foot.
- `R`: `R+` or `Re` on 12r l.2, 11, 14, 17; 11v l.18 (`Re`), 21, 24; 12v l.12 (`R+`).
- `C`: `C^` on 12r l.2, 17; 11v l.14, 24; `Ce` on 12r l.22.
- `n`: `n^` on 11v l.27, 29 and 12v l.3; `n_` on 11r l.25 (twice).
- `v`: `v<bar>`, `v^`, `v^<bar>`, `v_`, `v+<bar>`, `ve` on 12r l.1 (`ve`), 10, 11, 19; 11v l.22 (`v^`), 29 (`ve`). Often with a long bar standing to the left of the v.
- `m`: `me` once, 11v l.1 ("m" with hook).
- `h`: `h+` once, 12r l.6.
- `<rho>`: 34 times (large loop with tail dropping below the line). `<venus>`: 6 times.
- `<sign:...>`: two, both line-end flourishes: 12v l.8 (terminal hooked stroke after "entendieredes") and 12r l.21 (heavy ink blot with a looped
  swash after the word at the end of the first line of the final paragraph).
- Numbers outside 2-23: none. `7` occurs (`7e`, `7+`, `7.`); `8` occurs (`8e`, `8.`, `8^`, `8_`); `9` as `9e`, `9+`, `9.`, `9^`, `9_`; `10` as `10`, `10.`.
- `20`/`22`: the shape "z o" is read `20`; where two small z-forms precede an o I read `2 20` (11v l.20, 26); on 12v l.9 I read `22+`.
  22 vs `2 20` vs `22 0` is a real ambiguity.

## Hardest places
1. The small sign with a loop and a stem and a dot or bar below (`<venus>`): 11v l.24, 25, 28; 11r l.26. Sometimes a bar, sometimes only a dot.
2. `5_?`: an S-form with a descending tail like a cedilla (12r l.2, 5, 14; 11v l.14 appears with dot). I read it as `5` with a dot below, not as `5e`.
3. 12r l.10-11 and 11v l.1: dot above vs. dot below is often decided by about 20 px; I wrote `?` where the dot sits between two lines.
4. 11v l.15: `20+?`: the 2 is a long horizontal dash with an upright at its left end, then `0+`. Possibly an elongated 2.
5. 11v l.2 and 4-6: signs at the right edge are under the shadow band; line 4 is truncated after `11 1?`, and line 6 after `14 pe`.
6. 12v l.9 and 12r l.3: `22+` / `2 20+` / `18+` vs `18e` (cross plus hook together on one sign).
7. 12v l.2: the last sign `11 11?` is partly cut by the leaf edge.
8. 12r l.21: a heavy ink blot with a swash follows the last word; I could not decide whether it is a sign or a signature-like flourish.

## Pass 2 (right-to-left re-check)

overlap zones (about 150 px at each joint) was read twice; (b) a right-to-left re-read at 140% of 12r l.5, 12r l.14, 11v l.17, 11v l.26, 11r l.26 (stacked
strips, rightmost first), plus extra zooms of 12v l.3, 11v l.15, 11v l.25, 12r l.3, 12r l.13, 11r l.24 for dots.

12r l.13 `11^ 18` resolved (zoom). No token counts changed.
