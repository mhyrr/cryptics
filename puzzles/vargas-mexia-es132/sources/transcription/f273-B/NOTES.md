They are pen-drawn bracket strokes, not cipher signs. The first pair encloses `4^ 12+ 11+ 10 18e`; the second `(` opens at the start of line 29 near the end `4^ 13 19. 19 C+` and its closing `)` is at the start of line 33 (so the span covers lines 29-33). Treat as uncertain pairing.

## Signs used that need comment
- `<venus>`: 273r line 1 (before the first `2`), line 21 (`<venus>+`), 274r line 8 (`<venus>+?`), 274r line 28 (`<venus>^`, `<venus>e`), 274v marginal line 2.
- `<sign:small round o with cross>`: a small circle with a cross; 273r lines 23, 27, 34; 273v lines 6, 24; 274r lines 6, 20.
- `<sign:o-like><bar>`: 273r line 1, first sign (a double-loop "oo"/"08" with a bar above); `<sign:small o with bar above>` 273r line 3.
- `<sign:tick above>`, `<sign:cross above>`, `<sign:thick vertical stroke>`: single occurrences, see lines.
- `v<bar>`: a `u`/`v`-shaped sign with a bar above (273v line 14, 274r line 17, and a few more); plain `v` without bar also occurs (273v, 274r). Both written as the guide's letter-form `v`.
- Letter forms used: `f` (a long f with a descender: 273r lines 1, 2, 17; 274r lines 2, 20, 27), `p` (a long p with a descender, often with `+` or a bar), `n` (n with a cross `n+`), `m` (274r lines 2, 6, 7), `R` (large capital R, very frequent, often followed by a 2 or 10), `C` (capital C, nearly always `C+`), `P` (capital P: `Pe`, `P_`), `<rho>` was NOT used: I wrote the large descender forms as `p` / `pe`; the guide's `<rho>` (a large free rho) did not appear separately as far as I could tell, but `18 pe` / `20 pe` shapes (a p with a tail after 18/20) are very frequent; a reader with the key may want to re-check whether those are `<rho>`.
- `g?`: 274v line 14 (a g-shaped sign that is not a clear 9).
- `7`: I wrote `7` for a sign like a hooked, cross-barred 7; very frequent in the form `7e` or `7e|3e` (a 7-like sign with a small hook that may be a 3 with a hook). The reading 7 versus 3 is uncertain for every `7e|3e`.
