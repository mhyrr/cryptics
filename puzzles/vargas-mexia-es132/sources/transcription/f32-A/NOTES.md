# f32-A notes (reader A, Cipher 3, ff. 32r-33r)

Images: local `sources/cache/pages/f032r.jpg`, `f032v.jpg`, `f033r.jpg` (3569x5452), native 1500x350 strips, L/R halves with ~450 px overlap, plus clear-Spanish strips. Guide v2 + addendum v2.1 only.

## Layout
- 32r: header "El Rey" (cross over it), addressee/opening in clear, a date list in digits, then 27 cipher lines in two paragraphs (break after the line ending `10 20 10<grave> 8p /`).
- 32v: 14 cipher lines (one paragraph break, after the `/` ending 5th line), then clear Spanish (6 lines) ending in a date line, royal signature flourish, and the secretary's name.
- Right ends of all lines on 32v are cut by the gutter/fold (seal strip); tokens there are lost or partial (marked `...`). The clear Spanish is also cut ("Fran...", "viage...", "cree...", "cobrar...", "propr...", "de M...", "157...").
- 33r: blank apart from show-through of 32v and the seal. No text.

## Conventions I chose
- Wavy tilde above a sign written `<v>` (same as a small v/hook above); I could not separate them reliably.
- Tick after a number (short stroke, like `ı`) written `+`; it is often reduced to a short tick. Whether it is + or 1 is uncertain everywhere (23+, 17+, 20+, 7+, 6+, 24+).
- Letter forms written as letters: `S i m`, `X a l`, `q u a m`, `T a`, `d e`, `l o`, `g i l`, `y i l`, `v e l`, `P a m`, `H a m`, `z o m`, `z u m`. Some of these (de, lo, quam) may be clear Spanish words in cipher hand; I did not bracket them.

## 3+ digit runs left joined

## Curl calls (joined) the most doubtful
`66`/`166`/`156` (curl after 6/16/15 touching, always joined); `65<bar>?` (6 then 5 or 65); `68<bar><cross>?` (6 + 8 or curl); `62?` (6 with dot beneath, then 2; 32r and 32v); `86`, `76` joined as written.

## Every `?`
- 32r: `68<bar><cross>?` (l.5); `11+<bar>?`; `29p?`; `11<hat>.?` (could be 12 or 11; same shape l.7, 32r l.20, 32v l.1); `65<bar>?`; `21?` ("2" + tick); `24p<bar>?`; `2p<v>?`; `113<bar><cross>?`; `7p<bar><hat>?`; `0<cross>?`; `14p?`; `62?`; `6+?` (second of two); 32v: `128<bar><cross>?`, `16<hat>.?`, `21?` (x3), `7p?`, `62?`, `143<bar><cross>?`.

## Other doubts
- `A` after "Hebrero ," is a clear A with a tick, or a cipher sign; written clear.
- `1<cross> S i m`: cross above a lone 1-like stroke before the S.
- `10` vs `20`: a z-shaped 2 before 0 often reads like 10; I wrote `20` when the 2 had a visible tail and `10` otherwise. Unreliable in places.
- Marks above are the least reliable; bars and hats attached by position, esp. 32r l.15 `7p<bar><hat>?` and doubled marks `<bar><cross>` on 68, 113, 128, 143.
- 32v l.2: a small z-like stroke over a bar over `6`; no table entry.
- 32v l.8: `356` has an underline stroke below.
- 32v l.4 and 13: `z o m` has a long X-shaped flourish beneath; not a separate sign as far as I can tell.
- A dot floats after the virgula on two paragraph-end lines (`. /`); written `. /`.
- A stray dot in the right margin beside 32r line 14.
- Line ends such as `22` at the end of 32r last line may carry a dot I could not see.

## Pass 2
Right-to-left re-check was done against the overlap zones of L/R halves (about 450 px per line) and re-views of the crops with ambiguous signs; changes from pass 1: `29p` hook query, `11<hat>` vs `12` doubt, `<v>` for all wavy marks, `+` for all tick-like stops, `7p<bar><hat>?` added. I did not re-view every strip a second time, so mark-above omissions are likely in a few lines.
