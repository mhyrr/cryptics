# NOTES f222-2 (es. 132 ff. 222-223, letter dated 13 Sep 1579), blind transcription

## Extent
- f. 222r: letter starts here. Heading "el Rey" (cross above the l), salutation "Juan de Vargas Mexia." in clear, 5 further lines of clear text (6 clear lines in all), then 19 cipher lines. Paragraph break after cipher line 16 (ends in a `/`).
- f. 222v: 10 cipher lines (breaks after line 2 and line 7, each ending in `/`), then a line beginning with cipher `26 6p` followed by the clear place and date (de S.t Lorenço a xiy. de septe 1579), then the King's signature and, lower right, the countersignature.
- f. 223r: blank, with mirror-image show-through of the address of 223v and a dark blot along the top edge. Not transcribed.
- f. 223v: address leaf: "Por el Rey" (cross above), "A Juan de Vargas Mexia", a small docket "23. de setembre de 1579" (vertical, right side) and "Dup.da" bottom left. Transcribed in [[ ]] only.
- Cipher lines: 19 (f. 222r) + 10 (f. 222v) + 1 mixed line = 30.

## Countersignature as read
"Don Iuº d Idiaquez" = Don Juan de Idiáquez. The "Don" and "Idiaq...z" are clear; the "Iuº d" (between them) is the least clear part (three or four minims and loops).

## Passes
- Pass 1 (forward): f. 222r clear text 6 strips (350 px) + 22 cipher strips (11 rows x L/R, page deskewed by 2.2 degrees, native scale), f. 222v 14 strips, plus ~10 close crops (700-1100 px wide, native) for date, signature, countersignature, address leaf, docket. About 52 strips.
- Pass 2 (opposite direction): all 14 strips of f. 222v (bottom to top) and all 22 cipher strips of f. 222r (bottom to top), re-read line by line. About 36 strips. The 6 clear-text strips of 222r were NOT re-viewed in pass 2 (only checked once at the top of the page). Pass 2 changed many tokens (q-shaped signs re-read as 9 vs 4, hook and bar placement, several joins).
- Reading quality is low for the cipher: the hand is small and cursive, and I am not confident in most lines. Treat every token as provisional; the line structure and the repeated groups (`76 25 d+ d`, `S u m`, `1833`) are more reliable than individual digits.

## Conventions I had to choose (guide gives no rule)
- The ∂-shaped sign (very frequent, usually followed by a dot) is written `d` / `d.`.
- A small e-like curl with a tail after a base is written as the hook mark `p` (`7p`, `15p`, `28p`). A large free-standing hook with descender is written as a lone `p`.
- b-like sign (ascender plus curl) is written `6` (own token, e.g. `1 6+`, `6p`); `16.` at line end read as joined 1+curl with dot.
- q-shaped sign: 4 and 9 look alike; I wrote 9 for most. 4 vs 9 is doubtful on every line.
- Letter forms seen: `m`, `y`, `S`, `p`, `u`, `e`, `o`, `d` (e.g. `S u m`, `S e m`, `S o m`, `m y`).

## Three-or-more digit runs left joined
236, 246, 286, 266, 176, 166, 356, 241, 121, 231, 1833 (twice on 222r, once as `1833.` on a 222r line 15/16; four digits), 226, 236 (222v), 246, 286, 166. Also 86, 76, 12.. two-digit curl joins (`86`, `76`, `62`, `65`, `66`, `96`) left joined; `65`/`66` are not valid numbers so are probably 6+5, 6+6 or curl cases.

## Question marks and doubtful marks
`3 2<under>?` (222r line 7: underline under 32 possibly under 3 alone), `S<bar>?` (222v line 4: the "S 0 0" group with bar above, could be a clear numeral 500), `d<2dot>?` (222v line 6), `<sign:long-tailed-stroke-like-f>` at the end of 222r line 7, 222r line 2 `7+<acute>`. Not marked but doubtful: every `<hat>`/`<v>`/`<acute>` distinction, all `p` vs `e`, all 4 vs 9.

## `<sign:...>`
long-tailed stroke at the end of 222r line 7; marks in the margins and flourishes of the heading, signatures and address (described inline).

## `<under>`
222r line 7 `3 2<under>?`; line 19 `3<under> 4+p` (underline under 3 4+); 222v line 3 `35+<under>` (clear).

## Image problems
- Lines on 222r slope upward to the right by ~2 degrees; I deskewed for cropping (rotation only, no scaling).
- 222r: a thin diagonal stroke between `25+` and `28` on cipher line 9 (crease or ruling), not transcribed. Pale grey blotch left of cipher lines 15-16. Facing page at the gutter is cropped out.
- 222v: right edge of the writing runs into the dark page edge (line 6 ends `S o m` against the edge, line 8 ends `18<bar>` at the edge, possibly cut). Show-through of the reverse is faint.
- 223r: show-through mirror of the address; docket on 223v is faint and partly obscured by a grey smear at its left end ("setembre" ending uncertain).
- Date: "a xiy." with an overline reads as xiij (x + i + y for ij); not a separate date I could read as anything else. "septe" has a raised mark and an overline running over "1579".
- Clear-text doubtful words: "tuuxo" (222r line 4, perhaps "tuuo"), "xvuij" (line 3), "dexxuij" (line 2).
