# Cipher keys for BnF es. 132 (Vargas Mexia), as published by Tomokiyo

Source (SECONDARY/CLAIMANT-adjacent, Tomokiyo is a specialist; keys derive from Devos 1950 PRIMARY-ish print): S. Tomokiyo, Cryptiana, "Finding the Keys to Philip II's Cipher Letters to Juan de Vargas Mexia", saved 2026-10-03; local copy `../cache/cryptiana/spanish3D.htm` plus key images in the same folder. Files here are transcriptions of the images and page text, nothing more. Glyph symbols that cannot be typed are described in words in the `symbol` column. Rows marked `conjecture` are bracketed in the source or flagged by the page as Tomokiyo's conjecture; `tomokiyo-addition` is what the page says he added.

Files: `cipher1.tsv`, `cipher2.tsv`, `cipher3.tsv` (Cp.30), `cipher3_borgia_variant.tsv` (his independent "Cipher with Don Juan de Borgia (1579)" reconstruction of the same cipher), `cipher4.tsv`.

Row counts: C1 136, C2 34, C3 (Cp.30) 76, C3 Borgia 49, C4 50.

## Cipher 1 (1577)
Mixed alphabet of non-numeric glyphs for some letters (a b c d e g h o p u x y z; none shown for f i l m n q r s t) plus a 2-digit syllable table (consonant + vowel, numbers 6-100) and above/after marks for -l -n -m -r -s and medial -r- -l- -h- -u-. Page: "Representation of syllables by two-digit figures is similar to Cp.28 (1577)."
- Dates/folios: "Used in the first letter from the king to Vargas Mexia in the volume (f.3) dated 16 December 1577." Only f.3 is labelled Cipher 1 in the page's table of contents.
- Printed: "reconstructed by Devos (1950), p.418, from Vargas Mexia's letter of 12 December 1577", supplemented "with information kindly provided by Demetrio Martín Vilela".
- Rules quoted: "The substitution table above does not include some symbols in Devos (1950) that could not be confirmed in my specimen. In particular, Devos assigns 12 for 'a', 21 for 'e', and 31 for 'u'." (These collide with table values 12=co, 21=fu, 31=hu; encoded as separate rows, certainty printed.) "Devos records code elements with figures with a plus sign above (61 for 'paz', 85 for 'qual', 93 for 'rey') and monosyllables (bis for 'reyno', nos for 'Vra. Magd.', to for 'hermanos')."
- Image-only legends: null (small 2 over box; tilde over box), arch above = "double the consonant", plus/bar above = "code elements", curl = "numbers"; examples: theta+dot = en, 80 with slash = tra, 12 with small circle = cho.
- Unencoded/ambiguous: exact glyph identities (described only); a "[b,v]" note above the u column in the image is unexplained; no h-row/zero issues; "(At first, I thought use of diacritics was not consistent with this cipher...)" only history.

## Cipher 2 (1578)
Simple monoalphabet 2=a, 3=b, ... 23=z (alphabet a b c d e f g h i l m n o p q r s t u x y z) plus vowel marks after the symbol; page: "the simplest of the four, and possibly for that reason, it was short-lived." Cluster symbols exist (br cr fr gr pr tr) with letter-forms f (cr), n (pr), p (tr); br/fr/gr cells blank.
- Dates/folios: "Used at least from January to March 1578." TOC: f.11 (24 Jan 1578), f.17 (8 Mar), f.22 (dup), f.26, no.13 (dup of f.26, no folio given), f.34 (16 Mar), and f.273 (no.124, undated).
- Printed: not in Devos as far as the page says; "I cracked this in August 2020."
- Rules quoted: marks (image) rho-like after = -a, + = -e, dot below = -i, dot above = -o, dot right = -u, overbar = "null?". Page convention: "+ ... E; e (e or ρ-like sign) ... A; . (underdot) ... I; ^ (overdot) ... O; dot to the right ... U; ~ (overbar) ... null?" with counts 137, 128, 93, 84, 13, 8. "numbers occurring in the ciphertext were limited to 1-22" (table runs to 23).
- Unresolved on the page: "I am still unsure of the reading of '6 with diacritics' as in 'los lieos' and 'so ee'. The latter occurs multiple times, and does not seem to be an enciphering error." Why cr="f", pr="n" (the page reads p+ as tr-syllable); how the clusters are written beyond the 'p' tr example.

## Cipher 3 = Devos Cp.30 (1578-1580)
Numeric alphabet with a second number for vowels, plus extra row-2/row-3 variants, 2-letter and cluster numbers (30-37, T), and vowel and consonant-appended marks ("symbols that occur with diacritics can be known to be consonants"; "15 represents l, 15+ la, 15. le").
- Dates/folios: "Used at least from March 1578 to May 1580." Also used by Bernardino de Mendoza and Don Juan de Borgia. TOC folios: 32, 37, 39, 41, 44, 46, 50, 54, 58, 60, 62, 71, 73, 75, 79, 81, 83, 85, 89, 93, 103, 105, 113, 119, 129, 132, 134, 138-146, 150, 161, 165, 167, 171, 174, 181, 185, 195, 200, 202, 206-215, 218-231, 233-241, 245, 247, 251-271, 275 (not re-verified; see page TOC).
- Printed: "printed as Cp.30 in Devos (1950)" (page number not given); Tomokiyo's independent reconstruction is "Cipher with Don Juan de Borgia (1579)"; "so did Bernard Allaire and Devos himself".
- Cp.30 image: marks + -a, dot -e, curl -i, curl-with-red-hook -o, T-bar -u; / -l, \ -m, ^ -n, v -r, overbar -s. Borgia image adds: two dots above = double consonants, + above = code word, and an unlabeled small v above a box beside the -r hook.
- Ambiguities: meaning of Cp.30 row 2 and row 3 (letter-forms printed as the plain letter, with / \ Λ v - as row 3 variants under l m n r s) is not explained in the text; underlined cluster numbers (cl, fl, gl) unexplained; red annotations in the Cp.30 image (dot, o-hook) unexplained; the Borgia version brackets dr=[32], gr=[34], [5], [14], [19], [26] as unconfirmed and writes h as the letter "h" where Cp.30 has 12; Cp.30 gives ch=T (Borgia omits ch, cl, fl, gl, vr).

## Cipher 4 (1578-1579)
Base numbers 1-23 for the 22-letter alphabet (a=12, b=11 ... o=23 ... z=14), extra letter-form symbols, cluster letters (B, C, f, g, p, t, with bracketed conjectures), vowel marks after/over the symbol, and a few word codes. Page: "similar to Cg.8 (1572) in that two-letter syllables are represented by a base symbol (arabic numeral) and a vowel indicator and that the base symbol is the same as in the cipher alphabet."
- Dates/folios: "Used at least from September 1578 to April 1579"; image title "(1578-1579)". Also used by Antonio Perez. TOC folios: 87 (13 Sep 1578), 123 (20 Oct), 154 (4 Dec), 157 (8 Dec), 179 (26 Jan 1579), 198 (15 Apr 1579).
- Printed: "reconstructed by Devos (1950), p.422, from Vargas Mexia's letter of 16 May 1579."
- Quoted rules: "In the substitution alphabet above, 'a' and 'v' (cursive) representing 'u' and 'o', respectively, are my additions. Double consonants 'fl', 'gl', 'pl', 'br', 'cr' are my conjectures by analogy from Cg.8 (some of which I confirmed by actual examples)." "Devos (1950) notes two letter symbols: 'vo' for 'Vra. Magd.' and 'o' for 'hu', to which I may add 'co' for 'carta' (f.123)." "According to Devos' reconstruction, a haček (ˇ) is a null, and a plus sign (+) or a caret (^) over a character doubles the letter. In the ciphertexts I examined, a caret (^) seems to be used to indicate a null." "symbols for bla, cla are formed by combining capital letters B, C with a vowel indicator, while symbols for gra, fra, pra, tra are formed by combining small letters g, f, p, t and a vowel indicator." Image marks: dot after = -a, + = -e, dot below = -i, "6" after = -o, dot above = -u, caret above = "null?".
- Ambiguities that matter: (1) caret above is "null?" in the image, doubling per Devos in the text, and "seems" null in the ciphertexts; encoded as two rows, unresolved. (2) The "+" above for doubling is not in the image. (3) Code "o" = hu collides with letter-symbol o (23). (4) Meaning of three-letter cluster for [F] [G] [P] [b] [c] is conjecture; their vowel-mark combinations are untested here. (5) Which of y, d, o (extra letters) are Devos-printed is inferred only from the page naming a and v as additions. (6) The dot-above = -u mark vs. a dot above as a null/other not distinguished. (7) Whether other glyphs ("o" and "v") are cursive forms or the Latin letters is not stated beyond "(cursive)".
