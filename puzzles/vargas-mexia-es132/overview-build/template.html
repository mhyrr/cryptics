BnF Espagnol 132 · Philip II & Antonio Pérez to Juan de Vargas Mexía · 1577–1580

The Vargas Mexía Letters

Philip II's secret letters to his ambassador in Paris, 1577–1580, written in cipher and called "imposible descifrar" in 1844. Read now with published keys, they follow the King and his secretary Antonio Pérez through the murder of Juan de Escobedo, the death of Don John of Austria, and the months before and after Pérez's arrest.
