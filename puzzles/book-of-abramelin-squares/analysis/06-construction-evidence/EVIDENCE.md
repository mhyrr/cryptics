ambiguity abstains. No evidence inspected supplies a rule assigning all
remaining inner orbits, and we found no source-verified selector that chooses
among these constructions before seeing letters.

## Decision and precise next step

Do not build or score a historical solver on this provisional audit. There is
enough to motivate central-line and overlap hypotheses, but not enough to call
one an independently specified construction. The immediate missing evidence
is the **actual layout of the cited source examples**, followed by an
**answer-independent applicability and placement rule**. The deferred lexical
and name installments, if accessible, or explicit construction instructions
could resolve the latter. A deliberately exploratory rule could also be
specified from verified discovery diagrams, with its limitations stated.

After that decision, write a separate specification for inputs, caption mapping,
all lexical alternatives, normalization, paths, overlaps, symmetry, corruption
handling and falsifiers. Preselect a contiguous dictionary block including
misses, record all exposure, and freeze it with masks and code before evaluation.
Use the unchanged experiment 05 whole-orbit and interior-only tests, competing
completions and controls. Reused Mathers grids remain internal evaluation.

No contiguous evaluation sample is acquired now; doing so would not repair
the missing construction inputs. The earlier comparison edition remains a
separate attribution question and was not pursued. No independent interior
prediction or incomplete-square prediction is issued. This is **no adequate
