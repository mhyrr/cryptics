## Elementary generators

Fixed-step cyclic row generators: **0/81** exact fits. Even allowing a separately chosen rotation for each row: **0/81**.

Exact boundary-arithmetic fits (A=0, modulo 26): `row_+1_column_+1`: 0/81; `row_+1_column_-1`: 0/81; `row_-1_column_+1`: 0/81; `row_-1_column_-1`: 0/81.

This rejects those exact families in this export. It does not rule out other alphabets, richer generators, or witness corruption.

Transpose symmetry alone cannot infer interior letters from that boundary. The frequency baseline selects the most frequent visible letter, with alphabetical tie-breaking; it is compared on the identical cells. No claim of statistical significance is made.
