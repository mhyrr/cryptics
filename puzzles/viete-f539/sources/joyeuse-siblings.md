# News items per letter (for cribs)

## f. 544 (to Montpezat, Rome 15 [Feb])
- Joyeuse has learned via his man in Madrid that someone ("vous", struck) was expected there about the 12th of last month, so news is hoped soon.
- Everything depends on the addressee's location ("le premier mouvement"); the negotiation's resolution depends on what the addressee decides.
- Pope: continues in the firm resolution declared publicly in consistory before Nevers left: not to receive the King of Navarre, given his impenitence, the scandal, and the peril to religion; repeated to Joyeuse and Senecey in audiences.
- Pope says he awaits, for the Infante's marriage and the forces to be expected, what the Catholic King (Spain) writes after speaking with the addressee.
- Nevers (Henri IV's envoy) has already left Rome. No mention of Villars, Rouen, truce, money, cipher.

## f. 551 (to Archbishop of Lyon, undated, Rome)
- Nothing much new since last letter; several audiences with the Pope.
- Pope: very concerned for "noz affères"; holds that Navarre has not shown enough signs of penitence to be trusted with the religion of the whole kingdom.
- Pope satisfied with Mayenne's "sages deportements"; will defer to Mayenne's opinion and judgment; zeal for the salvation and conservation of France; will act with maturity and without precipitation, awaiting advice from Catholics.
- League ambassadors: Joyeuse and the Baron de Senecay spoke with the Pope "hier" about Mayenne's wish to honor the archbishop with "la dignité" (cardinal's hat); Pope's answer "fort bonne"; particulars to be sent by Senecay.
- Lyon affairs may improve through the archbishop's conduct; Joyeuse has not dared to press the hat matter yet, fearing a refusal.
- Cipher: "Si j'avois le bien d'avoir un chiffre avec vous": no cipher in use between them; letter is in clear. No truce, Villars, Rouen, money, Spain.

## f. 553 (to duc de Joyeuse, Rome 15 febvrier)
- Receipt of three letters (Toulouse the 12th; Montgiscard 14 Dec.; Narbonne 1 Jan.).
- Affairs of "Monsieur le marquis" and Montpezat in Languedoc; Joyeuse's presence in Rome claimed useful; "l'ordre de Languedoc".
- Offices: Chalabre / Seneschal of Lauragais; his own apprehension about "commission et chambres" (judicial commission/courts), advice to persist, with cipher groups.
- Advice to answer someone firmly or satisfy them "a quelque pris que ce soit" lest persons (cipher groups) "prennent occasion de vous courir sus".
- Truce: surprised the truce was not yet signed on 20 January; eager for news.
- verso: help for the Cordeliers of Narbonne and the Capuchins (awaiting the next ordinary courier); will decide on staying or leaving Rome from Mayenne's first despatches; waiting to see what becomes of "toutes ces affères"; writes in favour of Mr de Carcassonne, to have the Pope write about him; "un petit billet en chiffre" mentioned.
- No Villars, Rouen, Pope on Navarre, money or Spain in the legible text.
