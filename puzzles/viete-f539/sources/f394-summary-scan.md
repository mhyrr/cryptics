## 3. Letter of 15 February 1594 from Rome

Yes, but only in outline. The heading (f. 394r) lists the Agocchi letters to the Cardinal of Piacenza as dated "xxix Janvier, v[?] et xv febvrier 1594". The summary is a single continuous digest keyed by date markers; the 15 February material is NOT set off by its own heading, so identification of its extent is by the "Le [xv?] de febvrier" marker (f. 398v bottom). If that reading is right, the 15 Feb part runs f. 398v bottom to f. 399v end, and says, in digest:

- f. 398v-399r: audience of 15 Feb [?]: Cardinal de Joyeuse, the Baron de Senecey and the Abbé d'Orbes before the Pope. The Baron argues "il n'y auoit moyen d'achemmer les affaires sans le Duc du Maine et qu'il ne falloit point le mescontenter"; the end of the discourse is that "le dernier remede sera qu'il faudra penser a Nauarre et le mettre en consideration". The Pope answers harshly, in Italian: "Non voler sentir parlare di Navarra in modo alcuno etiandio se le cose fossero del tutto disperate", and says he knows for certain that otherwise "non seulement il perdroit la France mais l'Italie et tout le pays de la chrestienté et souffriroit toutte espece de violence".
- f. 399r: reply to the solicitors of the Legate's congé (leave to return): "il n'a esté faict Cardinal et n'a eu le chappeau que pour les affaires de France", "le pis qui luy peult aduenir est vne prison", "aequistarasi immortal gloria appresso di Dio et appresso il mondo".
- f. 399v: "Et sur les lres qu'il auoit escriptes qu'en tout cas il estoit resolu a sortir de Paris demander pour sourete [?] vn passeport de Nauarra ... ainsi sont les Catholicques mal assistez" and the reply: forces "de dix mil Vualons et autant de Suisses oultre cinq mil cinq cens qui s'estoient reti[?]rez ... six mille Napolitains ... cinq mille Espagnols ... en Bretaigne ... forces du Dumayne et celles du Duc de Savoye"; Spanish three million gold with the Genoese; 200,000 ducats a month "remis en Flandres a continuer iusques au Mois d'Octobre prochain"; the Legate is advised to take these promises as cash and have courage, and to tell the Pope he has always governed prudently; ends mid-clause with Italian "il sostegno di Parigi et di tutto il stato". (Ends at the bottom of f. 399v; f. 400r onward is blank.)

(I have no firm evidence that the f. 399v material is still within the 15 Feb letter rather than a separate item; the page shows no new heading.) The text also records Aldobrandini and "Possevin" (Antonio Possevino, Jesuit) matters (ff. 397v-398v) probably from the 31 Jan / 5 Feb parts; and the "mauvaises nouvelles de Meaux, Aix et Pontoise" (the League cities' submissions to Henri IV, f. 398r) that the Duc de Guyse/Dumaine are suspected of engineering "pour ruyner la ligue et principalement son nepueu" (an inked bracket in the margin).
