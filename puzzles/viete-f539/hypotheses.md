# Hypotheses

Numbered, never renumbered. Status: `open` / `supported` / `weakened` /
`refuted` / `superseded by Hn`. Every hypothesis names the test that would
move it. Results are recorded in `research.md` and in `analysis/`.

| # | Hypothesis | Status | Test that would move it | Evidence so far |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | F. 539 is a three-layer cipher: a homophonic letter alphabet of unmarked signs, a marked-sign syllabary or small nomenclature, and numeric code groups for names. | open | Blind transcription: do marked and unmarked signs have different frequency profiles? Do numbers repeat in name-like positions? | Bourdeau's reading of the structure (one reader). |
| H2 | F. 539 is breakable ciphertext-only the way Marmont was (annealing on French n-grams). | refuted (for the text as it stands) | Reopens only with more same-key text (≥ ~700–1,400 tokens in all) or seeded values: rerun `01-annealing` with them. | 2026-10-01: gate passes on length (344 ≥ 300), but the matched control fails, median 0.093 against 0.986 for the Marmont-shaped positive control (`analysis/01-annealing/`). | H3 | F. 539 shares a key with f. 555 (Lasry), f. 530 (Pelissier–Joyeuse) or the Caulet–Joyeuse cipher of August 1593. | weakened | Shape comparison of the sign inventories; trial decode with a published key. | Tomokiyo and Bourdeau say no for all three. |
