## General limits

- The strips are low resolution for this hand: the strokes are thin and small. Many marks above (`^o` vs
  `^tick`, `^wave` vs caret) and dots inside rings (`circdot` vs `o`, `moondot` vs `c`) are at the limit of
  what I can see. A third look was not taken: the half strips are the highest resolution available and a
  third view of the same pixels would not resolve them.
- `qj` vs `q`: the bowl-with-straight-stem sign is very common, nearly always before `s` or `r`. Most
  copies seem to end in a small turn to the left at the foot, so I wrote `qj` throughout. Where I could not
  see the foot at all I wrote `qj?` or `q?`. A reader who wrote `q` for some of them is not necessarily wrong.
- `bbar^o`: every "b with ring" on the page (lines 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12 x2) looks like the sheet's
  `bbar^o` (a t-like stem with a crossbar and a bowl, ring above-right), so all are `bbar^o`. Plain `b`
  (no crossbar, no ring) occurs in runs `b b` and is kept as `b`.
- `sigma` vs `s`: I used `sigma` for the larger c/5/G shape with a tick in its mouth (`Ϛ`), `s` for the
  plain small s. Where I could not tell: `s|sigma` (line 6).
- `z|2`: the small sign before `3`, `h y` or `^o`; its top looks between flat and round. Written `z|2` or
  `z^o|2^o` everywhere I met it (lines 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11).
