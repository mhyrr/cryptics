# Reader A — V140-1r — notes

Strips used: V140-1r-01 to -11 (a and b halves). 33 manuscript lines. Line numbers below count from the
first row after the page comment.

## General

- **Resolution.** The half strips are small. Marks above (`^o`, `^tick`, `^wave`, `^s`) and the difference
  between `circdot` and `theta`, `t` and `bbar`, `q` and `g`, `r` and `2` were often at the limit of what
  could be seen. Doubt marks are given where a second reading was really possible, not on every small sign.
- **"c f" read as one `f`.** Many times a small open hook at x-height runs straight into a long stroke with
  a crossbar that goes below the line (lines 10, 11, 18 twice, 19, 22, 23, 27, 29, 30, 31). On the first pass
  I wrote these as `c f`. On the second pass I took the hook to be the hooked top of `f` (guide 3a: "hooked
  top, crossbar, stem far below the line") and wrote one `f`. If the hook is a separate `c`, each of those
  places gains one token. Where a clearly separate `c` or `e` stands before an ordinary `f` (lines 19 and 28
  `d e f c`, line 21 `e f c`, line 8 `e f r`), I kept two signs.
- **`p^tick`.** Where a short stroke stands over a `p`, I checked whether a tail from the line above ends
  there. Line 2 `p b b`: the stroke over the p is the foot of the long `f` of line 1, so no mark. Elsewhere
  I kept `p^tick`; the ones I am least sure of carry `?` (lines 5, 11, 25).
- **Tall raised `delta`.** A delta-like sign (ring at the bottom, stem rising, flag bent left) is written
  tall, its foot often at or above the top of the small letters: lines 5, 13, 20 (twice), 24, 26, 29, 30, 33,
  and line 2. It often stands right after a `3` or `2`. I wrote it as a sign `delta` every time, not as a
  mark `^delta`, because in line 24 its foot is on the line like its neighbours. If it is a mark, it belongs
  to the sign on its left.
- **`ex` versus `e` + cross.** Where a loop sits on a cross with one long stroke down-left, I wrote `ex`.
  Lines 3 and 26 have an e-like loop followed by a cross with dots round it; I wrote `e? xdots` (it could
  be one `ex` with stray dots).
- **`y` versus figure `7`.** Several `y` have one flat top arm instead of two arms meeting (lines 7, 16, 25,
  31). The guide warns of a `7`. I wrote `y` and marked the clearest one `y|7` (line 16).
- **"1 with a cap"** (lines 8 twice, 19) was written `arch`. A plain short stroke (`r i x` in lines 2, 5, 10,
  11, 32) was written `i`.

## Clear words

- Line 1: `[[Dopo lemmia rd]]`. Read letter by letter: D o p o, then l e m m i a (the minims are hard to
  count; could be `lemma` or `lennia`), then r and a sign like `c` with a tall loop rising from it (written
  `d`, the "cl" shape). The tall loop is the long stroke the guide calls the tail of the last clear word. A
  wavy flourish with a dot stands before the D in the margin; not written.

## Names I made up

- `stem2bar` — one long upright stroke going well below the line, crossed by two short horizontal bars,
  like a double dagger ‡. Not `hash` (only one stem), not `f` (no hook, two bars). Line 10 (after `r q^o`),
  line 29 (after `r h b h r`).
- `pbar` — a `p` whose stem below the bowl is crossed by a short horizontal stroke. Not `phi` (the stroke
  does not go through the ring). Lines 15, 23, 25 (both 23 and 25 in the run `f pbar xdots s`).

Other plain letter forms not in the tables:
- `v` — line 19, last sign (`d e f c r v`), a plain v.
- `l` — line 31, `tt? b l? b`: a plain tall stroke between two b's; could be a `b` whose bowl I cannot see.

## Stray dots, blots, damage

- Line 1: dot after `bbar^o` ring (as in the guide's example); no token.
- Line 2: heavy blot after `a a n`, one sign hidden (`?`).
- Line 3: blot on `plus`.
- Line 19: dot between `r^o` and `d?`.
- Line 29: small hole/blot below `a a dotcross`.
- Line 32: two dots after `theta`.
- Library stamp in the left margin (lines 4 to 7) and the margin flourish before line 1: not written.

## Cancels and insertions

- No sure cancel. Possible strike over `e dotcross` in line 28 (kept). The tall raised `delta` signs (see
  General) could be insertions; written in place.

## Not covered by the guide / rules I could not apply

- The `c` + `f` question (General): the guide does not say whether a hook at x-height before a barred long
  stroke is part of the `f`.
- Two marks side by side at about the same height (`q^o^s`, `m^o^s?`): rule "lower to upper" was applied
  by rough height.
- A raised tall sign (delta) that may be a mark or a sign: guide gives no test.
- Clear words: the guide says letter by letter; the minims of the second word could not be counted with
  confidence.
- Token counting: the one clear-words group `[[Dopo lemmia rd]]` holds spaces; counted as one token.
