## What the guide did not cover / could not apply

- The guide's distinction between a raised small letter as a mark (section 4) and a raised sign as an
  insertion (2.12) does not say how to decide between them; applied for `^m^o` and `^w` with doubts noted.
- `r` written as a v shape (line 1 `a a r b`) is written `r`.
