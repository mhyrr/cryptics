## Names I made up (guide 3e)

- `xtail` — a diagonal cross whose lower-left arm runs on as a long tail below the line; no loop (so not
  `alpha`/`ex`), no dots (not `xdots`), and it does have a long tail (so not plain `x`). Almost always right
  after an `i` (`r i xtail`, `c i xtail`). Lines 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12. It may be what the guide meant by
  `x` in sheet cell 3.3.5's neighbour; it could also be read as one sign `i`+`x`. I kept `i` separate per
  guide 2.8.
- `slashbar` — one long straight stroke slanting from upper right down below the line to the left, crossed
  by two short horizontal bars (like ≠ / ǂ). No s-curve, so not `dollar`; only one stem, so not `hash`.
  Lines 3, 4, 11.
- `^caret` — a mark like a circumflex (inverted v) above `y`, distinct from the tilde-like `^wave` seen on
  other `y`s. Lines 7, 10 (twice). Could be a variant of `^wave`.
- `^8` (mark, not a base) — a tiny 8- or delta-shaped mark raised above the left end of the `7` in line 11.

## Shapes not in the tables / figures

- `7` — line 11: flat top bar and a long straight diagonal down-left, no fork, no cross stroke. I am fairly
  sure; guide says to list it.
- `4` — only as an alternative (`q|4`, `q^o|4^o`) in lines 6, 8, 11: a bowl-on-stem whose top may be open.
- `ybar` — I used `ybar` for a recurring 7-like shape with a short stroke across its tail, mostly before `8`
  (`ybar 8` in lines 6, 10, 10, 12, 12) and in `f ybar g` (lines 3, 5). Where marked `ybar?` the cross

## General notes

- `bbar^o`: every b carrying a raised ring (lines 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12) shows a short stroke at or near the
  top of the stem. I wrote `bbar^o` throughout. At this resolution I cannot rule out that the stroke is
  the joining stroke of the `^o` or a hooked top (`beta`). A reader who sees plain `b^o` should not be
  counted as disagreeing on the base without a closer look.
- `sigma` vs `s`: I used `sigma` for the 5/G-shaped sign with a tick in its mouth and `s` for the plain
  s; where the tick was not clear I added `?`.
- `tt` written as π (two stems hanging from one flat top bar) occurs in lines 5, 8, 9, 10 and I wrote
  it `tt` per guide 2.6; where it could be `n` I wrote `tt|n`.
- Ink blot: line 4, above the first `g` after `s s s`.
- No cancels and no insertions seen.
- Strokes from other lines: long tails of `ex` (lines 6, 12) and `f`/`qj` descend through the lines below;
  I did not count them.
