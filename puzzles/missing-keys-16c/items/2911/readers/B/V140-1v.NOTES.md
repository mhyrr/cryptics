## General caveat on resolution and hand

The page is in a different, smaller and faster hand than the sample page. At the strip resolution many
small distinctions in the guide could not be made with confidence: `b`/`bbar`/`h`, `c`/`e`, `g`/`q`/`qj`,
`n`/`tt`, `tt`/`ttt`, `sigma`/`moondot`, `theta`/`circdot`, `alpha`/`ex`/`x`, `r`/`gamma`. I marked the cases
I noticed, but the unmarked tokens of those classes carry more doubt than the `?` count suggests. Count the
signs in each line as fairly reliable (both passes agreed, except where noted below). The finer shape choices
are less reliable.

Choices applied across the page:
- `g` vs `q`: bowl with a straight stem → `q`; tail swinging left or curling → `g`. The hand often
  sits between the two.
- `sigma` vs `moondot`: the small `c` with a comma or tick hanging under or inside it (like ç / ς) → `sigma`.
  The taller crescent with a dot in its middle, "(·" → `moondot`. On the first pass I did not separate these
  consistently. The second pass corrected the cases I re-checked, but some may still be swapped.
- `x` with a long tail down-left and a small loop or bend at the upper left ("ꭓ") → `alpha`. The loop is
  often too small to see, so some `alpha` may be `x` or `y`.
- "ı x" (a short upright stroke with a small diagonal cross touching at its right foot) occurs often (L1, 2,
  4, 6, 8, 11, 21, 23, 28, 31). I wrote it `i x`, two signs. It could be one fused sign (the guide's `Lx`, or a
  new one). The true `Lx`, a capital L with an upright cross on its foot, as on sheet 3.3.2, appears twice in
  L19 and is written `Lx`.
- "π" with an arched top → `n`. "π" with a flat bar running past the stems → `tt`. "Ħl", two stems under one
  bar plus a third stroke → `ttt`.
- `y` is used for the "ч" form: a short fork or bend with a long straight tail.
- The degree-like small ring above is always `^o`. A tilde, breve or caret above → `^wave`. I did not
  separate the caret from the tilde (L5 and L14 `y^wave`, L29 `t^wave`).

## New names (guide 3e)

- `cbar`: a small c or σ-like open bowl with a stroke that runs through or below it and on down to the left,
  like "ꝯ" / "ɕ" with a long tail. Not a `sigma` (no comma in the mouth) and not an `alpha` (no cross).
  Occurs often before `o A g sigma` (L4, L13), before `3 r` (L19 twice, L21), before `phi` (L5), and
  elsewhere (L4, L11, L17, L18, L20, L23, L29, L30). It may be the same sign as `alpha` written loosely, or a
  form of `sigma`.
- `stem2bar`: one long stem crossed by two short parallel bars, like ≠ or ǂ. Unlike `hash`, there is only
  one stem. L7, L8, L10, L11, L15, L27 (L27 opens the line with it, and its stem runs well below the line).
- `angle`: a ">" shape, two strokes meeting at a point on the right. L22, between `q` and `q`
  (`boxdot q angle q o o`). It could be a damaged `alpha` or `x`.

  `dotcross` were taken as part of those signs.
