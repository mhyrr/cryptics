# V140-2r — reader B notes

The page has one band only (`V140-2r-01`, `-01a`, `-01b`): four manuscript lines, the last ending in clear
words. The hand looks different from the sign-sheet sample: smaller and more compressed. At the strip
resolution, many small signs and marks are at the edge of what can be read. Treat every small mark on this
page as less certain than its plain token suggests.

Counting rule used: a `[[...]]` group counts as one token.

## Names I made up

- `slash2bar`: one long slanted stem, crossed by two short horizontal bars, with the stem running below the
  line (looks like a slanted `≠` or `ŧ` with two bars). It is not `hash` (that has two stems) and not `f`
  (no hooked top). Line 1, three times: after the first `moondot|sigma`, and either side of the first
  `dollar` (`n bbar^o? slash2bar dollar slash2bar 8`).

## Doubts, by line

### Line 1
- `3|z` (sign 1): a figure 3 with a flat-looking top. It could be `z`.
- `t^s|t^wave` (sign 3): the mark above the t is small and could be a tiny s/5 or a wave.
- `moondot|sigma` (sign 4, and again before `y^o`): a crescent with a dot, about the height of the small
  letters. It is between the tall `moondot` and the small `sigma`.
- `c c d` (signs 6 to 8): written as `‡ c c( d`. The second curve may be the bowl of `d` (making it `c d`),
  or the second `c` may carry a dot (`moondot`). I wrote two c's and a d.
- `bbar^o?` (twice, in `s bbar^o? 8 s s` and `r n bbar^o?`): this looks like a t with a bowl at its foot and
  a ring above-right, the same group as the guide's example. It could be `b^o`.
- `boxdot` (twice): the box seems to have a dot inside. If it has none, it is `box`.
- `t^bar` (before the final `g s q^o`): a short straight mark above. It could be `^wave`.
- `dollar` (twice): an s shape with a stroke through it and a long tail below the line.

### Line 2

  be one `ex`.
- `tt i^o?` (after `p^o`): two stems under one bar, then a short stroke with a ring. It could be `ttt` with
  a mark, or `tt` followed by `h^o`.
- `e|c` and `o|theta` (before the second `bbar^o?`): small curve, then a heavy ring that may have a bar.
- `xdots odiv`: a diagonal cross with dots either side, then a ring over a bar over a dot.
- `3tail^s?`: a 3/z shape with a tail below the line and a tiny s/5 mark after it, raised.
- `tt|hash tt|hash`: two fused stem groups with bars (`#t` and `ŧ` shapes). I could not count stems and bars

- `phi?`: ring with a stroke through it, tail below. It could be `p`.
- `gamma|r` (before `s s s`): an r with a flat arm at the top, a little taller than the plain `r` around

  two tokens. A long diagonal stroke runs up to the right through `e g`. I took it to come from the mark
  above the end of line 3 (see line 3), not as a sign.

### Line 3
- `phi a a 3`: there may be a third `a` (open-topped, like u) between `phi` and `a a`.
- `q|g`: a bowl with a straight stem, which could be a 9-shaped g.
- `alpha^m^o?`: a raised small `m` with a ring stands above the sign after `e`. It may be an insertion and
  not a mark. I wrote it as two marks on the `alpha`. The `alpha` itself could be `y`.
- `tt|dbbar` (after `r a`): two tall stems crossed by a bar. It may be the fused d-b with a double stroke.
- `c c g`: the second c may carry a dot (`moondot`).
- `c h y`: the h touches the y. There may be a bar through the h.
- `ybar?`: the y has a long tail running down into line 4, with a short bar across its foot.
- `ex?` (after `a^o`): a loop on top of a cross, with one long stroke down-left. It could be `e alpha`.
- `y|4`: two strokes meeting at an angle with a tail. This is the same shape as the second sign of line 4.
- `o t? y`: a crossed stem fused to a following y. It could be `f`.
- `m^s?`: the mark above m is a tiny s/5 or a 3.
- `dotcross`: an upright cross with a dot each side. There is one extra dot after it (stray, no token).
- `3tail^o^w? o o`: a z/3 with a tail below, then a small raised ring, then two o's on the line. It could be
  `3tail o o o` (three o's on the line). Above this group, between lines 2 and 3, there is a larger
  w-shaped squiggle with a stroke trailing down-left through the end of line 2. I wrote it as `^w` on the
  3tail. It may be an insertion, or a flourish that is not a sign.
