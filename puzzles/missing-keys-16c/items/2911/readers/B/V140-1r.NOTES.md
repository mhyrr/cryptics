Line numbers count from the first row after the page comment. Strips: 11 bands (`V140-1r-01` to `-11`).

## General limits of this reading

- **Resolution.** The half strips are about 1385 px wide, which puts roughly 6–8 px on the small letters. This
  is too little to tell apart reliably: `theta` from `circdot` from `o`; `b` from `bbar` (a `b^o` with a
  crossbar or without); `h` from `b`; `2` from `z` from `r`; `q` from `g` (a straight stem against a tail
  that swings left); `n` from `arch`. Many tokens carry `?` or `a|b` for this reason alone. The page needs a
  sharper image before any of these pairs can be settled.
- **Last line.** Band 11 ends at line 33 (`dbbar p^tick w xdots ...`). No strip shows anything below it, so I
  cannot tell whether the text continues or ends with clear words on the sheet.
- **Line 1** agrees with the guide's worked example (§6), which was cut from this very page. I checked it against
  the strip and kept the example's tokens. Doubt: at this resolution I see what looks like `9 9 φ` before
  `p^o` and again after it (`ybar g phi p^o g phi` in the example). Each could be `g g phi`, which would add one `g`
  in each place. I left the example's reading in. Line 27 has the same `g g phi p^o g phi` group,
  and there I wrote `g g? phi` for the first group.
- **The `of`-like sign.** A loop that closes into a cross with a long tail down to the left (it looks like "of"
  or "ꝼ") occurs all over the page. Sheet 1.3.3 shows that this shape is `alpha`, and I wrote it so. Where it
  stood upright and could be read as a crossed `f` or `t` with a tail, I wrote `alpha|f`: lines 22, 23, 27,
  29, 30, 31.
- **The crossed `t` with a tail below the line** (one stem, one crossbar, the stem running down): I wrote it
  `f?` when there was no hook at the top. Lines 14, 21, 29.
- **The raised small `δ`**, sitting above-right of a base at the height of the marks (`3ᵟ`, `ıᵟ`): I wrote it
  as a mark, `^delta`. The guide lists no `^delta` mark, so this is a name I made up. Lines 5 (`3^delta?`),
  20 (`3^o^delta? 3^delta?`), 26 (`i^delta?`), 33 (`i^delta?`). A full-size `delta` on the line, with its flag rising to
  ascender height, stays the sign `delta` (lines 2, 3, 12, 13, 24, 29, 30).

## Rules of the guide I could not apply cleanly

- §2.9 (strokes from other lines): long tails cross almost every line. At this resolution I often could not follow a
  stroke back to its source. This affects every `dbbar?`, `ex?` and `bbar` call.
- §3a `x`: several `x` shapes have a long tail down to the left, which the table says `x` lacks. They also
  have no loop, which rules out `alpha`. I wrote them `x|y` (L2, L5, L10, L11, L32).
- Marks above: the guide gives no rule for a raised small sign that is not a letter (the raised `δ`). I used
  `^delta`.
- Clear words: I could not tell whether the long-tailed `q` at the end of `rcq` is a letter of the clear word
  or a cipher sign. Following the worked example, I treated it as part of the clear word.
