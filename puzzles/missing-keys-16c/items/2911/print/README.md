# 2911 print witness: Niccolini, Rome, 5 August 1510, as printed in Opere VI (1877)

Fetched 2026-11-17. Tier: **SCHOLARLY** (19th-century critical edition).

## What was found

*Le opere di Niccolò Machiavelli*, ed. L. Passerini and G. Milanesi, vol. VI (Firenze, Tipografia
Cenniniana, 1877), Legazione XXXII (third legation to France), letter no. 26, "GLI STESSI AL
MEDESIMO" (the Ten to Machiavelli), "Ex Palatio fiorentino, die vij augusti M. D. X." (pp. 53-55).
Footnote 1 on p. 54 prints the Rome letter under the heading "COPIA D'UNA LETTERA DA ROMA DE'5 DEL
PRESENTE" (Copy of a letter from Rome of the 5th of this month). It runs from p. 54 to p. 55.

- archive.org: `leoperediniccol03milagoog`, https://archive.org/details/leoperediniccol03milagoog
  (Google scan of an Oxford copy; the only scan of vol. VI found by the archive.org search API).
- Leaves: n61 = p. 53, n62 = p. 54, n63 = p. 55.
- Transcription: `opere6-niccolini-1510-08-05.txt`.

## Editors' source

Note 1, p. 54: "La lettera da Roma è la seguente, e sta copiata a tergo di questa, ⟨pe⟩r mano del
Machiavelli" (The letter from Rome is the following, and is copied on the back of this one, in
Machiavelli's hand). "Questa" is the covering letter, whose source note on p. 53 reads "Biblioteca
detta. — Carte del Machiavelli, cassetta V, num. 139" (Library aforesaid, i.e. the Biblioteca
Nazionale, Florence. — Machiavelli papers, box V, no. 139). So the editors printed **the BNCF copy in
Machiavelli's hand on the back of V/139**. They do not mention the cipher enclosure (V/140) or an ASF
register.

## Complete or abridged?

- No "etc.", ellipsis or editorial omission mark appears in the printed letter. The editors put "(?)"
  twice (after a word ending "-ri" and after "intendendo"), meaning they were unsure of the reading.
- But the printed text **begins "Intendesi, come pare..."**, not "Dopo l'ultima mia". It has no
  salutation, no date line and no signature, and it ends on the Tripoli news. That is consistent with
  Machiavelli's copy being an extract or partial copy (the BNCF record cited in `../gate.md` calls the
  decipherment "inexatta ed incompleta", inexact and incomplete). The edition faithfully prints an
  incomplete copy. It is not a complete text of Niccolini's letter.

## How the text was checked

The OCR (`_djvu.txt`) for p. 54 is very poor: the note is in small type and the scan is cropped. Every
word was read by eye from the page images, enlarged 3x for p. 54 (strips) and about 0.6x for p. 55.
The OCR was used only to locate the passage.

**Wall on p. 54.** Leaf n62 is a 763 px wide scan whose left edge is cropped at the gutter. The first
1-3 letters of most full-width lines are not on the image (37 letter lines; 36 restorations). The
transcription gives conjectural restorations in ⟨ ⟩, most forced by hyphenation (e.g. "In-/⟨ten⟩desi",
"par-/⟨ten⟩dosene"). Some are not forced: line 7 "⟨…⟩no", line 13 "⟨d'⟩", line 23 "⟨…⟩ri (?)", and
the lost first words of lines 13, 14, 20 and 37. These are marked with [?] or ⟨…⟩. Short accents on
p. 54 ("di"/"dì", "fa"/"fà", "parti"/"partì") are read at the limit of the scan's resolution. p. 55
(2750 px) is sharp and fully verified. Breaching the wall needs another copy of vol. VI (HathiTrust
returned 403 to a scripted request; Google Books `OgcJAAAAQAAJ` is the same Oxford scan) or Bertelli
1964, app. A pp. 1352-53.

## Counts (letter only: excludes the heading and the editors' "*" sub-note)

- Lines: 59 (p. 54: 37; p. 55: 22).
- Words: 709, all Latin script (0 non-Latin letters). A word that is partly restored counts once.
- Restorations ⟨ ⟩: 36, all on p. 54. [?] flags: 4.

## Step 5: other August 1510 Dieci items in Opere VI

- Dieci letter of **17 Aug 1510**: **no**. The run goes from no. 32 (Machiavelli, Blois, 13 Aug, ends
  p. 68) to no. 33 (Machiavelli) and no. 34 "I Dieci al Machiavelli. Die 18 augusti 1510" (pp. 72-74).
  This is from the OCR, and it agrees with `../gate.md`.
- **"Summario di lettera de' 18" sent 22 Aug**: **not found**. Opere VI prints a Dieci letter of 22 Aug,
  no. 36 "I Dieci al medesimo", pp. 76-78, dated "Ex Palatio fiorentino, die xxij augusti 1510". Its
  OCR text (with postscript) carries no summary, and "summario" does not occur in the OCR near it.
  Its source note on p. 76 (leaf n84) is cut off at the right edge of the scan: "Biblioteca detta. —
  Carte del Machiavell[i …] — La lettera è scritta in cifra, ed in doppio […]" (the letter is written
  in cipher, and in duplicate…). I cannot rule out that the cut part of that note mentions an
  enclosure.
