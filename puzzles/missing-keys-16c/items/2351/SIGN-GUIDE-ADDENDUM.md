# Addendum to the sign guide (2026-11-18, after three readers of letter A)
Made from those readers' notes and from where their names differed. Shapes only; no value is involved.

New listed names (use these, do not make up others for the same shapes):

| name | what it looks like | do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| `bbar` | a `b` or `6` whose tall stem is crossed by a short bar near the top | `6`, `t`, `beta` |
| `yloop` | a `y` whose tail closes into a loop, often joined on the left to two or three stems under one bar. Write the stems as `tt` or `ttt` and the looped y as `yloop` | `hash` (two slanted stems, two bars, no y), `y` |
| `dot` | a dot standing on the line between two signs, most often between a `3` and a `4` | a `^.` mark above a base |
| `h` | tall stem with a hump to the right and a descender | `brace` (zigzag, no hump) |
| `cup` | a small U open at the top with a dot inside | `o`, `c` |
| `loop` | tall narrow closed loop | `delta`, `D` |
| `Li` | like `Lz`, with a small upright in the corner | `Lz` |
| `T` | capital T | `t`, `tt` |

Decisions:
- The small reversed c before a `t` is `7`.
- `i` covers every bare upright stroke, tall or short.
- A `^5` has a flat top stroke or an s-curve; a `^1` is one straight tick; a `^3` has two round bowls.
- A mark that sits over the gap between two bases belongs to the base on its **left**.
