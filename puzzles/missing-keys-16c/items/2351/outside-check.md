# No. 2351: outside check of the reading (2026-11-19)

This file checks the names, dates and events in `READING.md` against outside sources. It checks the setting
of the reading. It does not check the decipherment. The verdicts are "consistent", "inconsistent" and
"not found".

## The ambassador and the letters

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

No edition of his dispatches was found. |

## Events of September–October 1513 in letter B

| Claim (lines) | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The Swiss invasion of Burgundy, their "impeto" (onset), the fear "venissino in Borgogna" (that they come into Burgundy) (5–13) | **consistent** | [Dijon-wp]: "The Siege of Dijon between 8 and 13 September 1513 was a successful campaign of the Swiss army". [DC-2] p. 10 (SCHOLARLY): "les Suisses battent son armée à Novare, et la forcent à capituler sous les murs de Dijon" ("the Swiss beat his army at Novara and force it to capitulate under the walls of Dijon"). |
| The King's anger at the Swiss, who "tried to make me lose it", and talk of whether they had wronged him (9–18) | **consistent** with the treaty of Dijon and its rejection | [Dijon-wp]: "King Louis XII of France was informed of the treaty's contents on 14 September and rejected it, arguing that La Trémoille lacked the authority to accept territorial concessions. He also refused to pay the 400,000 écus". Hérodote (POPULAR, https://www.herodote.net/13_septembre_1513-evenement-15130913.php, search snippet) says the same. In October the King was refusing a treaty the Swiss held binding, so a quarrel over who had wronged whom fits. |
| "la paura dello esercito inglese" (the fear of the English army) (13) | **consistent** | [DC-2] p. 10: Henry VIII and Maximilian "l'envahissent par le nord … et lui enlèvent Thérouenne et Tournay" ("invade it from the north … and take Thérouanne and Tournai from it"). [DC-2] p. 588, Acciaiuoli from Amiens, 26 Sept 1513: "la terra de Tournay … era accordata e che venerdì, che furono alli xx [?], furono date le porte alli nemici" ("the town of Tournai … had come to terms, and on Friday … the gates were given to the enemy"). |
| Troops sent to Burgundy with "… cento lance" (… hundred lances) and "… mila" (… thousand) foot; troops "non sono ancora andate" (have not yet gone); an army "si ne licentia" (is being dismissed); "in queste frontiere" (on these frontiers) (1–4, 22–32) | **not found** | No source consulted gives the French dispatches of October 1513 to Burgundy or the dismissal of an army. A winter stand-down after Tournai is plausible ([DC-2] p. 589, 26 Sept: "Lo esercito Francese viene verso Péronne", "the French army is coming towards Péronne"). The specific orders are not verified. |
| "Circa lo spirituale" (as to spiritual matters): an order "del mandare" (of sending) being made; "passeranno molti giorni" (many days will pass); peace for Christendom (33–41) | **consistent** with the end of the Pisan schism, if that is what it means | [Lat-wp]: at Lateran V "a similar announcement [rejecting Pisa] was made by Louis XII's ambassador"; the council annulled the acts of the "quasi-council of Pisa". The *Catholic Encyclopedia* ("Lateran Councils", https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09018b.htm, SECONDARY, search snippet): "In the eighth (17 December, 1513), an act of King Louis XII was read, disavowing the Council of Pisa and adhering to the Lateran Council". The 1911 Britannica "Leo X" (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Leo_(popes)/Leo_X, SECONDARY, search snippet) gives 9 December for the ratification. The sources differ on the day, but all put the French submission in December 1513, after 23 October. So a letter of 23 October saying the sending is being ordered and "many days will pass" fits the chronology. That "lo spirituale" means the schism is an interpretation. |

## Names in letter A

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "Robertet" | **consistent** | Florimond Robertet. [DC-2] note to an earlier legation: "Florimond Robertet, secrétaire des [finances]" (the OCR breaks off at "secrétaire des"), a constant interlocutor of the Florentine ambassadors ("par le cardinal d'Amboise, par Chaumont, par Robertet"). |
| "Monsignor de Marseille" | **consistent** | [DC-2] p. 613, note to a letter from Rome of 22 March 1514: "L'évêque de Marseille était Claude de Seyssel" ("The bishop of Marseille was Claude de Seyssel"). Catholic-Hierarchy (https://catholic-hierarchy.org/bishop/bseys.html, SECONDARY, search snippet): appointed bishop of Marseille 3 December 1511. The *Catholic Encyclopedia* "Diocese of Marseilles" (https://www.catholic.com/encyclopedia/diocese-of-marseilles, SECONDARY, search snippet) calls him "ambassador of Louis XII at the Lateran Council, 1513". So he was the King's man for papal business in 1513, which fits "keep them to Monsignor de Marseille alone". |
| "the embassy of obedience" to Leo X | **not found** as a named embassy | Leo X was elected 11 March 1513 ([DC-2] p. 586). A French embassy of obedience would follow the reconciliation, and the reconciliation was formalised at Lateran V in December 1513 (above). No source consulted describes the French embassy of obedience or its date. |

## Unverified claims
- That letter B is unprinted anywhere. The check covers [DC-2] only. The Guicciardini *Carteggi*, Sanuto's
  *Diarii* (which sometimes summarise Florentine letters from France), and the ASF inventory of MAP filza LXXII
  were not checked.
- The French orders of October 1513 to send lances and foot into Burgundy, and the dismissal of an army.
- The name of the "Monsignor" sent to Burgundy (line 2) and of the "Monsignore" who answered the Swiss
  (lines 14–18). La Trémoille is the obvious candidate for Burgundy, but no source was checked for October 1513.
- The French embassy of obedience to Leo X: its members and its date.
- The day of the Lateran session that received Louis XII's renunciation of Pisa: 17 or 19 December, or 9 December
  for the ratification. The sources disagree. None was checked against the council's acts.
- The date of the Tournai surrender. The OCR of [DC-2] p. 588 reads "alli xx" and is not certain.
