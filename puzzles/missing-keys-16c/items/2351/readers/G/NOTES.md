## Names I made up (section 3e)
- `eq` (lines 3, 4, 31, 33): a `z`/`2`-like sign drawn as two short parallel bars, like `=` or `z`
  with a doubled base, sitting on the line. Possibly just a `z`; I kept it separate.
- `theta` (lines 8, 17, 18): an 8-like bowl with a stroke rising out of it to the right and a short
  tail, like `ϑ` or `θ` with a stroke. Not `delta` (no left-bending stroke), not `8`.
- `dcross` (line 17): a sign like `4` or `#` drawn heavily, a crossed upright with two bars, under a `3`.
  Possibly `hash`.
- `eps` (line 24): a small `ε`/`E`-like sign on the line between `z^5` and `D^n`.
- `colon` (line 27): two dots one above the other on the line.
- `wave` (line 27): a low open wavy stroke like `~` or `ɷ` standing on the line, not a mark.
- `qloop` (line 31): a `q`/`9` whose tail drops far below the line and closes into a long
  triangular loop (like `g` with an angular loop).
- `vflag` (line 37): a large `V` whose right arm runs up into a long stroke with a small flag or blob
  at the top left, like a check mark with a flag.
- `uhook` (lines 37, 38): a large open curve like `ϑ` or `√`: a hook on the left, a round bottom, and
  a tall right arm. Not `vflag` (no flag).
- `u` (line 37): a small u-shape on the line (doubtful).
- `d` (line 14): a tall `d`-like sign, a round bowl with a stem rising on the right (`q d 3`). Could
  be `delta`; I kept it apart because the stem rises straight, not bending left.

## Guide decisions I took
- `L4` used for the large 4 whose bar runs left (`ʟ4`, often drawn with two short legs like `4ᴴ`).
- `R` used for the squarish capital with the foot running out right and up (I first called it
  `Lfoot` and then replaced it with `R`, which the guide describes as having a ring or dot after its foot).
- `n` used for the low `n`/`m` sign in front of `hash` (`n hash`); some have three stems, which would be `m`.
  I did not separate them.
- `zm` used for the looped `2M`/`2h` sign.
- `hash` with a dot after it on the line (`hash dot`): the dot is written as its own `dot` token, as the addendum says.
- `S ttt` in line 38: a capital S followed by three stems under one bar.
