## Conventions I used
- A doubtful base that carries a mark: `base?^mark` (e.g. `x?^3`); a doubtful mark on a sure base: `base^mark?`.
- `z|2` = two close readings (likelier first).
- Marks were assigned by the guide's rule: directly above a base, otherwise the base on the left. The hand sets
  most marks above-right of their base, so marks between two bases usually go left; doubtful cases have `?`.
- Clear words lowercased, letter by letter as I could read them.

## Names I made up (not in guide or addendum)
- `L` — row 1: a small upright with a foot to the right, like a small L, just before `tt?`. One instance.
- `eq` — two short flat strokes, one above the other, like `=`, no diagonal. Rows 3 (`eq^5`), 4 (`eq^~`), 15 (`eq^1`).
- `Ltt` — tall stem (often slanting, sometimes with a small flag at the top) running into a long flat foot to the
  right, the foot crossed by two short uprights (`Ⴗ` + `ıı`, looks like "L4" with two stems). Common (14).
  Distinct from guide `L4` (whose flat stroke runs left) and from `Lz`.
- `Li` (guide name, used here for) the same shape as `Ltt` with only one upright crossing the foot. Rows 7, 25, 30.
- `dasho` — a long flat stroke on the line with a small loop or hump in its middle, stroke continuing on the right
  (`—o—`, `—a—`). Rows 3, 15, 26 (×2), 28, 35, 36. When the hump is at the right end with a flat entry only, I wrote `2`
  per the guide's `-2`; the two may be the same sign — readers should be compared here.
- `loopn` — a small n whose first stem has a closed loop at the top (`ϑn`). Rows 8 (end), 17, 18.
- `u` — a small u (two short stems joined at the foot) sitting inside the sweep of a `Q`'s tail. Rows 21 (`Q u`),
  26 (`Q Q u`, underlined), 24 (`u?`, very uncertain). Possibly part of the Q.
- `colon` — two dots one above the other on the line. Row 27 only, in a faded passage.
- `V` — a large V whose left arm curls over at the top (`ʋ`). Rows 37, 38.
- `vstroke` — a V / downward triangle with a filled top left and a long slanted stroke rising through it to the
  upper right (`∀`-like with a stroke). Row 37, just before `V`.
- `m` — three short stems joined at the top (guide mentions m beside n). Rows 4 (`m?`), 33, 38.

## Recurring ambiguities the guide did not settle
- `5` vs `S`: I used `S` for a large, full-height S and `5` for the smaller flat-topped one; some are borderline
  (rows 20, 28, 29, 36).
- `^5` vs `^1`: many marks are a thin curved tick; I wrote `^5` when there is an s-curve or flat top, `^1` when straight.
- `6` with a long rising stem vs `bbar`: `bbar` only when a crossbar on the stem is visible.
- `brace` vs `h`: `brace` = bulb at top, zigzag, no hump; `h` = hump to the right.
- Line-end horizontal strokes (rows 3, 7, 15): look like fillers rather than cancellations; I left out the sign
  only in row 3, where the stroke crosses the sign's body. Compare with other readers.
