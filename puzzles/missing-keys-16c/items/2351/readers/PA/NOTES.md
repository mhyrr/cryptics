this resolution most words cannot be resolved letter by letter. I wrote `[...]` wherever I could not read a
word and did not fill gaps from context. I counted 30 manuscript lines: a heading plus 29 lines of text,
the last a short one at the foot. That is more than the "about 25" in the brief. Line division follows
the slope: the right end of each line sits level with the left end of the line above.
