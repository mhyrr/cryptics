# Reader PD — p233 notes

Read twice: once on the full strips (q233-01 … 13, L and R), once on 160% normalized half-crops made
from those strips (kept in `crops/`). The JPEG blocking at that size limits how much an enlargement gains.
Line numbers below count the heading as line 1 (row 2 of `p233.txt`).

General: this is a fast, heavily abbreviated hand and much of the page I could not read with confidence.
Wherever I could not read a word I wrote `[...]` and did not reconstruct. The joins between L and R halves
were made on shared words (e.g. "Bastiano" l.3, "de borbone" l.4, "allasalvezza / della" l.6, "a modo suo"
l.7, "solier/ et li ho" l.8, "loro sono" l.9, "strade amm" l.12, "voluto andare" l.17, "ier mattina" l.25).
The join is approximate in a few lines (noted below); a word may be doubled or dropped at the seam.
