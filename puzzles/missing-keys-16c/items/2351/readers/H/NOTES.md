## Decisions applied throughout
- **Reversed c (ↄ, like `>` or a small `ɔ`)**: I always wrote it `7`, not only before `t` as the addendum says. On the first pass I marked the cases not before `t` `7?`, then took the `?` off for consistency. Places where it is not before `t` include: L01 after `bbar`; L02 before `delta`; L04 before `flong`; L05, L09 before `delta`; L13 three times; L18; L23; L26; L34; L35; L38.
- **`2` with a long flat entry stroke and a small loop (`—a—`)**: I wrote one `2` (guide, 3a). On the first pass I read it as `dash` + a separate hump in L03, L12 and L15, then fixed it after comparing with L17. Where the flat stroke stands apart from the loop, a reader could see `dash 2`.
- **Horizontal line through the end of a line on the right margin** (L03 end `bbar y`; L07 end `6 beta delta`; L15 end `7 flong D`; B03c and B08c): this looks like a crease or ruling, not a strike. It runs on into empty margin with no signs under it. **I read the signs under it and left nothing out.** If it is a strike, the struck signs are L03 `bbar y` (last 2), L07 `6^3 beta delta` (last 3) and L15 `flong D` (last 2).
- A `6` whose tall stem has a short crossbar near the top is `bbar` (addendum). A `6` with a long curved stem but no bar stays `6`.
- `beta` covers both the `ß` and `R`-like tall looped forms. I used `R` only where a small ring follows the foot (L10, L28).
- A `q` whose tail swings into a closed loop or triangle below the line, followed by two short stems under a bar, I wrote `yloop tt` (addendum). This happens in L04 and L26. On its own I wrote `yloop`, for example L31 and L34.

## Names I made up (guide 3e)
- `eq`: two short parallel flat strokes, like `=` (L03, L04, L31).
- `divide`: faint short dash with dots above and below, like `÷` or `:` (L03 after `brace`; L27 after `r^3`, with a 4 above). Both are doubtful.
- `Ptail`: a `P`/`D`-shaped bowl with a dot at its foot, whose foot runs out as a long flat stroke to the right and then turns down-left like a y-tail (L04, c third).
- `hook`: a curl like `?` or a reversed `ɔ` with a looped top, at the end of L04.
- `loophook`: an upright closed loop like `8`/`ϑ`, whose top runs right into an arch that comes down to the line (L08 last sign; L17; L18 after `beta`).
- `u`: two short stems joined at the **bottom**, like `u` (L07 `bbar u D`; L21 and L26 inside the tail of a `Q`: `Q u`; L24 `beta u`).
- `invtri`: a small inverted triangle, its top filled, crossed by a long stroke that rises from below the line to the upper right (L37).
- `bigv`: a large `V` or `√` with a curl at the top of its left arm and a tall right arm, much bigger than `r` (L37, L38; in L38 it has a small ring at the top).
- `m`: used once (L38 `bbar m hash`), for three stems joined at the top (guide, under `n`).
