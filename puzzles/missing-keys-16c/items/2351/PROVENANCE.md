## The pair, checked by eye
- Letter A opens in clear: "Mag.ci Viri etc. Ultra le lettere pubb. delle qual sara con questa el doppio non
  ho molto che dire se non accusar le vostre de x portate da Bastiano mandato di costi dal R.mo di
  Sanseverino, el quale arrivo adi xxi la sera." Then cipher.
- The copy on c. 233 opens "Oltre alle lettere pubbliche … el doppio … accusare le vostre de x portate da
  Bastiano mandato di costi dal R.mo di Sanseverino el quale arrivo adi xxi la sera" and goes on in clear.
- So the copy is the chancery's decipherment of letter A. English of the shared opening: "Besides the public
  letters, of which the duplicate will be with this, I have little to say except to acknowledge yours of
  the 10th brought by Bastiano, sent from there by the Most Reverend of Sanseverino, who arrived on the 21st
  in the evening."
- Letter B has no clear text here beyond its greeting and close. It is the bare letter.
- Not yet checked: whether the copy follows letter A to its end, and how freely.
