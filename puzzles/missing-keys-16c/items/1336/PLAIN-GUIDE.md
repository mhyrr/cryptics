# Plain-text guide — item 1336

You transcribe Italian written about 1574–75 in a secretary's hand. Write what the scribe wrote.
- One manuscript line per row. Keep the spelling, including u/v, i/j, doubled letters, `et`, missing h.
- Do not expand abbreviations silently: write the letters you see and put the supplied letters in round
  brackets, `mons(igno)r`, `S(ua) S(anti)ta`. A raised ending is written on the line.
- A word you cannot read: `[...]`. A doubtful word: the word then `[?]`.
- A word struck out: `<struck>word</struck>`. A word inserted above the line: `^word^` at the place it belongs.
- No punctuation is required. Keep capital letters as written.
