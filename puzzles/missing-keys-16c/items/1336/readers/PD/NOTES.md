## Line 1 (p14-01 + p14-01b)
- `V(ostra) S(ignoria) Ill(ustrissi)me`: written `V. S. Ill` with raised `me`. Expansion is mine.
- `vedrà`: there is a mark over the final a; I read it as an accent.
- `scrivo`: the minims are cramped (could look like `scinno`); `scrivo` is the reading that fits the strokes.
- `d(e)l Abbate[?]`: written `dl` then a large looped capital and `-bata`/`-bate`, partly under a brown blot.
  `Abbate` is a guess at the shape; it could be a proper name (`Rata`, `Rosta`?). Treat as doubtful.
- `e`: a single e-shaped letter, not clearly `et`.
- `Mons(igno)r`: written `Mons` with a raised hook/mark; I took the hook as the r.
- `Iacobelli[?]`: capital I/J then `acobelli`; the c/o may be `w`-like. Name reading uncertain.
- `il tutto e`: last word on the line is a lone `e` at the right edge of p14-01b; nothing follows on the strip.
