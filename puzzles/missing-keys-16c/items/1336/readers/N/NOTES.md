  `N` of `c652-05a` best; `M` second. Same family as L7 s22 `N` (clearer there).
- L6 s10 `u|M`: under the watermark; two strokes joined low are visible, a third may be hidden.
- L6 s17, s33, s37 and L7 s1 `tri|A`: a Λ whose left foot runs out to the left and whose feet are joined
  by a stroke at or near the line, leaving a closed hole. Read as a closed base (`tri`); `A` if that
  stroke is a low crossbar. No mid-height bar, so not `tribar`. L7 s36 is a plain `A` (crossbar mid).

- L6 s38 `8|R`: straight left upright with a closed loop at the top right; lower part unclear.
