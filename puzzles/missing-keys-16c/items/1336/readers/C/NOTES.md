## Method
- Pass 1, top-down: lines 1–4 from band 1, 5–6 from band 2, 7–8 from band 3, 9 from band 4, 10–13 from
  band 5 (and 6 for the ends of 11–13), 14–15 from band 7. Followed each line across thirds by its overlapping
  signs, not by height.
- Pass 2, top-down: re-read lines 7–15 on the 2x halves of bands 4 and 6, which I had not used in pass 1,
  plus targeted re-crops where pass 1 had doubts (lines 2, 3, 5, 6). For lines 1–6 the second look was
  limited to those spots. I settled differences by looking again. Changes after pass 2: L9 first sign `?` →
  `plus?`; L10 `A` → `A|tri` and `4?` → `4|a`; L12 `pis?` → `pi?` and `qx?` → `q`; L13 `n-d` → `pi?`.

## Names I made up
- `n-hash3`: like `hash`, but with **three** uprights crossed by one or two bars (like ⱡⱡⱡ). Not `rom3`:
  the bars cross the middle, not the top and bottom. L1, L3, L4, L9, L12, L13.
- `n-pi3`: a roof on **three** legs (like m with a bar over it, or a three-legged Π). Seen once, L5, after
  `q?`. It could be `zhe` or `rom3` without its bottom bar.
- I made up `n-d` for an upright closed by a curve on the right (D) at L13 position 7, then replaced it with
  `pi?` on pass 2. This scribe's `pi` legs curl so far that it often looks like D or Ↄ.

## Recurring calls the guide does not settle
- **E**: the scribe's E is mostly a `t`-like upright with a crossbar and a foot turning right (ŧ / Ŀ), not
  a printed E. I wrote `E` for all of them (as "upright with two or three side strokes").
- **a vs box**: many small squares have the right upright going a little above the top (ɑ written square,
  like ロ). I wrote `a` for those and `box` only where all four corners are clean (L1 `box`, L13 the two
  `box`). Where I could not tell, `a?` or `box?`.
- **n-shapes**: a small n or Ω with a lead-in or out-turned foot (ᘉ, ɲ). I wrote `omega` (round top). Some
  may be meant as `pis`/`pi`.
- **dark ovals with a stroke**: many `phi`/`q` are blotted. Where the stroke shows above the bowl I wrote
  `phi`. Where only a tail below shows, `q`. Where neither shows, `phi|q` or `?`.
- **6 vs b**: always `6`, as the guide says. Several are tall, b-like.
- **8 vs slong vs 3**: a tall looped stroke with a descender = `slong`. A closed figure 8 = `8`. An open-topped
  loop over a bowl (ϑ) without a long descender = `8?`. The z-like 3 with a tail (ʒ) = `3`.
- **Λ with a stroke rising above it** (Ⱥ, often blotted): written `A?`. L4, L5, L8 (twice), L9, L12, L13.
