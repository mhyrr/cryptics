## Names I made up
- `n-tribar` (line 4, once): a closed triangle, point up, with a horizontal bar through its middle that
  runs out well beyond both sides (like a struck-through Δ). It is not a stroke under the sign: the bar
  crosses the triangle at mid-height.
- `n-dollar` (line 4, once): two stacked loops (an 8 or S shape) with one upright running through both and
  showing above the top loop and below the bottom loop, like `$`. Lighter, browner ink than its neighbours.
- `n-c` (line 12, first sign, with `?`): a ring open on the right, like a plain `c`. Not `rc` (that opens left).
  Could be an `o` whose right side is faint.

## Things the guide missed
- Strike-through lines over runs (lines 5 and 6), and underline strokes crossing `q` tails (lines 5, 6, 7,
  10, 11), which make `q` and `qx` hard to tell apart. A rule is needed.
- No entry for a struck triangle (`n-tribar`) or a `$`-like sign (`n-dollar`).
- The small н-form: the guide has `H` (capital) and `N` but not this small cursive form.
- `4` is listed but not described; here it often looks like A with a crossbar running out to the right.
