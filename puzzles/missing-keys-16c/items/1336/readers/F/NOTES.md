## Doubts, by position (1-based)
- 5 `omega?`: round-topped loop with a lead-in from lower left and a foot running into the following hash.
  Could be read as `a`, but it is tall and open at the bottom.
- 9 `a|u`: small bowl with an upright on its right that ends in a tick above x-height; the left part rises from
  the tail of the preceding `x`. Might be an и-like `u`. The following tall stroke is taken as a separate `l`.
- 22 `9?`: large loop high on the right with a stem down to the line; no descender. Larger than other signs.
- 23 `q|phi`: inside the brown blot; the centre is a hole (paper loss). Outline is a rounded, almost
  squarish bowl with a stem below the line slightly left of centre. Nothing visible above the bowl, so `q`;
  `phi` only if the upper stroke is lost in the hole.
- 24 `trid?`: top bar, a left upright with a small foot, and a diagonal from the top right down to the bottom
  left, making a closed point-down triangle. Not inked solid. Alternative: a `v` with a top bar.
- 26 `v|u`: two strokes meeting low; left one longer and curved. No hook visible.
- 27 `dcross|E`: an upright crossed by three bars (top bar ends in an upward tick at the right; middle bar;
  a bottom bar that may be broken). The bars seem to cross the upright rather than only project right, which
  argues `dcross`, but there are three bars, which the guide gives to neither. Under stain.
- 31 `pis|pi`: roof on two legs, legs short, about x-height (not full height, legs do not curl). Under stain.
- 32 `qx`: the crossing bar on the tail is long and low, running under position 31 as well.
- 33 `M?`: three minim strokes (small wavy m); a small separate dot/stroke at its left may be part of it.
- 35 `slong|8`: small upper loop, lower loop, and a descender well below the line, with a lead-in from the
  top right; reaches above x-height. Reads as an S-like looped stroke, so `slong`; `8` if the descender is a
  stain. Last sign before the closing bracket.
