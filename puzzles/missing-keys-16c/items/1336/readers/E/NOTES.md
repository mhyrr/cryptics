## Where the thirds join

## Doubts, by position (1-based)
- 5 `omega`: a lead-in stroke rises from the lower left into a round top, and a right leg comes down with its foot
  turned out to the right, running into the `hash`. I read it as omega with a lead-in. If not that, it could be `R`
  (the reversed form). I did not mark it, but it is the least certain of the unmarked signs.
- 8 `x`: a long stroke from upper left to lower right, crossed by a shorter one. The top of the long stroke almost
  touches the bar of the `7` before it. I read two signs, `7` and `x`, not one.
- 9 `A?`: a diagonal rising stroke, then a closed little bowl or loop on the right. It could be an A whose crossbar
  is written as a loop, or two signs (`l a`). I wrote one sign. The same shape comes again at 31.
- 24 `q|phi`: this sign is under a hole or blot, light inside with a dark rim. There is a wide closed outline and a
  plain upright below it. I cannot see whether the upright shows above the outline, so `phi` cannot be ruled out.
  The dark rim may be stain rather than ink.
- 25 `trid?`: a V-like triangle, point down, closed at the top by a bar, not inked solid. It could be `v`, with
  the bar belonging to the sign before.
- 26 `8?`: an S-like stroke from the top right with a closed lower loop. The upper loop is open, so this is not a
  plain 8. It sits on the line and is not tall, so it is not `slong`.
- 29 `n-tricross|dcross`: see the new names below.
- 31 `A?`: the same shape as at 9, a diagonal stroke with a small loop or bar, just before `K`.
- 33 `box|tri`: a Д-like trapezoid. The left side slants up to a flat top bar, the right side comes down, and the
  base runs out past the left side. It is flat on top, so it is not a point-up triangle, but its feet are like the
  c44 `tri` forms.
- 34 `qx`: a slanting oval with a tail below, crossed by a long bar. Nothing shows above the oval, so I wrote
  `qx`, not `phi`.
- 35 `M|u`: a small sign with three short downstrokes joined by humps and a flick at the end, before `6`. It could
  be one `M` (small wavy m) or a `u`. There might also be two small signs here. I wrote one.
- 37 `8`: the lower loop ends in a short tail going down to the left. I still read it as
  `8`.
- 23 `9`: a large looped head with a stem down to the line. The stem does not go below the line, and nothing
  crosses it, so I read `9`, not `q`/`qx`.

## Names I made up
- `n-tricross`: one upright, slanting down from upper right to lower left, crossed by three horizontal bars: a
  short one near the top (like t), a longer one at mid height, and a short one at the foot, which looks like two
  dashes either side of the upright. The nearest listed name is `dcross` (one upright, two bars), so I also gave
  `dcross` as the second choice. There may be a second, fainter upright on the left. If there is, `hash` with an
  extra bar is possible.

## What the guide missed
- No name for an upright with three crossbars (above).
- Blot over an outline with a descender, where you cannot tell whether a stroke shows above it (position 24). Section
  3 covers wholly unreadable signs, but not this half-visible case.
- No form of `A` with a looped crossbar (positions 9 and 31).
