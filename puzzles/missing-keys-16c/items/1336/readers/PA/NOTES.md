## p270 (line numbers count manuscript lines, 1–15)

- L1 `avocare[?]`: letters look like `avolce`/`avocare`; no visible `a` before `lei`. Faint ink.
- L2 `Frumento[?]`: initial could be F, J or a decorative S; ending -o/-e unsure.
- L2 `Gratti[?]`: could be `Grassi` (long s vs crossed tt not certain).
- L2 `ch(e)`: written `ch` with a looped stroke and a hyphen-like mark at the line end; read as `che` running into L3 `ando`.
- L4 `cercandole[?]`: written roughly `cerco'uosle`; reading is a guess at the letter shapes.
- L4 `et[?] s'alcuno[?]`: a stroke before the long S may be `et`, or the whole may be `n'alcuno`/`ch'alcuno`.
- L4 `q(ues)ta[?]`: a tall descending letter with a dot and raised `ta`; could be `S.ta` or `l.ta`.
- L5 `Carniglia`: read with fair confidence; p44 L1–2 has `Carni|glia`, same name.
- L8 `si b(e)ne[?] m[...] s'ha[?] detto secre`: `si bne` read as `sebbene`; after it `m.` with a raised mark (molto? mi?) not resolved; `detto` has a doubled ascender; the last word looks like `biere`/`breve` but L9 begins `tissimam(en)te` with raised `te`, so `secre|tissimamente` is the likely reading.
- L11 `s(e) ne[?]`: written as `S` (or `L`) joined to `ne`.
- L12 `fidar(e)`: `fidar` with a terminal flourish.
- L14 `[...]`: after `ch(e)`, a `d`-like sign then `qi`/`gli`; perhaps `di quei` or `d'egli`; not read.
- L15 `da part(e)[?]`: first sign is `&`-shaped, could be `d(a)`; `part` with flourish.
