   slip the drop is close to one line. Because of this, a band's c-third shows the end of the line *above*

   brief's "13" is right, two of my rows would have to be continuations, but every one of them starts at the

   between lines 10 and 11 (`odot 6 K 2 M E`), with a caret-like mark (a cross on a long stem pointing down)
   just before them. The caret sits right after the blot that follows `n-ess? 6` in line 11. I put the six
   signs into line 11 at the caret point and gave the caret no token. If they are in fact the overflow of
   line 10 (which ends at the frame with `hash`), move them there.
3. **Marks above signs, despite guide section 1.** c269 line 12: the third sign (`A`) has a small hook or

   with a dot.
4. **K forms in c44.** In c44-R, lines 2 and 3 (the three signs I wrote `zhe|K`) have a K-like form with an

5. **Rounded "a" form (`n-a`)**, very common in c269. It is not clearly a triangle and not clearly A.

## Names I made up

- `n-ess`: a tall S-like stroke, or an 8 with a long tail, with loops above and below the line, a little
  like §. Clearest in c269 line 1 (`8 v n-ess 8 l`) and line 2 (`pi n-ess theta`). It is less sure in line 6
  (`rc n-ess? 7`) and line 11 (`2 n-ess? 6`), where it may just be an 8 with a tail.
- `n-ya`: a reversed-R form like Я, with a loop at the top and a leg going down. It is bigger than R.
  c14 has it twice (`qx n-ya hash`, `2 n-ya ?`). In c269 it is in line 3 (`lam n-ya phi`) and line 7
  (`3 H n-ya x`), and less sure in lines 2 and 8. Where I doubted it I wrote `R?` instead (lines 10 and 14).
  It may be the same sign as `R`, or as `9`.
- `n-a`: a small rounded a-like or ɑ-like form, a closed bowl with a short stem or tail on the right. It
  has no flat base, so I did not call it `tri`, and it has no crossbar, so I did not call it `A`. It may be
  a quickly written `tri` or `A`, or a `q` with no tail. I used it throughout c269 and once in c14
  (`u|n-a`).
- `n-tau`: a T: one top bar on a single upright, with the top curling a little at the right. Only at the
  start of c269 line 10. It may be a `pi` with one leg lost against the frame line, or a `dcross` drawn
  short.
