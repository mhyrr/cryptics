## Line 1 (p14-01, right end checked against p14-01b)
- `V(ostra) S(ignoria) Ill(ustrissi)ma`: written `V. S. Ill.me` with `me` raised; expansion is mine.
- `vedra`: no clear accent mark on the final a; there is a small stroke above, maybe an accent.
- `scriuo [?]`: letters look like `s c ? i n n o` or `sinno`; `scriuo` is my best fit, could be `sono`.
- `del [?]`: written as `d` + `l` (perhaps `dl` with an abbreviation), followed by `S.` = `S(igno)r`.
- `Rota [?]`: capital R clear, then a word with one tall ascender under a brown blot; could be `Rota`, `Rocha`, `Rosta`.
- `e`: partly under the blot; could be `et`.
- `Mons(igno)r`: `Mons:` with a raised hook over the colon area; I take the hook as a raised `r`. Uncertain.
- `Sambelli [?]`: first letter is a long sweeping stroke from upper right: `S`, `L`, `G` or `Z` all possible. The rest `-ambelli` is fairly clear.
- `il tutto e`: from p14-01b; `il` may be `Il`. Final `e` is cut by the strip edge (could be `e` or the start of `et`).
