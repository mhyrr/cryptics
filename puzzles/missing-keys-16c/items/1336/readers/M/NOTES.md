Pass 1 top-down; pass 2 from line 12 to line 4, each run right to left. Crops (my own) are in `crops2/`.
I did not open `crops/` (left by an earlier, cut-off run). The strips are small, upsampled images; many
small signs are at the edge of what can be resolved, which explains the `?` density on lines 6, 7, 10.

## Names I made up
- `n-tribar` (line 4): a closed triangle (straight base, like `tri`) with a horizontal bar across the
  middle; both the bar and the base run out well to the left of the triangle. Not `A` (closed at the
  bottom) and not plain `tri` (has the bar).
- `n-dollar` (line 4): a tall sign of two stacked loops (like 8 or §) with an upright stroke running
  through both loops and out above and below the line. It may be the guide's `slong`; I kept it apart
  because of the straight upright through it.
- `n-vloop` (lines 4, 7, 10): a v whose left arm curls into a small closed loop at the top left (like ϑ
  or a v with an eye), often with a dark dot in the loop. Plain `v` (lines 4, 7) has no loop. It could
  be read as the guide's `odot` (ring open at the top); I did not, because the right arm rises as a v.
- `n-s` (line 5): a small S-shaped stroke sitting on the line, about half height, not reaching above or
  below (so not `slong`), with a dot at its lower end.
- `n-c` (line 12, first sign): a c open to the right (a normal c, the mirror of `rc`), with a small
  hook at the top.
