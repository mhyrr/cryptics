# Sign guide — cipher of items 2946 and 2947 (frozen 2026-11-19)

Frozen before any cipher reader runs. **Shapes and names only.** Nothing here says what a sign means, and you
must not guess. Made by the main thread from strips of c. 215 (`2946-c215-b01L`, `b01R`, `b04L`, `b04R`) and
of image 692 (`2947-692-b03L`, `b03R`).

## 1. What the cipher looks like
Runs of separate signs inside clear Italian lines, written by the main hand at the size of its letters. The
signs stand in groups with small spaces between them. A second, smaller, paler hand has written Italian words
**between** the lines on images 691–694. That writing is not cipher: never transcribe it, and do not let it
change what you see. Read only the signs that sit on your line.

Write tokens separated by one space, one manuscript line per row.

## 3. Points, gaps and figures
- `:` two dots one above the other between signs. `.` one dot between signs. They are tokens.
- `/` a clear gap between groups of signs, wider than about half a sign. Write it each time you see one.
  Do not write `/` at the start or end of a run.
- A group of Arabic figures inside a run (for example 19, 32, 66, 68, 39): one token, `#` and the figures as
  you read them, `#19`. If a figure is doubtful, `#3?`.
- A dot or tick **above** one sign: the sign's name with `^` after it (`tri^`). Describe it in NOTES.
- A stroke under, through or joining signs that is not part of a sign in the table: describe it in NOTES with
  the signs it touches; do not make it a token.

## 4. Doubt and new shapes
- A sign you can read but are not sure of: the name with `?` after it (`pi?`).
- Two candidates: `a|b` with your better guess first (`psi|psi0`).
- A sign you cannot read (blot, hole, overwritten by the gloss): a bare `?`, one per sign you believe is there.
- A shape not in the table: make a short lower-case name starting with `n-` (`n-hook`), use it every time, and
  describe it in NOTES (strokes, height, which strip and line shows it best). Do not force a new shape into a
  listed name.
- Where clear Italian words of the main hand stand in the line, write `[[clear]]` once for each unbroken
  stretch, so the row shows where the runs start and stop. Do not transcribe the clear words.
