# Reader CC: notes on 2946-c215 L01-L19

Sources I used: strips `2946-c215-b01L` to `b06R` only. My own enlargements are in `crops/`. Files: `pass1.txt` (top to bottom, each line read left to right, frozen once written), `pass2.txt` (bottom to top, each line read right to left, done from fresh crops `crops/P2_*`), `c215.txt` (settled). Positions below count tokens from the left of the row in `c215.txt`, starting at 1 and counting `[[clear]]`, `:`, `.` and `/` as tokens.

## The line list
Every listed line was found at about its listed y, so I kept the list. L06 and L19 hold only clear words, as listed. On the right half the lines rise by about 7 to 10 page px, so a band crop at one y does not hold a whole line.

## Small writing between the lines
I saw none.

## Names I made up
- `n-c`: a small forward c, opening right, about cup height. It usually has a short bar running in from the left that touches it (`-c`). Examples: L01 pos 8; L04 pos 16; L05 pos 13. In L08, L10, L14 and L18 it comes straight after an `rc` whose tail bar runs into it (`rc n-c`, which looks like ɔ-c).
- `n-bor`: a small circle with a bar out to the **right** only (o-), the mirror of `bol`. Examples: L02 pos 19; L09 pos 11; L12 pos 20 and 29; L16 pos 48; L18 pos 2. Its bar often runs into the next sign.
- `n-four`: an upright crossed by a bar that runs out both sides, with a small curl or tick on the left of the upright above the bar, so it looks like a 4 or ɟ. Only at L02 pos 24 (`b01R`, x about 1060, y about 180).
- `n-hook`: one heavy stroke that hooks over to the left at the top and then runs down as a stem, like a mirrored ſ-top or ɼ without the bar. Sometimes it has a hairline flourish going up and right. At L08 pos 4; L14 pos 31 (just before the clear 'pregemo', with a long flourish); L18 pos 13.
- `n-sqstem`: a square-bottomed U (`sqcup`) with an upright up its middle that rises well above the rim, like 山. Only at L11 pos 8 (`b03L`, x about 870, y about 250). It stands just before the clear words 'e cossi'.
- `n-f`: a tall upright that runs below the line, with a hook to the right at its top and a short crossbar, like an f. Only at L14 pos 26, straight after `ii` (`b04L` right edge / `b04R` x about 300, y about 120).
- `n-operp`: a small circle on a short stem that stands on a long foot bar, like a ⊥ with a circle on top (or a ♀ whose bar has dropped to the foot). Only at L17 pos 44 (`b04R`, x about 1660, y about 330).

## Shape notes on listed names (how I applied the guide)
- `L`: almost every L on this page also has a short bar at the **top** going left from the upright, so the shape is like a squared Z (ꓶ with a foot). It has the foot to the right, so I wrote `L`, not `Lr`. Examples: L02 pos 9; L05 pos 8; L16 pos 4.
- `rc`: a reversed c, usually with a tail bar to the right (ɔ-). In L05 pos 4 to 5 and L15 pos 35 to 36, an upright crosses the rc's tail bar. I wrote that as `rc plus`, but it could be one joined sign.
- `phi`: in L01 pos 12, L07 pos 8 and L15 pos 20 (each time just after `boxbar`) the sign is a small circle inked very dark, with an upright inside it that does **not** run out. That could be `phi`, `bof` or a filled o. I wrote `phi` all three times, so the three can be found together. At L03 pos 41 the phi has a long upright through it above and below, plus a bar out to both sides (⊕ with arms). At L17 pos 26 it is a plain Φ.
- `boxbar`: its bar runs out to the right only (⊟-). The top edge is faint at L07 pos 7.
- `bo`: many have the bar running through the circle (-θ-), not just out from its sides. I wrote them all as `bo`.
- `bof`: L14 pos 19, a circle inked solid with a bar out both sides.
- `pihat`: on this page it is a roof bar above a Λ (legs meet at the top). I used `pi` only for straight-legged Π. At L09 pos 21 the pihat has a dot above it (`pihat^`). At L17 pos 42 a dot sits **inside** the pihat, under the roof; that is not marked as a token.
- `pis` against `pi`: L03 pos 4 and L10 pos 21 are clearly smaller and leaning, so I wrote `pis`. L11 pos 6 and L14 pos 23 are about cup height but flat-topped with straight legs, so I wrote `pi`; they could be `pis`.
- `o^` at L03 pos 35: a circle with a dot above **and** a dot below. I wrote it `o^`; the dot below is not marked.
- `oh`: the circle is always joined on its right to a two-upright sign whose bar runs out (o-‡‡). At L15 pos 32 and pos 44 a descender from the line above (L14's 'p' of 'pregemo' and the long s of 'vostra') runs down into the circle, so it looks like b# or d#. I took that stem as part of L14 and wrote `oh`.
- `dloop`: L08 pos 13 and L15 pos 10. I checked that the stem in L15 does not touch L14.
- `eb`: most show two short bars to the right. L10 pos 10 shows only one clear bar (⊢), so it is `eb?`.
- `qs` and `venus`: `qs` is a loop on a stem with no bar on the stem. Many qs here also have a short bar out to the right at the top of the loop (ϙ-). I did not count that bar as crossing the stem.
- `dplus`: two uprights with one bar running out both sides. At L12 pos 9 the bar runs well out to the left and only slightly to the right.

## Doubts (position in `c215.txt`)
- L01 pos 1 `[[clear]]`: the clear run ends '... replicare /e'. I took the 'e' after the virgule as clear Italian. If it is a sign, it is missing.
- L01 pos 26 `plus`: there is a blob on the top end of the upright and a faint pale ring above it. I settled on `plus` (pass 2 had `plus|venus`); it might be `venus` or `plus^`.
- L03 pos 1 `ell?`: a c-like stroke and a foot at the bottom of an upright. That upright is also the descender of the clear 'p(er)' at the start of L02, which is crossed there by its abbreviation bar. The sign could be `ell`, or `n-c` plus a stroke, or nothing but L02's p and a flourish.
- L04 pos 26 `#19`, L05 pos 36 `#32`, L08 pos 24 `#66`, L10 pos 1 `#19`: these are figures. `#32` has a long tail stroke to the right. L08 pos 2 `#19?`: the "19" just after the clear 'chel' could be a clear abbreviation rather than figures. A descender from L07 crosses it.
- L08 pos 27 `qs?`: a loop on a stem that leans down to the right, crossed from the lower left by a stroke from the 66 or the line below.
- L10 pos 10 `eb?`: see above.
- L12 pos 33: I wrote the closing large cursive 'Et' (a C-like loop crossed by a long bar that runs on as a line-filler) as `[[clear]]`, preceded by `.` at pos 32. It might be an `ell`-type sign instead.
- L13 pos 2 `.`: the dot after the clear 'spazato' may be clear-text punctuation. I kept it as a token, and likewise L12 pos 32.
- L16 pos 26 `.`: a dot or short dash right next to the cup's right arm. It might belong to the cup.
- L16 pos 49: I read this sign as `L` both times (it might be `ell`).
- L14 pos 31: the hairline flourish from `n-hook` toward 'pregemo' might be part of the clear word.
- L07: I took the "2" after 'Landriano' (the tironian et) as clear, inside the first `[[clear]]`.
- L11: 'Λao' (before 'ch(e) si metano') is written in the clear hand and I took it as clear.
- The strips for b03R and b04R show small dot clusters at the far right edge (x about 1950). They are beyond the end of the lines, and I took them as stains, not tokens.

## Where the two passes differed (5 positions) and how I settled them
1. L01 pos 26, last sign: pass 1 `plus`, pass 2 `plus|venus`. I enlarged it (`crops/S_L01.png`, grey, normalised). It is a solid plus with a blob on top of the upright and only a pale ring above. Settled `plus` (doubt noted above).
2. L02, between `n-bor` and `H`: pass 2 had a `/`. In `crops/S_L02.png` the space from n-bor's bar end to H is no wider than the H-to-tri space, and the gap before n-bor is clearly wider. Settled: no `/` (pass 1).
3. L04, between `bo` and `ell`: pass 1 had no gap. In `crops/S_L04.png` the space from bo's bar end to the ell is about a sign wide. Settled `/` (pass 2).
4. L12 pos 9: pass 1 `dplus|H`, pass 2 `dplus?`. In `crops/S_L12.png` the bar runs out past the left upright and, faintly, past the right one. Settled `dplus`.
5. L16 pos 14, between `cup` and `perp` (after 'ell'): pass 1 `.`, pass 2 `/`. In `crops/S_L16c.png` and `S_L16d.png` there is no separate dot; the heavy tip is the end of the cup's right arm, and the space is a gap. Settled `/` (pass 2).

## Things the guide does not cover
- A reversed c whose tail bar is crossed by an upright (L05, L15), and an rc whose tail runs into an n-c (L08, L10, L14, L18). These need a rule on whether they are one sign or two.
- An L with a top bar to the left as well as a foot (the usual L on this page).
- The dark-circle sign after `boxbar` (phi? / bof?).
- A circle with a bar out to the right only (`n-bor`).
- A dot inside a sign (pihat at L17), and a dot below a sign (o at L03). The guide only gives `^` for a dot above.
- Whether figures that touch a clear word ("chel 19") are figures or clear text.
