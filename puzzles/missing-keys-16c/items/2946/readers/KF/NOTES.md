- `/` rule: I wrote `/` where the gap between two signs was at least about 45 strip px (about 15 page px). On this page the ordinary spacing between signs is 25 to 40 strip px, so the "half a sign" rule in the guide would put a `/` almost everywhere. `/` is the least reliable token in my rows.

## Things the guide missed, or that need a decision

1. **`:` is three dots.** Every `:` I enlarged (L42 twice, L48, L49, L44) is three dots in a column (⁝), not two. I wrote `:` for all of these vertical points rather than inventing a name, because the hand seems to have only one such point. If two-dot and three-dot points need to be told apart, every `:` on this page has to be re-checked.
2. **`n-vdiv` is a different mark from `:`.** It is one upright stroke with a dot above and a dot below (like ¡ with both dots, a vertical ÷). It is about a sign high, and the stroke is plainly a stroke. It occurs at L35 pos 26, L48 pos 3 and L49 pos 6. In L46 pos 14 the mark has a short stroke at the top and dots below it. I settled that one as `:`, but it could be `n-vdiv`.
3. **Arabic figures.** The pairs `7ϧ` and `3ϧ` are, by shape, a 7 or a 3 followed by a hooked figure: a short rising stroke, then an arch to the right that ends in a long tail sweeping down. I read the hooked figure as a period 9 and wrote `#79?` and `#39?`, with the doubt on the second figure. The 7 could instead be the sign `Lr`, which would make the pair `Lr` plus a new sign. The pair `7ϧ` occurs at L34 pos 13, L48 pos 1 and L51 pos 18, and `3ϧ` at L51 pos 10. The `#33` at L43 pos 40 is two clear 3s.
4. **`π` and `Π`.** The hand makes two shapes. One is a straight Π. The other is a π with curving legs and a top that overhangs, about as tall as H or slightly shorter, never half height. In the final file I wrote `pi` for both. In pass 1 I had written `pis?` for some of the π forms (L42 pos 6, L43 pos 14, L46 pos 30). If the two forms are distinct signs, the guide needs a name for the curved π.
5. **`Lr` and `L`.** I wrote `Lr` for the form that has a separate bar at the top running left from the top of the upright, usually also with a foot to the right (‾L, or a "7" with a foot). I wrote `L` for an upright with a foot and at most a small serif at the top. The guide's `Lr` has no foot, but I found no footless 7-shape distinct from this one, so I used `Lr` for the barred form.
6. **dplus versus hash.** Every double upright I enlarged (L27 pos 31, L34 pos 35, L37 pos 6, L38 pos 35) has a single long bar running out on both sides, so I wrote `dplus`. I found no true `hash` (two bars) in these lines.
7. **`bo` drawn through the circle.** Many `bo` are written as one stroke that crosses the circle (−θ−). I wrote them all as `bo`, not `theta`.
8. **`venus` standing on a foot.** At L47 pos 36, L49 pos 24 and L35 pos 41 there is a circle on a stem that ends in a long bar at its foot (♀ drawn on ⊥). I wrote `venus` for these.

## Names I made up

- `n-sqc`: a square bracket opening right (⊏): an upright with bars at its top and its foot that run right, full height. It is clearest at L47 pos 44 (end of the run, b12R) and L49 pos 28 (after the clear word, b13R). It also occurs at L34 pos 11 and L40 pos 1.
- `n-II`: two uprights standing on one shared foot bar, sometimes with serifs or a bar at the top (Ⅱ, ⫫). It is clearest at L51 pos 6 (b13L). It also occurs at L28, L31, L35, L38 and L50.
- `n-lc`: a c, open to the right, with a bar running left from its top end (‾c, like τ). It is clearest at L49 pos 13 (b13L) and is frequent.
- `n-qsr`: a circle on a stem (as `qs`) with a short bar running out to the right from the circle (♀−). It is clearest at L47 pos 24 (b12R) and L40 pos 47 (b10R, end of line).
- `n-or`: a small circle with a bar out to the right only (o−), the mirror of `bol`. It is clearest at L44 pos 13 (b11L). Where the bar runs into a following `+` (L28 pos 13) or `tp`, it may be that sign's left arm. At L42 pos 2 I wrote `o` for that reason.
- `n-tee`: an upright with one bar out to its right at mid height (⊢). It is clearest at L44 pos 24 (b11L). Some of these may be `eb` whose second bar is faint.
- `n-8`: two small circles stacked one above the other (8). It is clearest at L39 pos 2 (b10L). L44 pos 45 is a slanted, looser 8 with a tail, so I wrote `n-8?` there.
- `n-shan`: a square-bottomed U with a middle upright rising above its sides (山). It is clearest at L30 pos 8 (b08L) and also occurs at L48 pos 38.
- `n-boxstem`: a closed square with a short upright rising from its top (凸). It is clearest at L35 pos 47 (b09R) and L27 pos 8 (b07L).
- `n-boxin`: a closed square with short uprights inside it (▥). It is clearest at L35 pos 45 (b09R) and also occurs at L32 pos 19. It differs from `boxbar`, which has a horizontal bar across.
- `n-ocross`: a circle with arms in all four directions, an upright through it and a bar out to both sides (⌖). It is clearest at L45 pos 22 (b12L/b12R). At L32 pos 1 the top arm may be the descender of `quando` from L31, so I wrote `?` there.
- `n-ebl`: an upright with two short bars out to its left (the mirror of `eb`). It occurs at L43 pos 43 (b11R).
- `n-i`: one plain upright. It occurs only between two dots, `. n-i .`, at L38 pos 21, L46 pos 37 and L47 pos 26.
- `n-t`: a lower-case t: an upright with a crossbar and a foot curving to the right, with no loop. It occurs at L32 pos 2 and L47 pos 30, and at L29 pos 19 (gloss over it, so `?`).
- `n-c`: a small c, open to the right, with no bar. It occurs at L39 pos 40 (b10R) and is faint.
- `n-w`: an ω or w shape (a round cup with a middle upright). It occurs at L40 pos 13 (b10L).
- `n-G`: a G or 6 shape, a circle open at the upper right with a hook turned in. It occurs at L27 pos 28 (b07R).
- `n-curl`: a large crossed flourish: two curved strokes crossing like an X, the right one closing into a loop, about 1.5 signs high. It occurs at L33 pos 35 (b08R, b09R). It could be a main-hand abbreviation rather than a sign.
- `n-oot`: two joined circles (like `oo`) with a long tail descending from the lower left far below the line. It occurs at L51 pos 20 (b13R), just before the clear words `e tandem`.
- `n-r`: an r-like shape, a short upright with a flag to the right at the top and a tilde running on. It occurs at L51 pos 14 (b13L). Together with `M?` at pos 12 (three minims, m-like) it sits just before the clear `in questo tempo`, and both may be clear letters of the main hand rather than signs.
