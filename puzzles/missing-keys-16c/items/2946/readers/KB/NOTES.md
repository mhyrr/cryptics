## Conventions I used (where the guide left room)
- `bo` covers both -o- and -θ- (circle with one bar through it running out both sides); e.g. the θ form at L03 pos 11, 27, 30, 39; L17 pos 22; L24 pos 10, 41; L26 pos 21. `theta` only at L14 pos 1, a large circle whose inner bar does not run out.
- `dplus` vs `hash`: every two-upright sign I enlarged had **one** bar running past both uprights, so `dplus`. Only L20 pos 28 may show a second bar (`hash|dplus`).
- `L` vs `ell`: `ell` = an L whose upright ends at the top in a loop or curl (£ / ℒ). Many plain L's have a small top serif that is hard to tell from a loop at this resolution; most pass differences were here, and this is the least reliable distinction in the file.
- `dloop` vs `phi`: `dloop` where a tall stem rises from the top of the circle; `phi` where the upright visibly runs through it. Several are in between and carry both names.
- `qs` vs `venus`: a loop on a stem; `venus` only where a bar crosses the stem. `psi0` where no bar crosses a fork's stem.
- Small comma-like ticks at the baseline after a sign are not tokens: after L03 pos 9 (T), L05 pos 8 (H), L14 pos 16 (H), L15 pos 17 (T), L16 pos 7 (H), L17 pos 10 (T) and pos 23 (ell), L20 pos 10 (T), L25 pos 6 and 28 (H), L12 after pos 29 (cup). They may be either hand's.
- Figures as `#nn` (counted as signs in the counts): L04 pos 2 `#8`; L08 pos 12 `#33?`; L10 pos 21 `#32`; L12 pos 9 `#68`, pos 13 `#19`; L13 pos 10 `#3?`; L15 pos 27 `#68`; L18 pos 31 `#32`; L21 pos 30 `#18?`; L22 pos 10 `#68`.

## What the guide missed
- A circle with a bar to the right only (`n-bor`); the guide has only the left-only `bol`.
- Γ (`n-Gam`) beside `Lr`; Z / ⌐L (`n-Z`).
- Boxes with inner uprights (`n-boxii`, `n-boxv`) and a box with a stem rising out of it (`n-boxst`); `boxbar` does not say whether an upright bar counts.
- Λ with a loop on top (`n-lamo`), frequent.
- ¡ (`n-bang`), ⊙ (`n-odot`), ⊏ (`n-sqc`), ш and ⊥⊥ (`n-shin`, `n-iibar`), E with three bars (`n-E`), G (`n-G`), ʊ (`n-ucurl`), g (`n-g`), x (`n-x`), τ-like (`n-tau`).
- No rule for a circle with a bar through it that runs out both sides (θ running out); I used `bo`.
- No rule for comma-like ticks after signs; left out of the tokens and listed above.
