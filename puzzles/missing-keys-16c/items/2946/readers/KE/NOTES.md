## Things the guide missed
- A sign shaped like a squared Z (`n-zed`) is very common here and is easy to misread as `Lr` (7) or `L`.
  It deserves a guide name.
- Circles with a bar through them that runs out one or both sides (`-θ-`): the guide's `bo` says "bar out to
  both sides" but does not say whether the bar crosses the circle. I used `bo` for both (crossing or not),
  `theta` only when the bar stays inside.
- `dloop` vs `phi` cannot be told apart at strip resolution for most instances.
- A `:` with a third dot or a stroke (L29), and `!` (n-excl) standing between signs.
- Figures: `33` (L43), `36` (L51), and possibly `8` (`n-eight`).
- Line list: I found one run per listed line and no extra line; I believe the list is right. L41, L46 start
  with the clear words as listed. L31 starts with "quando".
