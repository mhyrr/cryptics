## Names I made up
- `n-ced`: a small c open to the right with a short bar out to its left (like "-c" or ç), half to full height.
  Best seen at the end of L02 (b01R, about x 1400). Also in L11, L17, L19, L22, L24, L25, L26.
- `n-zed`: square Z. A short bar at the top running left, an upright down, a foot running right (like a
  lowered "⅂" joined to an L). H height. Best seen in L13 (start) and L21 after `H` (b06R, about x 400).
- `n-gam`: the mirror of `n-zed`. Upright with a bar at the top running right and a short bar at the foot
  running left (like Γ with a foot). L03, L04, L05, L10 (b03R, about x 700), L12.
- `n-excl`: an upright stroke with a dot under it (like "!"), often with a small tick or cross at its top.
  L05, L07, L13, L15, L22 (b06R, about x 430, the clearest).
- `n-lamo`: a Λ with a small loop or circle at its apex (like a little stick figure, "Å"). L03, L05, L06,
  L12, L15, L22, L24, L25. Clearest in L24 (b06R, about x 1260).
- `n-z`: a plain Z (top bar, diagonal, foot bar). Only at L05 pos 26, before the clear words.
- `n-or`: a small circle with a bar out to the right only (o-), the mirror of `bol`. L07 pos 1, L21, L24.
- `n-sqc`: a square bracket open to the right (⊏), H height. L08, L10, L12, L14.
- `n-x`: a small x or χ-like crossing of two strokes. Only in L08 pos 27.
- `n-boxv`: a closed square divided by one or two inner uprights (like Ш inside a box). L10, L11, L20, L24,
  L26 (b07L, about x 1480).
- `n-iibar`: two short uprights standing on a bar (‖ with a foot line). L10 and L12 (b03L, about x 1540).
- `n-t`: a small hook or c joined to a t-like cross ("ɾt"). L12 pos 29, L15. Uncertain as a separate shape.
- `n-g`: a looped g-like sign. Only in L13 pos 19.
- `n-curl`: a small c or 6-shape with a curl inside (like ɕ or ɞ). L16 pos 3 and pos 25.
- `n-odot`: a circle with a dot in its centre (⊙). L21 pos 24, L22 pos 39.
