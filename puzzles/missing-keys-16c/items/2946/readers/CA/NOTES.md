# Reader CA — 2946-c215 L01-L19, notes

Source: strips `cache/strips/2946-c215-b01..b06 L/R` only, with enlargements I made in `crops/` (pass 1) and
`crops/p2/` (pass 2 and settling). Positions count tokens from the left of the row in `c215.txt`; `[[clear]]`, `:`,
`.`, `/` and figure tokens all count as positions.

## Names I made up

| Name | Shape | Best seen |
|---|---|---|
| `n-rbar` | A small hook opening to the left (like a reversed c, or `ɔ`/`ɤ`), often with a dot or curl at its lower end, with a bar running out to its **right** at mid height. The bar often touches the next sign (`n-c` or `plus`); I give the bar to `n-rbar`. | b01L L01 first sign after the clear words; b02L L05 pos 3; b01L L03 pos 20 |
| `n-c` | A plain small c, open to the right, no bar of its own. In this page it always follows `n-rbar`, whose bar reaches it (`)-c`). | b02L L08 pos 7; b05L L18 pos 16 |
| `n-bc` | A c-shape (open to the right) with a bar out to its **left** (`-c`), like `bol` but with an open c in place of the circle. | b01R L01 pos 8; b02L L05 pos 12; b04R L16 pos 43 |
| `n-rct` | A tall `)`-shaped stroke whose tail runs down below the line, with a short separate tick to its upper right (looks like `ɣ` or `r`). | b02L L08 pos 4; b04R L14 pos 30; b05L L18 pos 13 |
| `n-zl` | Upright with a foot to the right (as `L`) **and** a top bar running out to the left about as long as the foot (like a squared Z, `⌐` over `L`). Where the top bar is only a short serif I wrote `L`. | b01L L02 pos 7; b02L L05 pos 7; b04L L14 pos 4 |
| `n-bor` | Small circle with a bar out to the **right** only (`o-`), the mirror of `bol`. | b01R L02 pos 18; b03L L09 pos 11; b04R L16 pos 46 |
| `n-qbar` | A loop on a stem (as `qs`) with a bar running out to the right from the loop itself, at loop height; the stem is not crossed (so not `venus`). | b01L L02 pos 5; b01L L04 pos 16; b02R L07 pos 9 |
| `n-odiv` | Small circle with a dot above it and a dot below it (like `div` with a circle for the bar). | b01R L03 pos 34 (only case) |
| `n-boxv` | Closed square divided by an upright that runs out above the top (like 由). | b03L L11 pos 8 (only case) |

- L08 27 `qs|psi0`: loop or fork, open at upper right, on a slanting stem; a long slanting stroke from lower left crosses it (I take that stroke to come from another line, not the sign). No bar on the stem.
- L08 38 `cup^`: a clear dot above the cup.
- L09 1 `div`: its bar runs far out to the left.
- L09 5 `eb?`: upright with a bar (one, possibly two close together) to its right, touching `ii`; could be `plus`.
- L09 21 `pihat^`: a dot above the bar of `pihat` (and a dot inside the Λ, as in most `pihat` here).
- L10 1 `#19`: first token of the line (the list says the line starts with signs).
- L10 27 `H?`: two uprights joined low with a rounded bottom and a bar, like `ʉ`; between H and a barred cup.
- L10 the H at pos 22 and the cup at pos 24 (before `oh` at 25) are touched by descenders from L09 ("manifeste si").
- L11 1 `psi|venus`: fork top curls almost closed; stem with two bars; a stroke from L10 runs into its top.
- L12 22 `dcross?`: upright with a clear top bar and a faint lower bar.
- L12 29 `.` stands just before the large clear "Et" at the end of the line.
- L13 3 `L|ell`: the top curls, but the curl may be the tail of L12's "Et" above.
- L13: a dot after clear "spazato" before the signs; I took it as clear punctuation, not a token.
- L14 25 `ii?`, 26 `tp?`: two short uprights, then a right-curling loop on the descender of L13's "magnifico" f. Pass 1 and 2 agree on this reading but I cannot rule out that these are clear letters.
- L14 27-28 `n-rbar n-c`, 30 `n-rct`: these groups stand just before clear "pregemo"; the same groups occur before clear words in L08 and L18 and after `#19` in L08 and L10. I transcribed them as signs; if the clear-word readers took them as words, that is the reason.
- L14 end: after the final `plus` a long thin stroke runs to the right edge with a mark at its end; a line filler, not a token.
- L15 10 `dloop?`: circle with a tall stem; the stem runs up into L14 and may be a descender from there, leaving an `o` or `bo`.
- L15 31 and 42 `oh`: each circle has a stem rising from it, which I take to be descenders from L14 ("pregemo", "vostra"); without them the shapes are plain `oh`.
- L15 33 `n-rbar plus`: the hook's bar crosses the plus upright. Same pattern in L05 3-4.
- L16 40 `n-qbar`, 46 `n-bor`: clear on enlargement.
- L17 42 `venus?`: a loop on a stem that ends in a long foot bar (like `ϙ` standing on `⊥`); the bar is at the foot rather than across the stem.
- Many `tp` have a dot below them (L03 24, L04 28, L08 42, L09 4, L09 13, L14 12, L15 41 and others); the guide only names dots above, so I left these unmarked here.
- Many `bo` have a dot or small fill in the circle, and some are drawn with a bar inside the circle (θ-like) running out both sides; all written `bo`. One is solid (`bof`, L14 19).
- `pi` against `pis`: several π are between the two heights; I wrote `pis` only where clearly half height (L03 4, L10 20).

## What the guide missed

- The nine shapes above.
- Dots **below** a sign (frequent under `tp`), and dots inside `pihat` and `bo`.
- Whether a bar that runs from one sign into the next belongs to the first or the second (`n-rbar` + `n-c`,
  `n-rbar` + `plus`, `div` with a long bar).
- An `L` with a long top bar to the left, and `oh` whose right part has a bar running past both uprights.
