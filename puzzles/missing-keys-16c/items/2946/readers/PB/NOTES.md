## Conventions I applied

- A line wholly or mostly in cipher with normal word-gaps between sign groups is one run. I split runs
  only where clear words intervene.
- Gloss written past the end of a run's last sign is given as `{margin: ...}` only when it stands wholly
  beyond the signs; gloss that starts over the run and trails past its end is kept in the run's braces
  (noted below by line).
- `ch` with a stroke in the main hand is written `ch` (no expansion bracket added beyond what is seen).
