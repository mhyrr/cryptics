## Guide notes (things the guide missed)

- Vertical dot groups here are three dots (`n-3dot`), not the guide's two-dot `:`. I kept `:` only where I
  saw two dots. 15 `n-3dot` in `691b.txt`. They work like points; I counted them as signs in the final counts.
- `Lr` on this page often has a short foot to the right as well as the top bar to the left (like ⅂ with a
  foot, close to Z). I wrote `Lr` for all of them.
- `pihat` here is a long roof bar over two legs that splay like Λ (ㅠ/Ѫ-like).
- I found no clear `hash` with two bars. Everything I first wrote as `hash` turned out to be one bar across two
  uprights, running out both sides, so `dplus`.
- Figures: `#79?` (L34, L48, L51), `#39?` (L51), `#33` (L43), `#8` (L38, L39 x2, L44, L48). The "9" is a
  tailed shape (ɥ-like). The "7" of `#79?` has the same shape as `Lr`; I read it as a figure because it is
  joined to the 9 the same way each time. The `#8` sign is a figure-8 / § shape that stands alone among signs.
- Dot marks above signs: `cup^` (L38 pos 23: a dot over the cup), `n-odot^` (L47 pos 11).
