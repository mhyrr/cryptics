# Reader CB — notes, 2946-c215 L01–L19

Sources: strips `2946-c215-b01L/R` to `b06L/R` only. I did not open the page image. Enlargements I made are in
`crops/` (pass 1 and settling) and `crops2/` (pass 2). Positions count tokens from the left of the row in
`c215.txt`, including `[[clear]]`, `/`, `:` and `.`.

## Line list
The list matches what I see: 19 lines. L06 and L19 hold clear words only. L13 is clear words with a short run of
three signs at the right end. L11 and L18 are short runs followed by clear words to the end of the line.

## Small writing between the lines
I saw no small, pale interlinear writing. Long descenders and ascenders of the clear words often cross into a
neighbouring line; I read those as strokes from the other line, not as parts of signs (see below).

## Names I made up

  `L` sharing one upright (a square Z). About H height. Clear in L02 pos 7 and L05 pos 7 (b02L, left of centre),
  and L18 pos 3.
- `n-bc`: a c (open to the right) with a bar out to its left at middle height (`-c`). Small. L01 pos 9, L05 pos 11
  (b02L), L16 pos 44.
- `n-or`: a small circle with a bar out to the **right** only (`o-`), the mirror of `bol`. L02 pos 16, L09 pos 11,
  L12 pos 19 and 28 (the bar runs on into the next sign), L16 pos 48, L18 pos 2.
- `n-qbar`: a loop on a stem (as `qs`) with a bar from the top of the loop out to the right (`ϙ` with a flag).
  L04 pos 17 (b02L right), L07 pos 9, L09, L16.
- `n-qperp`: a loop on a stem that stands on a foot bar (`ϙ` over `⊥`). L17 pos 41 only (b05R right).
- `n-ocross`: a circle with an upright through it **and** a bar through it running out on both sides (a `phi` and a
  `bo` in one, like a cross with a ring). L03 pos 40 (b01R centre). Only once.
- `n-sqstem`: a square-bottomed U (`sqcup`) with an upright rising from the middle of its floor to above the top
  (`凸` without a lid). L11 pos 8 (b03L, about x 800).
- `n-ft`: a tall looped l followed, joined, by a t-like stroke with a long bar out to the right (`ℓt—`). L12, last
  sign (b03R right). It may be a clear-hand abbreviation (an "et" sign) and not cipher; I left it in the run.
- `n-gam`: a `)`-shaped curve with a long thin tail rising from its top to the right (`)‾`, like a gamma or a
  turned `r`). L08 pos 4 (b02L left), L14 pos 30, L18 pos 13 (pale, `n-gam?`).
- `n-xc`: a reversed c joined by a short bar to a c (`ɔ-c`). L08 pos 6, L10 pos 3 (b03L left), L18 pos 15 (pale,
  `n-xc?`). L14's sign before `n-gam` (pos 28) is like it but its first half is more a hooked T with a dot under it and the c
  has a dot by it (`n-xc?`).
- `n-f`: a tall upright with a small loop at its top right and a crossbar at middle height (like a long-s or f).
  L14 pos 26 only, `n-f?`, because the upright runs on from a descender of L13 and I cannot tell how much of it
  belongs to the sign; it may be `tp`.

  with no `/`.
