# Reader CD — 2946-c215, L01–L19: notes

Read from the strips `2946-c215-b01..b06 L/R` only, using the SIGN-GUIDE names. My own enlargements are in
`crops/` (pass 1 and settling) and `crops2/` (pass 2 and settling). Positions count tokens from the left in
`c215.txt`, with `[[clear]]`, `/`, `:` and `.` each counted as one token.

## Names I made up

| Name | Shape | Best seen |
|---|---|---|
| `n-zed` | An upright with a bar out to the **left** at its top and a foot out to the **right** at its bottom, like a squared Z or ⅂ plus L. It is as tall as H. The top bar is as long as the foot, so I did not force it into `L` or `Lr`. This is very common; a plain `L` (no top bar) appears only at L13 pos 3. **If the guide's `L` was meant to cover this sign, every `n-zed` should read `L`.** | b04L L16 (pos 4, 11); b05L L18 pos 3 |
| `n-bor` | A small circle with a bar out to the **right** only (o-). It is the mirror of `bol`. | b05L L18 pos 2; b04R L16 pos 48 |
| `n-lc` | A c-shape opening to the right with a bar out to its **left** (-c). It often follows `rc`, with the bar joining them (`rc n-lc` looks like ")-("): L08 pos 6–7, L10 pos 3–4, L18 pos 15–16. It also stands alone (L16 pos 45, L05 pos 12, L01 pos 9, L04 pos 15). | b04R L16 pos 45; b03L L10 pos 3–4 |
| `n-rflag` | A curve like ")" or a hook on a short stem, with a thin stroke rising to the right from its top. | b04R L14 pos 33; b02L L08 pos 4; faint at b05L L18 pos 13 |
| `n-qflag` | A `qs` (loop on a stem) with a straight bar out to the right from the top of the loop (ϙ–). | b04R L16 pos 41; b03R L09 pos 24; b01L L04 pos 16 |
| `n-qbase` | A loop on a stem standing on a long foot bar (a `qs` on a ⊥). The bar might be an underline, but it meets the foot of the stem, so I read it as part of the sign. | b04R/b05R L17 pos 43 |
| `n-hookplus` | A plus with a small hook or loop on the left of the upright, above the bar. It looks a little like a "4". | b01R L02 pos 23 |
| `n-odiv` | A small circle with a dot above it and a dot below it, like ÷ with a circle in place of the bar. | b01R L03 pos 35 |
| `n-vdash` | An upright with **one** short bar out to the right at mid height (⊢). `eb` has two bars. | b03L L10 pos 10 |
| `n-lamloop` | Λ-like: a stem with a small loop at its head and a second leg out to the lower left. | b02R L08 pos 27 |
| `n-boxup` | A closed square with an upright inside it that runs out above the top. `boxbar` is horizontal. | b03L L11 pos 8 |

- **L14** 20 `bof`: a filled circle with a bar out to both sides. - **L15** 21 `phi?`: a circle with an upright inside that does not run out. 33 and 44 `oh`: each circle has a tall stroke rising from it. - **L16** 25 `.`: a dot at mid height right after `cup` 24. 36 `tp` has a mark below it. A dot stands in the left margin before 1 (b04L x≈280), and one stands in the right margin after 51 (b04R x≈1950). I did not transcribe either. 17 `psi`: its lower bar runs on under `plus` 18.
- **L17** 17 `:` has three dots in a column (⁝). The guide has no three-dot point, so I wrote `:`. 44 `H` is smaller and set higher than the other signs. 29–30 `ell bo`: the foot of the ell runs into the left bar of the bo.
- **L18** 13 `n-rflag?` and 15–16 `rc n-lc` are in much paler ink than 1–11. They may have been erased or written over. Then come clear "Offezendone di ..." and the rest of the line (all clear).

- **tp** often has a dot under its stem (L03 25, L04 29, L09 14, L14 13 and 19, L15 32 and 43). I did not make it a token, because it is under a sign, not between signs.
- **bo** is often drawn with the bar visibly crossing the circle (θ with the bar running out), e.g. L15 8 and 15, L16 43. The guide's `theta` does not run out, so I wrote `bo`.

## Where pass 1 and pass 2 differed, and how I settled each (12 positions)

Pass 1 ran from L18 up to L01, each line right to left. Pass 2 ran from L01 down, left to right. Pass 2 used
new crops (`crops2/*_p1..p3`). I settled each difference on a fresh enlargement (`crops2/s_*.png`).

1. L01 pass pos 17: pass 1 had `.` after `M?`, pass 2 had `/`. On the enlargement (`s_L01_dot`) there is no dot after M, only a wide gap. Settled `/`.
2. L01 last sign: pass 1 `plus`, pass 2 `dcross?`. The enlargement (`s_L01_end`) shows a firm plus with a faint smudge above the bar. Settled `plus|dcross`.
3. L02: pass 1 had `/` between `n-hookplus` and `tp`. The gap is about a third of a sign. Settled no `/`.
4. L04: pass 1 had `/` between `tp` and `oh` (after "meglio"). The gap is small (`s_L04_gap`). Settled no `/`.
5. L05: pass 2 had `/` between `psi` and `rc`. The gap is more than half a sign (`s_L05_gap`). Settled `/`.
6. L07: pass 2 had `/` between `psi` and `cup`. The gap is clearly wider than half a sign (`s_L07_gap`). Settled `/`.
7. L08: pass 2 had `/` between `ell` and `perp` (after `tp : psi`). The gap is about one sign wide. Settled `/`.
8. L09: pass 1 `pihat`, pass 2 `pihat^`. The dot stands well above the bar. In line with it above is the foot of L08's `tp`, which carries an under-dot (`s_L09_pihat`). Settled `pihat` (the dot belongs to L08).
9. L13: pass 1 `L`, pass 2 `L|ell`. Enlarged (`s_L13`), the sign is a plain upright with a foot. The loop above it is L12's "ℓt". Settled `L`.
10. L14: pass 2 had no `/` between `ell` and `eb`. On the pass-2 crop the gap measures more than half a sign. Settled `/`.
11. L14: pass 1 `n-rflag`, pass 2 `n-rflag?`. Enlarged (`s_L14_flag`), the curve and its rising thin stroke are clear. Settled `n-rflag`.
12. L15: pass 2 had `/` on both sides of `dplus` (after "`: cup`"), and pass 1 only before it. The gaps on both sides are equal and wide (`s_L15_gap`). Settled `/` on both sides.

## Things the guide missed or I would raise

- The Z-shaped `n-zed` is far more common than a plain L. The guide should say which name it gets.
- Arabic figures 19, 66 and 32 occur here as the guide expected. `#19` stands three times at the start of a run, twice followed by `rc n-lc` (L08 after `n-rflag`, L10 directly). `rc n-lc` and `n-rflag` might be figures or abbreviations, not signs. I could not tell by shape.
- A `:` drawn with three dots (⁝) appears at least at L17 17, perhaps at L15 27.
- The list of lines matches the page: 19 lines, L06 and L19 clear only. **L11 and L13 carry only a few signs (6 and 3).** I found no small writing between the lines.
