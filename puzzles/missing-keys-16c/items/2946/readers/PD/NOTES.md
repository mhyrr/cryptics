# Reader PD — notes (item 2946: pages 2946-c215, 2947-694, 2947-693)

## General conventions I used
- A `[cipher]` run = all signs between two clear words (or line edge). I did not split runs on ordinary
  word-gaps between sign groups, only where clear words intervene. Other readers may count more runs.
- Shapes that look like digits or letters inside sign runs ("19", "66", "32"/"ʒ2", "m", "M", "uf", "ɤ") I treated
  as cipher signs, except where noted.
- y values are read off the strip rulers at the tick marks (the figure label sits about 7 px below its tick;
  I first read the labels and then corrected c215 and 694 by +7). Accuracy about ±5 px. Signature rows lie
  below the strips; their y is taken from the whole page image (approximate).
- On 2947-693 the R strips sit visibly higher than the L strips at the same line; I matched L and R halves by
  continuing sign sequences, and gave each gloss to the line whose signs lie directly below it in the same half.
- Resolution: the page images are 1272 px wide; the gloss is 4–6 px x-height. My enlargements (in `crops/`)
  add size, not detail. Many gloss words are therefore marked `[?]` or `[...]`.
