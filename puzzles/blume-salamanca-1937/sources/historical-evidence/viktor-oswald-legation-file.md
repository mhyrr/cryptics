## Identity: this is our Viktor

The oswald-trail agent (`agent-oswald-trail.md`, row 10) treated this file as a probable namesake because of the
"1912" in its title. That is wrong. The French expulsion decree copied into Unterlagen_0000002 names
"OSWALD (Moritz Victor) né le 15 Novembre 1909" (born 15 November 1909), the birth date on telegram 1's red note.
A 1947 letter in the same unit has "M. Victor Oswald, né le 15 novembre 1909 à Lucerne, domicilié à
Saint-Sébastien" ("born 15 November 1909 in Lucerne, living in San Sebastián"). The "1912" appears beside
"Füs." (Füsilier, infantry private) in a 1939 military letter, "Füs. OSWALD Victor, 1912, 111/69"; it is
probably a military register entry, not a birth year. A second catalogue entry, E2001D#1000/1551#7346*,
"Oswald, Victor M., San Sebastian (Akten s. B.31.51.F. von 1946-1948) [kassiert]" ("files see B.31.51.F.;
destroyed"), 1937–1945, points to this file's reference.

## What is not in the file

No key, cipher, codebook, BLUME, Salamanca, wool, Patvag or Madrid firm name. OCR searches for *Blume, Salamanca,
chiffr, Schlüssel, Code, Wolle, lana, Patvag* returned nothing. The French consular report itself (the source of
row 4) is not in the file. The Swiss Legation in Burgos/San Sebastián files for 1937 are not here.
