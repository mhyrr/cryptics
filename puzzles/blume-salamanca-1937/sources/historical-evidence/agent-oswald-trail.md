# Oswald trail: businesses, correspondence, more cipher traffic (agent notes, 2026-10-20)

Scope: follow the documented Oswald businesses (Madrid trading company, Patvag AG, wool/compensation trade) to
letters, replies, telegrams, codebooks or keys. Read-only. English is given beside every German or French quotation.

## 1. Summary

The BAR dossier (E4320B#1974/47#277) contains no further Oswald traffic outside subdossier 0000066. The OCR of all
219 files holds the Oswald name only in units 68, 69, 70 and 71, and the image reads of every page I viewed in
subdossier 66 show no key, plaintext, codebook, Salamanca or Blume. The one new document in the dossier is Hunziker's
19 April 1937 letter to "Osswald in Burgos, Banco Guipuzoano, San Sebastian", which gives Viktor's 1937 postal
address as c/o a bank. It is not cipher traffic. Outside the dossier, BAR holds at least seven Oswald-related
dossiers, none digitised. The most promising are the Swiss Legation Madrid property-protection dossier
"Oswald, Viktor" (E2200.38-04#1000/483#415*) and the Foreign Affairs file "Oswald, Madrid" (E2001D#1000/1551#5627*,
1936-1938). The Bochsler book excerpt gives Victor Oswald as a Madrid arms dealer with close Franco-regime ties, but no Salamanca recipient, no firm name and no telegram detail. **The wall:** the Madrid firm's name and Viktor's Salamanca
correspondent appear in no digitised or openly readable source I could reach. The documents that would breach it are listed in section 5.

## 5. Access failures, gaps, and what would breach the wall

Failures: HLS (403); dodis (bot-protection page, not bypassed); BAR items 1689905 and the 1974 and Madrid-legation dossiers are not digitised and ordering needs a login, which I did not use. The Playwright browser was shared and was twice reset to unrelated pages during the work, so the earlier portal results were re-run.

Not done: PARES, BOE/Gaceta de Madrid (Registro Mercantil, 1932-36), Archivo General de la Administración, CDMH Salamanca, the Swiss Verrechnungsstelle (E7160) and Handelsabteilung (E7110) searches, Emser Werke/Bundesanwaltschaft E4320B files on Werner Oswald (a search for "Oswald Werner" found no such file titled that way), the full text of Bochsler's chapter "Bilder für Hitler, Bomben für Franco".

Documents that would breach the wall, in order of expected value:
1. E2200.38-04#1000/483#415* (Madrid legation, "Oswald, Viktor"): a request for access or a copy. It could name the Madrid firm, its premises and its Spanish partners.
2. E2001D#1000/1551#5627* "Oswald, Madrid" (1936-1938): access review is needed; it may carry his 1936-37 movements and addressees.
3. Bochsler, *Nylon und Napalm*, chapter "Bilder für Hitler, Bomben für Franco" and its endnotes. The notes may cite Oswald family papers or Bundesanwaltschaft files (it was written after the earlier archive visit, and the author has been contacted by this project: see sources/outreach-bochsler.md).
4. A Spanish telegraph address list for Salamanca 1936-37 or the receiving telegraph office's copies (not found).
5. The Banco Guipuzcoano's records (Burgos / San Sebastian branch) as Viktor's 1937 address: Bank archive, not searched.

## 6. Unverified claims

* That "Oswald, Madrid" (1689905), "Oswald, Viktor" (Madrid legation) and "Oswald Viktor M, weitere" (1974-75) concern Viktor M. Oswald b. 1909: no document links them; first names or initials only.
* That "Oswald, Victor, 1912, Madrid" is anyone related: the birth year differs.
* That the catalogue date range 1865-1954 on the Madrid legation dossier covers only part of the file.
* That the April 1937 "telegramm" Hunziker mentions was sent by Werner Oswald: the letter says only "von Zürich".
* That Viktor was a "Bankangestellter" at Banco Guipuzcoano: the police note says bank clerk; the bank is the address line only.
* HLS claim (via search summary) that Viktor and Rudolf co-founded Patvag in 1933 with Werner: HLS page not read.
* Bochsler's description of Victor as a Madrid-based arms dealer in the sample refers to the mid-1950s structure, not 1937.
