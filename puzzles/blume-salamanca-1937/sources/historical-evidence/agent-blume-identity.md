No identification of "BLUME" was achieved. Every route that could be reached
returned no relevant hit, and the primary-source routes that would decide the
question (Spanish directories with telegraphic addresses, Salamanca newspapers,
PARES, Bundesarchiv/PAAA) were not searchable in full text from this

No candidate was identified, so no Oswald link is documented.

### Update (second pass, browser)
Still no identification. Newly established: (i) the full OCR text of DGFP Series D vol. III
(Germany and the Spanish Civil War, 1936-39; 2.5 MB text, 67 hits for "Salamanca") contains
no "Blume"/"BLUME" or near-OCR variants; (ii) BOE Gazeta historical text search "Blume" returns
9 documents, none dated 1936-39 (1851-1892 and 1958 only); (iii) one weak lead: a Google Books
snippet from Ramon Hidalgo Salazar, La ayuda alemana a Espana 1936-1939 (1975), p. 223,
Appendix II "Condecorados de la Legion Condor", shows "... Salamanca ... Blume Otto ...",
i.e. an Otto Blume among decorated Condor Legion personnel. Snippet only, adjacency of
"Salamanca" unverified, no Oswald link; the telegram address has no first name. Prensa
Historica, PARES and BNE remained blocked (429/403) even in a normal browser.
