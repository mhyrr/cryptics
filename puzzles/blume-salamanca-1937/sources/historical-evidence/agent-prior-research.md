## 1. Summary
Note 44 could not be read. The ebook's Google Books preview is limited and in-book search for Heinzmann, Salamanca, Burgos and Spanien returned nothing, and a sanity check was not possible (see §4). No English edition was found. The free front-matter sample (30 pages) shows the book's table of contents, so the telegrams most likely sit in the family-history chapter «Bilder für Hitler, Bomben für Franco», but this is inference. Cryptiana's 2023 post (re-read) adds that Bochsler herself found the telegrams and commented on Schmeh's Facebook post; it also records her doubt about Oswald's "wool business" explanation. No new trace of Heinzmann/Matteotti/Liniger/Viveksanth was found, and no further Oswald cipher traffic or other Bochsler archive cites around note 44. The main remaining routes are contacting Bochsler via the publisher, or seeing a print copy.

## 2. What note 44 says
Not seen. Only second-hand: Bourdeau's NOTES.md says note 44 "records a failed 2020 decipherment attempt by Peter Heinzmann, Carlo Matteotti" (and Liniger, Viveksanth) and "their report was not found online" (CLAIMANT, fetched via raw.githubusercontent.com). Wording of the note, page number and the chapter are all unknown.

What the book's own front matter does give (PRIMARY, publisher sample, read):
- Chapter list includes «Bilder für Hitler, Bomben für Franco» (*Pictures for Hitler, bombs for Franco*) under "Familiengeschichten", and «Treibstoff (1936–1945)». The telegrams (Jan 1937) fit the former or the start of the latter.
- Vorwort: Bochsler says Ems-Chemie refused archive access; her main source was the Swiss Federal Archives (13 running metres of subsidy-control files), plus "Dossiers der Bundesanwaltschaft, der Fremdenpolizei sowie des Militär- und Volkswirtschaftsdepartements" (*dossiers of the Federal Prosecutor, the aliens police, and the Military and Economic departments*), Archiv für Zeitgeschichte ETH, state/city/community archives, private papers, and in Germany the Bundesarchiv (Militärarchiv Freiburg), Institut für Zeitgeschichte Munich, Krupp-Thyssen, Bayer, Stinnes archives. No Spanish archive is named there. She says she quotes sources "ausführlicher als üblich" (*at more length than usual*), so the telegram text may be reproduced in the book.
- Same sample: Victor Oswald is "ein in Madrid lebender Geschäftsmann und Waffenhändler mit besten Beziehungen zum Franco-Regime" (*a businessman and arms dealer living in Madrid, with excellent connections to the Franco regime*). The archive sections (Archive, Anmerkungen) are listed in the ToC but not in the sample.
