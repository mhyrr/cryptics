### 1219 — 2018-12-15T23:13:33 — -16.0, 168.0 — Ambrym / Malakula, Vanuatu
- **Verdict:** REPORTED-VOLCANIC
- **Event:** Ambrym intra-caldera eruption, lava-lake drainage, SE-rift dike intrusion, caldera subsidence (14–18 Dec 2018). Ambrym caldera is at roughly -16.25, 168.12. Reported timeline (UTC), from Shreve et al. 2019:
  - 14 Dec, 13:00–20:00: crisis begins with M<3 caldera events.
  - 14 Dec, ~23:20–23:40: Himawari-8 detects the intra-caldera eruption.
  - **15 Dec, 20:21: a Mw 5.6 strike-slip earthquake, after which "a sharp increase in the seismic moment release" marks the start of magma propagation into the SE rift.**
  - **15 Dec, 23:10: Sentinel-2 shows the lava flows at full extent and the dike tip still leaving the caldera.**
  - 16 Dec 15:34 and 17 Dec 01:49: two Mw ~5.6 CLVD events with long-period content.
  - At least four further LP signals at SANVU (~150 km) on 16–17 Dec.
  - Caldera subsidence of ~2.5 m between 15 Dec 00:24 and 18 Dec 06:10 (SAR). The Methods put its onset on 16 Dec based on seismicity.
  - Magma volume >0.4 km³. Dike propagation ends ~17 Dec 16:00, with a submarine eruption at the rift tip.
- **Distance from grid point:** ~30 km to the caldera. The rift extends ~30 km further east.
- **Already linked to a seismic detection by someone?** Not to this Poli detection. The paper uses the VMGD VT catalogue for moment release and notes LP events at SANVU on 16–17 Dec. It does not mention VLP signals or a 23:13 UTC signal on 15 Dec.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC6906323/fullTextXML (SCHOLARLY). Shreve et al. 2019, *Sci. Rep.* 9:18868, doi:10.1038/s41598-019-55141-7. Gives the timeline and quotes above.
  - https://www.usgs.gov/news/volcano-watch-rise-and-fall-lava-lakes (SECONDARY; USGS HVO). Summit earthquakes began 14 Dec, followed by an intrusion along the SE rift with ground cracking; pumice indicated a submarine eruption.
  - https://www.loopvanuatu.com/node/82530 (POPULAR). Relays Vanuatu Geohazards: the lava lakes in Benbow and Marum disappeared from 16 Dec 2018.
- **Searches run:** `Ambrym eruption 15 December 2018 caldera subsidence seismic`; `Ambrym 15 December 2018 earthquakes lava lakes disappeared Vanuatu Geohazards dike intrusion`; `"2018-12-15" OR "15 December 2018" Ambrym seismic long-period OR "very long period" caldera collapse detection`
- **Notes / near-misses:** The detection falls ~3 h after the catalogued Mw 5.6 (20:21) and inside the reported window of sharply increased moment release and dike propagation. No source names a specific physical event at ~23:10 UTC. What is published is that a large dike intrusion and subsidence episode was under way at that hour, ~30 km away. The input says no catalogued M≥4 matches the detection itself, so it is not the 20:21 event. The GVP page returned 403.
