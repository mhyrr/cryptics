# Reference sweep, batch 1 (10 detections)

Method: web search (English plus Spanish/Russian where local reporting is likely), then fetches of the best hits. I also ran catalog queries around each grid point: USGS ComCat FDSN (M≥2.5, 400 km, ±~12–36 h) and EMSC seismicportal FDSN (M≥2.0, 3.2° ≈ 355 km, same windows). Of the twenty catalog queries, only the Iceland window and two off-target windows (Queen Charlotte, Hindu Kush) returned events. None of these readings decides the source. Each entry reports only what has been published.

### 178 — 2011-02-27T17:28:58 — 65.5, -21.0 — north-west Iceland
- **Verdict:** REPORTED-OTHER (a smaller earthquake in the Krýsuvík swarm, SW Iceland)
- **Event:** Krýsuvík earthquake swarm, Reykjanes Peninsula, about 24 km S of Reykjavík. The main shock is 2011-02-27 17:27:36.3 UTC at 63.928, -22.034, depth 1 km, M4.1 (EMSC page, IMO as data provider). The EMSC seismicportal FDSN gives 17:27:36.6 UTC, 63.914, -22.034, M3.7, with a foreshock at 17:25:21.8 UTC (M3.4) at the same spot. The main shock origin is about 1.4 min before the Poli detection time.
- **Distance from grid point:** about 183 km (computed from the coordinates above)
- **Already linked to a seismic detection by someone?** no (no published link found)
- **Sources:**
  - https://emsc-csem.org/Earthquake_information/earthquake.php?id=210372 (PRIMARY, catalog): M4.1, 2011-02-27 17:27:36.3 UTC, 63.928, -22.034, depth 1 km, "Iceland Region".
  - EMSC seismicportal FDSN event query (PRIMARY, catalog): 17:27:36.6Z, 63.9143, -22.0336, M3.7; 17:25:21.8Z, 63.92, -22.03, M3.4.
  - https://globalrumblings.blogspot.de/2011/02/major-quake-swarm-krisuvik-volcano.html (POPULAR): describes a major Krýsuvík swarm in late Feb 2011, with an IMO map that separates the 27 Feb events.
  - USGS ComCat FDSN query, 400 km, M≥2.5, same window: returned no events (PRIMARY, catalog). The event does not appear in ComCat at M≥2.5 within 400 km of the grid point.
- **Searches run:** "27 February 2011 Iceland landslide OR avalanche OR earthquake north Iceland"; "Krýsuvík earthquake swarm 27 February 2011 magnitude 4 Icelandic Met Office"
- **Notes / near-misses:** The timing fits closely. The earthquake is a local M3.7–4.1, and the agencies disagree on its magnitude. Whether a quake that size can account for an Mw_d 4.71 long-period detection is a question for the pilot, not for this sweep. It also sits near the pilot's "catalogued M≥4" exclusion line: EMSC/IMO give 4.1, while EMSC's own FDSN gives 3.7 and USGS has no event at M≥2.5. No north Iceland landslide was found for this date. Tröllaskagi slides on 14 Oct 2011 and 20 Sep 2012 were found, both wrong dates. I did not find the IMO's own catalog page.

## Unverified claims

- I recall the Chilcotin River / Farwell Canyon river-blocking landslide (night of 30–31 July) as 2024. The search results did not give the year, and I did not verify it. Either way it is not near 10 July 2018.
- I recall Johnsons Landing (Kootenay Lake) as 12 July 2012. The search summary implied 2018, which I believe is wrong. Not fetched.
- The 24.kg / Current Time reports of deadly mudflows in Kulob that surfaced in the Tajikistan search were undated in the results. I believe they are from 2024, not 2022, but did not verify.
- The Queulat hanging-glacier collapse is dated September 2022 by Gulf News and Reuters-derived reports. I did not find a 2021 Queulat event, but I did not search Chilean SENAPRED/ONEMI archives directly.
- The EMSC page attributes the 2011-02-27 Krýsuvík M4.1 to the Icelandic Met Office. I did not find the IMO's own catalog entry or its magnitude type.
- No Lamont/Ekström long-period landslide-detection list for these dates was found online. Their catalog is not publicly browsable by date as far as these searches showed, so "already linked" stays "unknown", not "no", for most entries.
