### 808 — 2015-07-31T17:05:43Z — 24.0, 92.5 — Mizoram / Manipur / Chin State / Sylhet–Tripura border
- **Verdict:** REPORTED-LANDSLIDE
- **Event:** **Tonzang landslide**, Chin State, Myanmar. NASA links coordinates 23.54385 N, 93.88092 E (NE of Tonzang). Runout 5.9 km; seismic inversion mass 395 Mt, about 160 million m³ at 2.5 t/m³; main runout about 160 s; peak speed about 50 m/s. It was triggered by the Cyclone Komen rains of "late July / early August" 2015. **I could not find the exact date or time of its seismic signal in any source.** A second reported landslide in the window: the **Joumol landslide**, Chandel district, Manipur, "about 168 km south of [Imphal]", reported at "around 6.30 in the morning" (IST) on 1 Aug 2015. That is about 01:00 UTC on 1 Aug, roughly 8 h after the detection. Twenty killed, 12 houses destroyed, no volume given.
- **Distance from grid point:** Tonzang about 150 km. Joumol about 160–180 km (location only roughly placed from "168 km south of Imphal").
- **Already linked to a seismic detection by someone?** Yes for Tonzang: Colin Stark and Göran Ekström (Lamont-Doherty) found its signal in global seismic data (Landslide Blog, 9 Nov 2015; NASA EO, 8 Nov 2015). The source does not say whether it is the same signal as Poli #808. In the Landslide Blog's 29 Oct 2015 post, the figure filenames encode two earlier Ekström landslide detections, `E201507311547A` (31 Jul 2015 15:47 UTC) and `E201508010228A` (1 Aug 2015 02:28 UTC). The post says these two "do not appear to correspond to this landslide". Reading the filenames as timestamps is my inference. Neither time is within 1–3 min of the Poli detection (15:47 is 78 min earlier).
- **Sources:**
  - https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/satellite-spots-massive-tonzang-landslide-86924 (SECONDARY; NASA Earth Observatory, 8 Nov 2015) — Tonzang slide after the Komen rains (landfall about 30 Jul), coordinates as above, 395 Mt, about 160 s, 50 m/s. Stark and Ekström found it in seismic data. Imaged by EO-1 on 23 Oct 2015. No event date given.
  - https://blogs.agu.org/landslideblog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/16760 (POPULAR, expert author D. Petley; 9 Nov 2015) — "Colin and Goran Ekstrom have gone back to the seismic data and have now found the signal". Mass 395 Mt, about 160 million m³. The rainfall was "end of July / early part of August". No signal time is given.
  - https://blogs.agu.org/landslideblog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/16612 (POPULAR; 29 Oct 2015) — two Ekström landslide seismic events, with figure filenames E201507311547A and E201508010228A. They "do not appear to correspond to this landslide" (Tonzang). The post says a cluster of at least three exceptional landslides exists.
  - https://assamtribune.com/amp/landslide-kills-20-in-manipur (POPULAR; dateline Imphal, 1 Aug) — Joumol, Khengjoi sub-division, "about 168 km south of here", "around 6.30 in the morning", rain "non-stop from Wednesday to Friday".
  - https://www.thesangaiexpress.com/Encyc/2025/11/15/chongboi-haokip-mcihort-on-august-1-2015-the-hills-of-chandel-district-in-manipur-gave-way-after-nearly-a-week.html (POPULAR; 2025 retrospective) — Joumol, 1 Aug 2015, 20 killed, 12 homes.
- **Searches run:** "Manipur landslide 31 July 2015 OR 1 August 2015 Joumol Chandel"; "Joumol landslide Khengjoy Chandel "Friday" OR "night" 2015 time village buried 20 killed"; "landslide 31 July 2015 Mizoram OR Tripura OR Chittagong OR Bandarban OR Chin State Myanmar Komen"; "Tonzang landslide Chin State 2015 Ekström Stark seismic date landslide blog"; ""Tonzang" landslide "31 July 2015" OR "July 31, 2015" OR "1 August 2015""
- **Notes / near-misses:** This is a strong spatial and size candidate in the right rain window, but the date is not established. Treat it as candidate, not match, until the Stark/Ekström origin time for Tonzang is found.
